Common questions

When is the best time to visit Himachal Pradesh?

Across the 42 curated destinations in Himachal Pradesh, April, May, October and November are the months with the most places in peak season. January has the fewest, at 1.

How many places are worth visiting in Himachal Pradesh?

This catalogue lists 42 in Himachal Pradesh, chosen for the landscape or the heritage rather than for size. Plain cities are deliberately left out unless the trip is the heritage itself. The strongest themes here are mountains (35), offbeat (30), trek (20), heritage (13) and culture (10).
