Common questions

When is the best time to visit Kerala?

Across the 31 curated destinations in Kerala, January, February, November and December are the months with the most places in peak season. April has the fewest, at 0.

How many places are worth visiting in Kerala?

This catalogue lists 31 in Kerala, chosen for the landscape or the heritage rather than for size. Plain cities are deliberately left out unless the trip is the heritage itself. The strongest themes here are offbeat (19), culture (11), trek (10), wildlife (8) and lakes (7).

What kind of places are in Kerala?

19 offbeat, 11 culture, 10 trek, 8 wildlife and 7 lakes. Every entry carries its own peak, shoulder and avoid months, so nothing is recommended in a month it should not be.
