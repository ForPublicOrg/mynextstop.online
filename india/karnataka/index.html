Across the 39 curated destinations in Karnataka, January, February, November and December are the months with the most places in peak season. April has the fewest, at 0.

How many places are worth visiting in Karnataka?

This catalogue lists 39 in Karnataka, chosen for the landscape or the heritage rather than for size. Plain cities are deliberately left out unless the trip is the heritage itself. The strongest themes here are offbeat (28), heritage (17), spiritual (10), trek (9) and waterfalls (7).

What kind of places are in Karnataka?

28 offbeat, 17 heritage, 10 spiritual, 9 trek and 7 waterfalls. Every entry carries its own peak, shoulder and avoid months, so nothing is recommended in a month it should not be.
