Common questions

When is the best time to visit Jammu and Kashmir?

Across the 19 curated destinations in Jammu and Kashmir, May, June, September and October are the months with the most places in peak season. March has the fewest, at 2.

How many places are worth visiting in Jammu and Kashmir?

This catalogue lists 19 in Jammu and Kashmir, chosen for the landscape or the heritage rather than for size. Plain cities are deliberately left out unless the trip is the heritage itself. The strongest themes here are offbeat (13), mountains (11), trek (8), heritage (6) and lakes (4).

What kind of places are in Jammu and Kashmir?

13 offbeat, 11 mountains, 8 trek, 6 heritage and 4 lakes. Every entry carries its own peak, shoulder and avoid months, so nothing is recommended in a month it should not be.
