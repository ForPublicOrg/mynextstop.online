How many places are worth visiting in Maharashtra?

This catalogue lists 39 in Maharashtra, chosen for the landscape or the heritage rather than for size. Plain cities are deliberately left out unless the trip is the heritage itself. The strongest themes here are offbeat (17), heritage (15), mountains (12), trek (10) and spiritual (9).
