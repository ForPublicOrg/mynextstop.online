Haa Valley season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Dec can be crisp and clear, but Jan and Feb bring sub-zero nights and snow that can shut the Chele La road from Paro.

Summer March to May Prime time

Apr and May are lovely: rhododendrons on Chele La, yaks moving up and green barley fields along the Haa Chhu.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jul and Aug are wet, with cloud on the passes and slips on the Chele La road; Jun and Sep are greener and calmer.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct and Nov bring the clearest Jomolhari views from Chele La and the Haa Tshechu in the Lhakhang Karpo courtyard.

Signature festival

Haa Tshechu (Oct or Nov)

What Haa Valley is actually like

Farmhouse homestays with hot stone baths, buckwheat hoentay dumplings and very few other visitors.

Things to do in Haa Valley

The local places worth your time in and around Haa Valley, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Lhakhang Karpo

    Temple · 3 km out

    The White Temple, said to date from the 7th century, whose courtyard hosts the Haa Tshechu masked dances.

  • Lhakhang Nagpo

    Temple · 3 km out

    The Black Temple, twin shrine to Lhakhang Karpo a short walk away, tied to the same founding legend.

  • Chele La

    Viewpoint · 7 km out

    Road pass at 3,988 m between Paro and Haa; dense prayer flags and Jomolhari views at sunrise on clear days.

  • Kila Gompa

    Monastery · 6 km out

    Cliffside nunnery below Chele La on the Paro side, reached by a forest walk from the pass road.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Haa Valley?

April to May and October to November is peak season in Haa Valley. March, June, September and December is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid January to February and July to August. Apr and May are lovely: rhododendrons on Chele La, yaks moving up and green barley fields along the Haa Chhu.

How many days do you need in Haa Valley?

2 days is the usual stay. Farmhouse homestays with hot stone baths, buckwheat hoentay dumplings and very few other visitors.

How do you get to Haa Valley?

About 61 km from Paro over Chele La, around 2.5 h; Paro airport is the nearest. Route permit needed. Practical ways in: flight and road.

Is Haa Valley expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Haa Valley?

Haa Tshechu (Oct or Nov). Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Haa Valley?

Lhakhang Karpo, Lhakhang Nagpo, Chele La and Kila Gompa. The White Temple, said to date from the 7th century, whose courtyard hosts the Haa Tshechu masked dances.
