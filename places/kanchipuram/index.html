Kanchipuram season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb at 20-30 C is when you can walk Kailasanathar, Ekambareswarar and Varadaraja in one long unhurried day.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-Jun hits 40 C on open sandstone courtyards with no tree cover, and even the weavers stop work through the afternoon.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

June still touches 38 C; Jul-Sep stays hot and mostly dry at 35 C, as the southwest monsoon largely passes Kanchipuram by.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Oct-Nov gets the northeast monsoon in hard evening bursts; mornings stay clear and the temple tanks finally fill up.

What Kanchipuram is actually like

Chennai day-trippers arrive by 10am and are gone by 4pm; stay the night and 8th-century Kailasanathar is yours alone.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Kanchipuram?

December to February is peak season in Kanchipuram. March and November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Dec-Feb at 20-30 C is when you can walk Kailasanathar, Ekambareswarar and Varadaraja in one long unhurried day.

How many days do you need in Kanchipuram?

1 day is the usual stay. Chennai day-trippers arrive by 10am and are gone by 4pm; stay the night and 8th-century Kailasanathar is yours alone.

How do you get to Kanchipuram?

Chennai airport 70 km, trains from Chennai Egmore and Beach, buses every 15 min from CMBT taking about 2 h. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Kanchipuram expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.
