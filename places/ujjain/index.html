Ujjain season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Cool mornings for the 4am Bhasma Aarti queue. Most comfortable season for the ghats.

Summer March to May Shoulder

April-June heat punishes marble floors and exposed ghats. Go before sunrise or skip.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Kshipra rises, city greens up: decent for the temple circuit if you don't mind rain queues.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Temperatures drop through October, pleasant for temple-hopping, quieter than December's rush.

Signature festival

Oct-Nov: Kartik Mela fair on the Kshipra ghats

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Ujjain?

October to February is peak season in Ujjain. March and July to September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Temperatures drop through October, pleasant for temple-hopping, quieter than December's rush.

How many days do you need in Ujjain?

2 days is the usual stay. Pilgrim town through and through. You'll be one of few solo leisure travellers.

How do you get to Ujjain?

Direct trains to Ujjain; Indore airport 55km, 1h taxi. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Ujjain expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

Is there a festival in Ujjain?

Oct-Nov: Kartik Mela fair on the Kshipra ghats. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
