Lo Manthang (Upper Mustang) season by season

Winter December to February Do not go

Avoid: snow and bitter wind, many families move south for the season, most lodges close and the road can be blocked.

Summer March to May Prime time

Tiji Festival fills the walled city with masked dances in May; Mar and Apr are cold but clear and very quiet.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

The rain shadow keeps trails dry and open while the rest of Nepal is wet, but landslides below Jomsom still disrupt travel.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct is crisp and golden with clear skies over the canyons; by late Nov villagers start leaving for the winter.

Signature festival

Tiji Festival (May)

What Lo Manthang (Upper Mustang) is actually like

Mud-brick lodges run by Loba families, butter tea and tsampa, and a handful of guided trekking groups and jeep tours.

Things to do in Lo Manthang

The local places worth your time in and around Lo Manthang, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Jampa Lhakhang

    Monastery · in town

    15th century temple inside the walls with mandala murals, carefully restored by an international team.

  • Thubchen Gompa

    Monastery · in town

    Huge red assembly hall from the 15th century with restored wall paintings; ask at the gate if it is locked.

  • Lo Manthang Royal Palace

    Palace · in town

    Four-storey palace of the former Raja of Mustang, standing at the heart of the walled town.

  • Namgyal Gompa

    Monastery · 1 km out

    Active hilltop monastery on a ridge just outside the walls, with views back over the walled city and its fields.

  • Jhong Cave (Chhoser)

    Cave · 6 km out

    Multi-storey cave complex carved high into the cliff at Chhoser, a short jeep or horse ride north of town.

  • Ghar Gompa

    Monastery · 9 km out

    Lo Gekar, one of the oldest gompas in the region, linked to Guru Rinpoche, on the trail south of town.

  • Charang (Tsarang)

    Village · 10 km out

    Charang village with an old five-storey palace and a big red gompa above a deep canyon, on the way up.

Getting to Lo Manthang (Upper Mustang)

6 to 7 h by jeep from Jomsom. Upper Mustang RAP (USD 50 a day) through a registered agency, plus ACAP.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Lo Manthang (Upper Mustang)?

May and September to October is peak season in Lo Manthang (Upper Mustang). March to April, June to August and November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid December to February. Tiji Festival fills the walled city with masked dances in May; Mar and Apr are cold but clear and very quiet.

How many days do you need in Lo Manthang (Upper Mustang)?

4 days is the usual stay. Mud-brick lodges run by Loba families, butter tea and tsampa, and a handful of guided trekking groups and jeep tours.

How do you get to Lo Manthang (Upper Mustang)?

6 to 7 h by jeep from Jomsom. Upper Mustang RAP (USD 50 a day) through a registered agency, plus ACAP. Practical ways in: road.

Is Lo Manthang (Upper Mustang) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Splurge by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

Is there a festival in Lo Manthang (Upper Mustang)?

Tiji Festival (May). Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Lo Manthang?

Jampa Lhakhang, Thubchen Gompa, Lo Manthang Royal Palace, Namgyal Gompa, Jhong Cave (Chhoser) and Ghar Gompa, and more. 15th century temple inside the walls with mandala murals, carefully restored by an international team.
