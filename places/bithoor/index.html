Bithoor season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Peak at 8-24 °C: mist over the Ganga at Brahmavart Ghat and the Valmiki ashram steps empty by mid-morning.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-Jun is 45 °C with the Ganga low and the ghat stone too hot to stand on; nothing here is worth that heat.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Aug-Sep the Ganga rises over the lower ghat steps, the 1857 sites turn to mud and Kanpur's traffic worsens.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Kartik Purnima in November turns the ghats into a one-night mela; otherwise Oct-Nov is the calmest it gets.

Signature festival

Nov: Kartik Purnima bathing mela at Brahmavart Ghat

Things to do in Bithoor

The local places worth your time in and around Bithoor, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Brahmavart Ghat

    Ghat · in town

    The main Ganga ghat, where Brahma is said to have held his yajna; busy at dawn aarti, empty by mid morning

  • Patthar Ghat

    Ghat · 2 km out

    Sandstone ghat and Siddhnath temple built by Tikait Rai in the 1780s; the carved terrace is best in late light

  • Valmiki Ashram

    Temple · 3 km out

    Hilltop ashram where Sita is said to have raised Lav and Kush; a short flight of steps, and the Ganga view is the point

  • Dhruv Teela

    Heritage · 1 km out

    Mound with a small temple where the boy Dhruv meditated; free, and the top gives the best view over the river bend

  • Nana Saheb Smarak

    Heritage · 1 km out

    Memorial on the site of the Peshwa palace burnt after 1857; signage is thin, so read up before you come

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Bithoor?

November to February is peak season in Bithoor. March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to September. Kartik Purnima in November turns the ghats into a one-night mela; otherwise Oct-Nov is the calmest it gets.

How many days do you need in Bithoor?

1 day is the usual stay. Ghat-side calm 25 km from Kanpur's mills, with priests and a few 1857 history buffs; sleep back in Kanpur.

How do you get to Bithoor?

Kanpur Central 25 km, then a bus or auto up the bypass in about 50 min; Lucknow is 100 km by road. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Bithoor expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Bithoor?

Nov: Kartik Purnima bathing mela at Brahmavart Ghat. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Bithoor?

Brahmavart Ghat, Patthar Ghat, Valmiki Ashram, Dhruv Teela and Nana Saheb Smarak. The main Ganga ghat, where Brahma is said to have held his yajna; busy at dawn aarti, empty by mid morning.
