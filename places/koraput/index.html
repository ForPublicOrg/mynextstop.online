Koraput season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb mornings sit near 13 C at 870 m with mist over the coffee and pepper plots; dry, clear and cheap.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar-May tops out near 33 C, hot for the Eastern Ghats but mild by Odisha standards, and hazy with dust.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun-Aug the ghats are fog-bound and the hill roads to Deomali and Duduma get slick and often washed out.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is the best month: the Kolab valley is green and Duduma still in spate; Parab follows in December.

Dec: Parab tribal dance festival

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Koraput?

October to February is peak season in Koraput. March to May and September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to August. October is the best month: the Kolab valley is green and Duduma still in spate; Parab follows in December.

How many days do you need in Koraput?

2 days is the usual stay. Slow tribal-district town with cheap hotels and a Jagannath temple; travellers arrive off the Araku train.

How do you get to Koraput?

Koraput Junction is on the Vizag-Kirandul line, a slow 7-8 h climb; buses from Jeypore and Rayagada. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Koraput expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Koraput?

Dec: Parab tribal dance festival. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
