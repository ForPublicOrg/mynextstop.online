Ramtek season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

December to February is the good stretch: 12 C mornings for the climb to the Gadh Mandir and low evening light on the ghats of Ambala tank.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March is warm but fine early; May is Vidarbha at its worst, 45 C on bare rock, and only the shade at Khindsi lake is any use before dusk.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

July to September is the Meghadoot's own season: cloud dragging over Sindur hill, Khindsi full and the Nagardhan fort track soft but drivable.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October and November are the pick: mild days, the hill green after the rains and Pench's Khursapar gate open 40 km on for an easy add-on.

Signature festival

Mar/Apr: Ram Navami fair climbs the temple hill

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Ramtek?

October to February is peak season in Ramtek. March and July to September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May. December to February is the good stretch: 12 C mornings for the climb to the Gadh Mandir and low evening light on the ghats of Ambala tank.

How many days do you need in Ramtek?

2 days is the usual stay. Lake resorts at Khindsi and simple town lodges; evenings are the ghats at Ambala tank and a bell on the hill.

How do you get to Ramtek?

Nagpur airport and railhead 55 km, buses every 30 minutes on the Jabalpur road, about 1.5 h. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Ramtek expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Ramtek?

Mar/Apr: Ram Navami fair climbs the temple hill. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
