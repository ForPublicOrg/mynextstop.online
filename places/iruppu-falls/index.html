Iruppu Falls (Kurchi) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Jan the falls still come down hard off the Brahmagiri and the ridge trek to Narimale runs on a permit; February starts to thin it out.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar-May the drop shrinks to a trickle and Kodagu warms up, but the sanctuary path stays open until the first rain and the forest is silent.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jul-Aug the sanctuary gate shuts, the Kutta road slips and leeches take the steps; June and late September are the only honest windows.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov is the best of it: full flow, mist in the evergreen forest and Nagarhole's Kutta gate close enough for a dawn safari.

Signature festival

Feb/Mar: Shivaratri bathing in the Lakshmana Tirtha

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Iruppu Falls (Kurchi)?

October to January is peak season in Iruppu Falls (Kurchi). February to March, June and September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid July to August. Oct-Nov is the best of it: full flow, mist in the evergreen forest and Nagarhole's Kutta gate close enough for a dawn safari.

How many days do you need in Iruppu Falls (Kurchi)?

2 days is the usual stay. Coffee and pepper homestays around Srimangala and Kurchi; evenings are frog noise and one shared dinner table.

How do you get to Iruppu Falls (Kurchi)?

Bus from Gonikoppal to Srimangala, then 8 km by auto to the gate; Mysuru is 120 km and about 3h30 out. Practical ways in: road.

Is Iruppu Falls (Kurchi) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Iruppu Falls (Kurchi)?

Feb/Mar: Shivaratri bathing in the Lakshmana Tirtha. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
