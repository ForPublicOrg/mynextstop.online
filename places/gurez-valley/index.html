Gurez Valley (Dawar) season by season

Winter December to February Do not go

Dec-Feb Razdan Pass is buried and Dawar is cut off; the valley is shut to outsiders for roughly six months a year.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar-Apr the pass is still shut; it usually clears in May, but late snow and slides make the first sumos a real gamble.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Jun-Sep is Gurez at its best: 8-22 C, dry-ish, Habba Khatoon peak lit at dusk, and every homestay in Dawar open.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Early Oct is gold and empty, then snow starts closing Razdan through November and the last sumos out get nervous.

What Gurez Valley (Dawar) is actually like

Wooden-house homestays in Dawar and Tulail, no hostels, patchy signal, and soldiers outnumbering travellers.

Getting to Gurez Valley (Dawar)

Srinagar 123 km via Bandipora and Razdan Pass, 5-6 h by shared sumo; carry original photo ID for checkposts.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Gurez Valley (Dawar)?

June to September is peak season in Gurez Valley (Dawar). May and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid November to April. Jun-Sep is Gurez at its best: 8-22 C, dry-ish, Habba Khatoon peak lit at dusk, and every homestay in Dawar open.

How many days do you need in Gurez Valley (Dawar)?

3 days is the usual stay. Wooden-house homestays in Dawar and Tulail, no hostels, patchy signal, and soldiers outnumbering travellers.

How do you get to Gurez Valley (Dawar)?

Srinagar 123 km via Bandipora and Razdan Pass, 5-6 h by shared sumo; carry original photo ID for checkposts. Practical ways in: road.

Is Gurez Valley (Dawar) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
