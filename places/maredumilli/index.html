Maredumilli season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Jan is ideal: 28 C, clear forest tracks, Amruthadhara still flowing and the camps at their most comfortable.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-Jun the streams dry out, the forest turns to tinder and the valley bakes near 43 C, which is not why you came.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun-Sep the rain is heavy and leeches are everywhere; the Rampachodavaram ghat slips, but the falls run full.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov is the sweet spot, water still moving and the ghat road clear, and the camps take walk-ins on weekdays.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Maredumilli?

October to January is peak season in Maredumilli. February to March and July to September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Oct-Nov is the sweet spot, water still moving and the ghat road clear, and the camps take walk-ins on weekdays.

How many days do you need in Maredumilli?

2 days is the usual stay. Forest-department cottages and tribal-run camps with village guides; midweek you may be the only guest there.

How do you get to Maredumilli?

Rajahmundry 90 km by APSRTC bus via Rampachodavaram; forest camps must be booked with the range office. Practical ways in: road.

Is Maredumilli expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.
