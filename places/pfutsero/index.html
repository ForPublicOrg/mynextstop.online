Pfutsero (Glory Peak) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb nights drop below freezing and the Glory Peak dawn opens clean sightlines over the Chakhesang hills; Sukrunye on 15 Jan fills the town. Carry a real jacket.

Summer March to May Prime time

March is crisp and clear; April warms and the plains haze softens the views, while wild orchids and the kiwi and persimmon orchards flower around Chizami and Khezhakeno.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

June rain sets in hard and Jul-Aug bring landslides on the Kohima hill road and fog on the ridge; September is still wet but the terraces glow before harvest.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov is peak: harvest on the Khezhakeno and Chizami terraces, then mid-November's wild cherry blossom turns the town lanes pink for about two weeks.

Signature festival

mid Jan: Sukrunye, the Chakhesang purification festival

What Pfutsero (Glory Peak) is actually like

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Pfutsero (Glory Peak)?

October to March is peak season in Pfutsero (Glory Peak). April to June and September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid July to August. Oct-Nov is peak: harvest on the Khezhakeno and Chizami terraces, then mid-November's wild cherry blossom turns the town lanes pink for about two weeks.

How many days do you need in Pfutsero (Glory Peak)?

2 days is the usual stay. A government tourist lodge and plain guesthouses; Chizami weavers' cooperatives, church bells at dawn, tea shops and an early bedtime.

How do you get to Pfutsero (Glory Peak)?

Kohima to Pfutsero is 70 km of hill road, 2.5-3h by shared Sumo; Dimapur airport and railhead are 5-6h by Sumo; ILP needed. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Pfutsero (Glory Peak) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Pfutsero (Glory Peak)?

mid Jan: Sukrunye, the Chakhesang purification festival. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
