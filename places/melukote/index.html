Melukote season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is ideal: 27 °C, clean light on the Kalyani tanks, and the hill temple climb comfortable at any hour.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-May is 36 °C on bare rock with almost no shade between the two temples; only Vairamudi justifies the heat.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun-Sep is light rain rather than a washout here, greenish and very quiet, though the rock steps get slippery.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov is the other good stretch: warm, dry, harvest colour on the plateau and no crowd beyond local pilgrims.

Signature festival

Mar/Apr: Vairamudi Brahmotsava

What Melukote is actually like

A working temple town rather than a tourist one; a couple of basic lodges, and most people come out from Mysuru

Things to do in Melukote

The local places worth your time in and around Melukote, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Cheluvanarayana Swamy Temple

    Temple · in town

    The town's Vaishnava temple, Ramanuja's own seat; it shuts about 1 pm and reopens near 5, so plan around that

  • Yoganarasimha Temple

    Temple · 1 km out

    Hilltop shrine reached by a few hundred steps full of monkeys; best air and light in the hour after sunrise

  • Raya Gopura

    Heritage · 2 km out

    Unfinished stone gateway standing alone on the ridge, the sunset spot; last stretch is a walk over bare rock

  • Kalyani (stepped tank)

    Heritage · in town

    Deep stepped tank below the temple that film crews keep using; the lower steps stay green and slippery

  • Thonnur Lake

    Lake · 18 km out

    Big old tank ringed by rock hills, birds on it at dawn; half an hour by bus down the hill, no food stalls

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Melukote?

November to February is peak season in Melukote. March and September to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to May. Oct-Nov is the other good stretch: warm, dry, harvest colour on the plateau and no crowd beyond local pilgrims.

How many days do you need in Melukote?

1 day is the usual stay. A working temple town rather than a tourist one; a couple of basic lodges, and most people come out from Mysuru.

How do you get to Melukote?

Mysuru railhead 50 km or Mandya railhead 35 km, then frequent KSRTC buses up the hill. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Melukote expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Melukote?

Mar/Apr: Vairamudi Brahmotsava. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Melukote?

Cheluvanarayana Swamy Temple, Yoganarasimha Temple, Raya Gopura, Kalyani (stepped tank) and Thonnur Lake. The town's Vaishnava temple, Ramanuja's own seat; it shuts about 1 pm and reopens near 5, so plan around that.
