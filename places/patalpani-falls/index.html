Patalpani Falls (Mhow) season by season

Winter December to February Quiet, but open

Dec-Feb the falls shrink to a thin trickle down the dark basalt cliff; fine for a cool walk and the train ride, not for water.

Summer March to May Do not go

Mar-May the Choral runs almost dry and the cliff is bare basalt in the heat, so skip the falls until the rains return.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Jul-Sep is the reason to come, as the river drops about 91 m into a misty gorge; weekends are packed, so go midweek and keep off the edge.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

October still has a good flow under clearing skies, and November is calm but thin; both are quieter for the Kalakund train ride.

What Patalpani Falls (Mhow) is actually like

A day trip from Indore with chai and bhutta stalls and weekend crowds; the Kalakund stop is known for its kalakand milk sweet.

  • Bhim Janmabhoomi

    Heritage · 7 km out

    Memorial at Mhow marking the birthplace of Dr B R Ambedkar, busiest on his birth anniversary in April.

  • Kalakund

    Village · 7 km out

    Ghat section stop on the heritage metre gauge line from Patalpani, famous for the kalakand milk sweet sold here.

  • Choral Dam

    Reservoir · 11 km out

    Reservoir in wooded hills on the Choral river, a quieter picnic spot with sunset views.

  • Janapav

    Temple · 15 km out

    Hilltop said to be the birthplace of Parashurama, with a temple on top and views over the Malwa plateau.

  • Tincha Falls

    Waterfall · 20 km out

    Another tall monsoon fall dropping into a wooded gorge in the same hills, best seen Jul to Sep.
