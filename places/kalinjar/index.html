Kalinjar Fort season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Peak: cool enough to climb the seven gates to the rock-cut Neelkanth shrine, 244 m above the Bundelkhand plain.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Banda district is among India's hottest at 47 °C in May, on bare rock with no shade and no water on the climb.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Avoid Jul-Sep: the stepped gate path runs with water and turns genuinely slick, and buses to the village stop.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov is ideal, and the Kartik Purnima fair fills the Neelkanth cave with pilgrims for one long night.

Signature festival

Nov: Kartik Purnima fair at the Neelkanth temple

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Kalinjar Fort?

November to February is peak season in Kalinjar Fort. March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to September. Oct-Nov is ideal, and the Kartik Purnima fair fills the Neelkanth cave with pilgrims for one long night.

How many days do you need in Kalinjar Fort?

1 day is the usual stay. A ruined fort with a village at its foot and near-zero tourists; rooms here are very basic, most base in Banda.

How do you get to Kalinjar Fort?

Atarra station 36 km or Banda 65 km, then shared jeeps to Kalinjar village and a 1 km climb to the main gate. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Kalinjar Fort expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

Is there a festival in Kalinjar Fort?

Nov: Kartik Purnima fair at the Neelkanth temple. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
