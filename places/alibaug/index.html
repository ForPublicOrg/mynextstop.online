Alibaug season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Best months - dry and breezy, good for cycling out to Kolaba Fort at low tide.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Hot and humid, beaches empty out, only worth it for the ferry ride and seafood.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Sea turns rough and brown, the fort causeway floods at high tide - mostly a wash-out.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October dries out and cools down, ferries and fort visits pick back up.

What Alibaug is actually like

Weekend crowd from Mumbai - beach shacks, homestays, easygoing, not a backpacker scene

Things to do in Alibaug

The local places worth your time in and around Alibaug, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Kolaba Fort

    Fort · 1 km out

    Maratha sea fort you walk out to at low tide from the town beach; check the tide chart or pay for a horse cart

  • Nagaon Beach

    Beach · 9 km out

    Casuarina lined sand south of town, the local base for jet ski and banana boat; loud on weekends, empty midweek

  • Kashid Beach

    Beach · 30 km out

    White sand bay 30 km down the Murud road with shacks and horse carts; the undertow is strong, stay near the lifeguards

  • Kihim Beach

    Beach · 12 km out

    Quiet north beach where Salim Ali birded; good for a morning walk, few shacks, so carry water and cash

  • Kanakeshwar Devasthan

    Temple · 13 km out

    Hilltop Shiva temple above Mapgaon up about 750 stone steps; start by 7 am, the climb takes roughly an hour

  • Magen Aboth Synagogue

    Heritage · in town

    Bene Israel synagogue of 1848 on Israeli Ali street, still in use; ring for the caretaker, modest dress, no fee

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Alibaug?

November to February is peak season in Alibaug. March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to September. October dries out and cools down, ferries and fort visits pick back up.

How many days do you need in Alibaug?

1 day is the usual stay. Weekend crowd from Mumbai - beach shacks, homestays, easygoing, not a backpacker scene.

How do you get to Alibaug?

Ferry from Gateway of India (1h) to Mandwa, then 30min drive. Practical ways in: flight and road.

Is Alibaug expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Alibaug?

Kolaba Fort, Nagaon Beach, Kashid Beach, Kihim Beach, Kanakeshwar Devasthan and Magen Aboth Synagogue. Maratha sea fort you walk out to at low tide from the town beach; check the tide chart or pay for a horse cart.
