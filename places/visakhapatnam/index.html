Visakhapatnam (Vizag) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Driest, coolest, safest window on this coast - clear skies and the lowest cyclone risk.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Hot and humid off the Bay of Bengal; beaches work early morning, sticky by midday.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Heavy humid rain, sea often rough for swimming - fine for the city, not for the beach.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Oct-Nov is peak Bay of Bengal cyclone season - Hudhud hit Vizag itself in Oct 2014.

Things to do in Visakhapatnam

The local places worth your time in and around Visakhapatnam, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Kailasagiri

    Park · 8 km out

    Hilltop park with the big Shiva and Parvati statue and a ropeway up; go for sunset over the port, skip the toy train.

  • Simhachalam Temple

    Temple · 16 km out

    Hill temple whose Narasimha idol stays caked in sandalwood all year except for one spring day; steep road, buses from the RTC complex.

  • Ramakrishna Beach

    Beach · 3 km out

    The city promenade beach, better for an evening walk past the war memorials than for swimming; the water pulls hard.

  • INS Kurusura Submarine Museum

    Museum · 3 km out

    A real Soviet built submarine beached on RK Beach and opened up inside; closed Mondays, tight ladders, bags left outside.

  • Yarada Beach

    Beach · 16 km out

    Crescent of sand behind the hills past Dolphin's Nose, the prettiest stretch here; no lifeguards and the seabed drops fast.

  • Erra Matti Dibbalu

    Dunes · 20 km out

    Red laterite badlands on the Bheemili coast road, a protected geo site; go early or late, there is no shade anywhere.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Visakhapatnam (Vizag)?

December to February is peak season in Visakhapatnam (Vizag). March and September to November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to August. Driest, coolest, safest window on this coast - clear skies and the lowest cyclone risk.

How many days do you need in Visakhapatnam (Vizag)?

2 days is the usual stay. A workaday port city - solo travelers mostly pass through en route to Araku.

How do you get to Visakhapatnam (Vizag)?

Is Visakhapatnam (Vizag) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Visakhapatnam?

Kailasagiri, Simhachalam Temple, Ramakrishna Beach, INS Kurusura Submarine Museum, Yarada Beach and Erra Matti Dibbalu. Hilltop park with the big Shiva and Parvati statue and a ropeway up; go for sunset over the port, skip the toy train.
