Vanjangi (Paderu) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Nov-Feb dawns deliver the cloud ocean, most reliably in Jan; start the 45 min ridge climb by 4:30 am, near 5 °C up top.

Summer March to May Quiet, but open

By March the cloud bed is gone and mornings are merely clear; not worth the 4 h ghat drive unless you want empty hills.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

Jun-Aug the trail is a leech-and-slush slog with the ridge socked in fog, and the ghat road from Vizag crawls in rain.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct is the gamble month: lush slopes and early cloud beds on some dawns, flat grey nothing on others; November firms up.

What Vanjangi (Paderu) is actually like

Telugu bikers and tent campers at dawn, tribal villages by day; sleep in a basic Paderu lodge or pre-booked ridge tent.

Getting to Vanjangi (Paderu)

Road only: 100 km ghat road from Visakhapatnam to Paderu, then 8 km to the base point by bike, jeep or cab.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Vanjangi (Paderu)?

November to February is peak season in Vanjangi (Paderu). October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to August. Oct is the gamble month: lush slopes and early cloud beds on some dawns, flat grey nothing on others; November firms up.

How many days do you need in Vanjangi (Paderu)?

2 days is the usual stay. Telugu bikers and tent campers at dawn, tribal villages by day; sleep in a basic Paderu lodge or pre-booked ridge tent.

How do you get to Vanjangi (Paderu)?

Road only: 100 km ghat road from Visakhapatnam to Paderu, then 8 km to the base point by bike, jeep or cab. Practical ways in: road.

Is Vanjangi (Paderu) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
