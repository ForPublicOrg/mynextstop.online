Kunkeshwar (Devgad) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

December to February gives firm dry sand at the temple steps, calm water for a swim and the Devgad windmill ridge clear right down to the fort.

Summer March to May Prime time

Late February to April is alphonso season and the Shivratri yatra; May turns sticky at 34 C with the sea like a bath, so keep to dawn and dusk.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

June to September the surf reaches the temple wall and swimming stops, but the laterite cliffs and the Devgad harbour road are at their greenest.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is humid with the last showers, November clean and empty: the whole beach is yours and the mango trees are just coming into flower.

Signature festival

Feb/Mar: Mahashivratri yatra fills the temple sands

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Kunkeshwar (Devgad)?

November to March is peak season in Kunkeshwar (Devgad). April and September to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. No month is a write-off here. December to February gives firm dry sand at the temple steps, calm water for a swim and the Devgad windmill ridge clear right down to the fort.

How many days do you need in Kunkeshwar (Devgad)?

2 days is the usual stay. Temple trust rooms and mango-farm homestays; evenings are the aarti, the lighthouse road and no bars anywhere near.

How do you get to Kunkeshwar (Devgad)?

Kankavli railhead 55 km on the Konkan line, bus to Devgad then 13 km on to Kunkeshwar. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Kunkeshwar (Devgad) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Kunkeshwar (Devgad)?

Feb/Mar: Mahashivratri yatra fills the temple sands. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
