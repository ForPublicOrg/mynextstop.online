Malvan-Tarkarli season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Calm, clear water - peak scuba diving season and the best beach weather.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Hot and humid, visibility drops before the monsoon, fishing boats prep to head in.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Rough seas shut down diving and boat trips, and the seasonal fishing ban empties the harbour.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Seas calm back down through October, diving season restarts by November.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Malvan-Tarkarli?

November to February is peak season in Malvan-Tarkarli. March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to September. Seas calm back down through October, diving season restarts by November.

How many days do you need in Malvan-Tarkarli?

2 days is the usual stay. Quiet Konkan fishing-village feel - scuba schools and homestays draw a small diving crowd.

How do you get to Malvan-Tarkarli?

Overnight train/bus to Kudal/Sindhudurg, then 20-30min local transport. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Malvan-Tarkarli expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.
