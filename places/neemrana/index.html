Neemrana season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

The classic season for the fort-palace terraces and zipline. Cool, clear days.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Plains heat makes the stone fort punishing by midday, pool and shade only go so far.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

A genuinely popular monsoon-getaway window. The valley below the fort turns green.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October still runs 33C on the open ramparts, but November settles near 28C with cool dry nights - the quietest stretch of the season.

What Neemrana is actually like

A Delhi-couples weekend crowd at the fort hotel; not built for solo mixing.

Things to do in Neemrana

The local places worth your time in and around Neemrana, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Neemrana Fort Palace

    Palace · in town

    1464 hillside fort turned hotel, ten terraced levels of courtyards; non guests pay a day entry that includes lunch.

  • Neemrana Baori

    Heritage · 1 km out

    Nine storey stepwell below the village, dry, unrailed and dropping into the dark; go in daylight and wear firm shoes.

  • Flying Fox Neemrana

    Activity · in town

    Five zipline runs over the ravines behind the fort, about two hours; book a morning slot, they stop it in high wind.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Neemrana?

November to February is peak season in Neemrana. March and July to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. October still runs 33C on the open ramparts, but November settles near 28C with cool dry nights - the quietest stretch of the season.

How many days do you need in Neemrana?

2 days is the usual stay. A Delhi-couples weekend crowd at the fort hotel; not built for solo mixing.

How do you get to Neemrana?

1.5h drive/bus from Delhi or Jaipur on NH48. Practical ways in: flight and road.

Is Neemrana expensive, and does it get crowded?

Splurge by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

What are the best things to do in Neemrana?

Neemrana Fort Palace, Neemrana Baori and Flying Fox Neemrana. 1464 hillside fort turned hotel, ten terraced levels of courtyards; non guests pay a day entry that includes lunch.
