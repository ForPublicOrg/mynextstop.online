Churdhar (via Nohradhar) season by season

Winter December to February Do not go

December-March bury the trail and the temple sarai shuts; this becomes serious snow terrain, not a casual yatra, stay away.

Summer March to May Prime time

Snow patches linger into April; May-June is prime, an 18 km one-way climb from Nohradhar through deodar and rhododendron.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

June holds dry; July-August is a slippery viewless slog on clay tracks, and September clears into a superb climbing window.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October gives the big clear-sky payoff, plains shimmering one way and snow ridges the other; November is cold and crystalline.

What Churdhar (via Nohradhar) is actually like

Pilgrim-trekker mix: langar meals and dorm blankets at the summit sarai, weekend groups up from Chandigarh.

When is the best time to visit Churdhar (via Nohradhar)?

May to June and September to October is peak season in Churdhar (via Nohradhar). April and November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid July to August and December to March. Snow patches linger into April; May-June is prime, an 18 km one-way climb from Nohradhar through deodar and rhododendron.
