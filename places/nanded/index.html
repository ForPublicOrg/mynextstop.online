Nanded (Hazur Sahib) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is 13-31 C and the gurudwara's marble courtyard stays walkable barefoot at any hour of day.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-Jun is 43-45 C Marathwada heat; the sarai has fans, the streets outside offer nothing but glare.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

Jul-Sep is humid with modest rain, the Godavari runs full and langar and kirtan carry on regardless.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Gurta Gaddi Diwas lights up the takht in Oct or Nov and sangats arrive from Punjab by whole trainloads.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Nanded (Hazur Sahib)?

November to February is peak season in Nanded (Hazur Sahib). March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Gurta Gaddi Diwas lights up the takht in Oct or Nov and sangats arrive from Punjab by whole trainloads.

How many days do you need in Nanded (Hazur Sahib)?

2 days is the usual stay. Free sarai beds, round-the-clock langar and Punjabi sangats; the easiest solo stop in all Marathwada.

How do you get to Nanded (Hazur Sahib)?

Nanded station sits on the Secunderabad-Manmad line, 2 km from the takht; flights are thin and erratic. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Nanded (Hazur Sahib) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.

Is there a festival in Nanded (Hazur Sahib)?

Oct/Nov: Gurta Gaddi Diwas at Takht Sachkhand Hazur Sahib. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
