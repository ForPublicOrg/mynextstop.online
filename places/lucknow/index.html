Lucknow season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Best season: comfortable for walking through Bara Imambara and the old-city food lanes

Summer March to May Shoulder

April-June heat is severe and exploring the old quarters on foot becomes a real chore

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Humid and occasionally waterlogged streets, but indoor monuments and markets stay enjoyable

Autumn October to November Prime time

Weather turns pleasant by late October, a good time to explore before winter's peak crowds

Things to do in Lucknow

The local places worth your time in and around Lucknow, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Bara Imambara

    Heritage · in town

    1784 famine relief hall with the Bhool Bhulaiya maze upstairs; one ticket also covers Chota Imambara, take a guide for the maze

  • British Residency

    Heritage · 2 km out

    Shell scarred ruins of the 1857 siege in quiet lawns, small museum in the cellars; ASI ticket, open sunrise to sunset

  • Chota Imambara

    Heritage · 1 km out

    1838 Hussainabad hall hung with Belgian chandeliers and gilt mirrors; late afternoon light is best, same combined ticket

  • Rumi Darwaza

    Heritage · in town

    The 60 foot Awadhi gateway of 1784, two minutes from Bara Imambara; nothing to enter, shoot it early or floodlit at night

  • Tunday Kababi

    Food · 3 km out

    Aminabad original for the galouti kebab everyone comes to Lucknow for; cash only, no frills, queues build from 8 pm

  • Hazratganj

    Market · 1 km out

    Cream colonnaded shopping street and the evening walk locals call ganjing; Ram Asrey for sweets, many shops shut Sunday

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Lucknow?

October to February is peak season in Lucknow. March and July to September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Weather turns pleasant by late October, a good time to explore before winter's peak crowds.

How many days do you need in Lucknow?

2 days is the usual stay. More food-and-heritage city than backpacker hub. Solo dining beats meeting travelers.

How do you get to Lucknow?

Flights or train from Delhi 6-7h; well connected by air. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Lucknow expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Lucknow?

Bara Imambara, British Residency, Chota Imambara, Rumi Darwaza, Tunday Kababi and Hazratganj. 1784 famine relief hall with the Bhool Bhulaiya maze upstairs; one ticket also covers Chota Imambara, take a guide for the maze.
