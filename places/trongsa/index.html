Trongsa season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Dec and Jan are cold but clear, and the Trongsa Tshechu often falls in this window; Yotong La and Pele La may see snow.

Summer March to May Prime time

Mar to May is bright and mild, with rhododendron and magnolia on the passes and clear views across the gorge.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun to Aug bring heavy rain and slides on the highway over Pele La; Sep is greener and roads usually reopen fast.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct and Nov are ideal: blue skies, a sharp view of the dzong from across the gorge and easy days at the Ta Dzong museum.

Signature festival

Trongsa Tshechu (Dec or Jan)

What Trongsa is actually like

A one-street hill town of family hotels facing the dzong; drivers and travellers break the cross-country trip here.

Things to do in Trongsa

The local places worth your time in and around Trongsa, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Ta Dzong

    Museum · in town

    Old watchtower above the dzong, now the Royal Heritage Museum on the Wangchuck dynasty and its Trongsa roots.

  • Trongsa Dzong Viewpoint

    Viewpoint · 2 km out

    Roadside lookout across the gorge on the approach from the west, the classic view of the dzong on its ridge.

  • Kuenga Rabten Palace

    Palace · 10 km out

    Winter palace of the second king, built in 1929 on a terrace above the Mangde Chhu, about 23 km south by road.

  • Yotong La

    Viewpoint · 12 km out

    Pass at about 3,400 m on the road to Bumthang, through fir and rhododendron forest with long views east.

  • Chendebji Chorten

    Shrine · 16 km out

    Large white 18th-century chorten in the style of Swayambhunath, a tea stop on the road west to Pele La.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Trongsa?

March to April and October to November is peak season in Trongsa. May, September and December to February is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to August. Mar to May is bright and mild, with rhododendron and magnolia on the passes and clear views across the gorge.

How many days do you need in Trongsa?

1 day is the usual stay. A one-street hill town of family hotels facing the dzong; drivers and travellers break the cross-country trip here.

How do you get to Trongsa?

68 km from Bumthang, 2 to 3 h; 129 km from Wangdue, about 5 h; Thimphu 199 km. Route permit needed. Practical ways in: flight and road.

Is Trongsa expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Trongsa?

Trongsa Tshechu (Dec or Jan). Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Trongsa?

Ta Dzong, Trongsa Dzong Viewpoint, Kuenga Rabten Palace, Yotong La and Chendebji Chorten. Old watchtower above the dzong, now the Royal Heritage Museum on the Wangchuck dynasty and its Trongsa roots.
