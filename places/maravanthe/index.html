Maravanthe season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is the season: calm sea, empty sand past the highway pull-off and sunsets that light water on both sides of the road.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar-May turns humid at 34 °C and hazy by noon, but the sea stays swimmable and beach resort rates drop before the rains.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Jun-Sep the surf eats the beach, red flags go up and spray blows across the highway; a dramatic drive, a pointless stay.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October reopens the beach with the Souparnika backwater green and full; by November the settled blue-sky sea is back.

What Maravanthe is actually like

Sunset selfie traffic on the highway shoulder, then quiet; a couple of beach resorts at Trasi, homestays in Kundapura.

Getting to Maravanthe

Kundapura railhead 15 km on the Konkan line; Udupi-Bhatkal buses stop at Maravanthe, Mangaluru airport 100 km.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Maravanthe?

November to February is peak season in Maravanthe. March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. October reopens the beach with the Souparnika backwater green and full; by November the settled blue-sky sea is back.

How many days do you need in Maravanthe?

1 day is the usual stay. Sunset selfie traffic on the highway shoulder, then quiet; a couple of beach resorts at Trasi, homestays in Kundapura.

How do you get to Maravanthe?

Kundapura railhead 15 km on the Konkan line; Udupi-Bhatkal buses stop at Maravanthe, Mangaluru airport 100 km. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Maravanthe expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
