Darjeeling season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Crisp, clear Kanchenjunga views most mornings; cold nights near freezing, pack real layers.

Summer March to May Prime time

Mar-Apr is peak: rhododendrons bloom and skies are clearest, but it's crowded, book ahead.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Landslide season on the hill roads. The town itself is wet and foggy, views vanish for days.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Best window of the year: post-monsoon clarity, Kanchenjunga visible daily, Puja-season crowds.

Things to do in Darjeeling

The local places worth your time in and around Darjeeling, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Tiger Hill

    Viewpoint · 11 km out

    Sunrise over Kanchenjunga from a crowded terrace; jeeps leave about 4 am and seats are arranged the evening before.

  • Padmaja Naidu Himalayan Zoological Park

    Wildlife · 2 km out

    Red pandas and snow leopards in hillside enclosures; one ticket also covers the Mountaineering Institute, shut Thursdays.

  • Happy Valley Tea Estate

    Tea estate · 3 km out

    Working garden twenty minutes downhill from Chowrasta; a worker walks you round the factory in plucking season, tip him.

  • Batasia Loop

    Viewpoint · 5 km out

    Spiral where the toy train turns above the Gorkha war memorial garden; small entry fee, time it for a steam run.

  • Yiga Choeling (Ghum Monastery)

    Monastery · 8 km out

    Oldest Tibetan gompa here, 1850, with a fifteen foot Maitreya; a donation is expected, five minutes from Ghum station.

  • Tibetan Refugee Self Help Centre

    Heritage · 3 km out

    Settlement from 1959 where weavers and carpet makers still work; workshops and shop open on weekdays, not Sunday.

  • Glenary's

    Food · in town

    Old bakery and restaurant on Nehru Road, the default warm room in town; the ground floor counter is the cheap way in.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Darjeeling?

March to April and October to November is peak season in Darjeeling. May and December to February is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. Best window of the year: post-monsoon clarity, Kanchenjunga visible daily, Puja-season crowds.

How many days do you need in Darjeeling?

4 days is the usual stay. Backpacker cafés on Nehru Road; easy to link a Sikkim loop or Sandakphu trek.

How do you get to Darjeeling?

Fly Bagdogra, 3hr jeep up; or NJP rail for the toy train. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Darjeeling expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.

What are the best things to do in Darjeeling?

Tiger Hill, Padmaja Naidu Himalayan Zoological Park, Happy Valley Tea Estate, Batasia Loop, Yiga Choeling (Ghum Monastery) and Tibetan Refugee Self Help Centre, and more. Sunrise over Kanchenjunga from a crowded terrace; jeeps leave about 4 am and seats are arranged the evening before.
