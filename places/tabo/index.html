Tabo season by season

Winter December to February Do not go

December-March hits -25C at night; the Kinnaur road often stays open, but water lines freeze and most guesthouses shut.

Summer March to May Shoulder

May-June is when Tabo opens up, 5-25C by day, and the caretaker will unlock the pitch-dark thousand-year-old assembly hall.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Tabo sits in rain shadow and stays dry, but July-August landslides in the Kinnaur gorge below can add a full day to the drive.

Autumn October to November Prime time

September-October is the clear, dust-free peak: cold nights, warm sun, and the mud stupas glowing gold around 5 pm.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Tabo?

June and September to October is peak season in Tabo. May and July to August is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid December to March. Tabo sits in rain shadow and stays dry, but July-August landslides in the Kinnaur gorge below can add a full day to the drive.

How many days do you need in Tabo?

2 days is the usual stay. Monastery guesthouse and a handful of homestays; Spiti-circuit travellers, monks and the odd art historian, no party.

How do you get to Tabo?

47 km from Kaza, 190 km from Reckong Peo; one HRTC bus daily each way plus shared taxis in season. Practical ways in: road.

Is Tabo expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.
