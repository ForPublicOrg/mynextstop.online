Neil Island (Shaheed Dweep) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Calm water, good snorkeling at Bharatpur: this laid-back island is at its easiest to reach.

Summer March to May Prime time

Warm and humid through April-May but ferries usually still run. Fewer crowds than Havelock.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Same story as the rest of the archipelago. Ferries cancel in rough seas, island quietens hard.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Reliable ferry schedule resumes by late October, still noticeably quieter than peak winter.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Neil Island (Shaheed Dweep)?

November to March is peak season in Neil Island (Shaheed Dweep). April to May and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. Reliable ferry schedule resumes by late October, still noticeably quieter than peak winter.

How many days do you need in Neil Island (Shaheed Dweep)?

3 days is the usual stay. Sleepier than Havelock: a few guesthouses and the kind of quiet that invites chat.

How do you get to Neil Island (Shaheed Dweep)?

Fly to Port Blair, then 1-1.5h ferry, or hop from Havelock. Practical ways in: flight.

Is Neil Island (Shaheed Dweep) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.
