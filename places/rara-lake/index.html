Rara Lake season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Dec is cold and clear; by Jan and Feb snow can block the trail and flights, and the few lodges by the lake close.

Summer March to May Prime time

Apr and May bring wildflowers and rhododendron, warm days for the shore walk and the clearest water.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jul and Aug rain cancels Talcha flights and turns roads to mud, though Sep greens the meadows beautifully.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Best season: deep blue water under clear skies, golden meadows and the climb to Murma Top for the whole lake.

What Rara Lake is actually like

A few simple lodges and camping inside the park, Thakuri villages nearby and very few outsiders this far west.

Things to do in Rara Lake

The local places worth your time in and around Rara Lake, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Murma Top

    Viewpoint · 5 km out

    Ridge viewpoint west of the lake at about 3700 m, the classic spot to fit the whole lake in one frame.

  • Rara Lake circuit

    Walk · in town

    Shoreline trail of about 13 km through pine and juniper forest around the lake, a 4 to 5 h walk.

  • Thakur Baba Temple

    Temple · 2 km out

    Shrine of the local deity Thakur at the lake's south-east corner, tied to a legend about taming the lake's floods.

  • Gamgadhi

    Village · 7 km out

    Mugu district headquarters east of the lake, with simple hotels, shops and jeeps toward Talcha.

Getting to Rara Lake

Fly Nepalgunj to Talcha in Mugu, about 35 min, then 2 to 4 h on foot; Rara National Park fee at the gate.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Rara Lake?

April to May and October to November is peak season in Rara Lake. March, June, September and December is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid January to February and July to August. Apr and May bring wildflowers and rhododendron, warm days for the shore walk and the clearest water.

How many days do you need in Rara Lake?

3 days is the usual stay. A few simple lodges and camping inside the park, Thakuri villages nearby and very few outsiders this far west.

How do you get to Rara Lake?

Fly Nepalgunj to Talcha in Mugu, about 35 min, then 2 to 4 h on foot; Rara National Park fee at the gate. Practical ways in: flight and road.

Is Rara Lake expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Rara Lake?

Murma Top, Rara Lake circuit, Thakur Baba Temple and Gamgadhi. Ridge viewpoint west of the lake at about 3700 m, the classic spot to fit the whole lake in one frame.
