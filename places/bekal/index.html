Bekal season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Peak season - cool sea breeze off the ramparts, calm water, far fewer tourists than the southern beaches

Summer March to May Shoulder

Hot and humid; the fort's laterite walls give little shade so midday visits get rough by April-May

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Rough Arabian Sea swell and heavy rain batter the coast; the fort stays open but beach time is a write-off

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is decent once the monsoon tail ends, and November onward is genuinely pleasant

What Bekal is actually like

Sleepy and uncrowded - the ramparts to yourself, but little backpacker scene

Things to do in Bekal

The local places worth your time in and around Bekal, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Bekal Fort

    Fort · in town

    Kerala's largest fort, a laterite keyhole on the sea with an observation tower over the surf; 8 am to 5.30 pm, small ticket.

  • Kappil Beach

    Beach · 6 km out

    Empty sand under the Kodi cliff looking back at the fort; no shacks or lifeguards, so carry water and watch the current.

  • Chandragiri Fort

    Fort · 16 km out

    Ikkeri-era ruin on a bluff where the Chandragiri river meets the sea; free, best at sunset, boats run below the walls.

  • Ananthapura Lake Temple

    Temple · 35 km out

    Kerala's only temple set in the middle of a lake, reached by a causeway; morning darshan, and it closes over midday.

  • Madhur Temple

    Temple · 22 km out

    Gabled Ganesha temple above the Madhuvahini river, copper roof and carved wood; no photography once you are inside.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Bekal?

November to February is peak season in Bekal. March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to September. October is decent once the monsoon tail ends, and November onward is genuinely pleasant.

How many days do you need in Bekal?

2 days is the usual stay. Sleepy and uncrowded - the ramparts to yourself, but little backpacker scene.

How do you get to Bekal?

Nearest airport Mangalore; or train to Kanhangad, taxi to fort. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Bekal expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

What are the best things to do in Bekal?

Bekal Fort, Kappil Beach, Chandragiri Fort, Ananthapura Lake Temple and Madhur Temple. Kerala's largest fort, a laterite keyhole on the sea with an observation tower over the surf; 8 am to 5.30 pm, small ticket.
