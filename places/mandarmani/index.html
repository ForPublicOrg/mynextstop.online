Mandarmani-Tajpur season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Prime beach weather: dry and breezy, good for the famous bike-on-the-sand rides.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Hot and increasingly humid through May as the Bay of Bengal builds toward monsoon.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Rough surf, frequent rain and real cyclone risk off the Bay of Bengal. Not a beach window.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct is still a bit unsettled post-monsoon; by Nov sea and weather have properly calmed.

What Mandarmani-Tajpur is actually like

Weekend crowd of Kolkata twenty-somethings, beach shacks and bonfires.

Things to do in Mandarmani-Tajpur

The local places worth your time in and around Mandarmani-Tajpur, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Tajpur Beach

    Beach · 8 km out

    Quieter sand than Mandarmani with red crabs at low tide; the approach road is bad, and nothing stays open past dark

  • Mandarmani Mohana

    Beach · 4 km out

    Creek mouth at the end of the beach where the fishing boats land; walk or cycle at low tide, the sand is soft by noon

  • Shankarpur Fishing Harbour

    Market · 16 km out

    Trawler harbour where the catch is landed and auctioned in the afternoon; wear shoes you can rinse, the jetty is slippery

  • Marine Aquarium and Regional Centre

    Wildlife · 26 km out

    ZSI aquarium at Digha holding Bay of Bengal species; small ticket, an hour is plenty, mornings before the bus groups

  • Chandaneswar Shiv Temple

    Temple · 35 km out

    Border shrine just inside Odisha, packed for the Chaitra Sankranti mela in mid April; shoes and phones stay at the counter

  • Talsari Beach

    Beach · 38 km out

    Casuarina beach in Odisha where the Subarnarekha meets the sea; cross the creek by boat at low tide, carry your own water

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Mandarmani-Tajpur?

November to February is peak season in Mandarmani-Tajpur. March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to September. Oct is still a bit unsettled post-monsoon; by Nov sea and weather have properly calmed.

How many days do you need in Mandarmani-Tajpur?

2 days is the usual stay. Weekend crowd of Kolkata twenty-somethings, beach shacks and bonfires.

How do you get to Mandarmani-Tajpur?

3hr drive/bus from Kolkata via Digha Road. Practical ways in: road.

Is Mandarmani-Tajpur expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Mandarmani-Tajpur?

Tajpur Beach, Mandarmani Mohana, Shankarpur Fishing Harbour, Marine Aquarium and Regional Centre, Chandaneswar Shiv Temple and Talsari Beach. Quieter sand than Mandarmani with red crabs at low tide; the approach road is bad, and nothing stays open past dark.
