Alchi (Sham Valley) season by season

Winter December to February Do not go

Dec-Feb drops to -15 C, the Indus ices at the bridge and most Alchi guesthouses and orchard cafes are shut for it.

Summer March to May Prime time

Mar is bare and cold; Apr brings apricot blossom and by May Alchi is open at only 3,100 m, the gentlest start in Ladakh.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Jun-Sep stays dry with apricots ripening in July; the Choskor murals are unlit, so come when light reaches the doors.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Oct turns the poplars yellow and empties the lanes; nights hit -5 C and the last homestays close by the month end.

What Alchi (Sham Valley) is actually like

Guesthouses and orchard cafes; Leh day-trippers clear out by 4 pm and leave the Choskor lanes to you.

Things to do in Alchi

The local places worth your time in and around Alchi, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Alchi Choskor

    Monastery · in town

    The 11th century temple group with Kashmiri murals; no photography inside, and the dark Sumtsek bays need late morning light

  • Saspol Caves

    Heritage · 5 km out

    Cliff caves above Saspol holding 15th century Buddhist wall paintings; steep 20 min scramble up, bring your own torch

  • Likir Monastery

    Monastery · 25 km out

    Hillside gompa under a 23 m gilt Maitreya with a small thangka and mask room; ask the monk on duty to unlock it

  • Basgo Gompa

    Fort · 24 km out

    Crumbling mud citadel above the Indus with three Maitreya temples; 15 min climb from the village, best at sunset

  • Rizong Monastery

    Monastery · 22 km out

    Strict Gelug monastery at the head of a side gorge, with Chulichan nunnery on the walk in; 2 km up from the highway

  • Alchi Kitchen

    Food · in town

    Ladakhi home cooking on the temple lane, khambir bread and apricot dishes; small room, so it fills when day trippers land

Getting to Alchi (Sham Valley)

Leh 65 km, 2 h by Kargil bus to Saspol then 4 km down; Likir and Basgo sit on the same road.
