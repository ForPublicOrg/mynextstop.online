Doodhpathri season by season

Winter December to February Do not go

Dec-Feb snow buries the meadow and the road past Khansahib shuts for days after each dump; huts close, go only as a cleared-road day trip.

Summer March to May Prime time

Mar is slush and brown turf; by May the Shaliganga runs milk-white through knee-high grass and days sit at 15-22 C.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Jul-Aug is green and warm rather than wet, but weekend picnic crowds and pony touts peak; Jun and Sep are the quiet wins.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Oct frosts the turf gold and empties the meadow by dusk; November turns raw, stalls pack up and the huts start closing.

What Doodhpathri is actually like

Day-trip picnic scene till 4 pm, then just JKTDC hut guests, Gujjar herders and a few chai stalls by the river.

Getting to Doodhpathri

Srinagar 42 km via Khansahib, 1.5-2 h; shared taxi to Khansahib then local cab, no evening bus back.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Doodhpathri?

May to June and September is peak season in Doodhpathri. April, July to August and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid December to February. Mar is slush and brown turf; by May the Shaliganga runs milk-white through knee-high grass and days sit at 15-22 C.

How many days do you need in Doodhpathri?

1 day is the usual stay. Day-trip picnic scene till 4 pm, then just JKTDC hut guests, Gujjar herders and a few chai stalls by the river.

How do you get to Doodhpathri?

Srinagar 42 km via Khansahib, 1.5-2 h; shared taxi to Khansahib then local cab, no evening bus back. Practical ways in: flight and road.

Is Doodhpathri expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.
