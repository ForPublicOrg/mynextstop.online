Barnawapara Sanctuary season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Nov-Feb is 10-28 C with misty sal-forest drives, gaur herds in the glades and machan evenings at the Hareli eco resort.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar-May pushes past 42 C but waterholes bunch the gaur and chital; drive at dawn and sleep through the white afternoons.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

Closed for safaris 1 July to 31 October; the sal canopy shuts over flooded tracks and the resort all but winds down.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is still shut, so aim at the 1 November reopening: fresh-washed forest and Siddhakhol's falls still flowing.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Barnawapara Sanctuary?

November to February is peak season in Barnawapara Sanctuary. March to May is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid July to October. October is still shut, so aim at the 1 November reopening: fresh-washed forest and Siddhakhol's falls still flowing.

How many days do you need in Barnawapara Sanctuary?

2 days is the usual stay. CG Tourism's Hareli eco resort by the forest plus basic gate rooms; couples, birders and no crowds at all.

How do you get to Barnawapara Sanctuary?

Raipur airport-railhead 100 km: NH-53 to Pithora then 40 min north; book resort and safari jeep ahead. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Barnawapara Sanctuary expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
