Dalma Wildlife Sanctuary season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Peak: cool dawn treks from Makulakocha gate to the 900 m summit shrine, with hazy-blue views over the Subarnarekha valley.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March-April improve elephant odds at shrinking waterholes, but by May the open climb is a 42 °C slog; start at first light.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Jun-Sep the ghat track is slick red mud under sitting cloud; views vanish and the trails belong to leeches.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov the forest is washed and green, the track dries out and weekend trekkers from Jamshedpur come back.

Things to do in Dalma Wildlife Sanctuary

The local places worth your time in and around Dalma Wildlife Sanctuary, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Dalma Top Shiva Temple

    Temple · in town

    Summit shrine that fills for the Shivratri fair; the jeep track up from Makulakocha gate takes an hour, walking it takes three.

  • Pindrabera

    Wildlife · 5 km out

    Waterhole and watchtower below the summit, the best chance of elephants or deer; sit still near dusk, arrange the stop at the gate.

  • Dimna Lake

    Lake · 25 km out

    Reservoir at the foot of the Dalma range with boating and a long dam wall; busy Sundays with Jamshedpur, quiet at dawn.

  • Chandil Dam

    Reservoir · 26 km out

    Subarnarekha reservoir with wooded islands below the Dalma range; on the Ranchi highway, sunset is the reason to go.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Dalma Wildlife Sanctuary?

November to February is peak season in Dalma Wildlife Sanctuary. March to April and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to September. Oct-Nov the forest is washed and green, the track dries out and weekend trekkers from Jamshedpur come back.

How many days do you need in Dalma Wildlife Sanctuary?

1 day is the usual stay. Forest rest house and bamboo huts at Makulakocha gate, weekend trekkers from Jamshedpur, nobody after sundown.

How do you get to Dalma Wildlife Sanctuary?

Makulakocha gate on the Ranchi-Jamshedpur highway, about 30 km from Tatanagar Jn; jeep track to the summit. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Dalma Wildlife Sanctuary expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

What are the best things to do in Dalma Wildlife Sanctuary?

Dalma Top Shiva Temple, Pindrabera, Dimna Lake and Chandil Dam. Summit shrine that fills for the Shivratri fair; the jeep track up from Makulakocha gate takes an hour, walking it takes three.
