Toranmal season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Nov-Feb is ideal: 8-25 C, boating on Yashwant lake, Sitakhai and Sunset Point clear, and you may be the only outsider in town.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-May the Khandesh plains below hit 44 C and the plateau turns brown and hazy; the season here is simply over until the rains.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jul-Sep the ghat road from Shahada threads waterfalls and fog; the plateau goes green, drives are slow and occasionally blocked.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov clears to washed skies and a full lake, lotuses bloom on Kamal Talav and evenings already ask for a light jacket.

Signature festival

Feb/Mar: Mahashivratri yatra at the Gorakhnath temple (dates shift yearly)

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Toranmal?

November to February is peak season in Toranmal. March and August to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to May. Oct-Nov clears to washed skies and a full lake, lotuses bloom on Kamal Talav and evenings already ask for a light jacket.

How many days do you need in Toranmal?

2 days is the usual stay. Forest rest house and a handful of plain lodges by the lake; pilgrims, school trips, almost no travellers.

How do you get to Toranmal?

ST buses from Shahada (52 km) and Nandurbar, the nearest railhead 90 km; slow ghat climb, arrive before dark. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Toranmal expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

Is there a festival in Toranmal?

Feb/Mar: Mahashivratri yatra at the Gorakhnath temple (dates shift yearly). Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
