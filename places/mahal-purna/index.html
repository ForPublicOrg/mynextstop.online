Mahal (Purna Sanctuary) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec to Feb the Purna runs clear, valley mornings drop near 10 C and giant squirrels are easy to spot from the track behind camp

Summer March to May Shoulder

March is still green under the teak; by late April the leaf drops, the river shrinks to pools and the road in hits 40 C by noon

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jul and Aug bring 2,500 mm of rain, the river crossings go under and the camp shuts; June and September are green and passable

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is the best month here: full river, leeches gone, the side streams still falling and Dangi hamlets out harvesting nagli

Signature festival

Feb/Mar: Dang Darbar at Ahwa, just before Holi

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Mahal (Purna Sanctuary)?

October to February is peak season in Mahal (Purna Sanctuary). March, June and September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid July to August. October is the best month here: full river, leeches gone, the side streams still falling and Dangi hamlets out harvesting nagli.

How many days do you need in Mahal (Purna Sanctuary)?

3 days is the usual stay. Forest department tents and cottages by the river, no shops and patchy signal; evenings are a fire and the cook's nagli rotla.

How do you get to Mahal (Purna Sanctuary)?

Bilimora or Navsari railhead, bus 3h to Ahwa, then a jeep 35 km on through Subir; Surat airport is 140 km. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Mahal (Purna Sanctuary) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

Is there a festival in Mahal (Purna Sanctuary)?

Feb/Mar: Dang Darbar at Ahwa, just before Holi. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
