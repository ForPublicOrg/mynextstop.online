Agatti Island (Lakshadweep) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Peak season - flat calm lagoon, best snorkeling visibility, though limited flights mean booking months ahead

Summer March to May Prime time

April is still workable with warm calm seas; by May pre-monsoon swell and humidity climb

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Ferries are frequently suspended and several resorts close entirely - the sea is too rough to reach or enjoy

Autumn October to November Shoulder

October the sea is only just calming; November onward is a good value window with thinner crowds

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Agatti Island (Lakshadweep)?

December to March is peak season in Agatti Island (Lakshadweep). April to May and October to November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. Peak season - flat calm lagoon, best snorkeling visibility, though limited flights mean booking months ahead.

How many days do you need in Agatti Island (Lakshadweep)?

5 days is the usual stay. Package-tour visitors mostly - no budget stays, but tour-mates become fast friends.

How do you get to Agatti Island (Lakshadweep)?

Fly Kochi-Agatti; mandatory Entry Permit via Lakshadweep Tourism. Practical ways in: flight.

Is Agatti Island (Lakshadweep) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Splurge by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
