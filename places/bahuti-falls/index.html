Bahuti Falls (Mauganj) season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Dec-Feb the Odda thins to a few white threads down the cliff; cool, clear days suit the walk to the rim, but expect little water.

Summer March to May Mixed, read the detail

March is the last month with any flow; by Apr-May the plateau bakes, the falls run dry and the bare rim has no shade, so skip it.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Aug-Sep is the show, the full river leaping the scarp in one long plume; July lanes turn to mud and the unfenced rim is slick with spray.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October still carries strong water under clear skies; by November the flow drops, but the gorge stays green and the light is soft.

What Bahuti Falls (Mauganj) is actually like

No stays or food at the falls; sleep in Mauganj or Rewa and carry lunch. Weekdays are near empty, Sundays bring local picnickers.

    Fort · 6 km out

  • Deotalab Shiv Mandir

    Temple · 14 km out

    Old Shiva temple at Deotalab, a tahsil town of Mauganj district, listed by the district among its main sights.

  • Deur Kothar

    Heritage · 25 km out

    Mauryan era Buddhist stupas and painted rock shelters on a Vindhyan ridge off the Rewa to Prayagraj road.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Bahuti Falls (Mauganj)?

August to October is peak season in Bahuti Falls (Mauganj). July and November to December is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Aug-Sep is the show, the full river leaping the scarp in one long plume; July lanes turn to mud and the unfenced rim is slick with spray.

How many days do you need in Bahuti Falls (Mauganj)?

1 day is the usual stay. No stays or food at the falls; sleep in Mauganj or Rewa and carry lunch. Weekdays are near empty, Sundays bring local picnickers.

How do you get to Bahuti Falls (Mauganj)?

Mauganj town 13 km by road; Rewa railhead about 75 km (2 h), then a taxi for the last stretch. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Bahuti Falls (Mauganj) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

What are the best things to do in Bahuti Falls?

Naigarhi Fort, Deotalab Shiv Mandir and Deur Kothar. Old fort in Naigarhi town, 17 km from Mauganj; the Ashtbhuja Mata temple, a Durga shrine, is in the same town.
