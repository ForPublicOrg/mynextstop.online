Kokkare Bellur season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb the colony is full: spot-billed pelicans and painted storks on nests over the street, and villagers who count the mess as luck.

Summer March to May Prime time

March and April are the loudest weeks, fledglings on every branch; by May the trees empty and the Deccan sun gets to 36 C.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

Jul-Sep the birds have gone and this is simply a farming village; there is nothing to come for until they drift back in October.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

October brings the first pelicans back to last year's nests; November is cool and quiet, good for watching them rebuild without a crowd.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Kokkare Bellur?

January to March is peak season in Kokkare Bellur. April and November to December is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid July to September. Dec-Feb the colony is full: spot-billed pelicans and painted storks on nests over the street, and villagers who count the mess as luck.

How many days do you need in Kokkare Bellur?

1 day is the usual stay. One street of tiled houses under nesting trees, a community homestay and a village volunteer who walks you round.

How do you get to Kokkare Bellur?

On the Bengaluru to Mysuru line: train or bus to Maddur, then 12 km by auto into the village. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Kokkare Bellur expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.
