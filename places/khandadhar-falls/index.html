Khandadhar Falls season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

December still runs well; by Jan-Feb the flow thins but the pool is clear and the 2 km walk in is cool.

Summer March to May Shoulder

By March to May the Korapani stream is a thin ribbon and the sal forest hits 42 C; not worth the drive.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun-Aug the fall is enormous and the last fair-weather stretch turns to red slush and slip-prone rock.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is the payoff: full flow, clean air and the forest still green after the rain pulls back.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Khandadhar Falls?

October to December is peak season in Khandadhar Falls. January to March and September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to August. October is the payoff: full flow, clean air and the forest still green after the rain pulls back.

How many days do you need in Khandadhar Falls?

1 day is the usual stay. Forest-department cottages and tents at the base; local picnic parties at weekends, tourists almost never.

How do you get to Khandadhar Falls?

104 km from Rourkela via Bonaigarh, the last stretch rough forest road, then 2 km on foot. Practical ways in: road.

Is Khandadhar Falls expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
