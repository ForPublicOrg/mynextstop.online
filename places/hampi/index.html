Hampi season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Peak season - cool mornings for ruin-hopping, coracle rides, and Hampi Utsav some years in Feb.

Summer March to May Do not go

Skip it - 38-40C mean highs with 45C spikes, radiating off bare granite, and ruin-hopping here is a full day on foot.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Tungabhadra river fills up, boulders turn green, fewer tourists, still hot but humid.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Post-monsoon green fading to gold, cool temps arriving - genuinely the sweet spot of the year.

Signature festival

Feb/Mar: Hampi Utsav (Vijaya Utsav) cultural festival

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Hampi?

October to February is peak season in Hampi. June to September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid March to May. Post-monsoon green fading to gold, cool temps arriving - genuinely the sweet spot of the year.

How many days do you need in Hampi?

2 days is the usual stay. Hippie Island guesthouses draw a laid-back backpacker crowd for sunset boulder views.

How do you get to Hampi?

Train to Hospet Jn, 15min auto; overnight bus from Bengaluru. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Hampi expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.

Is there a festival in Hampi?

Feb/Mar: Hampi Utsav (Vijaya Utsav) cultural festival. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
