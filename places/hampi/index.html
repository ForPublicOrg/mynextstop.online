Hampi season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Peak season - cool mornings for ruin-hopping, coracle rides, and Hampi Utsav some years in Feb.

Summer March to May Do not go

Skip it - 38-40C mean highs with 45C spikes, radiating off bare granite, and ruin-hopping here is a full day on foot.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Tungabhadra river fills up, boulders turn green, fewer tourists, still hot but humid.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Post-monsoon green fading to gold, cool temps arriving - genuinely the sweet spot of the year.

Signature festival

Feb/Mar: Hampi Utsav (Vijaya Utsav) cultural festival

Things to do in Hampi

The local places worth your time in and around Hampi, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Virupaksha Temple

    Temple · in town

    The living temple at the head of Hampi Bazaar, still in daily worship; token entry fee, the sanctum shuts for a long midday break.

  • Vittala Temple

    Heritage · 3 km out

    Stone chariot and the ringing pillars, the finest of the ruins; one ASI ticket covers this and the Zenana enclosure the same day.

  • Matanga Hill

    Viewpoint · 1 km out

    Steep 30 minute climb to the high point over the whole ruin field; sunrise is the reason, go in a group, thefts happen in the dark.

  • Hemakuta Hill

    Heritage · in town

    Cluster of small early shrines on bare rock right above Virupaksha; free, five minutes from the bazaar, come for sunset.

  • Royal Enclosure

    Heritage · 3 km out

    Mahanavami Dibba platform and the stepped tank on open ground; no shade at all, come early and carry water, no ticket needed.

  • Anjanadri Hill

    Viewpoint · 8 km out

    Around 570 steps up to the Hanuman temple across the river; ferry across or the long road loop via Kampli, monkeys grab food.

  • Sanapur Lake

    Lake · 9 km out

    Green reservoir among boulders past Anegundi with coracle rides; no lifeguards and deep water, people have drowned jumping in.

  • Daroji Sloth Bear Sanctuary

    Wildlife · 20 km out

    Scrub hills where sloth bears come out to a rock face at dusk; the afternoon slot only, ticket at the gate, bring binoculars.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Hampi?

October to February is peak season in Hampi. June to September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid March to May. Post-monsoon green fading to gold, cool temps arriving - genuinely the sweet spot of the year.

How many days do you need in Hampi?

2 days is the usual stay. Hippie Island guesthouses draw a laid-back backpacker crowd for sunset boulder views.

How do you get to Hampi?

Train to Hospet Jn, 15min auto; overnight bus from Bengaluru. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Hampi expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.

Is there a festival in Hampi?

Feb/Mar: Hampi Utsav (Vijaya Utsav) cultural festival. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Hampi?

Virupaksha Temple, Vittala Temple, Matanga Hill, Hemakuta Hill, Royal Enclosure and Anjanadri Hill, and more. The living temple at the head of Hampi Bazaar, still in daily worship; token entry fee, the sanctum shuts for a long midday break.
