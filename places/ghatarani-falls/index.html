Ghatarani Falls (Gariaband) season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Dec-Feb the falls shrink to a thin veil over the rock steps, but cool air makes the forest walk and the temple visit pleasant.

Summer March to May Mixed, read the detail

March leaves only a trickle, and by Apr-May the rock is bare and the plains roast in the mid 40s C; skip it until the rains return.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

July brings the water back; Aug-Sep it runs full over the wide rock tiers, but the wet rock is slippery, so bathe only at the edges.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is the time the district itself recommends, full water after the monsoon with Navratri crowds at the temple; November is calmer.

Signature festival

Navratri (Mar-Apr and Sep-Oct)

What Ghatarani Falls (Gariaband) is actually like

Weekend picnic families and temple pilgrims, with few places to eat; no stays here, so base in Raipur or Rajim and pack lunch.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Ghatarani Falls (Gariaband)?

August to October is peak season in Ghatarani Falls (Gariaband). July and November to December is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. July brings the water back; Aug-Sep it runs full over the wide rock tiers, but the wet rock is slippery, so bathe only at the edges.

How many days do you need in Ghatarani Falls (Gariaband)?

1 day is the usual stay. Weekend picnic families and temple pilgrims, with few places to eat; no stays here, so base in Raipur or Rajim and pack lunch.

How do you get to Ghatarani Falls (Gariaband)?

Raipur airport about 78 km and Raipur Junction about 90 km; taxis from Raipur take around 2 h. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Ghatarani Falls (Gariaband) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

Is there a festival in Ghatarani Falls (Gariaband)?

Navratri (Mar-Apr and Sep-Oct). Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Ghatarani Falls?

Jatmai Mata Mandir, Bhooteshwarnath Mahadev and Rajivlochan Temple. Durga temple built between seasonal falls in the same hills, about 25 km round by road; fairs at both Navratris.
