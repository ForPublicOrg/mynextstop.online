Things to do in Vrindavan-Mathura

The local places worth your time in and around Vrindavan-Mathura, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Banke Bihari Temple

    Temple · in town

    Vrindavan's busiest shrine, the curtain drawn open in glimpses; take the 8 am darshan on a weekday, leave phones outside

  • Prem Mandir

    Temple · 3 km out

    Marble temple lit up after dark with Radha Krishna dioramas and fountains; free, evening gates from about 4.30 pm, big crowd

  • ISKCON Krishna Balaram Mandir

    Temple · 2 km out

    The international temple, 4.30 am mangala aarti and Govinda's canteen; the guesthouse books out through Kartik

  • Nidhivan

    Garden · in town

    Tangled tulsi grove that empties at dusk because of the story told about it; small, eerie, best in the first hour of day

  • Krishna Janmabhoomi

    Temple · 12 km out

    Krishna's birth prison beside the mosque in Mathura; heavy security, no phones, bags or pens past the gate, slow line

  • Vishram Ghat

    Ghat · 12 km out

    Mathura's main Yamuna ghat, evening aarti about 7 and turtles in the water; boatmen quote high, agree a price first

  • Kusum Sarovar

    Heritage · 26 km out

    Sandstone pavilions on a tank at Govardhan, the pretty stop on the 21 km parikrama; go at sunset, monkeys take glasses

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Vrindavan-Mathura?

October to February is peak season in Vrindavan-Mathura. March and July to September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Excellent weather resumes by late October, with Diwali on Krishna's home turf drawing crowds.

How many days do you need in Vrindavan-Mathura?

2 days is the usual stay. ISKCON's international devotees give this temple town a surprisingly easy-to-meet vibe.

How do you get to Vrindavan-Mathura?

Train Delhi to Mathura 2-3h, then auto to Vrindavan 15km. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Vrindavan-Mathura expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Vrindavan-Mathura?

Mar: Holi, incl. Barsana's Lathmar Holi. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Vrindavan-Mathura?

Banke Bihari Temple, Prem Mandir, ISKCON Krishna Balaram Mandir, Nidhivan, Krishna Janmabhoomi and Vishram Ghat, and more. Vrindavan's busiest shrine, the curtain drawn open in glimpses; take the 8 am darshan on a weekday, leave phones outside.
