Murudeshwar season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Jan is peak: 15-20 m visibility out at Netrani, calm boats, and the statue lit up over an empty beach at dusk.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar-May the sea stays diveable into early May, but the headland is 34 °C and the town has almost no shade.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Jun-Sep every Netrani boat stops, the beach is closed to swimmers and the temple town shrinks back to its bus stand.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October reopens the dive season once the swell drops, but visibility only settles properly by late November.

Signature festival

Feb/Mar: Maha Shivaratri

Things to do in Murudeshwar

The local places worth your time in and around Murudeshwar, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Murudeshwar Beach

    Beach · in town

    Grey sand under the statue with fishing boats at one end; the shelf drops away fast, so stay near the shore

  • Netrani Island

    Island · 20 km out

    Heart shaped rock 19 km offshore, the clearest diving on this coast; boats run October to May, book a day ahead

  • Idagunji Mahaganapati Temple

    Temple · 25 km out

    Busy Ganapati shrine inland from Honnavar; the queue is shortest before 9 am, share autos run from Honnavar

  • Apsarakonda Falls

    Waterfall · 28 km out

    Short falls into a pool with a cliff viewpoint over the sea just below; only worth it once the rains start

  • Chandranatha Basadi, Bhatkal

    Temple · 17 km out

    Jain basadi from Bhatkal's port years with a tall manasthambha; the old stone mosques are the same walk

  • Sharavathi backwaters, Honnavar

    River · 32 km out

    Mangrove creeks where the Sharavathi meets the sea; boatmen wait at the Honnavar jetty, go on a rising tide

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Murudeshwar?

November to January is peak season in Murudeshwar. February to March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. October reopens the dive season once the swell drops, but visibility only settles properly by late November.

How many days do you need in Murudeshwar?

2 days is the usual stay. A pilgrim town more than a beach scene; budget hotels by the temple, dive shops the only other travellers.

How do you get to Murudeshwar?

Murdeshwar station on the Konkan line, temple 2 km; Gokarna 60 km north, Udupi 100 km south. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Murudeshwar expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Murudeshwar?

Feb/Mar: Maha Shivaratri. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Murudeshwar?

Murudeshwar Beach, Netrani Island, Idagunji Mahaganapati Temple, Apsarakonda Falls, Chandranatha Basadi, Bhatkal and Sharavathi backwaters, Honnavar. Grey sand under the statue with fishing boats at one end; the shelf drops away fast, so stay near the shore.
