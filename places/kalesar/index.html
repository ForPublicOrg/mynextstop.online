Kalesar National Park season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Nov-Feb is the season: 5-22 C, sal in leaf, and the Shivalik corridor elephants occasionally crossing the Yamuna.

Summer March to May Prime time

March still works for sightings at waterholes, but by May the Shivalik foothills sit at 42 C and dust.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

June is 42 C of pre-monsoon dust and the park then shuts July to September, so there is simply no way in.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Gates reopen in October to a green, tick-heavy forest; by November the undergrowth thins and sighting improves.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Kalesar National Park?

November to March is peak season in Kalesar National Park. April and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to September. Gates reopen in October to a green, tick-heavy forest; by November the undergrowth thins and sighting improves.

How many days do you need in Kalesar National Park?

2 days is the usual stay. Almost nobody here: forest rest house beds, a chowkidar cooking dal, and Haryana Tourism huts at Kalesar.

How do you get to Kalesar National Park?

Yamunanagar rail 55 km then bus; short morning and evening gate slots, rest house via Haryana Forest Dept. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Kalesar National Park expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
