Mawphanlur season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is crisp and empty: near-frost mornings, mirror ponds for a slow paddle, and bonfire evenings at the village guesthouses.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar-Apr gives clear ridge walks and wildflowers on the knolls; May opens the big pre-monsoon downpours, so keep plans loose.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun-Aug sits in rain and fog for days on end; the grass is at its greenest but you will see almost none of it, come later.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov is peak: rain-rinsed green hills, kayak-calm ponds and long easy ridge walks in shirtsleeve sunshine.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Mawphanlur?

October to February is peak season in Mawphanlur. March to April and September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to August. Oct-Nov is peak: rain-rinsed green hills, kayak-calm ponds and long easy ridge walks in shirtsleeve sunshine.

How many days do you need in Mawphanlur?

2 days is the usual stay. Village-run guesthouses on bare green hilltops; Shillong weekenders paddle, solo walkers get whole ridges to themselves.

How do you get to Mawphanlur?

78 km from Shillong via Mairang, 2.5 h; take a Nongstoin sumo to the turn-off and a local drop; no permits. Practical ways in: road.

Is Mawphanlur expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.
