Udaipur season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Best weather of the year. Lake boat rides and rooftop dinners without the sweat.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Lakes shrink and heat climbs past 38C, though evenings by the water stay tolerable.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Genuinely underrated: lakes fill, hills turn green, Sajjangarh's monsoon views are the year's best.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Post-monsoon lakes are full and the weather turns pleasant. Arguably the best month to visit.

Things to do in Udaipur

The local places worth your time in and around Udaipur, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • City Palace

    Palace · in town

    Mewar's lakeside complex of courtyards and mirrored rooms; enter at Badi Pol by 9.30 am, the Crystal Gallery costs extra.

  • Jagdish Temple

    Temple · in town

    1651 Vishnu temple up a 32 step stair in the old city; come for the evening aarti and leave shoes with the stall boy.

  • Lake Pichola

    Lake · in town

    Boats from Rameshwar Ghat inside the palace loop past Jag Mandir; the sunset slot sells out, so book earlier in the day.

  • Bagore ki Haveli

    Museum · in town

    18th century haveli museum on Gangaur Ghat; its 7 pm Dharohar folk dance is worth turning up an hour early for.

  • Sajjangarh (Monsoon Palace)

    Palace · 8 km out

    Hilltop monsoon palace above the lakes, inside a sanctuary so there is a second gate fee; go for the last hour of light.

  • Fateh Sagar Lake

    Lake · 4 km out

    The second lake, with a walkable promenade and boats to Nehru Garden island; evenings fill with families and chai carts.

  • Saheliyon ki Bari

    Garden · 4 km out

    Small 18th century garden of fountains and lotus pools built for royal women; 20 minutes, fountains run on request.

  • Badi Lake

    Lake · 12 km out

    Quiet reservoir with the short Bahubali Hill scramble above it, the sunrise the hostel crowd rides out for on scooters.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Udaipur?

November to February is peak season in Udaipur. March and July to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Post-monsoon lakes are full and the weather turns pleasant. Arguably the best month to visit.

How many days do you need in Udaipur?

3 days is the usual stay. Lakeside rooftop cafes are full of backpackers swapping Rajasthan routes nightly.

How do you get to Udaipur?

Fly into Udaipur or overnight train from Delhi/Jaipur. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Udaipur expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.

Is there a festival in Udaipur?

Mar: Mewar Festival (Gangaur). Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Udaipur?

City Palace, Jagdish Temple, Lake Pichola, Bagore ki Haveli, Sajjangarh (Monsoon Palace) and Fateh Sagar Lake, and more. Mewar's lakeside complex of courtyards and mirrored rooms; enter at Badi Pol by 9.30 am, the Crystal Gallery costs extra.
