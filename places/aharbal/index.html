Aharbal Falls season by season

Winter December to February Quiet, but open

Dec-Feb the fall shrinks to an icy ribbon between snowy pines; the Kulgam road usually stays open, check after big dumps.

Summer March to May Prime time

Apr-Jun snowmelt is peak thunder: the Veshaw explodes through the notch and spray soaks the lower ledge, go early on weekends.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Jul-Sep rain keeps the fall fat and the walk up to Kungwattan meadow green; short showers, slick steps, local day-trippers.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Oct-Nov the flow thins but the gorge is crisp and empty; JKTDC huts are easy to book and nights start touching zero.

What Aharbal Falls is actually like

Picnic families at the railing till mid-afternoon; stay in the JKTDC hut and the gorge and trout stream go quiet.

Getting to Aharbal Falls

Srinagar 70 km via Kulgam, 2.5 h; buses reach Kulgam, the last 17 km is taxi or hitching territory.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Aharbal Falls?

April to June is peak season in Aharbal Falls. March and July to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. No month is a write-off here. Apr-Jun snowmelt is peak thunder: the Veshaw explodes through the notch and spray soaks the lower ledge, go early on weekends.

How many days do you need in Aharbal Falls?

1 day is the usual stay. Picnic families at the railing till mid-afternoon; stay in the JKTDC hut and the gorge and trout stream go quiet.
