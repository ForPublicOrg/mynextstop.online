Jispa (Bhaga Valley) season by season

Winter December to February Do not go

Dec-Mar not now: the Leh road is shut at Baralacha, the camps and lodges are boarded up and snow cuts the Keylong-Darcha stretch for days

Summer March to May Shoulder

March is still snowbound; April thaws the village; by late May the Keylong-Darcha buses run again, though Baralacha La usually opens only in June

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Jun-Sep is the season: Baralacha La and Suraj Tal a 2 h drive up, Deepak Tal 8 km on, Manali-Leh buses and bikers passing through; rain rarely reaches this far

Autumn October to November Shoulder

October is clear and cold, gold poplars by the Bhaga and empty roads before Baralacha shuts in November; by then the camps are packed away and lodges close

What Jispa (Bhaga Valley) is actually like

Leh-road bikers and Zanskar trekkers in riverside camps and a few lodges; evenings are cold, quiet and loud with the Bhaga

Getting to Jispa (Bhaga Valley)

Manali-Keylong bus via the Atal Tunnel (4 h), then Keylong-Darcha bus 40 min; the HRTC Manali-Leh bus stops here Jul-Sep

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Jispa (Bhaga Valley)?

June to September is peak season in Jispa (Bhaga Valley). May and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid December to March. Jun-Sep is the season: Baralacha La and Suraj Tal a 2 h drive up, Deepak Tal 8 km on, Manali-Leh buses and bikers passing through; rain rarely reaches this far.

How many days do you need in Jispa (Bhaga Valley)?

2 days is the usual stay. Leh-road bikers and Zanskar trekkers in riverside camps and a few lodges; evenings are cold, quiet and loud with the Bhaga.

How do you get to Jispa (Bhaga Valley)?

Manali-Keylong bus via the Atal Tunnel (4 h), then Keylong-Darcha bus 40 min; the HRTC Manali-Leh bus stops here Jul-Sep. Practical ways in: flight and road.

Is Jispa (Bhaga Valley) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.
