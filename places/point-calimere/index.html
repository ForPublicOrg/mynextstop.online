Point Calimere (Kodiakkarai) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Jan is the entire reason to come, with thousands of flamingos on the Great Swamp and blackbuck herds in the scrub.

Summer March to May Mixed, read the detail

The migrants thin through February and are gone by March; Apr-May is 38 C of saltpan glare with no birds and no shade.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Jun-Sep is hot, humid and birdless, and the southwest monsoon misses this coast entirely, so there is no payoff at all.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

The first migrants land in October, but it is also the wettest month here, with cyclone warnings and flooded saltpan tracks.

What Point Calimere (Kodiakkarai) is actually like

A forest department rest house, a lighthouse and salt flats; book ahead or you sleep at Vedaranyam 11 km back.

Getting to Point Calimere (Kodiakkarai)

No practical railhead: bus Nagapattinam to Vedaranyam, then local bus 11 km to Kodiakkarai, last about 5pm.

When is the best time to visit Point Calimere (Kodiakkarai)?
