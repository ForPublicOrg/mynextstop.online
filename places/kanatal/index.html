Kanatal season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Dec-Feb brings real snowfalls at 2400 m; many tent camps shut, so book a cottage, carry chains for icy bends and enjoy the quiet.

Summer March to May Prime time

Apr-May is the sweet spot: 12-25 °C, orchard bloom and buzzing weekend campsites while the plains cook 2000 m below.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jul-Aug is fog, leeches and slips on the Chamba road and most camps close; Jun and Sep are usable between showers.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov is peak-clear: Bandarpunch and the Gangotri peaks line the horizon from Surkanda Devi and nights dip near freezing.

Signature festival

May/Jun: Ganga Dussehra mela at Surkanda Devi (dates shift)

Things to do in Kanatal

The local places worth your time in and around Kanatal, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Surkanda Devi Temple

    Temple · 8 km out

    Shakti peeth at 2757 m above Kaddukhal, a 1.5 km climb or the ropeway; the Himalayan line clears right after rain

  • Dhanaulti Eco Park

    Park · 10 km out

    Deodar and oak park at Amber and Deolsari with rope walks and benches; small ticket, weekends fill with families

  • Tehri Lake at Koti Colony

    Lake · 32 km out

    Reservoir jetty for boats and jet skis under the dam; an hour down by taxi, rates are fixed at the counter

  • Kaudia Forest

    Walk · 5 km out

    Pine and deodar range below the road with a jungle trail and a mountain bike track; no signage, take someone local

  • Chamba

    Market · 12 km out

    Junction bazaar where the Mussoorie and Rishikesh roads meet; nearest ATMs, chemist and shared jeeps, busy till 8 pm

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Kanatal?

April to May and October to November is peak season in Kanatal. June, September and December to March is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid July to August. Apr-May is the sweet spot: 12-25 °C, orchard bloom and buzzing weekend campsites while the plains cook 2000 m below.

How many days do you need in Kanatal?

2 days is the usual stay. Delhi weekenders around bonfires, one small backpacker hostel, and quiet orchard homestays a lane off the road.

How do you get to Kanatal?

Dehradun (Jolly Grant) airport 80 km, about 2.5 h; buses Rishikesh to Chamba then 12 km taxi; rail is 3 h out. Practical ways in: flight and road.

Is Kanatal expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Kanatal?

May/Jun: Ganga Dussehra mela at Surkanda Devi (dates shift). Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Kanatal?

Surkanda Devi Temple, Dhanaulti Eco Park, Tehri Lake at Koti Colony, Kaudia Forest and Chamba. Shakti peeth at 2757 m above Kaddukhal, a 1.5 km climb or the ropeway; the Himalayan line clears right after rain.
