Tso Kar season by season

Winter December to February Do not go

Dec-Feb is -30 C at 4,530 m with the Manali road shut; the Rupshu camps are packed up and nobody stays out here.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar-Apr the Rupshu plateau is frozen and empty; May opens the Leh side but nights still run below -10 C at 4,530 m.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Jun-Sep is warm, dry and the whole point: cranes on nests, kiang on the flats, nomad camps pitched at Thukje village.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Early Oct has the sharpest light and the whitest salt rim; by late Oct camps fold and nights drop past -15 C again.

What Tso Kar is actually like

Nomad tents and parachute cafes at Thukje, one homestay cluster, and altitude that flattens anyone who rushes.

Getting to Tso Kar

Leh 155 km, 5 h via Taglang La; no public bus, share a taxi; foreigners need a Protected Area Permit.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Tso Kar?

June to September is peak season in Tso Kar. May and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid November to April. Jun-Sep is warm, dry and the whole point: cranes on nests, kiang on the flats, nomad camps pitched at Thukje village.

How many days do you need in Tso Kar?

2 days is the usual stay. Nomad tents and parachute cafes at Thukje, one homestay cluster, and altitude that flattens anyone who rushes.

How do you get to Tso Kar?

Leh 155 km, 5 h via Taglang La; no public bus, share a taxi; foreigners need a Protected Area Permit. Practical ways in: road.

Is Tso Kar expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
