Yellapur (Sathodi & Magod Falls) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb both falls still run on perennial rivers: January mornings are cold and clear and the forest tracks at their driest.

Summer March to May Mixed, read the detail

Mar-Apr the flow thins as heat builds to 35 °C; Magod's gorge stays worth it, Sathodi barely; May is empty for a reason.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Jun-Aug the streams across the Sathodi track run axle-deep and mist swallows Magod's gorge; wait for the rain to ease.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Sep-Nov is the show: Magod's two-step plunge of nearly 200 m in full force, Sathodi's curtain pounding its pool, roads passable again

What Yellapur (Sathodi & Magod Falls) is actually like

Arecanut farmstays and plain town lodges; you share the falls with langurs and a few Karnataka road-trippers.

Getting to Yellapur (Sathodi & Magod Falls)

Hubballi airport and railhead 70 km; buses from Sirsi and Hubballi, then hired jeep or scooter to the falls.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Yellapur (Sathodi & Magod Falls)?

September to December is peak season in Yellapur (Sathodi & Magod Falls). January to February is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to August. Jun-Aug the streams across the Sathodi track run axle-deep and mist swallows Magod's gorge; wait for the rain to ease.

How many days do you need in Yellapur (Sathodi & Magod Falls)?

2 days is the usual stay. Arecanut farmstays and plain town lodges; you share the falls with langurs and a few Karnataka road-trippers.

How do you get to Yellapur (Sathodi & Magod Falls)?

Hubballi airport and railhead 70 km; buses from Sirsi and Hubballi, then hired jeep or scooter to the falls. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Yellapur (Sathodi & Magod Falls) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
