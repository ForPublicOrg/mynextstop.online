Daman (Makwanpur) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Clearest views in Dec, with frost and the odd snowfall in Jan and Feb; pack warm layers for sunrise at the tower.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Rhododendron forests bloom in Mar and Apr along the Rajpath, but haze often hides the peaks by afternoon.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Cloud and rain sit on the pass, the winding Rajpath sees landslides and the mountain view is mostly gone.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Best time: Dhaulagiri to Everest across the horizon on clear mornings, crisp air and quiet resorts.

What Daman (Makwanpur) is actually like

A few resorts and lodges on the ridge, quiet pine forest, Kathmandu weekenders and bikers riding the Rajpath.

Things to do in Daman

The local places worth your time in and around Daman, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Daman View Tower

    Viewpoint · in town

    Old tower with telescopes for the full Himalayan panorama; come at sunrise before the cloud builds.

  • Simbhanjyang

    Viewpoint · 2 km out

    Highest point on the Rajpath, a short drive south, with tea stalls and walks into the rhododendron forest.

  • Chitlang

    Village · 9 km out

    Newar valley village on the old trade route to Kathmandu, known for goat cheese and homestays.

  • Indra Sarobar (Kulekhani)

    Lake · 8 km out

    Reservoir of the Kulekhani dam below Daman, with boating and fish lunches on the shore.

Getting to Daman (Makwanpur)

About 80 km from Kathmandu on the winding Tribhuvan Rajpath, 3 to 4 h by road; Hetauda is about 2 h south.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Daman (Makwanpur)?

October to December is peak season in Daman (Makwanpur). January to May and September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to August. Best time: Dhaulagiri to Everest across the horizon on clear mornings, crisp air and quiet resorts.

How many days do you need in Daman (Makwanpur)?

1 day is the usual stay. A few resorts and lodges on the ridge, quiet pine forest, Kathmandu weekenders and bikers riding the Rajpath.

How do you get to Daman (Makwanpur)?

About 80 km from Kathmandu on the winding Tribhuvan Rajpath, 3 to 4 h by road; Hetauda is about 2 h south. Practical ways in: road.

Is Daman (Makwanpur) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Daman?

Daman View Tower, Simbhanjyang, Chitlang and Indra Sarobar (Kulekhani). Old tower with telescopes for the full Himalayan panorama; come at sunrise before the cloud builds.
