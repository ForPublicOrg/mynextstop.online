Agumbe season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Waterfalls still running from the tail of monsoon, forest lush, no leeches - underrated.

Summer March to May Do not go

The rainforest without rain is just a forest - falls reduce to a trickle, skip it.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

This is the season - 700cm+ of rain, roaring falls, king cobras out, but leeches everywhere.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Monsoon runoff still feeding the falls, canopy dripping green, sunset point visible again.

What Agumbe is actually like

A handful of naturalists and photographers - homestay dinners are the social hub.

Things to do in Agumbe

The local places worth your time in and around Agumbe, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Agumbe Sunset Point

    Viewpoint · 1 km out

    Ghat edge platform looking over the coastal plain to the sea; only clear from November to February, monsoon is cloud

  • Doddamane

    Heritage · in town

    The old tiled house used for the Malgudi Days shoot, still a family home; visitors are let in during daylight hours

  • Onake Abbi Falls

    Waterfall · 5 km out

    Deep forest fall about an hour's walk off the ghat road; forest office permission needed and the path shuts in the rains

  • Jogigundi Falls

    Waterfall · 4 km out

    Small fall beside a cave shrine, twenty minutes on a leech heavy path; carry salt and socks in the wet months

  • Barkana Falls Viewpoint

    Viewpoint · 8 km out

    Roadside lookout across the valley to a fall of about 250 m; it runs June to October only, binoculars help

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Agumbe?

June to September is peak season in Agumbe. October to February is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid March to May. This is the season - 700cm+ of rain, roaring falls, king cobras out, but leeches everywhere.

How many days do you need in Agumbe?

2 days is the usual stay. A handful of naturalists and photographers - homestay dinners are the social hub.

How do you get to Agumbe?

Fly Mangalore, 3h drive; a rental car is close to essential. Practical ways in: flight and road.

Is Agumbe expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

What are the best things to do in Agumbe?

Agumbe Sunset Point, Doddamane, Onake Abbi Falls, Jogigundi Falls and Barkana Falls Viewpoint. Ghat edge platform looking over the coastal plain to the sea; only clear from November to February, monsoon is cloud.
