Hastinapur season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Jan brings 5 °C nights, dense fog that wrecks Delhi train timings and NCR-grade smog; February clears up.

Summer March to May Prime time

March is the best month here; from April the Ganga khadar bakes past 42 °C and the approach tracks are dust.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Jul-Sep the Ganga khadar floods, the approach tracks turn impassable and there is nothing dry to walk to.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is warm and clear before the stubble-burning haze sets in; the khadar tracks are dry and walkable.

Things to do in Hastinapur

The local places worth your time in and around Hastinapur, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Jambudweep Jain Tirth

    Temple · in town

    Digambar complex built around a huge model of the Jain universe; free to walk, and the dharamshala takes walk ins

  • Kailash Parvat Rachna

    Temple · in town

    Tall white model of Mount Kailash with shrines up its sides, in the Jambudweep grounds; climb it for the view

  • Shri Digambar Jain Bada Mandir

    Temple · 1 km out

    The old Jain temple of the town, far quieter than Jambudweep; the pujari can point out the Mahabharata mounds

  • Pandeshwar Mahadev Temple

    Temple · 1 km out

    Shiva temple the Pandavas are said to have founded; busy on Mondays and at Shivratri, a short walk off the main road

  • Hastinapur Wildlife Sanctuary

    Wildlife · 8 km out

    Ganga floodplain grassland with blackbuck, hog deer and winter cranes; no gate or jeeps, you need your own vehicle

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Hastinapur?

February to March and October is peak season in Hastinapur. November to January is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to September. Dec-Jan brings 5 °C nights, dense fog that wrecks Delhi train timings and NCR-grade smog; February clears up.

How many days do you need in Hastinapur?

1 day is the usual stay. Jain pilgrims at Jambudweep far outnumber tourists; Digambar Jain dharamshalas take walk-ins for very little.

How do you get to Hastinapur?

Meerut 37 km by bus in about an hour; Delhi 100 km, and there is no station in town so use Meerut City. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Hastinapur expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

What are the best things to do in Hastinapur?

Jambudweep Jain Tirth, Kailash Parvat Rachna, Shri Digambar Jain Bada Mandir, Pandeshwar Mahadev Temple and Hastinapur Wildlife Sanctuary. Digambar complex built around a huge model of the Jain universe; free to walk, and the dharamshala takes walk ins.
