Tadoba season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Cool mornings on safari with good sightings, though denser foliage can hide tigers.

Summer March to May Prime time

Brutal heat but the best sighting odds of the year as tigers cluster at waterholes.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Core zone closed to visitors for the breeding season from July-September - no safaris run.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Reopens in October as the closure lifts, forest still lush, sightings improve by November.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Tadoba?

February to April is peak season in Tadoba. May to June and October to January is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid July to September. Cool mornings on safari with good sightings, though denser foliage can hide tigers.

How many days do you need in Tadoba?

3 days is the usual stay. Photographers and wildlife fans sharing jeeps - safaris throw solo travellers together.

How do you get to Tadoba?

Fly to Nagpur (NAG), then 2.5-3h drive to Moharli or Kolara gate. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Tadoba expensive, and does it get crowded?

Splurge by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
