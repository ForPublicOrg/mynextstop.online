Jog Falls season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Usually reduced to a trickle - water's held back at the Linganamakki dam upstream.

Summer March to May Do not go

Skip it - 95 percent of the Sharavathi's rain falls Jun-Sep, so Mar-May is bare cliff unless the dam happens to release.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

The real deal - July-September the Sharavathi thunders down in full four-channel force.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

October-November still carries good residual flow, with far fewer crowds than July.

What Jog Falls is actually like

A viewpoint stop for photographers and day-trippers, not really a place to linger solo.

Things to do in Jog Falls

The local places worth your time in and around Jog Falls, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Linganamakki Dam

    Reservoir · 10 km out

    The reservoir upstream that decides how much water reaches the falls; gates open only in a full monsoon, the drive out is short.

  • Dabbe Falls

    Waterfall · 25 km out

    White cascade down a rock face in Sharavathi forest; jeep from Hosagadde then a steep path, leeches after rain, dry by March.

  • Sigandur Chowdeshwari Temple

    Temple · 33 km out

    Backwater goddess across the Sharavathi whose stickers ride on lorry cabs; a half day out from the falls, go in the morning.

  • Keladi Rameshwara Temple

    Temple · 38 km out

    Keladi Nayaka temple with a carved wooden ceiling and a small dynasty museum beside it; buses run from Sagara town.

  • Gerusoppa Chaturmukha Basadi

    Heritage · 28 km out

    Four faced Jain basadi in forest downstream, built by the pepper queen Chennabhairadevi; the last stretch is a rough track.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Jog Falls?

July to September is peak season in Jog Falls. June and October to December is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid January to May. The real deal - July-September the Sharavathi thunders down in full four-channel force.

How many days do you need in Jog Falls?

2 days is the usual stay. A viewpoint stop for photographers and day-trippers, not really a place to linger solo.

How do you get to Jog Falls?

Nearest hub Shimoga (2.5h drive) or fly Mangalore (3.5h). Practical ways in: flight and road.

Is Jog Falls expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

What are the best things to do in Jog Falls?

Linganamakki Dam, Dabbe Falls, Sigandur Chowdeshwari Temple, Keladi Rameshwara Temple and Gerusoppa Chaturmukha Basadi. The reservoir upstream that decides how much water reaches the falls; gates open only in a full monsoon, the drive out is short.
