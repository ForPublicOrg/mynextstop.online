Nandi Hills season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is the reason people come: 12 C at dawn, a cloud sea below the ramparts, and a long queue of cars behind you.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar-May the hilltop stays maybe 8 C cooler than Bengaluru, but the plain below is hazy and the cloud sea does not form.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun-Sep the hill sits inside the cloud rather than above it: green, cool, and some mornings you see fifty metres.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov is quietly the best value, clear air after the rains and thinner weekday crowds than you get in December.

What Nandi Hills is actually like

Sunrise crowds, cyclists grinding up the ghat, couples on the ramparts; a state-run hotel lets you sleep up top

Things to do in Nandi Hills

The local places worth your time in and around Nandi Hills, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Tipu's Drop

    Viewpoint · in town

    Cliff edge on the summit with a 600 m fall past the railing; be there before 6 am for the cloud sea below

  • Bhoga Nandishwara Temple

    Temple · 10 km out

    Ninth century temple at the foot of the hill with a stone Kalyani tank; opens 6 am and stays far quieter

  • Yoga Nandeeshwara Temple

    Temple · in town

    Small Shiva shrine inside the summit fort walls, cool and shaded; two minutes on foot from the top car park

  • Amrutha Sarovara

    Lake · in town

    Spring fed tank on the summit that never dries out; the path around it is the only quiet corner up top

  • Skandagiri

    Trek · 12 km out

    Night climb to a ruined fort for sunrise over cloud; forest department permits and a guide are now compulsory

  • Visvesvaraya Museum, Muddenahalli

    Museum · 12 km out

    The engineer's birth house and his notebooks and medals, below the ghat road; small fee, half an hour is enough

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Nandi Hills?

November to February is peak season in Nandi Hills. March to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. No month is a write-off here. Oct-Nov is quietly the best value, clear air after the rains and thinner weekday crowds than you get in December.

How many days do you need in Nandi Hills?

1 day is the usual stay. Sunrise crowds, cyclists grinding up the ghat, couples on the ramparts; a state-run hotel lets you sleep up top.

How do you get to Nandi Hills?

Chikkaballapur railhead 10 km below, autos up the ghat road; weekend vehicle quotas make a weekday far easier. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Nandi Hills expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.

What are the best things to do in Nandi Hills?

Tipu's Drop, Bhoga Nandishwara Temple, Yoga Nandeeshwara Temple, Amrutha Sarovara, Skandagiri and Visvesvaraya Museum, Muddenahalli. Cliff edge on the summit with a 600 m fall past the railing; be there before 6 am for the cloud sea below.
