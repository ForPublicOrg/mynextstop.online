Ghatshila season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is the classic Bengali winter break: 12C dawns on Phuldungri hill, picnics by Burudih lake and Bibhutibhushan's house at Dahigora open for a look

Summer March to May Shoulder

March is already 38C; Apr-May the Subarnarekha shrinks to sand and the town hits 44C with no shade on the hill roads, so not now

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun-Sep rains bring Dharagiri falls 13 km north to life and the Subarnarekha runs full and red-brown; humid, but the lodges empty out and the sal forest is green

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct is warm and washed clean, Durga Puja fills the lodges with Kolkata families; Nov cools, the river clears and the Galudih barrage reflects the hills

What Ghatshila is actually like

Kolkata couples and families in cottage lodges around Dahigora; evenings are the riverbank, the Rankini temple lane and early dinners

Getting to Ghatshila

Ghatshila station on the Howrah-Tatanagar line: 3.5 h from Howrah by Steel Express, 40 min from Tatanagar; autos to Burudih

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Ghatshila?

November to February is peak season in Ghatshila. March and July to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to May. Oct is warm and washed clean, Durga Puja fills the lodges with Kolkata families; Nov cools, the river clears and the Galudih barrage reflects the hills.

How many days do you need in Ghatshila?

2 days is the usual stay. Kolkata couples and families in cottage lodges around Dahigora; evenings are the riverbank, the Rankini temple lane and early dinners.

How do you get to Ghatshila?

Ghatshila station on the Howrah-Tatanagar line: 3.5 h from Howrah by Steel Express, 40 min from Tatanagar; autos to Burudih. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Ghatshila expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.
