Kishangarh season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

At 7-25 C the walk works: Phool Mahal above Gundalao lake, then the ateliers still painting in the Bani Thani style.

Summer March to May Shoulder

April-June hits 43 C and the marble slurry yard turns into blinding white glare with zero shade; go at sunrise or skip it.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

July-September brings most of the district's 500 mm; Gundalao fills and the slurry flats become sticky white mud.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October-November is dry and mild, and this is a real half-day escape from the dargah crush in Ajmer 27 km down NH-48.

What Kishangarh is actually like

A marble-trade town with a painting tradition: business hotels and dhabas, no travellers, artists glad to talk shop

Things to do in Kishangarh

The local places worth your time in and around Kishangarh, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Phool Mahal Palace

    Palace · in town

    Lakeside palace of the Kishangarh school painters, now part hotel; ask at reception to see the Bani Thani style work.

  • Gundalao Lake

    Lake · 1 km out

    The tank the fort and palace sit on, full only after a good monsoon; walk the far bank at dusk for the whole skyline.

  • Kishangarh Marble Dumping Yard

    Viewpoint · 7 km out

    Hills of white marble slurry that photograph like snow; go early, the dust is harsh by noon and trucks own the track.

  • Roopangarh Fort

    Fort · 26 km out

    1648 Kishangarh capital fort on a ridge, now a heritage hotel; non guests can usually see the courtyards, ask first.

  • Nimbarkacharya Peeth, Salemabad

    Temple · 30 km out

    Seat of the Nimbarka order, a working monastery with big courtyards; time it for the noon aarti and dress plainly.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Kishangarh?

November to February is peak season in Kishangarh. March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. October-November is dry and mild, and this is a real half-day escape from the dargah crush in Ajmer 27 km down NH-48.

How many days do you need in Kishangarh?

1 day is the usual stay. A marble-trade town with a painting tradition: business hotels and dhabas, no travellers, artists glad to talk shop.

How do you get to Kishangarh?

Kishangarh station on the Jaipur-Ajmer line; Ajmer 27 km with buses every 20 min on NH-48, Jaipur 100 km. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Kishangarh expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Kishangarh?

Phool Mahal Palace, Gundalao Lake, Kishangarh Marble Dumping Yard, Roopangarh Fort and Nimbarkacharya Peeth, Salemabad. Lakeside palace of the Kishangarh school painters, now part hotel; ask at reception to see the Bani Thani style work.
