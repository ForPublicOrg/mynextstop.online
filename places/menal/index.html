Menal (Falls & Temples) season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Dec-Feb the falls thin to a trickle, but the carved Mahanaleshwar temples and the empty gorge rim make a fine 12-25 °C highway stop.

Summer March to May Mixed, read the detail

Apr-Jun is 43 °C on the open Uparmal plateau, the gorge bone dry and the temple stones too hot to linger on; skip it entirely.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Jul-Sep is the show: the Menali river leaps off the plateau in full spate with spray drifting over the shikharas; wet rock, so mind the rim.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Oct usually keeps decent flow, washed green scrub and comfortable 20-32 °C days; by late Nov the river fades right back.

What Menal (Falls & Temples) is actually like

Local picnic crowds on monsoon Sundays, priests and langurs otherwise; sleep in Bundi or Chittorgarh, nothing stays here

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Menal (Falls & Temples)?

July to September is peak season in Menal (Falls & Temples). October to February is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Jul-Sep is the show: the Menali river leaps off the plateau in full spate with spray drifting over the shikharas; wet rock, so mind the rim.

How many days do you need in Menal (Falls & Temples)?

1 day is the usual stay. Local picnic crowds on monsoon Sundays, priests and langurs otherwise; sleep in Bundi or Chittorgarh, nothing stays here.

How do you get to Menal (Falls & Temples)?

On the Chittorgarh-Kota highway: buses drop at the gate; Chittorgarh railhead about 70 km, Bundi 75 km. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Menal (Falls & Temples) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
