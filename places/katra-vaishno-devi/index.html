Katra (Vaishno Devi) season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Cold but manageable, genuinely a good time - cool trekking weather, thinner crowds outside New Year.

Summer March to May Prime time

March-April brings Navratri crowds in good weather; by May plains heat climbs toward 40C.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Worst window - brutal heat plus monsoon rain that can trigger landslides and track closures.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October's Sharad Navratri is peak pilgrim season with huge crowds but excellent weather; Nov calmer.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Katra (Vaishno Devi)?

March to April and September to October is peak season in Katra (Vaishno Devi). November to February is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to August. March-April brings Navratri crowds in good weather; by May plains heat climbs toward 40C.

How many days do you need in Katra (Vaishno Devi)?

2 days is the usual stay. A pilgrimage crowd, not backpackers, but solo-safe - easy to join a group on the climb.

How do you get to Katra (Vaishno Devi)?

Fly/train to Jammu, then train/taxi to Katra (~2h). Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Katra (Vaishno Devi) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Katra (Vaishno Devi)?

Oct: Sharad Navratri peak pilgrimage rush. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
