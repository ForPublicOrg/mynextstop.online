Photoksar (Fotoksar) season by season

Winter December to February Do not go

Dec-Feb Sirsir La is under snow and the village lives on stored barley; there is no way in and no bed for a visitor.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar-Apr the passes are still shut, and May depends entirely on how fast BRO clears Sirsir La, so keep the plan loose.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Jun-Sep is the season: Ladakh sees almost no rain, the terraces turn green and trekkers drop in off the Sengge La trail.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

October is gold, cold and clear with the harvest in; by November snow shuts the pass and the shared jeeps stop running.

What Photoksar (Fotoksar) is actually like

Two dozen flat-roofed houses with homestay floors and a dung stove; no shop, no signal, trekkers in off the pass by dusk.

Getting to Photoksar (Fotoksar)

Leh to Lamayuru 5h, then a shared jeep or hired taxi about 4h up past Wanla and over Sirsir La.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Photoksar (Fotoksar)?

June to September is peak season in Photoksar (Fotoksar). May and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid November to April. Jun-Sep is the season: Ladakh sees almost no rain, the terraces turn green and trekkers drop in off the Sengge La trail.

How many days do you need in Photoksar (Fotoksar)?

2 days is the usual stay. Two dozen flat-roofed houses with homestay floors and a dung stove; no shop, no signal, trekkers in off the pass by dusk.

How do you get to Photoksar (Fotoksar)?

Leh to Lamayuru 5h, then a shared jeep or hired taxi about 4h up past Wanla and over Sirsir La. Practical ways in: road.

Is Photoksar (Fotoksar) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
