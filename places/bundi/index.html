Bundi season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Cool, quiet, ideal for wandering the palace and stepwells without crowds.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Plains heat hits past 42C with little shade in the narrow lanes. Best avoided.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

The town's famous stepwells fill dramatically and the surrounding hills turn green.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Temperatures ease and the post-monsoon countryside is at its greenest.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Bundi?

November to February is peak season in Bundi. March and July to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Temperatures ease and the post-monsoon countryside is at its greenest.

How many days do you need in Bundi?

2 days is the usual stay. Quiet and unpolished; you'll mostly meet the same handful of travelers staying in town.

How do you get to Bundi?

3h bus/train from Kota, or 5h from Jaipur. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Bundi expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Bundi?

Nov: Bundi Utsav. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
