Somnath season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Cool sea breeze and comfortable temple hours - best time to combine with Diu or Gir.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Hot and glaring on the open temple grounds, midday visits are tough going.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Dramatic crashing seas by the temple, but humid with occasional heavy downpours.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Comfortable and clear by October, a good lead-in to the winter pilgrim season.

Signature festival

Things to do in Somnath

The local places worth your time in and around Somnath, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Bhalka Tirth

    Temple · 5 km out

    Where a hunter's arrow is said to have struck Krishna, a small quiet shrine on the Veraval road; ten minutes, go early.

  • Triveni Sangam

    Ghat · 2 km out

    Ghats where the Hiran, Kapila and Saraswati meet the sea; cremations and an evening aarti, ask before you photograph.

  • Somnath Beach

    Beach · in town

    Rough dark sand right below the temple wall, camel rides at dusk; do not swim, the currents here are strong.

  • Prabhas Patan Museum

    Museum · 1 km out

    Carved stone salvaged from the earlier Somnath temples, laid out plainly; small fee, an hour is enough.

  • Gita Mandir

    Temple · 2 km out

    Birla built marble temple by the Hiran with the whole Gita cut into its pillars; free, quiet mid morning, shoes outside.

  • Surya Mandir (Prabhas Patan)

    Temple · 1 km out

    Small weathered Sun temple in the Prabhas Patan lanes, hardly visited; ask a local for the turning off the main road.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Somnath?

November to February is peak season in Somnath. March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to September. Comfortable and clear by October, a good lead-in to the winter pilgrim season.

How many days do you need in Somnath?

2 days is the usual stay. Devotional pilgrim crowds and a seaside sound-and-light show - quiet, little socialising.

How do you get to Somnath?

Fly to Diu (1h) or Rajkot (3h), then drive; also rail-connected. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Somnath expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

Is there a festival in Somnath?

Feb/Mar: Maha Shivratri - biggest pilgrim gathering of the year. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Somnath?

Bhalka Tirth, Triveni Sangam, Somnath Beach, Prabhas Patan Museum, Gita Mandir and Surya Mandir (Prabhas Patan). Where a hunter's arrow is said to have struck Krishna, a small quiet shrine on the Veraval road; ten minutes, go early.
