Mansar Lake season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is the season - 8-20 C, migratory duck on the water and the Sheshnag parikrama walk done in cool air.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar is still fine and Apr warms fast, but by May these Shivalik foothills push 40 C with an hour of usable sal shade.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Skip Jun-Sep: June is a 40 C furnace, then Jul-Sep runs 33 C and sticky with the forest circuit path a mud slog.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct dries out and cools, then Nov is properly good: wetland birds arriving at Surinsar-Mansar and boating back on.

What Mansar Lake is actually like

Jammu picnic families and mundan parties at the shrine; JKTDC huts on the bank and no traveller scene at all.

Getting to Mansar Lake

Jammu 60 km, Udhampur 40 km; Jammu-Udhampur road buses drop at Mansar chowk, about 1.5 h from Jammu.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Mansar Lake?

November to February is peak season in Mansar Lake. March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to September. Oct dries out and cools, then Nov is properly good: wetland birds arriving at Surinsar-Mansar and boating back on.

How many days do you need in Mansar Lake?

1 day is the usual stay. Jammu picnic families and mundan parties at the shrine; JKTDC huts on the bank and no traveller scene at all.

How do you get to Mansar Lake?

Jammu 60 km, Udhampur 40 km; Jammu-Udhampur road buses drop at Mansar chowk, about 1.5 h from Jammu. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Mansar Lake expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.
