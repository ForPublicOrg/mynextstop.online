Bakkhali & Henry Island season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

The one good window: 14-26 C, hard flat sand you can walk for kilometres, red crabs at low tide and Henry Island.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March is warm but usable; April-May is 38 C and humid, and the pre-monsoon Bay of Bengal cyclone risk starts up.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Jun-Sep the sea is rough and grey, hotels are half shut and the beach is a wind-blown mudflat; no swimming at all.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October and early November still carry post-monsoon cyclone risk, but late November is clean, cool and near empty.

What Bakkhali & Henry Island is actually like

Bengali families on cheap weekend packages; simple beachfront hotels, fish thali shacks, no bar or backpacker scene.

Things to do in Bakkhali & Henry Island

The local places worth your time in and around Bakkhali & Henry Island, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Frasergunj Fishing Harbour

    Market · 6 km out

    Trawlers unload at dawn and the catch is auctioned on the jetty; 6 km on by toto, go early or it is all over.

  • Mousuni Island

    Island · 28 km out

    Tent camps on an eroding beach across the Chinai river; boat from Namkhana then a toto, power and food are basic.

  • Kapil Muni Temple (Gangasagar)

    Temple · 35 km out

    The Gangasagar mela shrine at the mouth of the Ganga; take the Chemaguri ferry from Namkhana and start early.

  • Jambudwip

    Island · 12 km out

    Uninhabited forest island offshore once used by fishing crews to dry the catch; landing is barred, boats pass it only.

Getting to Bakkhali & Henry Island

Namkhana is about 3.5 h from Kolkata by Sealdah South local or bus, then an hour by bus or auto to Bakkhali.
