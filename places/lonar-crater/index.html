Lonar Crater Lake season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is 12-30 C, right for the rim walk and the early descent to the alkaline green lake at dawn.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-Jun is 43 C and the crater bowl traps the heat; the descent path has no shade and no drinking water.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

Jul-Sep greens the crater walls and lifts the lake; the rim path gets slippery but the temples stay empty.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov is the best of it, cool mornings, clean light in the crater bowl and almost nobody else around.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Lonar Crater Lake?

November to February is peak season in Lonar Crater Lake. March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Oct-Nov is the best of it, cool mornings, clean light in the crater bowl and almost nobody else around.

How many days do you need in Lonar Crater Lake?

1 day is the usual stay. One MTDC resort and a couple of lodges; you walk the rim past langurs and half-ruined shrines alone.

How do you get to Lonar Crater Lake?

Jalna railhead 90 km or Aurangabad 140 km, then a slow ST bus; the last bus back goes mid-afternoon. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Lonar Crater Lake expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
