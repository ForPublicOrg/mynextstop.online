Sultanpur National Park (Bird Sanctuary) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is the whole point: over 100 migratory species on the jheel at 4-18 C, though Delhi smog greys the light.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March still holds stragglers, but by May the jheel has shrunk to cracked mud and the plains sit at 44 C.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Jun-Sep is pointless: the migrants are still up north, the paths are mud and it is 35 C at brutal humidity.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Oct is green and near-empty; Nov brings the first waders, but NCR air in this corner routinely passes AQI 300.

Things to do in Sultanpur National Park

The local places worth your time in and around Sultanpur National Park, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Baoli Ghaus Ali Shah

    Heritage · 7 km out

    Deep 18th century stepwell in Farrukhnagar, dry and quiet; ask in the bazaar for the lane, no fee and no guide

  • Sheesh Mahal, Farrukhnagar

    Palace · 7 km out

    Faujdar Khan's 1730s palace from the town's salt trading years, faded mirror work; the caretaker opens it on request

  • Basai Wetland

    Lake · 14 km out

    Shallow wetland on Gurugram's edge that draws the same waders and storks; much is built over, view it from the road bund

  • Sheetla Mata Mandir

    Temple · 18 km out

    Big Devi temple in old Gurugram drawing crowds all year; Navratri queues are enormous, so come on a weekday morning

  • Aravalli Biodiversity Park

    Park · 25 km out

    Old mining land in Gurugram replanted as native Aravalli forest; several km of walking trails, busiest at dawn

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Sultanpur National Park (Bird Sanctuary)?

December to February is peak season in Sultanpur National Park (Bird Sanctuary). March and October to November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to September. Dec-Feb is the whole point: over 100 migratory species on the jheel at 4-18 C, though Delhi smog greys the light.

How many days do you need in Sultanpur National Park (Bird Sanctuary)?

1 day is the usual stay. Weekend birders with long lenses and school groups; you sleep back in Gurugram, not here.

How do you get to Sultanpur National Park (Bird Sanctuary)?

Gurugram 20 km by cab or Farrukhnagar bus; closed Tuesdays, gates roughly 7am to 4.30pm. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Sultanpur National Park (Bird Sanctuary) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Sultanpur National Park?

Baoli Ghaus Ali Shah, Sheesh Mahal, Farrukhnagar, Basai Wetland, Sheetla Mata Mandir and Aravalli Biodiversity Park. Deep 18th century stepwell in Farrukhnagar, dry and quiet; ask in the bazaar for the lane, no fee and no guide.
