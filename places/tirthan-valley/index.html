Tirthan Valley (Gushaini) season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Cold and sleepy, some guesthouses shut for lack of guests, but the river valley stays starkly beautiful.

Summer March to May Prime time

Best season - Mar-May bring clear river water, blooming forest and prime trout season.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun is workable; Jul-Aug bring flash-flood risk on the river and landslides on the Aut-Gushaini road.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Quiet and golden - GHNP trails run driest, river levels drop, ideal for birdwatching walks.

What Tirthan Valley (Gushaini) is actually like

Riverside guesthouses run by trout-farming families - solitude and nature, not a crowd.

Things to do in Tirthan Valley

The local places worth your time in and around Tirthan Valley, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Great Himalayan National Park

    Wildlife · 5 km out

    UNESCO park gate at Sai Ropa; permit and a guide from the range office, then the trail up the Tirthan to Rolla.

  • Tirthan River

    River · in town

    Brown trout water past every guesthouse; angling permit from the fisheries office at Banjar, catch and release.

  • Chhoi Waterfall

    Waterfall · 7 km out

    Forest fall above Nagini, 30 minutes up from the road over a footbridge; empty on weekdays, slippery after rain.

  • Shringa Rishi Temple

    Temple · 13 km out

    Carved cedar seat of the Banjar valley's ruling deity at Bagi; the god travels, so ask before photographing.

Getting to Tirthan Valley (Gushaini)

Fly Bhuntar (1.5h) or Delhi-Aut bus (10h), 1h taxi via Larji.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Tirthan Valley (Gushaini)?

March to May and October to November is peak season in Tirthan Valley (Gushaini). June, September and December to February is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid July to August. Best season - Mar-May bring clear river water, blooming forest and prime trout season.

How many days do you need in Tirthan Valley (Gushaini)?

4 days is the usual stay. Riverside guesthouses run by trout-farming families - solitude and nature, not a crowd.

How do you get to Tirthan Valley (Gushaini)?

Fly Bhuntar (1.5h) or Delhi-Aut bus (10h), 1h taxi via Larji. Practical ways in: road.

Is Tirthan Valley (Gushaini) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

What are the best things to do in Tirthan Valley?

Great Himalayan National Park, Tirthan River, Chhoi Waterfall and Shringa Rishi Temple. UNESCO park gate at Sai Ropa; permit and a guide from the range office, then the trail up the Tirthan to Rolla.
