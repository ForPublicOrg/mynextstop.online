Son Beel (Karimganj) season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Dec-Feb the water drops to under half its monsoon size, the edges turn to boro paddy and fish traps and duck flocks arrive; boats still run the deep middle

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar-Apr the beel is at its lowest and hazy at 30 C, fields where the lake was; May storms start refilling it and the boatmen return to the ghat

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Jun-Sep the beel is a lake of nearly 10,000 hectares between two lines of hills, fishing villages on stilts and boats for hire at about 800 rupees an hour

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is prime: full water, the rain gone and hyacinth in flower; by November the level drops and the eastern shore starts to show paddy again

What Son Beel (Karimganj) is actually like

Fishing families and Bengali day-trippers from Karimganj, almost no outsiders; sleep in a basic lodge in Karimganj or Hailakandi town

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Son Beel (Karimganj)?

September to October is peak season in Son Beel (Karimganj). May to August and November to February is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. No month is a write-off here. Jun-Sep the beel is a lake of nearly 10,000 hectares between two lines of hills, fishing villages on stilts and boats for hire at about 800 rupees an hour.

How many days do you need in Son Beel (Karimganj)?

2 days is the usual stay. Fishing families and Bengali day-trippers from Karimganj, almost no outsiders; sleep in a basic lodge in Karimganj or Hailakandi town.

How do you get to Son Beel (Karimganj)?

Silchar airport 85 km; train to Karimganj (Sribhumi) or Badarpur, then about 25 km by bus or taxi to the lake ghat. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Son Beel (Karimganj) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
