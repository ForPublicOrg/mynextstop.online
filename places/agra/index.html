Agra season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Peak season: cool, clear days are ideal for the Taj, though January fog can delay sunrise views

Summer March to May Shoulder

April-June plains heat hits 45C+; walking the marble courtyards at midday is genuinely dangerous

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Humid but the rains green the gardens and thin the crowds. An underrated window if you handle the stickiness

Autumn October to November Prime time

Excellent weather returns by late October. Comfortable temperatures, build-up to peak season

Things to do in Agra

The local places worth your time in and around Agra, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Taj Mahal

    Heritage · 1 km out

    Shah Jahan's marble tomb, closed Fridays; book online and take the east gate at sunrise, the queue is shortest then

  • Agra Fort

    Fort · 3 km out

    Red sandstone Mughal fort where Shah Jahan was held; Musamman Burj frames the Taj, and the ticket drops with a same day Taj stub

  • Itimad-ud-Daulah (Baby Taj)

    Heritage · 6 km out

    Nur Jahan's inlaid marble tomb across the Yamuna, the first of its kind; quiet all day, twenty minutes inside

  • Mehtab Bagh

    Garden · 8 km out

    Mughal garden on the far bank lined up with the Taj; come for the last hour of light and keep your auto waiting

  • Akbar's Tomb (Sikandra)

    Heritage · 13 km out

    Akbar's red and white tomb in a walled deer park on the Mathura road; blackbuck and langurs at opening time

  • Kinari Bazaar

    Market · 3 km out

    Tangled lanes of cloth, bangles and shoe stalls behind the fort; walk it in the evening and carry bags in front

  • Sheroes Hangout

    Food · 2 km out

    Cafe on Fatehabad Road run by acid attack survivors; pay what you like, and the chai comes with real conversation

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Agra?

October to February is peak season in Agra. March and July to September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Excellent weather returns by late October. Comfortable temperatures, build-up to peak season.

How many days do you need in Agra?

2 days is the usual stay. Taj Ganj guesthouses cluster travelers together, though touts can be relentless.

How do you get to Agra?

Gatimaan/Shatabdi Express from Delhi 1h45m, or drive 3.5h. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Agra expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Agra?

Feb: Taj Mahotsav. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Agra?

Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, Itimad-ud-Daulah (Baby Taj), Mehtab Bagh, Akbar's Tomb (Sikandra) and Kinari Bazaar, and more. Shah Jahan's marble tomb, closed Fridays; book online and take the east gate at sunrise, the queue is shortest then.
