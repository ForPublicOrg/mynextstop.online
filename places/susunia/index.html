Susunia Hill (Bankura) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is climbing season: Kolkata clubs rope up on the north face at dawn, the stone spring runs cold and the summit walk takes about an hour

Summer March to May Shoulder

Palash and simul redden the slope through March, then April and May sit past 43 C on open rock with no shade until you reach the tree line

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jul and Aug make the granite slick and bring leeches into the sal, but the whole hill greens over and September walks are fine again

Autumn October to November Prime time

October and November give clear air across the Bankura plain, cool nights at the eco centre and the quietest weekdays of the year

Signature festival

mid-Jan: Makar Sankranti fair at the Narasimha stone spring

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Susunia Hill (Bankura)?

November to February is peak season in Susunia Hill (Bankura). March and September to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. October and November give clear air across the Bankura plain, cool nights at the eco centre and the quietest weekdays of the year.

How many days do you need in Susunia Hill (Bankura)?

2 days is the usual stay. A youth hostel and an eco centre at the base, stone carvers chipping bowls by the road, climbers in tents on winter weekends.

How do you get to Susunia Hill (Bankura)?

Bankura town is 20 km off by bus or toto, and Bankura is 4 h from Howrah on the Adra line. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Susunia Hill (Bankura) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Susunia Hill (Bankura)?

mid-Jan: Makar Sankranti fair at the Narasimha stone spring. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
