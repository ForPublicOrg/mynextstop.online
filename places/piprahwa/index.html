Piprahwa (Kapilavastu) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec to Feb brings 8C mornings and mustard fields around the mound; Thai and Sri Lankan bus groups stop for an hour and are gone

Summer March to May Shoulder

March still walks well, April climbs past 40C and May and June bake an open mound with no shade at all; the museum shuts by 5 pm

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

Jul to Sep means mud lanes, heavy air and standing water, but the Ganwaria monastery ruins sit green and completely empty

Autumn October to November Prime time

October and November are the best weeks here: dry paths, clear light on the old brick courses and paddy being cut all around

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Piprahwa (Kapilavastu)?

November to February is peak season in Piprahwa (Kapilavastu). March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to June. October and November are the best weeks here: dry paths, clear light on the old brick courses and paddy being cut all around.

How many days do you need in Piprahwa (Kapilavastu)?

2 days is the usual stay. One monastery guest house and basic rooms at Birdpur; nights are village quiet and dinner is dal and roti at a roadside dhaba.

How do you get to Piprahwa (Kapilavastu)?

Naugarh in Siddharthnagar is the railhead 20 km away with the nearest hotels; shared autos run via Birdpur to the site. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Piprahwa (Kapilavastu) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

Is there a festival in Piprahwa (Kapilavastu)?

Apr/May: Buddha Purnima prayers at the stupa. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
