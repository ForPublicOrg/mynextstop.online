Ramappa Temple (Palampet) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Nov-Feb is the season: 28 C, low light raking the bracket figures and the tank behind the temple still full.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-Jun the district runs to 44 C and the open plinth gives no shade at all before five in the evening.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jul-Sep is humid and the tank fills, which looks good, but the last stretch of road from Mulugu turns rough.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov is the sweet spot, green paddy around the temple and enough light to read the ceiling carving.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Ramappa Temple (Palampet)?

November to February is peak season in Ramappa Temple (Palampet). March and August to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Oct-Nov is the sweet spot, green paddy around the temple and enough light to read the ceiling carving.

How many days do you need in Ramappa Temple (Palampet)?

1 day is the usual stay. Almost nobody on a weekday but the ASI guard; a day trip out of Warangal, with no real beds at Palampet.

How do you get to Ramappa Temple (Palampet)?

Warangal railhead 70 km, bus to Mulugu then auto 15 km; go and come back the same day from Warangal. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Ramappa Temple (Palampet) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Ramappa Temple?

Ramappa Lake (Ramappa Cheruvu), Ghanpur Group of Temples (Kota Gullu) and Sammakka Saralamma Gadde (Medaram). Kakatiya irrigation tank a short drive behind the temple, best at sunset; no stalls or shade, carry water.
