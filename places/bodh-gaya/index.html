Bodh Gaya season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Peak pilgrim season: cool days, and occasional Dalai Lama teachings draw international crowds.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-Jun plains heat is severe, well past 40C: early-morning temple visits only, if at all.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Humid and wet, though the Mahabodhi Temple complex stays green and quieter than winter.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Weather turns pleasant by November, a good lead-in to the winter pilgrim season.

Signature festival

May: Buddha Purnima

Things to do in Bodh Gaya

The local places worth your time in and around Bodh Gaya, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Mahabodhi Temple

    Temple · in town

    The Bodhi tree and the diamond throne, chanting from before dawn in a dozen languages; phones and bags go in lockers at the gate.

  • Great Buddha Statue

    Heritage · 1 km out

    Eighty foot Buddha of 1989 beside the Daijokyo temple, disciple figures in the garden; ten minutes on foot from the Mahabodhi.

  • Sujata Stupa

    Heritage · 3 km out

    Brick mound at Bakraur where the milk rice was offered, across the Falgu; walk the footbridge and add the small Sujata museum.

  • Dungeshwari Cave Temples

    Cave · 12 km out

    Hill caves where the Buddha starved before giving up austerity, gaunt Buddha inside; hire an auto to wait, then a steep climb.

  • Thai Monastery (Wat Thai Buddhagaya)

    Monastery · 1 km out

    Gold roofed wat of 1957, the first foreign monastery built here; free to enter, the row of national monasteries runs on from it.

  • Vishnupad Temple

    Temple · 13 km out

    Gaya's Vishnu footprint shrine on the Falgu, ringed by pind daan priests; non Hindus are kept out of the inner sanctum.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Bodh Gaya?

November to February is peak season in Bodh Gaya. March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to September. Weather turns pleasant by November, a good lead-in to the winter pilgrim season.

How many days do you need in Bodh Gaya?

2 days is the usual stay. Monastery guesthouses fill with Buddhist pilgrims from Japan, Thailand, Tibet.

How do you get to Bodh Gaya?

Fly/train to Gaya, 15km; or 2hr drive from Patna. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Bodh Gaya expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Bodh Gaya?

May: Buddha Purnima. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Bodh Gaya?

Mahabodhi Temple, Great Buddha Statue, Sujata Stupa, Dungeshwari Cave Temples, Thai Monastery (Wat Thai Buddhagaya) and Vishnupad Temple. The Bodhi tree and the diamond throne, chanting from before dawn in a dozen languages; phones and bags go in lockers at the gate.
