Naranag season by season

Winter December to February Do not go

Dec-Feb the Wangath spur ices over and Gangabal lies frozen under deep snow; the trek is off and every homestay is shut.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar is still snowbound up top; Apr-May the ruins and village are fine but Gangabal stays patchy with snow above 3,500 m.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Jun-Sep is the trekking window: two days up to Gangabal under Harmukh, with guides and ponies hired in the village.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Early Oct is cold, clear and empty at the lakes; by November the shepherds are gone and the homestays have closed.

Signature festival

Aug/Sep: Harmukh-Gangabal yatra from the temple

What Naranag is actually like

A trek-start village - Kashmiri guides, a few homestays and a campsite, plus Pandit pilgrims in yatra week.

Getting to Naranag

Srinagar 50 km via Ganderbal and Kangan, 2 h by bus plus a shared taxi up the Wangath side road.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Naranag?

June to September is peak season in Naranag. April to May and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid December to March. Jun-Sep is the trekking window: two days up to Gangabal under Harmukh, with guides and ponies hired in the village.

How many days do you need in Naranag?

3 days is the usual stay. A trek-start village - Kashmiri guides, a few homestays and a campsite, plus Pandit pilgrims in yatra week.

How do you get to Naranag?

Srinagar 50 km via Ganderbal and Kangan, 2 h by bus plus a shared taxi up the Wangath side road. Practical ways in: flight and road.

Is Naranag expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Naranag?

Aug/Sep: Harmukh-Gangabal yatra from the temple. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
