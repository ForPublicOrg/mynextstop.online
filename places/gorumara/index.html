Gorumara National Park (Lataguri) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Peak: gates open, 8-25 C, dawn jeep safaris and the Jatraprasad and Chandrachur watchtowers over rhino in the grass.

Summer March to May Prime time

April-May is 35 C and sticky, but the grass is short and animals crowd the waterholes so sightings are actually better.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Closed 16 June to 15 September for breeding and about 3000 mm of Dooars rain; the gates simply shut, so do not plan it.

Autumn October to November Prime time

It reopens mid-September into tall wet grass and poor visibility; from late October the grass drops and viewing lifts.

What Gorumara National Park (Lataguri) is actually like

Resorts and homestays along Lataguri bazaar; safaris book at the forest counter and jeeps are shared with strangers.

Getting to Gorumara National Park (Lataguri)

New Mal Junction is 30 min from Lataguri and NJP about 1.5 h; Bagdogra airport is roughly 2 h away.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Gorumara National Park (Lataguri)?

November to March is peak season in Gorumara National Park (Lataguri). April to May and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. It reopens mid-September into tall wet grass and poor visibility; from late October the grass drops and viewing lifts.

How many days do you need in Gorumara National Park (Lataguri)?

2 days is the usual stay. Resorts and homestays along Lataguri bazaar; safaris book at the forest counter and jeeps are shared with strangers.

How do you get to Gorumara National Park (Lataguri)?

New Mal Junction is 30 min from Lataguri and NJP about 1.5 h; Bagdogra airport is roughly 2 h away. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Gorumara National Park (Lataguri) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.
