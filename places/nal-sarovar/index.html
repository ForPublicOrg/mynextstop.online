Nal Sarovar season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Nov-Feb brings flamingos, pelicans and cranes; get there for first light and let a boatman punt you out.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar-May shrinks the lake to cracked mud in 43 °C heat, and by April there is little water and fewer birds.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Jun-Sep refills the marsh but every migrant has gone north, the heat stays heavy and the boatmen have nothing to show.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is the arrivals month and numbers build weekly, but the real spectacle is a November-January thing.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Nal Sarovar?

November to February is peak season in Nal Sarovar. March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to September. October is the arrivals month and numbers build weekly, but the real spectacle is a November-January thing.

How many days do you need in Nal Sarovar?

1 day is the usual stay. Dawn birders and Ahmedabad families; boatmen bargain hard, no stay at the lake so sleep back in the city.

How do you get to Nal Sarovar?

64 km west of Ahmedabad via Sanand, ST buses to Vekaria or a half-day taxi; gates open at 6 am. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Nal Sarovar expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.
