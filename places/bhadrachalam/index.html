Bhadrachalam season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is easy: 30 C, short darshan queues and the Godavari wide and calm below the temple steps at dusk.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar warms fast and Apr-Jun runs past 45 C in one of the hottest towns in India, with no shade in the queue.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Jul-Sep the Godavari floods hard here, the ghats go under and the town runs a standing evacuation routine.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Oct-Nov the river drops, Parnasala 35 km upstream becomes an easy add-on and pilgrim numbers stay low.

Signature festival

Mar/Apr: Sri Rama Navami Kalyanam

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Bhadrachalam?

December to February is peak season in Bhadrachalam. March and November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to September. Dec-Feb is easy: 30 C, short darshan queues and the Godavari wide and calm below the temple steps at dusk.

How many days do you need in Bhadrachalam?

2 days is the usual stay. Pilgrim town of queue barricades and cheap thali joints; devasthanam rooms and lodges, safe to arrive alone.

How do you get to Bhadrachalam?

Bhadrachalam Road (Kothagudem) railhead 40 km; direct RTC buses from Hyderabad 300 km and Rajahmundry. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Bhadrachalam expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Bhadrachalam?

Mar/Apr: Sri Rama Navami Kalyanam. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
