Trashigang season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Dec to Feb is mild at about 1,000 m with clear skies; the Tshechu can fall in Dec, with Brokpa herders in full dress.

Summer March to May Prime time

Mar and Apr are pleasant and the Gom Kora spring festival draws pilgrims; by May the valley turns hot and humid.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun to Aug bring heavy rain and frequent slides on the Mongar and Samdrup Jongkhar roads; Sep is more settled.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct and Nov are the best: dry roads, golden rice at Radhi and clear views from the dzong over the Drangme Chhu.

Signature festival

Trashigang Tshechu (Nov or Dec)

What Trashigang is actually like

Small family hotels and a busy square where Brokpa herders, students from Kanglung and traders mix over momos.

Things to do in Trashigang

The local places worth your time in and around Trashigang, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Gom Kora

    Temple · 10 km out

    Temple by a black rock where Guru Rinpoche is said to have meditated; its spring festival draws pilgrims from far around.

  • Rangjung Woesel Chholing Monastery

    Monastery · 12 km out

    Large Nyingma monastery founded in 1990 above Rangjung, with a lively community of young monks.

  • Radhi

    Village · 15 km out

    The rice bowl of the east, where women weave bura raw silk; terraced paddies turn gold before the harvest.

  • Drametse Lhakhang

    Monastery · 12 km out

    Monastery founded in 1511 by Ani Choeten Zangmo, granddaughter of Pema Lingpa; home of the Drametse Ngacham drum dance on UNESCO's list.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Trashigang?

March to April and October to November is peak season in Trashigang. May, September and December to February is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to August. Mar and Apr are pleasant and the Gom Kora spring festival draws pilgrims; by May the valley turns hot and humid.

How many days do you need in Trashigang?

2 days is the usual stay. Small family hotels and a busy square where Brokpa herders, students from Kanglung and traders mix over momos.

How do you get to Trashigang?

180 km from Samdrup Jongkhar border, 6 to 8 h; Guwahati airport to the border about 3 h. Route permit needed. Practical ways in: road.

Is Trashigang expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Trashigang?

Trashigang Tshechu (Nov or Dec). Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Trashigang?

Gom Kora, Rangjung Woesel Chholing Monastery, Radhi and Drametse Lhakhang. Temple by a black rock where Guru Rinpoche is said to have meditated; its spring festival draws pilgrims from far around.
