Dera Baba Nanak (Kartarpur Corridor) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec to Feb runs 5 to 20 C over mustard fields and the Ravi; the one catch is late December fog that holds up the morning buses.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March is still mild and Baisakhi crowds the gurdwara in April; by May the plains sit near 44 C and the corridor walk has no shade.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

June is furnace heat, then July to September turn humid at 35 C and Ravi floods have shut the corridor for days at a stretch.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October clears and cools, and November is the peak: Guru Nanak's gurpurab packs the sarais and corridor slots book out weeks ahead.

Signature festival

Nov: Guru Nanak gurpurab fills the town and the corridor

What Dera Baba Nanak (Kartarpur Corridor) is actually like

Gurdwara sarai beds and free langar, a bazaar of tea stalls; evenings are kirtan and border floodlights along the Ravi.

Getting to Dera Baba Nanak (Kartarpur Corridor)

Amritsar airport and railhead 60 km, buses via Batala 2h, Gurdaspur 35 km. Corridor permits are online, and can pause.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Dera Baba Nanak (Kartarpur Corridor)?

November to February is peak season in Dera Baba Nanak (Kartarpur Corridor). March to April and September to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to June. October clears and cools, and November is the peak: Guru Nanak's gurpurab packs the sarais and corridor slots book out weeks ahead.

How do you get to Dera Baba Nanak (Kartarpur Corridor)?

Is Dera Baba Nanak (Kartarpur Corridor) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Dera Baba Nanak (Kartarpur Corridor)?

Nov: Guru Nanak gurpurab fills the town and the corridor. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
