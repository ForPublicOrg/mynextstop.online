Things to do in Kurukshetra

The local places worth your time in and around Kurukshetra, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Brahma Sarovar

    Ghat · in town

    Vast stone bathing tank at the centre of town, lit after dark; calm most days, enormous at a solar eclipse.

  • Jyotisar

    Shrine · 8 km out

    The marked spot where Krishna spoke the Gita, a banyan and a small temple; a ticketed light and sound show runs at night.

  • Sheikh Chilli's Tomb

    Heritage · 3 km out

    Mughal tomb and madrasa on the Harsh ka Tila mound at Thanesar; ASI ticket, best light early, half an hour.

  • Sri Krishna Museum

    Museum · 1 km out

    Several floors of Mahabharata sculpture, bronzes and folk art; buy the combined ticket with the Panorama next door.

  • Bhadrakali Temple

    Temple · 2 km out

    Shakti Peeth where Krishna and Balram are said to have had their mundan; devotees leave terracotta horses, busy Tuesday.

  • Sannihit Sarovar

    Ghat · 1 km out

    The second sacred tank, shrines down one side and pind daan rituals going on; quieter than Brahma Sarovar.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Kurukshetra?

November to February is peak season in Kurukshetra. March and July to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Builds toward the big International Gita Mahotsav crowds arriving in December.

How many days do you need in Kurukshetra?

1 day is the usual stay. A pilgrim day-trip crowd around Brahma Sarovar; friendly but not a hangout.

How do you get to Kurukshetra?

3h train from Delhi, or 1.5h from Chandigarh. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Kurukshetra expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

Is there a festival in Kurukshetra?

Dec: International Gita Mahotsav. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Kurukshetra?

Brahma Sarovar, Jyotisar, Sheikh Chilli's Tomb, Sri Krishna Museum, Bhadrakali Temple and Sannihit Sarovar. Vast stone bathing tank at the centre of town, lit after dark; calm most days, enormous at a solar eclipse.
