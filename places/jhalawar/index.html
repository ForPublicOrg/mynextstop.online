Jhalawar (Gagron & Jhalrapatan) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

At 10-26 C and dry: Gagron's UNESCO-listed water fort, the 10th-century Padmanabh temple and the Bhawani Natyashala.

Summer March to May Shoulder

April-June is 43-45 C across the Hadoti plains, and the causeway out to Gagron fort has no shade at any hour of day.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

June is 43 C and dusty; then Jhalawar, Rajasthan's wettest district at about 940 mm, sees the Kali Sindh moat Gagron again.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October-November peaks with the Kartik Purnima Chandrabhaga fair at Jhalrapatan, a working cattle fair, not a staged one.

Signature festival

Nov: Chandrabhaga cattle fair at Jhalrapatan (Kartik Purnima)

What Jhalawar (Gagron & Jhalrapatan) is actually like

Small district town with one of Rajasthan's oldest government museums; simple hotels, no travellers, Gagron 3 km out.

Getting to Jhalawar (Gagron & Jhalrapatan)

Jhalawar City station runs passenger trains to Kota via Ramganj Mandi, 26 km; Kota is 85 km by road.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Jhalawar (Gagron & Jhalrapatan)?

November to February is peak season in Jhalawar (Gagron & Jhalrapatan). March and August to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. October-November peaks with the Kartik Purnima Chandrabhaga fair at Jhalrapatan, a working cattle fair, not a staged one.

How many days do you need in Jhalawar (Gagron & Jhalrapatan)?

2 days is the usual stay. Small district town with one of Rajasthan's oldest government museums; simple hotels, no travellers, Gagron 3 km out.

How do you get to Jhalawar (Gagron & Jhalrapatan)?

Jhalawar City station runs passenger trains to Kota via Ramganj Mandi, 26 km; Kota is 85 km by road. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Jhalawar (Gagron & Jhalrapatan) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Jhalawar (Gagron & Jhalrapatan)?

Nov: Chandrabhaga cattle fair at Jhalrapatan (Kartik Purnima). Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
