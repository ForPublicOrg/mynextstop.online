Nelliyampathy season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is the sweet spot at 12-25°C, with orange groves in fruit and clear views out over the Palakkad plains from Seetharkundu.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar-May stays in the twenties up here while Palakkad below hits 40°C, which makes this the region's obvious hot-weather escape.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

June-Aug is landslide season on the ten-hairpin ghat road: buses get cancelled for days and the viewpoints show only cloud.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct clears slowly with north-east showers; November is green, cold after dark and almost empty of other visitors.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Nelliyampathy?

November to February is peak season in Nelliyampathy. March to April and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to August. Oct clears slowly with north-east showers; November is green, cold after dark and almost empty of other visitors.

How many days do you need in Nelliyampathy?

2 days is the usual stay. Estate bungalows and a couple of homestays, no market and no nightlife; you come for cold mornings and nothing else.

How do you get to Nelliyampathy?

Bus from Palakkad to Nenmara, then a limited ghat bus 22 km up; Palakkad Junction 60 km, last bus down early. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Nelliyampathy expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
