Dambuk season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Harvest time: Dec and Jan hang heavy with Khasi mandarins, and the December festival fills the valley with stages and tents.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar and Apr are green and quiet with rising rivers; by May heavy pre-monsoon rain starts cutting the riverside tracks.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Avoid: the Dibang floods, rain is relentless and the area becomes hard to reach as roads and river crossings wash out.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct dries out slowly; by Nov the first oranges ripen and the gravel river banks are dry enough to explore.

Signature festival

Orange Festival of Adventure and Music (Dec)

What Dambuk is actually like

Adi farming villages, orchard owners selling fruit by the sack, and a crowd of campers only at festival time

Things to do in Dambuk

The local places worth your time in and around Dambuk, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Dibang River

    River · 1 km out

    Wide braided river along the orchards, with gravel banks and islands that shift every flood season.

  • Bomjir

    Village · 17 km out

    Village on the Roing to Dambuk road near the Dibang, an easy stop by the wide river bed on the drive in.

  • Sally Lake

    Lake · 40 km out

    Quiet lake on the edge of Roing with a walking path; an easy evening stop on the Roing side.

  • Mehao Lake

    Lake · 45 km out

    Forest lake inside Mehao Wildlife Sanctuary above Roing, reached on a steep uphill trek; go with a guide.

  • Bhismaknagar Fort

    Fort · 60 km out

    Brick ruins of an old fort linked in legend to King Bhismaka, in forest near Roing; a short archaeology stop.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Dambuk?

November to January is peak season in Dambuk. February to April and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to September. Oct dries out slowly; by Nov the first oranges ripen and the gravel river banks are dry enough to explore.

How many days do you need in Dambuk?

2 days is the usual stay. Adi farming villages, orchard owners selling fruit by the sack, and a crowd of campers only at festival time.

How do you get to Dambuk?

Roing is about 30 km away; Dibrugarh airport is about 190 km by road. Arunachal ILP needed for Indians. Practical ways in: road.

Is Dambuk expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Dambuk?

Orange Festival of Adventure and Music (Dec). Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Dambuk?

Dibang River, Bomjir, Sally Lake, Mehao Lake and Bhismaknagar Fort. Wide braided river along the orchards, with gravel banks and islands that shift every flood season.
