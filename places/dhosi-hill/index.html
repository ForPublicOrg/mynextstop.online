Dhosi Hill (Narnaul) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec to Feb is the window: 7 to 23 C, the haze low, and the Dohan riverbed and half of Mahendragarh laid out from the crater rim

Summer March to May Shoulder

March still works before dawn, April pushes past 39 C on bare rock, and by May the stone stairway is unwalkable after 8 am

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

June is the worst of it near 45 C. From July the hill greens over, the summit kunds fill and the climb works at first light on slick steps

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct and Nov give the best walking of the year, 15 to 30 C, dhak and kikar still green, and empty steps on any weekday

What Dhosi Hill (Narnaul) is actually like

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Dhosi Hill (Narnaul)?

October to February is peak season in Dhosi Hill (Narnaul). March to April and July to September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to June. Oct and Nov give the best walking of the year, 15 to 30 C, dhak and kikar still green, and empty steps on any weekday.

How many days do you need in Dhosi Hill (Narnaul)?

1 day is the usual stay. Pilgrim families and school groups on the steps by dawn; you sleep in a plain Narnaul hotel and come back up for sunrise.

How do you get to Dhosi Hill (Narnaul)?

Narnaul is 8 km off, on the Rewari-Ringas rail line; shared auto to Kultajpur village, then a stone stairway, 40 minutes up. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Dhosi Hill (Narnaul) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.
