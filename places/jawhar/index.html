Jawhar season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Dec to Feb sit near 12 C at dawn on the plateau and the Sahyadri wall is sharp from Hanuman Point, but Dabhosa is down to a thin ribbon.

Summer March to May Quiet, but open

March brings Warli Holi and the tarpa dance; by late April the plateau touches 38 C, the falls are dry and only Jai Vilas palace holds up.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

July to September is why you come: Dabhosa drops 300 ft into its gorge, cloud sits on the ridge and the rice terraces below the palace go green.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October and November keep the streams running without the leeches and mud, and the light over the Warli hamlets is the clearest of the year.

Mar: Warli Holi with tarpa drums in the hamlets

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Jawhar?

July to October is peak season in Jawhar. June and November to February is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. No month is a write-off here. July to September is why you come: Dabhosa drops 300 ft into its gorge, cloud sits on the ridge and the rice terraces below the palace go green.

How many days do you need in Jawhar?

2 days is the usual stay. MTDC cottages and a few Warli homestays; quiet evenings, painters selling work off a courtyard wall.

How do you get to Jawhar?

Kasara or Dahanu Road railhead, then a 2 h bus; 3.5 h drive from Mumbai via Vikramgad. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Jawhar expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Jawhar?

Mar: Warli Holi with tarpa drums in the hamlets. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
