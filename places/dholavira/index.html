Dholavira season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Nov-Feb at 10-28 °C is the only comfortable window to walk the citadel, the reservoirs and the white Rann edge.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-Jun on Khadir Bet passes 45 °C with zero shade over the excavation and the salt road shimmering all day.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Jul-Sep floods the Rann around the island, the approach roads get unreliable and it is a long wet drive for nothing.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is still hot by midday, but the light is good and you get a 5,000-year-old city entirely to yourself.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Dholavira?

November to February is peak season in Dholavira. March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to September. October is still hot by midday, but the light is good and you get a 5,000-year-old city entirely to yourself.

How many days do you need in Dholavira?

2 days is the usual stay. A UNESCO site with a museum, a few resorts and homestays, no bus culture and no other backpackers.

How do you get to Dholavira?

Road only: 220 km from Bhuj via Rapar, 4-5 h by car, and most of a day on two ST buses. Practical ways in: road.

Is Dholavira expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
