Ananthagiri Hills (Vikarabad) season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Dec-Feb mornings are misty and about 14 C, and the tracks around the Anantha Padmanabha cave temple walk well.

Summer March to May Mixed, read the detail

Apr-Jun the ridge is barely cooler than Hyderabad at 42 C and the forest is leafless, so the trip loses its point.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Jul-Sep is when this place earns the drive, the Musi headwaters running and the whole ridge properly dense.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov is the best combination, green left from the rains, clear air and no weekend queue on the ghat road.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Ananthagiri Hills (Vikarabad)?

August to November is peak season in Ananthagiri Hills (Vikarabad). July and December to February is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Jul-Sep is when this place earns the drive, the Musi headwaters running and the whole ridge properly dense.

How many days do you need in Ananthagiri Hills (Vikarabad)?

1 day is the usual stay. Hyderabad weekenders and Sunday temple crowds; a state-tourism resort here and cheaper lodges in Vikarabad.

How do you get to Ananthagiri Hills (Vikarabad)?

Vikarabad railhead 10 km, passenger trains from Hyderabad 90 km, then auto to the temple and the resort. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Ananthagiri Hills (Vikarabad) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.
