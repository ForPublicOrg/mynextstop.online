Kalaburagi (Gulbarga) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Nov-Feb is the only easy time: 28 °C, dry, and the fort's fully covered Jama Masjid cool enough to sit in.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-Jun is one of the hottest corners of Karnataka at 43 °C; the fort is bare rock with no shade anywhere.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jul-Sep gets patchy Deccan rain and stays near 32 °C, muggy but workable, with the dargah busy all year.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is still hot at 34 °C; wait for November, when the Haft Gumbaz tombs sit well in the low light.

What Kalaburagi (Gulbarga) is actually like

A working Deccan city with no traveller scene; business hotels near the station and a permanent dargah crowd

Things to do in Kalaburagi

The local places worth your time in and around Kalaburagi, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Khwaja Bande Nawaz Dargah

    Shrine · 1 km out

    Sufi shrine that keeps the old city busy day and night; head covered and shoes off at the gate, the annual urs fills the lanes.

  • Jama Masjid, Gulbarga Fort

    Mosque · 2 km out

    Bahmani mosque roofed right across with domed bays and no open courtyard; inside the moated fort, visit outside prayer times.

  • Haft Gumbaz

    Heritage · 3 km out

    Seven Bahmani royal tombs in a walled compound east of the city; the caretaker opens the doors, no ticket, go before the heat.

  • Sharana Basaveshwara Temple

    Temple · in town

    The city's own Lingayat shrine with a big tank beside it; the evening queue is steady, shoes come off well before the gate.

  • Buddha Vihar

    Temple · 5 km out

    Modern white vihara with four Sanchi style gateways on the city edge; free, open till evening, ten minutes by auto from the station.

  • Ganagapur Dattatreya Temple

    Temple · 42 km out

    Dattatreya shrine on the Bhima that draws people seeking cures; pilgrims bathe at the sangama first, buses run all day from town.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Kalaburagi (Gulbarga)?

November to February is peak season in Kalaburagi (Gulbarga). March and July to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. October is still hot at 34 °C; wait for November, when the Haft Gumbaz tombs sit well in the low light.

How many days do you need in Kalaburagi (Gulbarga)?

1 day is the usual stay. A working Deccan city with no traveller scene; business hotels near the station and a permanent dargah crowd.

How do you get to Kalaburagi (Gulbarga)?

Kalaburagi airport 12 km, Star Air to Bengaluru only; railhead in town on the Mumbai-Chennai line. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Kalaburagi (Gulbarga) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

What are the best things to do in Kalaburagi?

Khwaja Bande Nawaz Dargah, Jama Masjid, Gulbarga Fort, Haft Gumbaz, Sharana Basaveshwara Temple, Buddha Vihar and Ganagapur Dattatreya Temple. Sufi shrine that keeps the old city busy day and night; head covered and shoes off at the gate, the annual urs fills the lanes.
