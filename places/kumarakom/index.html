Kumarakom season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Peak season - migratory birds fill the sanctuary, cool mornings for canoe rides through the backwaters

Summer March to May Shoulder

Hot and still by April-May; the bird sanctuary empties out as migratory species leave

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

June-July downpours are heavy and the lake turns choppy; by August it calms into a quieter green window

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is a good transition - migratory birds start arriving back as monsoon humidity fades

Things to do in Kumarakom

The local places worth your time in and around Kumarakom, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary

    Wildlife · 1 km out

    Fourteen acres of old rubber and mangrove on the lake edge; opens at 6 am, migrants November to February, carry repellent.

  • Pathiramanal Island

    Island · 12 km out

    Uninhabited islet out in Vembanad, herons and darters, reached only by boat; hire at the jetty, allow three hours return.

  • Bay Island Driftwood Museum

    Museum · 2 km out

    One woman's collection of sea worn driftwood read as figures and animals; closed Mondays, half an hour and you are done.

  • Thazhathangady Juma Masjid

    Mosque · 14 km out

    One of India's oldest mosques, all carved teak, on the Meenachil at Kottayam; men only inside, ask before you photograph.

  • Aruvikkuzhi Waterfall

    Waterfall · 3 km out

    Small cascade in a rubber estate up the hill behind the resorts, a short walk in; only worth it during and just after rain.

  • Vaikom Mahadeva Temple

    Temple · 26 km out

    Ancient Shiva temple, site of the 1924 Vaikom Satyagraha; dress code applies, Hindus only, go in the morning before 11.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Kumarakom?

November to February is peak season in Kumarakom. March and August to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to July. October is a good transition - migratory birds start arriving back as monsoon humidity fades.

How many days do you need in Kumarakom?

2 days is the usual stay. Skews toward couples and resorts - peaceful for birding, not a place to meet backpackers.

How do you get to Kumarakom?

Fly to Cochin, 1.5h taxi to Kumarakom via Kottayam. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Kumarakom expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

What are the best things to do in Kumarakom?

Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary, Pathiramanal Island, Bay Island Driftwood Museum, Thazhathangady Juma Masjid, Aruvikkuzhi Waterfall and Vaikom Mahadeva Temple. Fourteen acres of old rubber and mangrove on the lake edge; opens at 6 am, migrants November to February, carry repellent.
