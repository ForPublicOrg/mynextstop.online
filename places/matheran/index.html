Matheran season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Cool, clear air and easy red-trail walks, toy train running - the best months to visit.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Hot on the plains but Matheran's forest cover gives real relief - a popular retreat.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Toy train suspended, trails turn to slick red mud with leeches - landslide risk, best skipped.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Rain falls from 660 mm in September to 30 mm in November and the Neral train usually restarts near 1 October, but days still hit 33C.

What Matheran is actually like

Quiet car-free forest town - mostly families and honeymooners, easy chats on the toy train

Things to do in Matheran

The local places worth your time in and around Matheran, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Charlotte Lake

    Lake · 2 km out

    The town's water source with the Pisarnath Mahadev temple beside it; swimming is banned, full only after the rains

  • Panorama Point

    Viewpoint · 4 km out

    Widest view on the hill, Neral and the toy train line below; 40 minutes on foot from the bazaar, come for sunrise

  • Louisa Point

    Viewpoint · 3 km out

    Looks straight across at the ruins of Prabalgad fort; a 30 minute walk on red mud, and there is no railing at the edge

  • Porcupine Point

    Viewpoint · 4 km out

    Also called Sunset Point, where the whole town heads at 6 pm; an easy 45 minutes, ignore the horse touts

  • One Tree Hill Point

    Viewpoint · 3 km out

    Lone tree on a spur above the old Shivaji ladder route up from Chowk; quiet by afternoon, 30 minutes from the market

  • MG Road

    Market · in town

    Red earth main street of chikki, honey and leather chappal shops; hand pulled rickshaws and horses only, no cars

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Matheran?

October to February is peak season in Matheran. March to May is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. Rain falls from 660 mm in September to 30 mm in November and the Neral train usually restarts near 1 October, but days still hit 33C.

How many days do you need in Matheran?

2 days is the usual stay. Quiet car-free forest town - mostly families and honeymooners, easy chats on the toy train.

How do you get to Matheran?

Train to Neral, then toy train; 2h from Mumbai. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Matheran expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Matheran?

Charlotte Lake, Panorama Point, Louisa Point, Porcupine Point, One Tree Hill Point and MG Road. The town's water source with the Pisarnath Mahadev temple beside it; swimming is banned, full only after the rains.
