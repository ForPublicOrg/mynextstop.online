Agasthyakoodam (Athirumala) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Jan-Feb is the only real window: the Bonacaud gate opens around mid January on a capped daily permit, the ridge is dry and Athirumala camp drops to 10 C.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Early March closes the season, usually around the 10th; after that the gate shuts and the whole Agasthyavanam sanctuary is off limits to walkers.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Jun-Sep the sanctuary is closed outright, the Karamana streams run chest deep and the Bonacaud road is a landslide track. Nothing to do here.

Autumn October to November Do not go

Oct-Nov the gate stays shut through the second rains, so this is when you book the January slot online, not when you travel to it.

  • Bonacaud

    Trek · 16 km out

    Forest station where the permit only trek begins; groups leave at 8 am for the 16 km walk in to the Athirumala camp

  • Vazhvanthol Falls

    Waterfall · 20 km out

    Falls inside Peppara sanctuary; take the right fork after the Kanithadam forest check post, where the road opens at 9 am

  • Peppara Dam

    Reservoir · 30 km out

    Masonry dam on the Karamana river inside Peppara Wildlife Sanctuary, below the Agasthya hills

  • Neyyar Dam

    Reservoir · 45 km out

    Dam at the foot of the Western Ghats with boating on the reservoir and a crocodile centre first set up in 1977

  • Kottur Elephant Rehabilitation Centre

    Wildlife · 45 km out

    Forest department centre at Kappukadu near Kottoor where rescued elephants are cared for, open to visitors

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Agasthyakoodam (Athirumala)?

January to February is peak season in Agasthyakoodam (Athirumala). March is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to December. Jan-Feb is the only real window: the Bonacaud gate opens around mid January on a capped daily permit, the ridge is dry and Athirumala camp drops to 10 C.

How many days do you need in Agasthyakoodam (Athirumala)?

3 days is the usual stay. Forest tents and a dorm shed at Athirumala, no shop and no signal; you walk with a Kani guide and eat what the camp kitchen cooks.

How do you get to Agasthyakoodam (Athirumala)?

Bus from Thiruvananthapuram to Vithura and on to the Bonacaud gate, about 2h, then 14 km on foot to Athirumala. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Agasthyakoodam (Athirumala) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Agasthyakoodam?

Bonacaud, Vazhvanthol Falls, Peppara Dam, Neyyar Dam and Kottur Elephant Rehabilitation Centre. Forest station where the permit only trek begins; groups leave at 8 am for the 16 km walk in to the Athirumala camp.
