Khiching (Kichakeswari Temple) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb dawns near 11 C and the black stone warms slowly; you get the temple courtyard, the sculpture shed and the ruin mounds alone.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March still works before 9 am. April climbs past 40 C and May bakes Mayurbhanj with no shade between the mounds, so leave it alone.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

June is hot and dusty until the rains land; Jul-Sep greens the paddy, fills the old tanks and wets the carving, but buses from Karanjia crawl and the lanes turn to mud.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov is the pick: clear light on the chlorite, fields cut, cool enough to walk the whole scattered site in one morning.

Things to do in Khiching

The local places worth your time in and around Khiching, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Khiching Museum

    Museum · in town

    Second oldest museum in Odisha, set up in 1922 by Purna Chandra Bhanjdeo; idols, coins and terracotta from the site

  • Chandrasekhar Temple, Khiching

    Temple · in town

    Shiva temple about 20 m from the south west corner of the Kichakeswari temple, built by an early Bhanja chief

  • Itamundia, Khiching

    Heritage · 1 km out

    Brick mound southeast of the Kichakeswari temple that yielded a large Buddha image and an early medieval monastery

  • Ramatirtha, Jashipur

    Wildlife · 40 km out

    Mugger crocodile rearing centre near Jashipur, set up in the 1970s beside the Khairi river at the edge of Similipal

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Khiching (Kichakeswari Temple)?

November to February is peak season in Khiching (Kichakeswari Temple). March and September to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May. Oct-Nov is the pick: clear light on the chlorite, fields cut, cool enough to walk the whole scattered site in one morning.

How many days do you need in Khiching (Kichakeswari Temple)?

2 days is the usual stay. A village of tank bunds and broken shrines; you sleep in a lodge or the government rest house and eat rice plates by the bus stand.

How do you get to Khiching (Kichakeswari Temple)?

Bus from Karanjia, 25 km, or Baripada 150 km; nearest railhead Rairangpur, about 70 km of slow road. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Khiching (Kichakeswari Temple) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

What are the best things to do in Khiching?

Khiching Museum, Chandrasekhar Temple, Khiching, Itamundia, Khiching and Ramatirtha, Jashipur. Second oldest museum in Odisha, set up in 1922 by Purna Chandra Bhanjdeo; idols, coins and terracotta from the site.
