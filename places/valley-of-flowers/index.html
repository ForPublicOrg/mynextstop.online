Valley of Flowers season by season

Winter December to February Do not go

Completely closed: the park and trek route are buried under snow from October to May

Summer March to May Do not go

Still closed; snowmelt is only beginning and the trail isn't passable until June at earliest

Monsoon June to September Prime time

The only real window: park opens in June but flowers aren't out yet; peak bloom is Jul-Aug, fading by mid-Sep

Autumn October to November Do not go

Closes by early October as snow returns. At most the first days are viable, don't risk it

Things to do in Valley of Flowers

The local places worth your time in and around Valley of Flowers, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Hemkund Sahib

    Gurudwara · 7 km out

    Sikh shrine at 4300 m by a glacial lake, 6 km of steep steps above Ghangaria; start by 6 am, it shuts in early October.

  • Pulna

    Village · 12 km out

    Shared jeeps from Govindghat stop here and the walk up begins; last mule hire and last cheap tea before the climb.

  • Bhyundar Village

    Village · 9 km out

    Last hamlet on the trail with tea and maggi in season only; it is the last shade before the steep pull to Ghangaria.

  • Govindghat Gurudwara

    Gurudwara · 16 km out

    Roadhead gurudwara on the Alaknanda with free langar and dorm beds; leave surplus luggage here before walking up.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Valley of Flowers?

July to August is peak season in Valley of Flowers. September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid October to June. The only real window: park opens in June but flowers aren't out yet; peak bloom is Jul-Aug, fading by mid-Sep.

How many days do you need in Valley of Flowers?

4 days is the usual stay. Trekkers and Hemkund Sahib pilgrims share the trail. Good camaraderie in Ghangaria.

How do you get to Valley of Flowers?

Drive Rishikesh to Govindghat 10h, trek 14km to Ghangaria (permit). Practical ways in: road.

Is Valley of Flowers expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Valley of Flowers?

Hemkund Sahib, Pulna, Bhyundar Village and Govindghat Gurudwara. Sikh shrine at 4300 m by a glacial lake, 6 km of steep steps above Ghangaria; start by 6 am, it shuts in early October.
