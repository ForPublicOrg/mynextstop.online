Eaglenest Wildlife Sanctuary (Lama Camp & Bompu) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb are cold at Lama Camp, frost most mornings and snow now and then on Eaglenest Pass, but skies are clear and altitude migrants come down to Bompu at 1,940 m

Summer March to May Prime time

Mar-May is breeding season: the Bugun liocichla sings below Lama Camp, tragopans call at dawn, and by May the pre-monsoon showers and leeches begin at Bompu

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Jun-Sep the sanctuary is closed to visitors: the jeep track slips, the camps pack up and leeches own the forest. Come back from October

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov the camps reopen as the rain stops; November gives the clearest light down the Kameng gorge and the first wintering birds, with the camps still half empty

What Eaglenest Wildlife Sanctuary (Lama Camp & Bompu) is actually like

Serious birders in the Bugun community's tented camps, hot dal by lamplight, 5 am wake-ups and no phone signal above Ramalingam

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Eaglenest Wildlife Sanctuary (Lama Camp & Bompu)?

March to April and November to December is peak season in Eaglenest Wildlife Sanctuary (Lama Camp & Bompu). January to February, May and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. Mar-May is breeding season: the Bugun liocichla sings below Lama Camp, tragopans call at dawn, and by May the pre-monsoon showers and leeches begin at Bompu.

How many days do you need in Eaglenest Wildlife Sanctuary (Lama Camp & Bompu)?

3 days is the usual stay. Serious birders in the Bugun community's tented camps, hot dal by lamplight, 5 am wake-ups and no phone signal above Ramalingam.

How do you get to Eaglenest Wildlife Sanctuary (Lama Camp & Bompu)?

Tezpur to Singchung by shared Sumo, 5 h, then a jeep 1 h up to Lama Camp, booked via the Bugun Welfare Society. ILP needed. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Eaglenest Wildlife Sanctuary (Lama Camp & Bompu) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
