Netarhat season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Cold, clear mornings: the best conditions for the sunrise/sunset points it's known for.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Warmer but still cooler than the Jharkhand plains; hazier skies dull the famous viewpoints.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Thick fog and cloud block the sunrise/sunset views entirely, and forest roads get slick.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Skies clear up nicely by November, matching the winter peak in quality.

What Netarhat is actually like

State guesthouses and little else after dark - this is solitude, not a scene.

Things to do in Netarhat

The local places worth your time in and around Netarhat, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Magnolia Point

    Viewpoint · 9 km out

    Cliff edge sunset point named for an English girl in the local legend; be there by 4.30 pm, the road back is unlit

  • Netarhat Sunrise Point

    Viewpoint · 2 km out

    Ridge above the plateau where the sal country turns pink at dawn; walk out by 5 am, mornings stay cold even in April

  • Lodh Falls

    Waterfall · 60 km out

    Jharkhand's highest fall, 143 m of the Burha river dropping in stages; 2 hr each way on rough road, do not leave it late

  • Upper Ghaghri Falls

    Waterfall · 4 km out

    Small stepped fall in sal forest below the plateau rim, ten minutes down a stone path; best just after the monsoon

  • Lower Ghaghri Falls

    Waterfall · 10 km out

    Bigger drop on the same stream, reached by a forest track off the Ranchi road; carry water, there are no shops

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Netarhat?

November to January is peak season in Netarhat. February to March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to September. Skies clear up nicely by November, matching the winter peak in quality.

How many days do you need in Netarhat?

3 days is the usual stay. State guesthouses and little else after dark - this is solitude, not a scene.

How do you get to Netarhat?

4hr drive from Ranchi; own vehicle recommended, buses are sparse. Practical ways in: road.

Is Netarhat expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

What are the best things to do in Netarhat?

Magnolia Point, Netarhat Sunrise Point, Lodh Falls, Upper Ghaghri Falls and Lower Ghaghri Falls. Cliff edge sunset point named for an English girl in the local legend; be there by 4.30 pm, the road back is unlit.
