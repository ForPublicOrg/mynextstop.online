Hoollongapar Gibbon Sanctuary (Jorhat) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Nov-Mar mornings are the ones to book: hoolock gibbons call at first light and the trails are dry and leech-free.

Summer March to May Prime time

April-May is humid and full of insects, but the gibbons still call by 6 am, so go early and be out by mid-morning.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Jun-Sep the forest floor is leech soup and the tracks flood; a guide will still take you and you will regret it.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is green and empty, and it pairs well with Majuli 35 km north once the Brahmaputra ferries settle down.

When is the best time to visit Hoollongapar Gibbon Sanctuary (Jorhat)?

November to March is peak season in Hoollongapar Gibbon Sanctuary (Jorhat). April to May and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. October is green and empty, and it pairs well with Majuli 35 km north once the Brahmaputra ferries settle down.

How many days do you need in Hoollongapar Gibbon Sanctuary (Jorhat)?
