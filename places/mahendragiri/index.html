Mahendragiri (Gajapati) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is the best window: clear views over the Eastern Ghats, cool climbs to the Kunti, Yudhishthira and Bhima temples, dry trails.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar-Apr the summit stays cooler than the plains, though haze dulls the views; Shivaratri can fall in March and fills the path with pilgrims.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

Jun-Aug bring heavy rain, leeches and slippery, rutted tracks to the top, so skip it; late September can clear but stays wet and misty.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is lush but can bring Bay of Bengal storms; November is the sweet spot, rinsed air, green forest and steady weather.

Signature festival

Maha Shivaratri (Feb or Mar)

What Mahendragiri (Gajapati) is actually like

Pilgrims and a few trekkers; basic stays in Paralakhemundi and simple food at the base, plus jeeps that ferry people up from Burakhat.

Things to do in Mahendragiri

The local places worth your time in and around Mahendragiri, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Burakhat

    Village · 1 km out

    The pass at the foot of the peak where cars stop; walk up or hire one of the few jeeps that run to the top.

  • Bhima Temple

    Temple · 1 km out

    Rough stone shrine near the summit, said to be built by the Pandavas, with some of the widest views on the hill.

  • Yudhishthira Temple

    Temple · 1 km out

    Triratha style stone temple on the summit ridge; the hill also holds an 11th century Rajendra Chola inscription.

  • Kunti Temple

    Temple · 1 km out

    Small shrine to Kunti, mother of the Pandavas, among the forest on the top, part of the Shivaratri circuit.

  • Gandahati Waterfall

    Waterfall · 14 km out

    Stepped forest waterfall south-west of the hill, a popular picnic spot with locals after the rains.

  • Gajapati Palace

    Palace · 36 km out

    Palace of the former Paralakhemundi rulers in the town centre; admire the facade from outside.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Mahendragiri (Gajapati)?

November to February is peak season in Mahendragiri (Gajapati). March to April and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to August. October is lush but can bring Bay of Bengal storms; November is the sweet spot, rinsed air, green forest and steady weather.

How many days do you need in Mahendragiri (Gajapati)?

2 days is the usual stay. Pilgrims and a few trekkers; basic stays in Paralakhemundi and simple food at the base, plus jeeps that ferry people up from Burakhat.

How do you get to Mahendragiri (Gajapati)?

Paralakhemundi, 115 km from Brahmapur railhead, then about 2 h of hill road to Burakhat at the foot of the peak. Practical ways in: road.

Is Mahendragiri (Gajapati) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

Is there a festival in Mahendragiri (Gajapati)?

Maha Shivaratri (Feb or Mar). Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Mahendragiri?

Burakhat, Bhima Temple, Yudhishthira Temple, Kunti Temple, Gandahati Waterfall and Gajapati Palace. The pass at the foot of the peak where cars stop; walk up or hire one of the few jeeps that run to the top.
