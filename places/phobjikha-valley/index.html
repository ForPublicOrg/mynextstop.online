Phobjikha Valley (Gangtey) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec and Jan are peak crane months, with hundreds of black-necked cranes on the marsh, frosty mornings and snow some nights.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Most cranes have flown back to Tibet by Mar; Apr and May bring rhododendron bloom, green meadows and easy trail walking.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun to Aug are wet and misty, with muddy trails and leeches in the forest; Sep dries out as the potato harvest begins.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Cranes arrive from late Oct and Gangtey Goemba celebrates them in Nov with the crane festival; days are clear and cold.

Signature festival

Black-Necked Crane Festival (Nov)

What Phobjikha Valley (Gangtey) is actually like

Farmhouse homestays and wood stoves, potato fields, monks from Gangtey Goemba, and birders with scopes at dawn.

Things to do in Phobjikha Valley

The local places worth your time in and around Phobjikha Valley, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Gangtey Goemba

    Monastery · in town

    Nyingma monastery founded in 1613 on a knoll over the valley, seat of the Gangteng Tulku, restored in local timber.

  • Black-Necked Crane Information Centre

    Wildlife · 2 km out

    RSPN centre with spotting scopes over the marsh where the cranes roost; check winter opening hours locally.

  • Gangtey Nature Trail

    Walk · in town

    Gentle 4 km trail from Gangtey down through pine forest and meadows to Khewang Lhakhang, about 1.5 h one way.

  • Khewang Lhakhang

    Temple · 3 km out

    Old village temple at the far end of the nature trail, on the valley floor below the eastern slopes.

  • Lawa La

    Viewpoint · 5 km out

    Prayer-flag pass on the road in from the highway, the first wide look down over the Phobjikha bowl.

  • Chendebji Chorten

    Shrine · 18 km out

    Large white 18th-century chorten in the style of Swayambhunath, beside the highway towards Trongsa.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Phobjikha Valley (Gangtey)?

October to February is peak season in Phobjikha Valley (Gangtey). March to May and September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to August. Cranes arrive from late Oct and Gangtey Goemba celebrates them in Nov with the crane festival; days are clear and cold.

How many days do you need in Phobjikha Valley (Gangtey)?

2 days is the usual stay. Farmhouse homestays and wood stoves, potato fields, monks from Gangtey Goemba, and birders with scopes at dawn.

How do you get to Phobjikha Valley (Gangtey)?

65 km from Wangdue, about 2.5 h, over Lawa La; Thimphu is about 130 km, 4 to 5 h. Route permit needed. Practical ways in: road.

Is Phobjikha Valley (Gangtey) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Phobjikha Valley (Gangtey)?

Black-Necked Crane Festival (Nov). Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Phobjikha Valley?

Gangtey Goemba, Black-Necked Crane Information Centre, Gangtey Nature Trail, Khewang Lhakhang, Lawa La and Chendebji Chorten. Nyingma monastery founded in 1613 on a knoll over the valley, seat of the Gangteng Tulku, restored in local timber.
