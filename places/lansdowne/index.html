Lansdowne season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Cold, foggy mornings, occasional light snow: quiet and atmospheric, some cafes shut

Summer March to May Prime time

Cool pine-forest air, a favorite quick escape from Delhi's heat without Mussoorie's crowds

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

June still works; Jul-Sep brings 200-425mm a month and Garhwal's landslide season cuts the Kotdwar ghat road - don't come.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Clear skies and comfortable temperatures. The best weather window of the year

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Lansdowne?

April to May and October to November is peak season in Lansdowne. June and December to March is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid July to September. Cool pine-forest air, a favorite quick escape from Delhi's heat without Mussoorie's crowds.

How many days do you need in Lansdowne?

2 days is the usual stay. Empty pine roads and military-town hush. Solitude more than social mixing.

How do you get to Lansdowne?

Train to Kotdwar 40km, then 1.5h taxi or shared jeep up. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Lansdowne expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
