Pondicherry (incl. Auroville) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Peak season - cool 20-29C, dry air, best for cycling the French Quarter and long beach walks

Summer March to May Do not go

Hot and humid (35C+), thin crowds, ashram and cafes still run but midday is rough going

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

SW monsoon barely touches this coast - warm, muggy, occasional showers, decent value season

Autumn October to November Do not go

NE monsoon lands hard - Nov is the wettest month, rough seas, sudden downpours, best skipped

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Pondicherry (incl. Auroville)?

December to February is peak season in Pondicherry (incl. Auroville). June to September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid March to May and October to November. Peak season - cool 20-29C, dry air, best for cycling the French Quarter and long beach walks.

How many days do you need in Pondicherry (incl. Auroville)?

3 days is the usual stay. French Quarter cafes and Auroville draw a backpacker-yogi crowd, easy to meet people.

How do you get to Pondicherry (incl. Auroville)?

Fly/train to Chennai, 3hr drive; or train to Villupuram + bus. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Pondicherry (incl. Auroville) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Made for solo travellers.

Is there a festival in Pondicherry (incl. Auroville)?

Aug 15: Sri Aurobindo's birthday celebrations at the Ashram. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
