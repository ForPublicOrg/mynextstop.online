Trishna Wildlife Sanctuary (Belonia) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb the grass is burnt short and gaur herds come out to the waterholes at first light; a Forest Rest House night and a 6 am walk with a guard is the whole point

Summer March to May Prime time

Mar-Apr give the best sightings as water shrinks to a few pools, but it is 33 C by noon and storms roll in from late April; May is hot, humid and empty

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Jun-Sep the tracks flood and the grass stands head-high, so nothing shows itself; the leeches are out and the 6 am walk is pointless, come back after October

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Oct is soggy with tall grass still standing and few sightings; by November the tracks dry, birding picks up and the sanctuary is at its greenest

What Trishna Wildlife Sanctuary (Belonia) is actually like

Forest guards, the odd birder, Sunday picnickers; the Forest Rest House booked at Belonia's forest office is the bed, nights pitch dark

Getting to Trishna Wildlife Sanctuary (Belonia)

Bus or train from Agartala to Belonia (3 h), then an auto 20 km to the gate; get the permit at the Belonia forest office

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Trishna Wildlife Sanctuary (Belonia)?

December to March is peak season in Trishna Wildlife Sanctuary (Belonia). April and November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. Dec-Feb the grass is burnt short and gaur herds come out to the waterholes at first light; a Forest Rest House night and a 6 am walk with a guard is the whole point.

How many days do you need in Trishna Wildlife Sanctuary (Belonia)?

2 days is the usual stay. Forest guards, the odd birder, Sunday picnickers; the Forest Rest House booked at Belonia's forest office is the bed, nights pitch dark.

How do you get to Trishna Wildlife Sanctuary (Belonia)?

Bus or train from Agartala to Belonia (3 h), then an auto 20 km to the gate; get the permit at the Belonia forest office. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Trishna Wildlife Sanctuary (Belonia) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
