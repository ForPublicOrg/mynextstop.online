Bandipur National Park season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is the comfortable window: 28 °C, good light, and herds of gaur and elephant out in the open teak blocks.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar-May is hot at 36 °C but gives the best odds on tiger and leopard as animals crowd the last water holes.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun-Sep the park stays open but heavy south-west rain and thick undergrowth cut sightings to almost nothing.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October greens up and the safari calendar restarts properly; November is excellent, cool and uncrowded.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Bandipur National Park?

November to February is peak season in Bandipur National Park. March to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. No month is a write-off here. October greens up and the safari calendar restarts properly; November is excellent, cool and uncrowded.

How many days do you need in Bandipur National Park?

2 days is the usual stay. Forest department jeep seats are cheap and shared; homestays at Melukamanahalli, no traveller scene beyond them.

How do you get to Bandipur National Park?

Mysuru railhead 80 km, then a 2 h bus on NH-766; the highway is shut to traffic 9 pm to 6 am. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Bandipur National Park expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.
