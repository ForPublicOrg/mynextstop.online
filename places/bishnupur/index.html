Bishnupur (Terracotta Temples) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is the season: 12-25 C, low clear light on the terracotta panels, and Bishnupur Mela fills late December.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March is still walkable at 33 C; by April-May the laterite radiates 40 C heat and the temple town empties out.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun-Aug is the write-off: 38 C before the break, then 1000 mm of rain on open laterite courtyards; September dries out.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October stays humid until the rains pull out around the 10th, then November turns crisp and rivals winter.

Signature festival

Dec: Bishnupur Mela (23-27 Dec)

What Bishnupur (Terracotta Temples) is actually like

Weekend Bengali families and craft buyers; you walk or rickshaw between temples and Baluchari weavers let you watch.

Things to do in Bishnupur

The local places worth your time in and around Bishnupur, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Rasmancha

    Heritage · in town

    Laterite pyramid of 1600 where every Krishna idol in town gathered at Ras; one ASI ticket covers it and the temples.

  • Shyam Rai Temple (Pancha Ratna)

    Temple · 1 km out

    Five pinnacle temple of 1643 carrying the finest terracotta panels here; the south face gets the morning light.

  • Jorbangla Temple (Keshta Rai)

    Temple · 1 km out

    Twin hut roof temple of 1655 with Ramayana battles cut into brick; five minutes on foot from Rasmancha.

  • Madan Mohan Temple

    Temple · 1 km out

    Single tower temple of 1694 still in daily worship and free to enter; the front wall carving is the deepest in town.

  • Dalmadal Cannon

    Heritage · 1 km out

    The Malla kings' huge iron cannon sitting in the open near the fort gate; two minutes to see, worth the walk over.

  • Panchmura

    Village · 22 km out

    Potters' village that makes the Bankura terracotta horse; kilns are open to visitors, the bus takes about an hour.

Getting to Bishnupur (Terracotta Temples)

Bishnupur has its own railhead, 4 h from Howrah; the temple circuit is walkable or a half-day by rickshaw.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Bishnupur (Terracotta Temples)?

November to February is peak season in Bishnupur (Terracotta Temples). March and September to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to August. October stays humid until the rains pull out around the 10th, then November turns crisp and rivals winter.

How many days do you need in Bishnupur (Terracotta Temples)?

2 days is the usual stay. Weekend Bengali families and craft buyers; you walk or rickshaw between temples and Baluchari weavers let you watch.

How do you get to Bishnupur (Terracotta Temples)?

Bishnupur has its own railhead, 4 h from Howrah; the temple circuit is walkable or a half-day by rickshaw. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Bishnupur (Terracotta Temples) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Bishnupur (Terracotta Temples)?

Dec: Bishnupur Mela (23-27 Dec). Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Bishnupur?

Rasmancha, Shyam Rai Temple (Pancha Ratna), Jorbangla Temple (Keshta Rai), Madan Mohan Temple, Dalmadal Cannon and Panchmura. Laterite pyramid of 1600 where every Krishna idol in town gathered at Ras; one ASI ticket covers it and the temples.
