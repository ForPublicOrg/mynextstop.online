Nilambur season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is dry at 20-33°C and made for it: Conolly's Plot of 1846, the teak museum and the Nedumkayam forest road at first light.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar-May hits 37°C down in the Nilambur valley, so it is shaded plantations and the Chaliyar river only, and you are out by ten.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

June-Aug is one of Kerala's wettest pockets; the Nedumkayam road washes out and the tribal hamlets beyond it get cut off.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct still rains hard some evenings, but by November the Chaliyar runs clear and the teak forest is at its absolute best.

Things to do in Nilambur

The local places worth your time in and around Nilambur, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Conolly's Plot

    Park · 3 km out

    The world's oldest teak plantation, sown in 1846 on the Chaliyar bank; small ticket, half an hour under 40 m trees.

  • Nilambur Teak Museum

    Museum · 4 km out

    The only teak museum anywhere, at Chandakunnu on the Gudalur road, run by the forest institute; closed Mondays, small fee.

  • Nedumkayam Rainforest

    Wildlife · 18 km out

    Evergreen forest with a colonial timber rest house and an old elephant kraal; the forest office decides entry, jeep only.

  • Adyanpara Waterfalls

    Waterfall · 20 km out

    Falls and rock pools on the Kurumbalangode stream; ticket at the gate, staff stop entry in heavy rain, rocks stay slippery.

  • Nilambur Vettakorumakan Temple

    Temple · 1 km out

    The Kovilakam family's temple in town; the week long Nilambur Pattu in January or February is the one time it fills up.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Nilambur?

November to February is peak season in Nilambur. March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to August. Oct still rains hard some evenings, but by November the Chaliyar runs clear and the teak forest is at its absolute best.

How many days do you need in Nilambur?

2 days is the usual stay. A small teak-trade town with basic lodges and no traveller scene at all; the forest office decides what you can enter.

How do you get to Nilambur?

Nilambur Road is the end of the branch line from Shoranur, 2 h; Calicut airport 55 km, buses from Kozhikode. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Nilambur expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

What are the best things to do in Nilambur?

Conolly's Plot, Nilambur Teak Museum, Nedumkayam Rainforest, Adyanpara Waterfalls and Nilambur Vettakorumakan Temple. The world's oldest teak plantation, sown in 1846 on the Chaliyar bank; small ticket, half an hour under 40 m trees.
