Velavadar (Blackbuck National Park) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Mar is peak: hundreds of blackbuck on open grass and one of the world's largest harrier roosts at dusk.

Summer March to May Prime time

Apr-May bakes at 43 °C on shadeless grassland; safaris run at dawn only, then the gates shut for the rains in June.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Shut through the rains for blackbuck and lesser florican breeding, and the black-cotton grass turns to gluey mud.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Gates reopen mid-October onto green grass and no visitors; harrier numbers build through November into the roost.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Velavadar (Blackbuck National Park)?

December to March is peak season in Velavadar (Blackbuck National Park). October to November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to September. Dec-Mar is peak: hundreds of blackbuck on open grass and one of the world's largest harrier roosts at dusk.

How many days do you need in Velavadar (Blackbuck National Park)?

1 day is the usual stay. Almost no independent travellers; a four-room forest guesthouse or one costly lodge, and you need a jeep.

How do you get to Velavadar (Blackbuck National Park)?

Bhavnagar railhead 65 km, Vallabhipur bus stand 32 km, then hire a jeep out to the park gate. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Velavadar (Blackbuck National Park) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
