Madurai season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Best months - mornings cool enough for long temple walks, pleasant evenings by the Meenakshi tank

Summer March to May Shoulder

Brutal - 40C+ by April-May, stone temple floors scorch bare feet by midday, only bearable dawn or dusk

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Dry and still hot (Madurai sits in a rain-shadow), 34-36C with humidity, not the SW monsoon's target zone

Autumn October to November Shoulder

NE monsoon brings scattered evening showers by Nov, temperatures ease off, a decent trade-off season

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Madurai?

December to February is peak season in Madurai. March and September to November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to August. Best months - mornings cool enough for long temple walks, pleasant evenings by the Meenakshi tank.

How many days do you need in Madurai?

2 days is the usual stay. Pilgrims and heritage travellers, not a party crowd; lodges near the temple ease day trips.

How do you get to Madurai?

Fly direct to Madurai airport, or overnight train from Chennai (8hr). Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Madurai expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Madurai?

Apr-May: Chithirai Festival, Meenakshi's temple procession. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
