Rameswaram season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Best months - cool sea breeze, calm seas for the Dhanushkodi drive, comfortable temple corridors

Summer March to May Shoulder

Hot and glaring on the exposed sandy terrain, 36-38C, the temple's long stone corridors get scorching

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Dry season here (SW monsoon misses this coast), hot with a steady sea breeze, workable if heat-tolerant

Autumn October to November Do not go

NE monsoon and Bay of Bengal cyclone season - rough seas, Dhanushkodi road can flood, boats get cancelled

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Rameswaram?

December to February is peak season in Rameswaram. March and June to September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to May and October to November. Best months - cool sea breeze, calm seas for the Dhanushkodi drive, comfortable temple corridors.

How many days do you need in Rameswaram?

2 days is the usual stay. A pilgrim town at heart, but the Dhanushkodi ghost-town trip draws solo photographers.

How do you get to Rameswaram?

Train/bus from Madurai (3hr) over the Pamban Bridge, or fly to Madurai. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Rameswaram expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Rameswaram?

Feb: Maha Shivaratri celebrations at Ramanathaswamy Temple. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
