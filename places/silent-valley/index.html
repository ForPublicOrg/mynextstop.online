Silent Valley National Park (Mukkali) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is the clear window at 15-30°C, permits are easier to secure and the forest bus runs the 23 km up to Sairandhri.

Summer March to May Prime time

March and April are warm but still workable at this altitude, and lion-tailed macaques drop lower; by May it is close and hazy.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

June-Sep brings 2,800-3,400 mm of rain, leeches on every trail and repeated closures, so do not plan a Silent Valley trip then.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Oct is still dripping and trips restart slowly; November is green, cool and the best month before the December permit rush.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Silent Valley National Park (Mukkali)?

December to March is peak season in Silent Valley National Park (Mukkali). April and November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. Dec-Feb is the clear window at 15-30°C, permits are easier to secure and the forest bus runs the 23 km up to Sairandhri.

How many days do you need in Silent Valley National Park (Mukkali)?

2 days is the usual stay. Almost nobody here: a forest office, a couple of Mukkali homestays and a permit queue at eight with a few naturalists.

How do you get to Silent Valley National Park (Mukkali)?

Mukkali is 40 km from Mannarkkad by bus; entry only on a Forest Department permit, applied for 24-48 h ahead. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Silent Valley National Park (Mukkali) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
