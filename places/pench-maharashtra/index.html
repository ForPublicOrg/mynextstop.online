Pench Tiger Reserve (Maharashtra) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb mornings start near 6 C in an open gypsy, but sightings are steady and the Pench river holds water.

Summer March to May Prime time

Apr-May gives the best tiger odds of the year at drying waterholes, paid for with 44-46 C in an open jeep.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

June hits 45 C, then cores shut 1 Jul to 30 Sep under NTCA rules; only buffer gypsies run, on slurry tracks.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Gates reopen on 1 October into waist-high grass and poor visibility, which improves fast through November.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Pench Tiger Reserve (Maharashtra)?

November to March is peak season in Pench Tiger Reserve (Maharashtra). April to May and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. Gates reopen on 1 October into waist-high grass and poor visibility, which improves fast through November.

How many days do you need in Pench Tiger Reserve (Maharashtra)?

2 days is the usual stay. MTDC and eco-village huts at Sillari; gypsy seats split six ways, so a solo safari stays affordable.

How do you get to Pench Tiger Reserve (Maharashtra)?

Nagpur airport and station 80 km, about 2 h to Sillari gate; book safari permits online well ahead. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Pench Tiger Reserve (Maharashtra) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
