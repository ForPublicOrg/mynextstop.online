Chikmagalur season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Crisp air, clear summit views from Mullayanagiri, coffee harvest in full swing.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Warmer but still cooler than the plains; mornings are great before the heat builds.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Kudremukh gets drenched, trails turn to landslide-prone mud, clouds erase the views.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October-November: waterfalls roaring, forests soaked green, air crystal clear.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Chikmagalur?

October to February is peak season in Chikmagalur. March to May is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. October-November: waterfalls roaring, forests soaked green, air crystal clear.

How many days do you need in Chikmagalur?

3 days is the usual stay. Trekking base camps and estate homestays draw Bengaluru weekenders - easy to join a hike.

How do you get to Chikmagalur?

Fly Mangalore or Bengaluru, 4-5h drive; nearest train Kadur (1h). Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Chikmagalur expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.
