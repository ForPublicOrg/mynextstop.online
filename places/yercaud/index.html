Yercaud season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is the dry, clear stretch at 12-26 C, when the Shevaroys actually give you the drop onto the Salem plain at dawn.

Summer March to May Prime time

March stays dry and clear; Apr-May is 28 C up here against 40 C in Salem below, with thundershowers and the May flower show.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

June is showery but workable; Jul-Sep turns properly wet at 190-250 mm a month, with the viewpoints inside cloud most days.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

October is the wettest month of the year at 250 mm; November dries back to 160 mm and the plain comes into view again.

Signature festival

May: Yercaud Summer Festival

What Yercaud is actually like

Coffee estates, an orange-seller lake and Tamil families at weekends; midweek the loop road is more or less yours.

Getting to Yercaud

Salem Jn 30 km, then a bus up 20 hairpins in an hour; Coimbatore and Trichy airports are both about 3 h away.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Yercaud?

December to March is peak season in Yercaud. April to June and November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid July to October. Dec-Feb is the dry, clear stretch at 12-26 C, when the Shevaroys actually give you the drop onto the Salem plain at dawn.

How many days do you need in Yercaud?

2 days is the usual stay. Coffee estates, an orange-seller lake and Tamil families at weekends; midweek the loop road is more or less yours.

How do you get to Yercaud?

Salem Jn 30 km, then a bus up 20 hairpins in an hour; Coimbatore and Trichy airports are both about 3 h away. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Yercaud expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Yercaud?

May: Yercaud Summer Festival. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
