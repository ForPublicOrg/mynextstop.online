Auli season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Peak season: Uttarakhand's best skiing, snow slopes backed by views of Nanda Devi

Summer March to May Shoulder

Green meadows and cable car views without snow. Pleasant, but skiing, the main draw, is gone

Monsoon June to September Do not go

The Joshimath approach road is landslide-prone and views fog out completely. Skip it

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Clear and quiet before the snow arrives. Good for the cable car, no skiing yet though

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Auli?

December to February is peak season in Auli. March to May and October to November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. Peak season: Uttarakhand's best skiing, snow slopes backed by views of Nanda Devi.

How many days do you need in Auli?

3 days is the usual stay. Ski instructors and Delhi weekenders; here for slopes and cable car, not nightlife.

How do you get to Auli?

Fly Dehradun 280km or train Rishikesh, then 9-10h via Joshimath. Practical ways in: road.

Is Auli expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

Is there a festival in Auli?

Jan: Auli Winter Games. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
