Thanjavur season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Ideal - cool mornings for the temple complex and palace museum without the midday stone-floor heat

Summer March to May Shoulder

Delta heat and humidity climb past 38C, temple visits only comfortable at opening or closing time

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Hot and muggy rather than wet (rain-shadow territory for the SW monsoon), no real relief yet

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Nov is the wettest month here - Cauvery delta NE monsoon rain can flood low areas, wait for Dec

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Thanjavur?

December to February is peak season in Thanjavur. March and September to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to August and November. Ideal - cool mornings for the temple complex and palace museum without the midday stone-floor heat.

How many days do you need in Thanjavur?

2 days is the usual stay. A quiet heritage stop, not a hangout town - mostly day-trippers and history buffs.

How do you get to Thanjavur?

Train from Chennai (6hr) or fly to Trichy (65km) then bus/auto. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Thanjavur expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Thanjavur?

Feb: Thanjavur Music & Dance Festival at Brihadeeswarar Temple. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
