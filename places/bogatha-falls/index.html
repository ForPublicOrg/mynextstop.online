Bogatha Falls season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Dec-Jan flow gentles into safe green pools below the tiers, and the Haritha cottages beside the falls are empty midweek.

Summer March to May Mixed, read the detail

Mar-May the curtain thins badly and the drive through 42 °C scrub is long; wait for the rains to switch it back on.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Jul-Aug the falls turn into a brown thunderhead, bathing is off, and Godavari backwater can cut the Eturnagaram road.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Sep-Nov is the show: a full white curtain, jungle still wet and green, and the pool reopening as the current calms.

What Bogatha Falls is actually like

Koya tribal villages, weekend picnic vans up from Warangal; Haritha cottages at the falls, carry cash and patience.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Bogatha Falls?

September to November is peak season in Bogatha Falls. June to August and December to January is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to May. Jul-Aug the falls turn into a brown thunderhead, bathing is off, and Godavari backwater can cut the Eturnagaram road.

How many days do you need in Bogatha Falls?

2 days is the usual stay. Koya tribal villages, weekend picnic vans up from Warangal; Haritha cottages at the falls, carry cash and patience.

How do you get to Bogatha Falls?

Road only: Warangal 140 km or Bhadrachalam 120 km via Eturnagaram; buses thin past Mulugu, self-drive easiest. Practical ways in: road.

Is Bogatha Falls expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

What are the best things to do in Bogatha Falls?

Medaram Sammakka Saralamma Gadde and Eturnagaram Wildlife Sanctuary. Koya goddesses' shrine in a forest clearing, empty most of the year; millions arrive for the jatara every second year.
