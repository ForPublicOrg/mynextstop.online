Kodachadri (Kollur base) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb gives dry grass slopes, a clear Arabian Sea line from the summit and genuinely cold nights up top.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar-May the trail is dust at 34 °C down at the base, and haze usually erases the coastline view you climbed for.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Jun-Sep the Hidlumane falls route is a leech-covered scramble under 5,000 mm of rain and the summit jeeps stop.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov is the reward: grass at its greenest, streams still flowing, and Navaratri filling Kollur down below.

Signature festival

Sep/Oct: Mookambika Navaratri at Kollur

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Kodachadri (Kollur base)?

November to February is peak season in Kodachadri (Kollur base). March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. Oct-Nov is the reward: grass at its greenest, streams still flowing, and Navaratri filling Kollur down below.

How many days do you need in Kodachadri (Kollur base)?

2 days is the usual stay. Weekend trek groups out of Bengaluru; basic rooms at Bhattara Mane near the top, proper hotels in Kollur.

How do you get to Kodachadri (Kollur base)?

Kollur is the base for jeeps and beds, buses from Udupi 80 km; forest check post permit before the climb. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Kodachadri (Kollur base) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Kodachadri (Kollur base)?

Sep/Oct: Mookambika Navaratri at Kollur. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
