Beypore season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is the run: 30 C, a flat evening sea, the uru yard working all day and the 2 km rubble jetty walkable to its far end at sunset.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar-May sit muggy at 34 C with a breeze only after 5 pm; the shipwrights stop by early afternoon, so see the yard in the morning.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun-Sep the Arabian Sea comes in hard and the jetty is closed off, Chaliyar boat rides stop until late September, but the uru yard works under its sheds.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov the sea settles and the estuary clears; the fishing harbour is at full noise before dawn and the light on the water is the year's best.

Things to do in Beypore

The local places worth your time in and around Beypore, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Beypore Beach

    Beach · 1 km out

    Beach at the mouth of the Chaliyar, where the 1 km Pulimuttu breakwater runs out to sea

  • Chaliyam

    Village · 3 km out

    Village across the Chaliyar mouth where the Portuguese built a fort in 1531; Kozhikode forces razed it in 1571

  • Kadalundi Bird Sanctuary

    Wildlife · 12 km out

    Islets where the Kadalundi river meets the sea; about 60 migrant species visit in season, among them terns and gulls

  • Mishkal Mosque

    Mosque · 10 km out

    14th century mosque at Kuttichira built by the shipowner Mishkal, partly burnt by the Portuguese in 1510 and rebuilt

  • SM Street

    Market · 9 km out

    Kozhikode's Sweetmeat Street, Mittai Theruvu, named for the halwa stalls that once lined it; now its busiest bazaar

  • Kappad Beach

    Beach · 30 km out

    Beach north of Kozhikode where Vasco da Gama landed in 1498, marked by a small stone memorial

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Beypore?

November to February is peak season in Beypore. March and September to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. No month is a write-off here. Oct-Nov the sea settles and the estuary clears; the fishing harbour is at full noise before dawn and the light on the water is the year's best.

How many days do you need in Beypore?

2 days is the usual stay. A few homestays by the estuary and cheap hotels 10 km up in Kozhikode; evenings are halwa, fish fry and the whole town on the jetty.

How do you get to Beypore?

Kozhikode railhead 10 km by bus or auto, Karipur airport 30 km inland. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Beypore expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.

What are the best things to do in Beypore?

Beypore Beach, Chaliyam, Kadalundi Bird Sanctuary, Mishkal Mosque, SM Street and Kappad Beach. Beach at the mouth of the Chaliyar, where the 1 km Pulimuttu breakwater runs out to sea.
