Lava & Rishop (Neora Valley) season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Dec-Feb drops near 2 C with a real chance of snow at Rishop; rooms are unheated and the Neora tracks can be iced up.

Summer March to May Prime time

March-May is the best walking: rhododendron and orchids in the Neora forest, 10-20 C, Kanchenjunga out most mornings.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Jun-Sep is out: the park shuts on 16 June, the Gorubathan road slides and leeches own the forest floor completely.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October-November gives the clearest air of the year for Kanchenjunga from Tiffin Dara, and the park is open again.

What Lava & Rishop (Neora Valley) is actually like

Homestays in Lava and a scatter of huts at Rishop; birders after satyr tragopan, plus Bengali families in season.

Things to do in Lava & Rishop

The local places worth your time in and around Lava & Rishop, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Lava Monastery

    Monastery · in town

    Kagyu Thekchen Ling gompa on the ridge above Lava bazaar, red and gold; morning prayers around 6, shoes off at the hall

  • Neora Valley National Park

    Wildlife · 3 km out

    Cloud forest with red panda and satyr tragopan; day permit, guide and vehicle are booked at the Lava range office, not online

  • Tiffin Dara

    Viewpoint · 6 km out

    Ridge lookout half an hour on foot above Rishop, straight at Kanchenjunga; leave by 5 am, cloud closes it in most mornings

  • Changey Falls

    Waterfall · 13 km out

    Waterfall dropping into a gorge on the Kolakham road, viewed from a rail; steep steps down, slippery for weeks after rain

  • Kolakham

    Village · 12 km out

    Handful of homestays on a shelf below the Neora forest, quieter than Lava; the last 4 km of road is broken, go by jeep

  • Lolegaon

    Walk · 24 km out

    Lepcha village in a heritage forest with a canopy walkway; the hanging bridge has been shut on and off, check in Lava first

Getting to Lava & Rishop (Neora Valley)

New Mal Junction is 2.5 h by hired car, NJP about 3.5 h; Neora day permits come from the Lava range office.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Lava & Rishop (Neora Valley)?

March to April and October to November is peak season in Lava & Rishop (Neora Valley). May and December to February is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. October-November gives the clearest air of the year for Kanchenjunga from Tiffin Dara, and the park is open again.

How many days do you need in Lava & Rishop (Neora Valley)?

3 days is the usual stay. Homestays in Lava and a scatter of huts at Rishop; birders after satyr tragopan, plus Bengali families in season.

How do you get to Lava & Rishop (Neora Valley)?

New Mal Junction is 2.5 h by hired car, NJP about 3.5 h; Neora day permits come from the Lava range office. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Lava & Rishop (Neora Valley) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Lava & Rishop?

Lava Monastery, Neora Valley National Park, Tiffin Dara, Changey Falls, Kolakham and Lolegaon. Kagyu Thekchen Ling gompa on the ridge above Lava bazaar, red and gold; morning prayers around 6, shoes off at the hall.
