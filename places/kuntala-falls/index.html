Kuntala Falls season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Dec-Jan the Kadam still runs but thin, and the long stair down to the pool is safe and nearly empty on weekdays.

Summer March to May Do not go

Mar-Jun the fall is a dry rock face and Adilabad district sits at 45 C, so there is simply nothing to come for.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Jul-Sep is peak flow and the roar is the whole point, but the lower deck shuts when the river is in spate.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov is the best of both, strong water without flood closures and the Neredigonda teak forest still green.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Kuntala Falls?

August to October is peak season in Kuntala Falls. July and November to December is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid March to June. Jul-Sep is peak flow and the roar is the whole point, but the lower deck shuts when the river is in spate.

How many days do you need in Kuntala Falls?

1 day is the usual stay. Locals picnicking at the upper deck in season; no stay at the falls, so base yourself in Nirmal or Adilabad.

How do you get to Kuntala Falls?

Nirmal 45 km or Adilabad 58 km by bus, then auto from Neradigonda 12 km; nothing runs after dark. Practical ways in: road.

Is Kuntala Falls expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
