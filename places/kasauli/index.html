Kasauli season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

December-February drops to 2-12C with the odd snow flurry, log fires in the old hotels and almost no day-trippers about.

Summer March to May Prime time

April-June is why Kasauli exists: 18-28C under deodar while Chandigarh below sits at 42C, but weekends are fully booked.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

July-August is fog, leeches on the Gilbert Trail and washed-out views; the Kalka road stays open but there is little to do.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October-November is the clearest window, when you can watch the Chandigarh grid light up from Sunset Point after dark.

What Kasauli is actually like

A quiet army cantonment where walking is the whole point; Chandigarh weekenders, old-school hotels, no hostel scene

Things to do in Kasauli

The local places worth your time in and around Kasauli, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Monkey Point

    Viewpoint · 4 km out

    Hanuman temple on the highest hill, inside an air force base; photo ID needed, no phones or cameras past the gate

  • Christ Church

    Church · in town

    1853 church on the Mall with stained glass and horse chestnuts in the yard; open through the day, service on Sunday

  • Gilbert Nature Trail

    Walk · 1 km out

    Level 1.5 km ledge path cut into the hillside near the air force gate; go at first light for birds and clear plains

  • Sunset Point

    Viewpoint · 1 km out

    Railed ridge at the end of Upper Mall looking over the Punjab plains; on a clear night you can pick out Chandigarh's lights

  • Dagshai Jail Museum

    Museum · 12 km out

    1849 cellular jail down the hill in Dagshai cantonment, cells that held the Connaught Rangers mutineers; small ticket at the gate

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Kasauli?

March to May and October to November is peak season in Kasauli. June and December to February is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid July to August. April-June is why Kasauli exists: 18-28C under deodar while Chandigarh below sits at 42C, but weekends are fully booked.

How many days do you need in Kasauli?

2 days is the usual stay. A quiet army cantonment where walking is the whole point; Chandigarh weekenders, old-school hotels, no hostel scene.

How do you get to Kasauli?

Kalka railhead 24 km on the Delhi line, Chandigarh airport 60 km; shared taxis up from Dharampur on NH-5. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Kasauli expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Kasauli?

Monkey Point, Christ Church, Gilbert Nature Trail, Sunset Point and Dagshai Jail Museum. Hanuman temple on the highest hill, inside an air force base; photo ID needed, no phones or cameras past the gate.
