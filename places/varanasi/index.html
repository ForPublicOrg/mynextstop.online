Varanasi season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Peak season: cool mornings for sunrise boat rides, and Dev Deepawali in November is unmissable

Summer March to May Shoulder

Brutal 45C+ heat with high humidity by May-June; the ghats are near-empty for good reason

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

The Ganges rises and can submerge lower ghat steps; muggy but atmospheric if you handle the heat

Autumn October to November Prime time

Pleasant temperatures return by late October, building to the spectacular Dev Deepawali festival

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Varanasi?

October to February is peak season in Varanasi. March and July to September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Pleasant temperatures return by late October, building to the spectacular Dev Deepawali festival.

How many days do you need in Varanasi?

2 days is the usual stay. Long-term travelers linger for weeks on the ghats; boat-sharing makes solo mixing easy.

How do you get to Varanasi?

Flights/trains from Delhi 12h; airport 25km from the ghats. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Varanasi expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.

Is there a festival in Varanasi?

Nov: Dev Deepawali. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
