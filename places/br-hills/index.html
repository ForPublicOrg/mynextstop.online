BR Hills (Biligiri Ranganatha) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is the sweet spot: 26 °C days, cold nights at 1,200 m, and elephant and gaur out on the K Gudi road.

Summer March to May Prime time

March is dry and excellent for sightings; by May it is 34 °C at the base and the hill air turns smoky.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun-Sep the sanctuary road up from Yelandur runs with water, safaris are patchy and the temple track gets slippery.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is still wet and quiet; November is superb, green, cool in the mornings and free of weekend traffic.

Things to do in BR Hills

The local places worth your time in and around BR Hills, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Biligiri Ranganathaswamy Temple

    Temple · 2 km out

    Cliff top Vishnu shrine the hills are named for, with a giant leather chappal offering; the ghat road shuts at 6 pm

  • K Gudi

    Wildlife · 10 km out

    Forest hamlet with the JLR camp and the safari zone; jeep drives leave about 6.30 am and 3.30 pm, book at the camp

  • Dodda Sampige Mara

    Shrine · 14 km out

    Giant champak tree the Soligas worship as an ancestor, beside a stream; a rough forest road, ask at K Gudi first

  • Honnametti

    Viewpoint · 8 km out

    Open ridge above coffee slopes with the plains laid out below; the view goes early, cloud builds through the afternoon

  • Gaurishvara Temple, Yelandur

    Temple · 25 km out

    Vijayanagara era stone temple in the market town below the hills; an easy stop on the way down, no ticket

Common questions

When is the best time to visit BR Hills (Biligiri Ranganatha)?

November to March is peak season in BR Hills (Biligiri Ranganatha). April and June to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. No month is a write-off here. October is still wet and quiet; November is superb, green, cool in the mornings and free of weekend traffic.

How many days do you need in BR Hills (Biligiri Ranganatha)?

2 days is the usual stay. Only the JLR camp at K Gudi and a couple of Soliga-run homestays; you will not meet other solo travellers here.

How do you get to BR Hills (Biligiri Ranganatha)?

Chamarajanagar railhead 50 km or Mysuru 90 km, then a hill bus; the last bus up leaves early afternoon. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is BR Hills (Biligiri Ranganatha) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

What are the best things to do in BR Hills?

Biligiri Ranganathaswamy Temple, K Gudi, Dodda Sampige Mara, Honnametti and Gaurishvara Temple, Yelandur. Cliff top Vishnu shrine the hills are named for, with a giant leather chappal offering; the ghat road shuts at 6 pm.
