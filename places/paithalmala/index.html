Paithalmala season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is the clean window: cool grassland, a firm trail and the watchtower's 360° sweep into Coorg's blue ranges.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March is the last good month; through April-May the grass browns and haze flattens the Coorg views, so start at first light.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun-Aug is hard rain on an exposed ridge, slippery laterite and zero visibility; admire the mist from the valley instead.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Sep is mist-and-drizzle roulette; Oct-Nov the hills turn deep green with streams still running, the prettiest weeks up here.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Paithalmala?

October to February is peak season in Paithalmala. March and September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to August. Sep is mist-and-drizzle roulette; Oct-Nov the hills turn deep green with streams still running, the prettiest weeks up here.

How many days do you need in Paithalmala?

1 day is the usual stay. A locals' sunrise trek with Kannur weekenders and a trailhead chai shack; sleep in Kannur or a rare village homestay.

How do you get to Paithalmala?

Pottenplave trailhead, 65 km from Kannur, 45 km from Kannur airport; bus to Kudiyanmala, auto on, 3 km walk. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Paithalmala expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
