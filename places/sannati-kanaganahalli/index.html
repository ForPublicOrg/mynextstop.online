Sannati & Kanaganahalli season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is the season: 30 C, the black-soil track in stays firm and a caretaker is usually there to open the shed.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-Jun the Kalaburagi plain runs 43-45 C with zero shade over the stupa mound and the Bhima shrinks back to sand.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

Jul-Sep the Bhima runs high and the last few km of black-cotton road turn to grease, so skip it after heavy rain.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov is the easiest run out from Kalaburagi, green fields all the way and the Chandralamba ghat busy at dusk.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Sannati & Kanaganahalli?

November to February is peak season in Sannati & Kanaganahalli. March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Oct-Nov is the easiest run out from Kalaburagi, green fields all the way and the Chandralamba ghat busy at dusk.

How many days do you need in Sannati & Kanaganahalli?

1 day is the usual stay. Farmers, an ASI caretaker and nobody else; sleep in Kalaburagi, because there is nothing to book at Sannati.

How do you get to Sannati & Kanaganahalli?

Kalaburagi railhead 65 km, bus to Chitapur then a share auto; from Raichur about 3 h by road via Shahabad. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Sannati & Kanaganahalli expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
