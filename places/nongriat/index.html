Nongriat (Double-Decker Root Bridge) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dry stone steps make the descent and climb back up far safer, cool weather helps.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Air stays a mild 26C, but Sohra already takes 790 mm of rain in April and 1,300 mm in May, so the 3,500 steps get greasy early.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Steps become a waterslide, genuinely dangerous plus leeches: most guesthouses say skip it.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Rainbow Falls swimming hole still full from monsoon, steps drying out nicely.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Nongriat (Double-Decker Root Bridge)?

November to February is peak season in Nongriat (Double-Decker Root Bridge). March to May and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. Rainbow Falls swimming hole still full from monsoon, steps drying out nicely.

How many days do you need in Nongriat (Double-Decker Root Bridge)?

3 days is the usual stay. Riverside guesthouses full of trekkers, communal dinners, easy to find company.

How do you get to Nongriat (Double-Decker Root Bridge)?

Drive to Tyrna (1h from Cherrapunji), trek 3,500 steps down. No ILP. Practical ways in: road.

Is Nongriat (Double-Decker Root Bridge) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.
