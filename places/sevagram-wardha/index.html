Sevagram (Wardha) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is 11-30 C, right for Bapu Kuti, Magan Sangrahalaya and a short hop out to Vinoba's Paunar ashram.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-Jun is 44-46 C Vidarbha heat and the ashram's mud-and-bamboo huts have no fans, by original design.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

Jul-Sep is warm and wet with about 1,000 mm; the ashram grounds go green and visitor numbers thin right out.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Gandhi Jayanti on 2 October fills the prayer ground; the rest of Oct-Nov is cool, clear and near empty.

Signature festival

Oct: Gandhi Jayanti gatherings at Sevagram Ashram on 2 Oct

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Sevagram (Wardha)?

November to February is peak season in Sevagram (Wardha). March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Gandhi Jayanti on 2 October fills the prayer ground; the rest of Oct-Nov is cool, clear and near empty.

How many days do you need in Sevagram (Wardha)?

1 day is the usual stay. Ashram dorms, 5 am prayers and no locks on the doors; students and Gandhians, almost no tourists here.

How do you get to Sevagram (Wardha)?

Sevagram and Wardha both sit on the Delhi-Chennai line; ashram 6 km from Sevagram halt, Nagpur 75 km. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Sevagram (Wardha) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Sevagram (Wardha)?

Oct: Gandhi Jayanti gatherings at Sevagram Ashram on 2 Oct. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
