Asan Conservation Reserve (Dakpathar) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec to Feb is the whole point: ruddy shelduck, red crested pochard and 50 odd species on the water, cold still mornings at 4 to 20 C.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March still holds late migrants; by May the Doon floor passes 40 C, the birds have gone north and the reserve is a hot mudflat.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

Jun to Aug the Yamuna runs high and brown, the barrage gates open and there is little to watch beyond egrets in sticky 35 C heat.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Oct and Nov the first waders and ducks drop in and the air clears; boating restarts and the Dakpathar canal walk is at its best.

What Asan Conservation Reserve (Dakpathar) is actually like

A GMVN rest house and a few lodges at Dakpathar; birders with scopes at dawn, canal-side chai stalls, nothing after 9 pm

When is the best time to visit Asan Conservation Reserve (Dakpathar)?

December to February is peak season in Asan Conservation Reserve (Dakpathar). March and October to November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to June.
