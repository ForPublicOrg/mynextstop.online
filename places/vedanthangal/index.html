Vedanthangal Bird Sanctuary season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb the tank's barringtonia trees sag with nesting storks, pelicans, ibis and spoonbills; dawn from the bund is the show

Summer March to May Shoulder

April sees the last chicks fledge and the colony empty; by May the tank is a hot, birdless bowl, so skip the season

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

Jun-Sep the tank lies low and quiet, everything waits on the NE monsoon; there is honestly nothing to see yet

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Oct-Nov rain fills the tank and painted storks arrive to build; by late November the colony is deafening

Things to do in Vedanthangal Bird Sanctuary

The local places worth your time in and around Vedanthangal Bird Sanctuary, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Karikili Bird Sanctuary

    Wildlife · 10 km out

    The quieter twin heronry, a Ramsar tank with almost no crowd; same November to February season, come on a weekday.

  • Madurantakam Lake

    Lake · 9 km out

    One of the biggest tanks in the district and the reason the birds are here; the bund walk at dusk is the local evening.

  • Eri Katha Ramar Temple

    Temple · 9 km out

    Madurantakam temple to the Rama said to have held the lake bund in a flood; small, old and usually empty on weekdays.

  • Uthiramerur

    Heritage · 25 km out

    Village whose tenth century inscription sets out election by palm leaf ballot, carved on the assembly mandapa wall.

  • Thirukazhukundram

    Temple · 35 km out

    Vedagiriswarar temple up 550 steps on a granite hill; climb at dawn, the big temple and tank sit at the bottom.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Vedanthangal Bird Sanctuary?

December to February is peak season in Vedanthangal Bird Sanctuary. March and November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to August. Dec-Feb the tank's barringtonia trees sag with nesting storks, pelicans, ibis and spoonbills; dawn from the bund is the show.

How many days do you need in Vedanthangal Bird Sanctuary?

1 day is the usual stay. School buses and Chennai birders crowd weekend mornings; on weekdays you share the watchtower with the herons.

How do you get to Vedanthangal Bird Sanctuary?

85 km from Chennai; rail to Madurantakam 8 km or Chengalpattu 30 km, then bus or auto; an easy day trip. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Vedanthangal Bird Sanctuary expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Vedanthangal Bird Sanctuary?

Karikili Bird Sanctuary, Madurantakam Lake, Eri Katha Ramar Temple, Uthiramerur and Thirukazhukundram. The quieter twin heronry, a Ramsar tank with almost no crowd; same November to February season, come on a weekday.
