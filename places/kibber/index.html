Kibber (Spiti) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is snow leopard season: minus 25 C nights, bukhari-heated homestays and village trackers on the gorge ridges; the Kinnaur road can shut for days

Summer March to May Prime time

March still has leopards and hard snow; Apr-May the thaw turns lanes to mud and Kunzum stays closed, so it is a long haul in from Shimla, but the village is empty

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Jun-Sep is easy Spiti: Kunzum open from Manali, the Chicham bridge 3 km on, the Kibber sanctuary walks, blue sheep on the slopes; hardly any rain this deep in

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is gold and cold with the clearest skies of the year and Kunzum open until early Nov; November empties the homestays as snow starts and only the Kinnaur road stays

What Kibber (Spiti) is actually like

Homestay families, wildlife photographers in winter and Kaza day-trippers in summer; evenings are butter tea, thukpa and stars

Getting to Kibber (Spiti)

Manali-Kaza bus via Kunzum (Jun-Oct, 10 h) or year-round via Shimla and Kinnaur (2 days); Kaza-Kibber bus daily, 20 km

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Kibber (Spiti)?

February to March and June to October is peak season in Kibber (Spiti). April to May and November to January is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. No month is a write-off here. Dec-Feb is snow leopard season: minus 25 C nights, bukhari-heated homestays and village trackers on the gorge ridges; the Kinnaur road can shut for days.

How many days do you need in Kibber (Spiti)?

3 days is the usual stay. Homestay families, wildlife photographers in winter and Kaza day-trippers in summer; evenings are butter tea, thukpa and stars.

How do you get to Kibber (Spiti)?

Manali-Kaza bus via Kunzum (Jun-Oct, 10 h) or year-round via Shimla and Kinnaur (2 days); Kaza-Kibber bus daily, 20 km. Practical ways in: road.

Is Kibber (Spiti) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.
