Longwa (Mon district) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Nov-Feb is dry at 6-20 C, the Mon road is passable, fog sits below the village and the hearth fires stay lit.

Summer March to May Prime time

Aoleang in the first week of April is the year's big draw, and by late May the hill road starts breaking up again.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Jun-Sep the Mon-Longwa road is deep mud, and the last 40 km can take five hours or simply not happen that day.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October dries week by week and roads improve, but the ride up from Mon stays rough and hard to time reliably.

Signature festival

Apr: Aoleang Monyu (Konyak new year, 1-6 Apr)

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Longwa (Mon district)?

November to April is peak season in Longwa (Mon district). May and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. October dries week by week and roads improve, but the ride up from Mon stays rough and hard to time reliably.

How many days do you need in Longwa (Mon district)?

2 days is the usual stay. Konyak homestays and a village guesthouse, tattooed elders, and no traveller scene outside festival week.

How do you get to Longwa (Mon district)?

ILP for Indians; Mon town 42 km on broken road, 2-3 h; Sonari railhead 90 km; Jorhat airport 200 km. Practical ways in: road.

Is Longwa (Mon district) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

Is there a festival in Longwa (Mon district)?

Apr: Aoleang Monyu (Konyak new year, 1-6 Apr). Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
