Kumbakonam (with Darasuram) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb at 22-31 C is when you can cycle Kumbakonam to Darasuram to Swamimalai in one day without wilting halfway.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-Jun the delta bakes at 38-40 C, the Airavatesvara courtyard is unshaded granite and the temple tanks drop low.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

June still bakes at 38 C; Jul-Sep is dry in the delta and green off the dam release, but no rain arrives to cool it.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Oct-Nov the northeast monsoon actually lands, floods a few delta roads, and fills the Mahamaham tank properly again.

Signature festival

Feb/Mar: Masi Magam at the Mahamaham tank

What Kumbakonam (with Darasuram) is actually like

Degree-coffee shops, the bronze-casters' street at Swamimalai and pilgrim lodges, with you the only outsider walking.

Things to do in Kumbakonam

The local places worth your time in and around Kumbakonam, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Airavatesvara Temple, Darasuram

    Temple · 4 km out

    UNESCO Chola temple whose mandapa is carved as a chariot with wheels and horses; the singing steps are roped off, opens 6 am.

  • Adi Kumbeswarar Temple

    Temple · in town

    The town's main Shiva temple and the centre of the Mahamaham festival; the bazaar at its gates is where the brass shops are.

  • Sarangapani Temple

    Temple · 1 km out

    Largest Vishnu temple here, its sanctum built as a stone chariot; the gopuram is visible all over town, shut through midday.

  • Mahamaham Tank

    Ghat · 1 km out

    Sixteen mandapas and twenty wells round a tank that draws millions every twelve years; on ordinary days it is yours alone.

  • Swamimalai Swaminathaswamy Temple

    Temple · 6 km out

    Hilltop Murugan temple of 60 steps, one per Tamil year; the bronze casters' workshops are on the lane below, open to visitors.

  • Nageswaraswamy Temple

    Temple · 1 km out

    Chola temple of 886 AD with the finest sculpture in town; sun falls straight on the lingam for three days each April.

Getting to Kumbakonam (with Darasuram)

Kumbakonam station is on the Trichy-Chidambaram line; Trichy airport 90 km, buses to Darasuram every 15 min.

When is the best time to visit Kumbakonam (with Darasuram)?

December to February is peak season in Kumbakonam (with Darasuram). March and November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Dec-Feb at 22-31 C is when you can cycle Kumbakonam to Darasuram to Swamimalai in one day without wilting halfway.

How many days do you need in Kumbakonam (with Darasuram)?

2 days is the usual stay. Degree-coffee shops, the bronze-casters' street at Swamimalai and pilgrim lodges, with you the only outsider walking.

Is Kumbakonam (with Darasuram) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Kumbakonam (with Darasuram)?

Feb/Mar: Masi Magam at the Mahamaham tank. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Kumbakonam?
