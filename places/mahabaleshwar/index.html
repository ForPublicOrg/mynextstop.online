Mahabaleshwar season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Clearest skies of the year, cool enough for a jacket, viewpoints at their best.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Strawberry harvest peaks in March-April before May's heat dries the hills out.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Among the wettest places in Maharashtra - viewpoints vanish into fog, roads landslide-prone.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October clears the mist and greens the hills, easing into the winter high season.

Signature festival

Mar-Apr: Strawberry Festival

Things to do in Mahabaleshwar

The local places worth your time in and around Mahabaleshwar, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Arthur's Seat

    Viewpoint · 12 km out

    The Sahyadri drop everyone comes for, Konkan on one side and Deccan on the other; 10 minute walk in, best before 9 am

  • Pratapgad Fort

    Fort · 22 km out

    Shivaji's fort where Afzal Khan died, 500 steps and a big Bhavani temple; shared jeeps from the market, last entry 5 pm

  • Venna Lake

    Lake · 2 km out

    Boating lake ringed with corn and strawberry cream stalls; pedal boats by the half hour, packed on weekend evenings

  • Lingmala Waterfall

    Waterfall · 6 km out

    Two decks, one at the top of a 500 ft drop and one at a bathing pool; ticketed forest path, water June to October

  • Panch Ganga Temple

    Temple · 6 km out

    Old Mahabaleshwar shrine where five rivers, the Krishna among them, run from one stone spout; Krishnabai temple is a walk on

  • Wilson Point

    Viewpoint · 2 km out

    Highest point of the plateau at 1439 m and the only place you get both sunrise and sunset; bare rock, no shade

  • Mapro Garden

    Food · 7 km out

    Strawberry farm shop on the Panchgani road doing crushes, pizza and free jam tasting; queues run long on holidays

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Mahabaleshwar?

November to February is peak season in Mahabaleshwar. March to April and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to September. October clears the mist and greens the hills, easing into the winter high season.

How many days do you need in Mahabaleshwar?

2 days is the usual stay. Popular honeymoon/family hill station - strawberry farms, sociable, not a backpacker hub.

How do you get to Mahabaleshwar?

4h drive or bus from Pune or Mumbai via Panchgani. Practical ways in: flight and road.

Is Mahabaleshwar expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Mahabaleshwar?

Mar-Apr: Strawberry Festival. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Mahabaleshwar?

Arthur's Seat, Pratapgad Fort, Venna Lake, Lingmala Waterfall, Panch Ganga Temple and Wilson Point, and more. The Sahyadri drop everyone comes for, Konkan on one side and Deccan on the other; 10 minute walk in, best before 9 am.
