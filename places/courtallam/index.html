Courtallam (Kutralam) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Jan still carries good flow off the northeast monsoon with far fewer bathers than Aadi; by February it thins out.

Summer March to May Do not go

Mar-May the falls dry to a dribble, the season lodges shut and Tenkasi below sits at 38 C, so there is nothing to see.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Jul-Aug is the Saral season, when southwest monsoon spilling through the Shencottah gap fills all nine falls and packs them.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Dec is the second and better spate, northeast monsoon flow with a fraction of the Aadi-month bathing crowds.

What Courtallam (Kutralam) is actually like

Whole families bathing fully clothed under the Main Falls, oil massage stalls, and cheap lodges that fill up in Aadi.

Things to do in Courtallam

The local places worth your time in and around Courtallam, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Main Falls (Peraruvi)

    Waterfall · in town

    The cascade everyone bathes under, separate men's and women's enclosures; police clear it when the flood siren sounds.

  • Five Falls (Aintharuvi)

    Waterfall · 4 km out

    Five streams over one rock face, the hardest pounding of the lot; busiest 7 to 10 am, leave valuables in a locker.

  • Old Courtallam Falls

    Waterfall · 4 km out

    Quieter falls upriver from town, a short walk off the road; where families go when the Main Falls is packed solid.

  • Thirukutralanathar Temple

    Temple · in town

    Shiva temple laid out on a conch plan at the foot of the falls; wet stone floor, go before the bathers come down.

  • Chitra Sabha

    Heritage · 4 km out

    One of Shiva's five dance halls, walls painted in natural dye on lime; near Old Courtallam, open only limited hours.

  • Shenbaga Devi Falls

    Waterfall · 3 km out

    Small falls up a forest path above the Main Falls, past a Devi shrine; about 30 min up, the path opens in daylight only.

  • Kasi Viswanathar Temple

    Temple · 6 km out

    Tenkasi's 16th century temple with a repainted tower and long carved corridors; 6 km out, town buses every 10 min.

Getting to Courtallam (Kutralam)

Tenkasi railhead 6 km has Chennai and Madurai trains; town buses every 10 min run the nine-falls circuit.
