Suru Valley (Panikhar) season by season

Winter December to February Do not go

Dec-Feb the upper valley is snowed in and Pensi La is shut; Panikhar keeps a road to Kargil but nothing opens for you.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar-Apr is still snow and shuttered homestays; May thaws the lower valley but Rangdum and Pensi La stay closed past it.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Jun-Sep is the whole season - barley terraces green under Nun Kun, the Parkachik glacier snout walkable, Pensi La open.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Oct gives the best light of the year with few vehicles; by November apricots are dried, homestays shut and buses stop.

What Suru Valley (Panikhar) is actually like

Purig farming villages, one JKTDC hut and a few homestays; you may be the only outsider staying in Panikhar.

Getting to Suru Valley (Panikhar)

Kargil 65 km, 2-3 h on the Sankoo or Padum bus; Kargil is 200 km from Srinagar over Zoji La.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Suru Valley (Panikhar)?

June to September is peak season in Suru Valley (Panikhar). May and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid November to April. Jun-Sep is the whole season - barley terraces green under Nun Kun, the Parkachik glacier snout walkable, Pensi La open.

How many days do you need in Suru Valley (Panikhar)?

3 days is the usual stay. Purig farming villages, one JKTDC hut and a few homestays; you may be the only outsider staying in Panikhar.

How do you get to Suru Valley (Panikhar)?

Kargil 65 km, 2-3 h on the Sankoo or Padum bus; Kargil is 200 km from Srinagar over Zoji La. Practical ways in: road.

Is Suru Valley (Panikhar) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
