Dudhwa National Park season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Peak: 4 °C dawns in an open gypsy, barasingha herds on the Sathiana grassland and rhino at Salukhapur.

Summer March to May Prime time

April is strong at the waterholes, but May pushes 44 °C in the terai and the park shuts on 15 June for rain.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Closed Jul-Oct: tracks flood, the Suheli and Mohana break their banks and Palia's road links go under.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Shut until 15 November so October is a write-off; the last fortnight of Nov is crisp, green and quietest.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Dudhwa National Park?

December to March is peak season in Dudhwa National Park. April and November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to October. Peak: 4 °C dawns in an open gypsy, barasingha herds on the Sathiana grassland and rhino at Salukhapur.

How many days do you need in Dudhwa National Park?

2 days is the usual stay. Forest rest houses at Dudhwa and Sathiana plus Tharu homestays near Palia; expect to share or fund a jeep.

How do you get to Dudhwa National Park?

Slow metre-gauge trains to Palia Kalan or Dudhwa, 4 km to the gate; Lucknow 230 km by road, about 6 h. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Dudhwa National Park expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
