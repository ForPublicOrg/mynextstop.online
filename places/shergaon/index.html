Shergaon season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb are frosty and clear: Khiksaba fills the village with masked dances and drums when it falls in December, then January nights drop below zero under dry sunny days

Summer March to May Prime time

Mar-May is orchid and rhododendron time in the pines; days reach 20 C, the forest walks are dry and the Eco Camp fills on weekends with Guwahati families

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun-Sep rain closes in most days and the Rupa-Shergaon road gets slips; the village stays reachable but the forest trails are leech-thick and views are rare

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov bring the driest air of the year, harvest in the terraces below the village and clear ridge views; the best walking weeks, and homestays rarely full

Signature festival

Nov/Dec: Khiksaba, the five-day Sherdukpen forest-spirit festival at Shergaon and Rupa

What Shergaon is actually like

A community Eco Camp of wooden cottages and a handful of homestays; evenings are wood smoke, local millet beer and an early bed

Getting to Shergaon

Tezpur to Rupa by shared Sumo, 5-6 h via Bhalukpong, then 25 km by taxi; Tezpur is the nearest rail and airport. ILP needed

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Shergaon?

March to April and October to December is peak season in Shergaon. January to February, May and September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. No month is a write-off here. Mar-May is orchid and rhododendron time in the pines; days reach 20 C, the forest walks are dry and the Eco Camp fills on weekends with Guwahati families.

How many days do you need in Shergaon?

2 days is the usual stay. A community Eco Camp of wooden cottages and a handful of homestays; evenings are wood smoke, local millet beer and an early bed.

How do you get to Shergaon?

Tezpur to Rupa by shared Sumo, 5-6 h via Bhalukpong, then 25 km by taxi; Tezpur is the nearest rail and airport. ILP needed. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Shergaon expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Shergaon?

Nov/Dec: Khiksaba, the five-day Sherdukpen forest-spirit festival at Shergaon and Rupa. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
