Pahalgam season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

December can be pleasant with light snow; Jan-Feb turn properly cold, glacier routes snowbound.

Summer March to May Prime time

March is quiet and cool; April-May the valley blooms - a great window before Yatra crowds.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

June is lovely, but Jul-Aug bring Amarnath Yatra convoys and traffic curbs; September calms again.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October keeps the good weather with fewer crowds; November turns cold and grey into winter.

Signature festival

Jul-Aug: Amarnath Yatra pilgrimage transits through Pahalgam

Things to do in Pahalgam

The local places worth your time in and around Pahalgam, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Betaab Valley

    Park · 7 km out

    Fenced meadow valley on the Chandanwari road, named after the film shot there; entry ticket, and it is packed by noon.

  • Aru Valley

    Village · 12 km out

    Roadhead hamlet up the Lidder, base for the Kolahoi and Tarsar treks; shared cabs run till evening, beds cost less here.

  • Baisaran

    Walk · 5 km out

    Pine ringed meadow above town, 40 minutes on foot or by pony; check locally that it is open before you pay for a horse.

  • Chandanwari

    Viewpoint · 16 km out

    Road end at the Amarnath yatra start, with a snow bridge over the Lidder into early summer; cabs run a fixed circuit.

  • Mamleshwar Temple

    Temple · 1 km out

    Small old stone Shiva temple with a spring tank across the river from the market; a 15 minute walk, no ticket, rarely busy.

  • Tulian Lake

    Lake · 11 km out

    Glacier lake at about 3350 m, a long day hike up past Baisaran and back; guide and an early start, snow closes it till June.

  • Kolahoi Glacier

    Trek · 25 km out

    Two to three day trek from Aru through Lidderwat to the glacier snout; guide, tent and food all arranged in Aru village.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Pahalgam?

April to June and September to October is peak season in Pahalgam. March and November to December is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid January to February and July to August. March is quiet and cool; April-May the valley blooms - a great window before Yatra crowds.

How many days do you need in Pahalgam?

3 days is the usual stay. Amarnath pilgrims in season plus trekkers for Kolahoi Glacier or Tarsar-Marsar treks.

How do you get to Pahalgam?

Fly to Srinagar, then ~2h taxi or shared sumo to Pahalgam (95km). Practical ways in: flight and road.

Is Pahalgam expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Pahalgam?

Jul-Aug: Amarnath Yatra pilgrimage transits through Pahalgam. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Pahalgam?

Betaab Valley, Aru Valley, Baisaran, Chandanwari, Mamleshwar Temple and Tulian Lake, and more. Fenced meadow valley on the Chandanwari road, named after the film shot there; entry ticket, and it is packed by noon.
