Bhigwan (Kumbhargaon) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

December to February is the whole point: flamingos in the shallows at first light, plus painted storks and ospreys, and a fisherman rowing you close.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March still holds flamingos as the water drops; by late April they have gone and the backwater bakes at 40 C with very little left to look at.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

June to September the birds are away and a full Ujani drowns the mudflats they feed on, so the boats have nothing to circle. Leave it for the cold months.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

October is empty and still hot; the first waders arrive in November and the boatmen restart the 6 am runs, well before the winter weekend crowds.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Bhigwan (Kumbhargaon)?

December to February is peak season in Bhigwan (Kumbhargaon). March and November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. December to February is the whole point: flamingos in the shallows at first light, plus painted storks and ospreys, and a fisherman rowing you close.

2 days is the usual stay. Fisherman homestays at Kumbhargaon with shared rooms and fish thalis; on the water by six, asleep by nine.

How do you get to Bhigwan (Kumbhargaon)?
