Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Best all-round season: calm water at Radhanagar, good dive visibility, full hostel scene.

Summer March to May Prime time

Hot and sticky (32C+) by April-May, but seas stay calm and diving visibility can peak pre-monsoon.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Ferries get cancelled without much notice, dive shops mostly shut, Radhanagar loses its calm.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Sea starts settling in October though still choppy early on; hostels reopen, notably quieter.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep)?

November to March is peak season in Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep). April to May and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. Sea starts settling in October though still choppy early on; hostels reopen, notably quieter.

How many days do you need in Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep)?

4 days is the usual stay. Beach hostel dorms and dive crews. Easiest island here to meet solo travellers.

How do you get to Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep)?

Fly to Port Blair, then 2-3h ferry to Havelock jetty. Practical ways in: flight.

Is Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Made for solo travellers.
