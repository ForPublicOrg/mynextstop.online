Marpha season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Bitter afternoon wind and nights below freezing; Jomsom flights run on clear mornings and the village is almost empty.

Summer March to May Prime time

Apple trees blossom in Mar and Apr, skies are clear and the Dhaulagiri and Nilgiri views are at their best.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

The rain shadow keeps Marpha fairly dry, but landslides on the Beni road and cancelled flights make getting here hard.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Apple harvest under clear skies: fresh juice, dried apples and apple brandy, then a walk to Jomsom along the river.

What Marpha is actually like

Flagstone lanes, Thakali dal bhat sets and apple pie in family lodges; jeep travellers to Muktinath pass through.

Things to do in Marpha

The local places worth your time in and around Marpha, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Marpha Monastery

    Monastery · in town

    Village gompa above the flat roofs, up a stone stair, with prayer wheels and views down the valley.

  • Jomsom

    Village · 6 km out

    Mustang's district hub and airstrip on the Kali Gandaki, with gear shops and the Mustang Eco Museum.

  • Tukuche

    Village · 6 km out

    Old Thakali salt trading village with grand courtyard houses and gompas, under the east face of Dhaulagiri.

  • Dhumba Lake

    Lake · 3 km out

    Small sacred green lake near Thini village, a short walk from Jomsom, with Nilgiri reflected on still mornings.

  • Kagbeni

    Village · 14 km out

    Mud-brick village at the gate of Upper Mustang, with a red 15th century gompa above the Kali Gandaki.

Getting to Marpha

Fly Pokhara to Jomsom (20 min), then 6 km by jeep, or 8 to 10 h by jeep from Pokhara via Beni. ACAP permit.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Marpha?

March to May and September to November is peak season in Marpha. June to August and December to February is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. No month is a write-off here. Apple trees blossom in Mar and Apr, skies are clear and the Dhaulagiri and Nilgiri views are at their best.

How many days do you need in Marpha?

2 days is the usual stay. Flagstone lanes, Thakali dal bhat sets and apple pie in family lodges; jeep travellers to Muktinath pass through.

How do you get to Marpha?

Fly Pokhara to Jomsom (20 min), then 6 km by jeep, or 8 to 10 h by jeep from Pokhara via Beni. ACAP permit. Practical ways in: flight and road.

Is Marpha expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.

What are the best things to do in Marpha?

Marpha Monastery, Jomsom, Tukuche, Dhumba Lake and Kagbeni. Village gompa above the flat roofs, up a stone stair, with prayer wheels and views down the valley.
