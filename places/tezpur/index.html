Tezpur season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Nov-Feb is clear and 10-25 C for Agnigarh at sunset, the sixth-century Da Parbatia door frame and the long bridge.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March-April is warm, green and loud with Bihu; May turns muggy at 34 C with heavy afternoon thunderstorms daily.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

Jun-Aug the Brahmaputra is enormous and brown, ghats close and the road up to Bhalukpong washes out repeatedly.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is the sweet spot, dry and empty, and Nameri 35 km upriver starts taking bookings again around then.

What Tezpur is actually like

Easy-going riverside town of students and army families, cheap hotels, the natural first stop before Tawang

Things to do in Tezpur

The local places worth your time in and around Tezpur, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Agnigarh

    Viewpoint · 1 km out

    Legend hill with sculpture panels on a spiral path and the Brahmaputra below; small ticket, come for sunset over the bridge.

  • Da Parbatia

    Heritage · 5 km out

    Gupta era carved stone doorframe, the earliest surviving sculpture in Assam, in a field of ruins; unstaffed, go in daylight.

  • Mahabhairab Temple

    Temple · 2 km out

    Hill temple over a stone lingam said to predate the town; busiest at Shivaratri, quiet at dawn, leave shoes at the stair.

  • Bamuni Hills

    Heritage · 5 km out

    Ruined temple panels carved around the tenth century on a low hill; a short climb, no shade and no water, carry your own.

  • Chitralekha Udyan (Cole Park)

    Park · 1 km out

    Town park round a pond with old carved stone pillars and boating; open into the evening, ten minutes by rickshaw from the bazar.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Tezpur?

November to February is peak season in Tezpur. March to April and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to August. October is the sweet spot, dry and empty, and Nameri 35 km upriver starts taking bookings again around then.

How many days do you need in Tezpur?

2 days is the usual stay. Easy-going riverside town of students and army families, cheap hotels, the natural first stop before Tawang.

How do you get to Tezpur?

Rangapara North railhead 20 km; buses to Guwahati 180 km, 5 h; Bhalukpong 60 km; no civil flights. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Tezpur expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Tezpur?

Agnigarh, Da Parbatia, Mahabhairab Temple, Bamuni Hills and Chitralekha Udyan (Cole Park). Legend hill with sculpture panels on a spiral path and the Brahmaputra below; small ticket, come for sunset over the bridge.
