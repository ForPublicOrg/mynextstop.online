Gangasagar (Sagar Island) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec and Feb give cool crossings and sand you can walk alone for an hour; around 14 Jan the Makar Sankranti mela packs a few million pilgrims onto the island

Summer March to May Shoulder

March is still easy on the sand, but from late April the flats bake and May is cyclone month on this coast, when vessel timings turn unreliable

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Jun-Sep squalls make the Muriganga crossing rough and launches stop on bad-weather days; the sea runs grey and the lodges shut, so skip it

Autumn October to November Prime time

October clears after the rains and November is the quiet sweet spot: warm water, empty beach, the pilgrim lanes still shuttered for the season

Signature festival

mid-Jan: Gangasagar Mela on Makar Sankranti

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Gangasagar (Sagar Island)?

November to February is peak season in Gangasagar (Sagar Island). March to April and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. October clears after the rains and November is the quiet sweet spot: warm water, empty beach, the pilgrim lanes still shuttered for the season.

How many days do you need in Gangasagar (Sagar Island)?

2 days is the usual stay. Pilgrim dharamshalas, a youth hostel and a few beach lodges; outside mela week you share the sand with fishing crews and dogs.

How do you get to Gangasagar (Sagar Island)?

Sealdah local to Kakdwip (3 h), the Lot 8 vessel across the Muriganga to Kachuberia, then a 30 km bus down the island. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Gangasagar (Sagar Island) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Gangasagar (Sagar Island)?

mid-Jan: Gangasagar Mela on Makar Sankranti. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
