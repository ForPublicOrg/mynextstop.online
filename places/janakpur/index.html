Janakpur (Janaki Mandir) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Best season: Vivah Panchami re-enacts the wedding of Ram and Sita, often in Dec, and days are mild though misty.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Ram Navami brings a big fair in Mar or Apr; May turns scorching, often above 40°C on the plains.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Skip: heavy heat, humidity and waterlogged lanes on the plains; the temples are open but the town is hard work.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Chhath on the sacred ponds after Diwali is a spectacle, and Nov days are warm and clear for temple rounds.

Signature festival

Vivah Panchami (Nov or Dec)

What Janakpur (Janaki Mandir) is actually like

Maithili temple town of pilgrims, sweet shops and Mithila painted walls; mostly Indian and Nepali devotees.

Things to do in Janakpur

The local places worth your time in and around Janakpur, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Ram Mandir

    Temple · in town

    Pagoda-style Ram temple a short walk from the Janaki Mandir, the focus of the Ram Navami celebrations.

  • Ganga Sagar

    Lake · 1 km out

    Large sacred pond where pilgrims bathe and an evening aarti is held, a few minutes from the main temple.

  • Dhanush Sagar

    Lake · in town

    Another of the town's holy ponds, next to Ganga Sagar, linked in legend to the bow Ram broke to win Sita.

  • Vivah Mandap

    Temple · in town

    Pavilion beside the Janaki Mandir marking the wedding of Ram and Sita, with painted dioramas inside.

  • Janakpur Women's Development Center

    Heritage · 3 km out

    Cooperative where Maithil women paint Mithila art on paper, pottery and cloth; buy direct from the artists.

  • Jaleshwar

    Temple · 15 km out

    Border town with the Jaleshwar Mahadev temple, its Shiva lingam set in a water-filled pit below floor level.

Getting to Janakpur (Janaki Mandir)

Janakpur airport has flights from Kathmandu; a cross-border train runs from Jaynagar in Bihar, about 30 km away.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Janakpur (Janaki Mandir)?

November to February is peak season in Janakpur (Janaki Mandir). March to April and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to September. Chhath on the sacred ponds after Diwali is a spectacle, and Nov days are warm and clear for temple rounds.

How many days do you need in Janakpur (Janaki Mandir)?

2 days is the usual stay. Maithili temple town of pilgrims, sweet shops and Mithila painted walls; mostly Indian and Nepali devotees.

How do you get to Janakpur (Janaki Mandir)?

Janakpur airport has flights from Kathmandu; a cross-border train runs from Jaynagar in Bihar, about 30 km away. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Janakpur (Janaki Mandir) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Janakpur (Janaki Mandir)?

Vivah Panchami (Nov or Dec). Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Janakpur?

Ram Mandir, Ganga Sagar, Dhanush Sagar, Vivah Mandap, Janakpur Women's Development Center and Jaleshwar. Pagoda-style Ram temple a short walk from the Janaki Mandir, the focus of the Ram Navami celebrations.
