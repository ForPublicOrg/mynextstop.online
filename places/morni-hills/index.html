Morni Hills season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Cool and misty around the Tikkar Tal lakes, though some resorts scale back service.

Summer March to May Prime time

A genuine escape from Chandigarh's plains heat, several degrees cooler up in the hills.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Haryana's wettest corner - green, the waterfall running, nothing closed, but the narrow ghat road turns slick and views haze over.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Clear skies and comfortable temperatures, one of the better windows before winter chill.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Morni Hills?

March to May is peak season in Morni Hills. June to February is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. No month is a write-off here. A genuine escape from Chandigarh's plains heat, several degrees cooler up in the hills.

How many days do you need in Morni Hills?

2 days is the usual stay. Chandigarh's weekend-escape crowd; a few camps make it easy to meet people.

How do you get to Morni Hills?

1.5h taxi from Chandigarh; no public transport up the hill. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Morni Hills expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.
