Kalinchowk Bhagwati season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Snow season: Nepalis flock up for snowfall from Dec, and the cable car from Kuri saves the icy climb; very cold.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar and Apr bring rhododendron forests and clear mornings; by May cloud rolls in after noon.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Skip: cloud and rain cover the ridge, roads past Charikot see landslides and there are no views at all.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Clear post-monsoon skies with the Gaurishankar, Rolwaling and Langtang ranges in view; cold but mostly snow-free.

What Kalinchowk Bhagwati is actually like

Basic hotels in Kuri village, dal bhat and noodle soup, and crowds of Nepali snow seekers at weekends.

Things to do in Kalinchowk Bhagwati

The local places worth your time in and around Kalinchowk Bhagwati, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Kuri

    Village · 1 km out

    Lodge village below the summit where vehicles stop and both the cable car and the walking trail begin.

  • Kalinchowk Cable Car

    Activity · 1 km out

    Ropeway of about 1 km from Kuri up to the temple in some 5 min, saving a steep walk of about 1.5 h.

  • Charikot

    Viewpoint · 11 km out

    Dolakha district hub on a ridge with views of Gaurishankar, the last big bazaar on the way up.

  • Bhimeshwor Temple

    Temple · 10 km out

    Old Bhimsen shrine in Dolakha bazaar, famous for an idol said to sweat before big national events.

Getting to Kalinchowk Bhagwati

About 150 km from Kathmandu, 6 to 7 h by road via Charikot to Kuri, then a 1 km cable car or a 1.5 h walk.

2 days is the usual stay. Basic hotels in Kuri village, dal bhat and noodle soup, and crowds of Nepali snow seekers at weekends.
