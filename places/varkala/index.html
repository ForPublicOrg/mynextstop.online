Varkala season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Peak season - calm turquoise water, warm days, cliff cafes and yoga shalas all in full swing

Summer March to May Shoulder

Increasingly hot and humid; sea still swimmable in March but pre-monsoon swell builds through May

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Rough seas and dangerous rip currents shut down swimming; shacks shutter for this coast's ayurveda season

Autumn October to November Shoulder

October is transitional as the sea calms by late month; November is close to ideal with thinner crowds

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Varkala?

December to February is peak season in Varkala. March and October to November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to September. Peak season - calm turquoise water, warm days, cliff cafes and yoga shalas all in full swing.

How many days do you need in Varkala?

3 days is the usual stay. Kerala's easiest place to meet solos - yoga classes, cliff cafes, year-round hostel crowd.

How do you get to Varkala?

Fly to Trivandrum, 45min train or taxi to Varkala. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Varkala expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Made for solo travellers.
