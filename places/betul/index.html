Betul (Cabo de Rama coast) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Nov-Feb the sea lies flat, trawlers unload at the jetty at first light and the cliff track south towards Kakolem is walkable; shacks open, never full.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March keeps a sea breeze. April and May run 33 C with heavy humidity, the water is bathwater warm and most small places shut by the middle of May.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

Jun-Jul the fishing ban keeps the trawlers in harbour, boats to Kakolem stop and lifeguards red-flag the water; by Aug and Sep the cliffs turn green.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct is quiet and rinsed clean, the Sal river runs high and the walk up to Cabo de Rama fort is all green; November brings back the boats and the shacks.

What Betul (Cabo de Rama coast) is actually like

Fishermen, a lighthouse hill and a few family guest houses; evenings are harbour lights and one bar, with no music scene at all.

Things to do in Betul

The local places worth your time in and around Betul, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Cabo de Rama Fort

    Fort · 12 km out

    Headland fort the Portuguese got from the Raja of Soonda in the 1760s; the Santo Antonio chapel inside is still in use.

  • Betul Lighthouse

    Viewpoint · 1 km out

    Lighthouse on the Betul headland at the Sal river mouth, with wide views over the beach and nearby villages.

  • Canaguinim Beach

    Beach · 5 km out

    Small, quiet cove south of Betul, an easy stop on the coast road to Cabo de Rama fort.

  • Kakolem Beach

    Beach · 12 km out

    Secluded cove below the Cabo de Rama cliffs, a steep walk of a couple of hundred steps; a seasonal waterfall runs into the sea.

  • Mobor Beach

    Beach · 12 km out

    Beach at the southern tip of the Salcete coast where the Sal river meets the sea, facing Betul across the water.

Getting to Betul (Cabo de Rama coast)

Madgaon railhead 30 km or Dabolim 45 km, then a Cuncolim bus to Betul; a scooter is the only way on south to Cabo de Rama.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Betul (Cabo de Rama coast)?

November to February is peak season in Betul (Cabo de Rama coast). March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to July.
