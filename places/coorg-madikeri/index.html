Coorg (Madikeri) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Cool misty mornings, coffee harvest underway, waterfalls still flowing - classic Coorg.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Warm and dry, but March-April brings the coffee blossom - hillsides turn white overnight.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Some of Karnataka's heaviest rain - landslide-prone roads, leeches, zero-visibility mist.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October-November: monsoon just ended, everything green, waterfalls at their fullest.

Signature festival

Nov-Dec: Puthari, the Kodava rice harvest festival

Things to do in Coorg

The local places worth your time in and around Coorg, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Raja's Seat

    Viewpoint · 1 km out

    Garden ledge where the Kodagu kings watched sunset over the valleys; small fee, be there by 5.30 pm for a seat

  • Abbey Falls

    Waterfall · 8 km out

    Fall inside a coffee and pepper estate, seen from a hanging bridge; full only in the rains, entry closes about 5.30 pm

  • Madikeri Fort

    Fort · in town

    Town centre fort holding the old palace, a church museum and stone tortoises; the museum is shut on Mondays

  • Omkareshwara Temple

    Temple · 1 km out

    1820 Shiva temple built with a dome and minarets round a fish filled tank; five minutes on foot from the fort

  • Mandalpatti

    Viewpoint · 22 km out

    Grass hills at 1600 m up the track past Abbey Falls; the last 8 km need a hired jeep, go for sunrise or skip it

  • Talakaveri

    Temple · 44 km out

    Tank at the source of the Kaveri on Brahmagiri, with 350 steps to the summit; cold, and in cloud most afternoons

  • Bhagamandala

    Temple · 36 km out

    Triveni sangama where three rivers meet, with a Kerala style tiled temple; pilgrims bathe here before Talakaveri

  • Dubare Elephant Camp

    Wildlife · 30 km out

    Forest camp across the Kaveri where the morning elephant session runs; cross on the boat at the counter, slots end near 11 am

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Coorg (Madikeri)?

October to January is peak season in Coorg (Madikeri). February to May is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. October-November: monsoon just ended, everything green, waterfalls at their fullest.

How many days do you need in Coorg (Madikeri)?

3 days is the usual stay. More honeymoon resorts than hostels, but homestays and trekking groups work fine solo.

How do you get to Coorg (Madikeri)?

Fly Mangalore/Bengaluru, 5-6h drive; nearest train Mysuru (3h). Practical ways in: road.

Is Coorg (Madikeri) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Coorg (Madikeri)?

Nov-Dec: Puthari, the Kodava rice harvest festival. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Coorg?

Raja's Seat, Abbey Falls, Madikeri Fort, Omkareshwara Temple, Mandalpatti and Talakaveri, and more. Garden ledge where the Kodagu kings watched sunset over the valleys; small fee, be there by 5.30 pm for a seat.
