Tharangambadi (Tranquebar) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb at 22-30 C with a steady sea breeze is when the Dansborg ramparts and the old Danish street walk are perfect.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-May is 36-38 C with heavy humidity and no shade at all on the seafront; the town goes airless by mid-morning.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun-Sep is dry on this coast rather than wet, and unexpectedly windy, so rooms go half price and the beach is deserted.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

October is the wet tail, walkable between showers; November is out, 490 mm of northeast monsoon and the month cyclones land here.

What Tharangambadi (Tranquebar) is actually like

A silent Danish-colonial grid of six streets, fishing boats, one heritage hotel and a few homestays in old houses.
