Hyderabad season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Best sightseeing weather - cool evenings for Charminar's night market and Golconda's show.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March is warm at 35C and workable; by April-May it is 38-40C and the old city lanes are not worth walking - leave it.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Moderate rain cools things down, the lakes fill up, though heavy spells snarl traffic.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October onward is the turning point - heat breaks, air clears, best months of the year.

Signature festival

Jul-Aug: Bonalu, Telangana's signature folk festival for Mahakali

Things to do in Hyderabad

The local places worth your time in and around Hyderabad, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Charminar

    Heritage · in town

    Four minaret gateway of 1591 at the centre of the old city; climb to the upper balcony, last tickets around 5 pm

  • Golconda Fort

    Fort · 11 km out

    Qutb Shahi capital on a granite hill, a long stepped climb to the Bala Hisar pavilion; start at 9 am, there is no shade

  • Qutb Shahi Tombs

    Heritage · 10 km out

    Seven domed royal tombs in a restored garden just north of Golconda; its own ticket, allow two hours, go early

  • Salar Jung Museum

    Museum · 2 km out

    One family's hoard of clocks, jade and daggers on the Musi bank; closed Fridays, three hours minimum, no big bags

  • Laad Bazaar

    Market · in town

    Lane of lacquer bangle shops running west from Charminar to Chowmahalla; bargain hard, it is brightest after dark

  • Chowmahalla Palace

    Palace · 1 km out

    Nizam's four courtyard palace with the durbar hall and a vintage Rolls Royce; closed Fridays, last entry about 4.30 pm

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Hyderabad?

October to February is peak season in Hyderabad. March and June to September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to May. October onward is the turning point - heat breaks, air clears, best months of the year.

How many days do you need in Hyderabad?

2 days is the usual stay. Growing hostel and cafe scene, huge student/IT crowd - easy to find company.

How do you get to Hyderabad?

Major international airport (HYD) with direct flights nationwide. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Hyderabad expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.

Is there a festival in Hyderabad?

Jul-Aug: Bonalu, Telangana's signature folk festival for Mahakali. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Hyderabad?

Charminar, Golconda Fort, Qutb Shahi Tombs, Salar Jung Museum, Laad Bazaar and Chowmahalla Palace. Four minaret gateway of 1591 at the centre of the old city; climb to the upper balcony, last tickets around 5 pm.
