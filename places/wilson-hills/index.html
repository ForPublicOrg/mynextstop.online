Wilson Hills (Dharampur) season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Dec-Feb is clear and 12-28 °C with long views off the escarpment; locals swear you can pick out the sea on the crispest mornings.

Summer March to May Mixed, read the detail

Mar-May is hot haze: mid-30s even up top by afternoon, brown hillsides and dried-up falls; wait for the rain to arrive.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Jul-Sep the plateau lives inside cloud: Shankar falls and the roadside cascades run full, everything drips green, and Surat mobs it on Sundays.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Oct-Nov keeps the green slopes and running streams once the weekend crowds go, and nights dip pleasantly below 20 °C.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Wilson Hills (Dharampur)?

July to September is peak season in Wilson Hills (Dharampur). June and October to February is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to May. Jul-Sep the plateau lives inside cloud: Shankar falls and the roadside cascades run full, everything drips green, and Surat mobs it on Sundays.

How many days do you need in Wilson Hills (Dharampur)?

1 day is the usual stay. Surat weekend picnic scene, near-empty midweek; a couple of roadside resorts, else sleep down in Dharampur or Valsad.

How do you get to Wilson Hills (Dharampur)?

Valsad railhead 55 km via Dharampur; frequent buses end at Dharampur, taxis or rare GSRTC runs do the hairpins. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Wilson Hills (Dharampur) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
