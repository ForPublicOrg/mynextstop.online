Madhubani (Mithila art villages) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb at 7-24 C is the time to sit in courtyards watching panels being painted, with artists home and dry.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March is warm but fine; April-June the plain hits 40 C and long power cuts shut most painting down by midday.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

June-September the Kamla Balan floods this district most years, cutting the village roads for days at a stretch.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is muggy but drying out; November brings Sama-Chakeva, when clay bird figures fill every Maithil courtyard.

Signature festival

Nov: Sama-Chakeva Mithila folk festival

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Madhubani (Mithila art villages)?

November to February is peak season in Madhubani (Mithila art villages). March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to September. October is muggy but drying out; November brings Sama-Chakeva, when clay bird figures fill every Maithil courtyard.

How many days do you need in Madhubani (Mithila art villages)?

2 days is the usual stay. Artist families sell straight from the house and will teach you a panel; basic hotels in Madhubani town only.

How do you get to Madhubani (Mithila art villages)?

Darbhanga airport 35 km with Delhi and Mumbai flights; Madhubani station on the Darbhanga-Jaynagar line. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Madhubani (Mithila art villages) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

Is there a festival in Madhubani (Mithila art villages)?

Nov: Sama-Chakeva Mithila folk festival. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
