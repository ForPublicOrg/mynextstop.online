Aryan Valley (Dha & Garkon) season by season

Winter December to February Do not go

Dec-Feb the Indus road mostly stays open but homestays shut and the villages turn inward; minus-15 nights, no reason to push it.

Summer March to May Prime time

Mid-Apr apricot blossom snows pink over the stone lanes; May brings green terraces, 20 C days and a trickle of travellers.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Jul-Aug barely sees rain in this rain-shadow gorge: apricots dry on rooftops, harvest in full swing, days around 25 C.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Sep is golden buckwheat and poplar with warm days; Oct still works in layers before homestay kitchens wind down for winter.

What Aryan Valley (Dha & Garkon) is actually like

A few travellers a day among Brokpa hosts in flowered headgear; village homestays in Dha and Garkon, no hotels at all.

Getting to Aryan Valley (Dha & Garkon)

Leh 160 km via Khaltse, 4-5 h by taxi; no permit for Indians since 2021, foreigners need PAP; no fixed buses.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Aryan Valley (Dha & Garkon)?

April to September is peak season in Aryan Valley (Dha & Garkon). March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid December to February. Mid-Apr apricot blossom snows pink over the stone lanes; May brings green terraces, 20 C days and a trickle of travellers.

How many days do you need in Aryan Valley (Dha & Garkon)?

2 days is the usual stay. A few travellers a day among Brokpa hosts in flowered headgear; village homestays in Dha and Garkon, no hotels at all.

How do you get to Aryan Valley (Dha & Garkon)?
