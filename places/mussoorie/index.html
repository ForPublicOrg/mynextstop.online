Mussoorie season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Cold and foggy, occasional snow draws a niche crowd, but many cafes shut for the season

Summer March to May Prime time

Peak season: Delhi's classic heat escape, great weather but Mall Road gets packed

Heavy rain and landslide-prone roads from Dehradun make the hillside genuinely risky

Autumn October to November Prime time

Best all-round window: clear skies, comfortable temperatures, fewer crowds than summer

Things to do in Mussoorie

The local places worth your time in and around Mussoorie, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Char Dukan, Landour

    Food · 4 km out

    Four tea shops at the top of Landour doing pancakes and ginger lemon honey; walk up from Picture Palace, about 45 minutes.

  • Camel's Back Road

    Walk · in town

    Flat 3 km walk from Kulri bazaar to the Library round the ridge, past a rock camel and the old cemetery; go at sunset.

  • Gun Hill

    Viewpoint · 1 km out

    Second highest point in town, reached by a short ropeway off the Mall or a 20 minute path; hazy by noon, so go early.

  • Lal Tibba

    Viewpoint · 5 km out

    Highest point at Landour with a paid telescope deck facing Bandarpunch; clearest in the hour after sunrise, then it hazes.

  • George Everest House

    Heritage · 7 km out

    The Surveyor General's estate on a windy spur, restored as a museum; 6 km past the Library, last stretch better on foot.

  • Kempty Falls

    Waterfall · 15 km out

    The famous roadside fall, commercial and packed but a cold plunge pool; get there before 10 am or skip it in season.

  • Company Garden

    Garden · 3 km out

    Municipal garden with a boating pond and an aviary, family heavy; ticketed, 20 minutes by shared taxi from the Mall.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Mussoorie?

April to May and October to November is peak season in Mussoorie. December to March is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. Peak season: Delhi's classic heat escape, great weather but Mall Road gets packed.

How many days do you need in Mussoorie?

2 days is the usual stay. Honeymooners throng Mall Road; solo travelers drift to quieter Landour nearby.

How do you get to Mussoorie?

Fly/train Dehradun 35km, then 1h taxi up the hill. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Mussoorie expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Mussoorie?

Char Dukan, Landour, Camel's Back Road, Gun Hill, Lal Tibba, George Everest House and Kempty Falls, and more. Four tea shops at the top of Landour doing pancakes and ginger lemon honey; walk up from Picture Palace, about 45 minutes.
