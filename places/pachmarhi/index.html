Pachmarhi season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Best season: crisp hill air, all viewpoints and caves open, Nagpur/Bhopal weekenders arrive.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Cooler than the plains (high 20s vs 42C+ below). A real escape, though falls run thin by May.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Falls run spectacular but authorities cordon off pools after past drownings. Trails get slick.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October rains clear, falls still full, forest green: less crowded than the December rush.

What Pachmarhi is actually like

Weekend crowd from Bhopal and Nagpur. Family groups more than solo backpackers.

Things to do in Pachmarhi

The local places worth your time in and around Pachmarhi, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Bee Falls

    Waterfall · 4 km out

    Falls and a drinking pool below a long stair, 20 minutes down and slower back up; go early, the gypsy queue builds by 10 am.

  • Dhoopgarh

    Viewpoint · 10 km out

    Highest point in the Satpuras at 1350 m and the sunset everyone drives to; only permitted gypsies go up, book one in the bazaar.

  • Pandav Caves

    Heritage · 2 km out

    Five rock cut chambers on a low hill in a garden, Buddhist rather than Pandava; ten minutes to see, and the light is best early.

  • Jata Shankar Cave

    Cave · 2 km out

    Natural cave shrine at the foot of a boulder ravine, cool even in May; ten minutes down the steps from the road head.

  • Handi Khoh

    Viewpoint · 4 km out

    A sheer 300 ft ravine wall a two minute walk from the gate; come before the afternoon haze fills the valley.

  • Rajat Prapat

    Waterfall · 4 km out

    The silver fall seen across the gorge from Apsara Vihar; the climb to its base is steep, needs a guide, and takes half a day.

  • Chauragarh

    Trek · 12 km out

    Trident covered summit above Mahadeo cave, roughly 1300 steps up; three hours return, and impassably crowded at Mahashivratri.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Pachmarhi?

October to February is peak season in Pachmarhi. March to May is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. October rains clear, falls still full, forest green: less crowded than the December rush.

How many days do you need in Pachmarhi?

3 days is the usual stay. Weekend crowd from Bhopal and Nagpur. Family groups more than solo backpackers.

How do you get to Pachmarhi?

Train to Pipariya (52km), then 1.5h shared taxi up the ghat. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Pachmarhi expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Pachmarhi?

Bee Falls, Dhoopgarh, Pandav Caves, Jata Shankar Cave, Handi Khoh and Rajat Prapat, and more. Falls and a drinking pool below a long stair, 20 minutes down and slower back up; go early, the gypsy queue builds by 10 am.
