Tawang season by season

Winter December to February Do not go

Sela Pass frequently snows shut, roads get cut off for days. Real risk of being stranded.

Summer March to May Prime time

Prime season: April-May roads are clear, monastery and high lakes fully accessible.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Landslides regularly cut the Bomdila-Tawang road. Genuinely the worst time to attempt it.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is stunning, clearest mountain views of the year; November turns cold again.

Signature festival

Dec/Jan: Torgya Festival at Tawang Monastery

What Tawang is actually like

Monks, army convoys, a few backpackers: quiet monastery evenings, no party scene.

Things to do in Tawang

The local places worth your time in and around Tawang, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Tawang Monastery

    Monastery · 1 km out

    Galden Namgey Lhatse, founded in the 1680s and the largest in India; the morning chant starts before six, museum upstairs.

  • Sela Pass

    Viewpoint · 78 km out

    4,170 m pass with a lake beside the road, the gate to Tawang; cross before noon, it shuts in snow with no warning.

  • Sangetsar Lake (Madhuri Lake)

    Lake · 35 km out

    Dead tree stumps standing in a lake made by the 1950 earthquake; needs the Bum La permit from the DC office in Tawang.

  • Bum La Pass

    Viewpoint · 37 km out

    Border post at 4,600 m where the two armies meet; Indian passport holders only, permit from the DC office and an army escort.

  • Nuranang Falls

    Waterfall · 40 km out

    A 100 m fall near Jang dropping straight off the cliff into a gorge; steps go down to the base, slippery after rain.

  • Urgelling Monastery

    Monastery · 5 km out

    Quiet little gompa on the birth site of the sixth Dalai Lama, born 1683; usually empty, ask next door if the hall is locked.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Tawang?

April to May and October is peak season in Tawang. March and November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September and December to February. Prime season: April-May roads are clear, monastery and high lakes fully accessible.

How many days do you need in Tawang?

5 days is the usual stay. Monks, army convoys, a few backpackers: quiet monastery evenings, no party scene.

How do you get to Tawang?

Guwahati + 2-day drive via Bomdila-Dirang. ILP + PAP. Practical ways in: road.

Is Tawang expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

Is there a festival in Tawang?

Dec/Jan: Torgya Festival at Tawang Monastery. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Tawang?

Tawang Monastery, Sela Pass, Sangetsar Lake (Madhuri Lake), Bum La Pass, Nuranang Falls and Urgelling Monastery. Galden Namgey Lhatse, founded in the 1680s and the largest in India; the morning chant starts before six, museum upstairs.
