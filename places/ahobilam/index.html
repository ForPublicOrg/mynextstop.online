Ahobilam season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Nov-Feb is the season for the 8 km climb between the lower and upper shrines, 28 C and dry gorge beds underfoot.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-Jun the Nallamala runs to 45 C and the rock gorge holds that heat, making the upper temples genuinely unsafe.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jul-Aug the gorge streams rise, the path to Jwala Narasimha turns slick and leech-ridden and jeeps stop running.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov the forest is green, pilgrim numbers are low and the walk up to Upper Ahobilam is at its most pleasant.

What Ahobilam is actually like

Things to do in Ahobilam

The local places worth your time in and around Ahobilam, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Ahobila Narasimha Swamy Temple

    Temple · 8 km out

    The main hill shrine at Upper Ahobilam, old and half rock cut; shared jeeps run the 8 km up, first darshan around 7 am.

  • Prahlada Varada Narasimha Temple

    Temple · in town

    The big lower town temple with carved Vijayanagara mandapas, easy darshan if you skip the climb; closed midday.

  • Ugra Stambham

    Trek · 11 km out

    The cleft rock pillar above Upper Ahobilam where the legend begins; four hard hours return, start by 6 am with water.

  • Jwala Narasimha Temple

    Temple · 10 km out

    Shrine beside a red stained stream pool on the way to Ugra Stambham; an hour up a rough path, no stalls beyond it.

  • Yogananda Narasimha Temple

    Temple · 2 km out

    Small shrine on the fields below the gorge, reached on a stony track; a 30 minute walk from Lower Ahobilam, go early.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Ahobilam?

November to February is peak season in Ahobilam. March and September to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to August. Oct-Nov the forest is green, pilgrim numbers are low and the walk up to Upper Ahobilam is at its most pleasant.

How many days do you need in Ahobilam?

2 days is the usual stay. Pilgrim rest houses and jeep drivers, very few tourists; fine alone, but carry water and start the climb early.

How do you get to Ahobilam?

Nandyal railhead 65 km, Allagadda 25 km; buses to Lower Ahobilam, shared jeeps the 8 km up to the shrines. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Ahobilam expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Ahobilam?

Ahobila Narasimha Swamy Temple, Prahlada Varada Narasimha Temple, Ugra Stambham, Jwala Narasimha Temple and Yogananda Narasimha Temple. The main hill shrine at Upper Ahobilam, old and half rock cut; shared jeeps run the 8 km up, first darshan around 7 am.
