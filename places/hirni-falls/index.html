Hirni Falls (Bandgaon) season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Dec-Feb the fall slims but stays pretty, and the cool sal forest makes the walk down pleasant; winter Sundays bring picnic crowds.

Summer March to May Mixed, read the detail

March still has some flow; Apr-May the plateau turns hot and the falls dwindle to a thread, so skip it and come back after the rains.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

July fills the river; Aug-Sep it pours off the plateau edge in a broad torrent, though the rocks get slick and swimming is unwise.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov is the best of it: strong water, clear skies and dry trails, an easy pairing with Panchghagh's five streams near Khunti.

What Hirni Falls (Bandgaon) is actually like

Munda village country with a few stalls and picnic groups; no lodges at the falls, so base in Ranchi or Khunti.

Things to do in Hirni Falls

  • Panchghagh Falls

    Waterfall · 19 km out

    The Banai splits into five low streams 8 km from Khunti; the gentlest falls around, good for a safe paddle.

  • Perwaghagh Falls

    Waterfall · 21 km out

    Forest falls on the Chata river in Torpa block, about 40 km from Khunti by road; quieter than Panchghagh.

  • Ulihatu

    Village · 23 km out

    Birthplace of the tribal hero Birsa Munda, with a memorial in this small Munda village.

  • Dassam Falls

    Waterfall · 39 km out

    The Kanchi river's big drop on the Ranchi to Tata road, a bigger and busier sight for a second day.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Hirni Falls (Bandgaon)?

August to October is peak season in Hirni Falls (Bandgaon). July and November to December is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. July fills the river; Aug-Sep it pours off the plateau edge in a broad torrent, though the rocks get slick and swimming is unwise.

How many days do you need in Hirni Falls (Bandgaon)?

1 day is the usual stay. Munda village country with a few stalls and picnic groups; no lodges at the falls, so base in Ranchi or Khunti.

How do you get to Hirni Falls (Bandgaon)?

Is Hirni Falls (Bandgaon) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

What are the best things to do in Hirni Falls?

Panchghagh Falls, Perwaghagh Falls, Ulihatu and Dassam Falls. The Banai splits into five low streams 8 km from Khunti; the gentlest falls around, good for a safe paddle.
