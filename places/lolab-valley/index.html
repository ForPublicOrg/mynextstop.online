Lolab Valley (Sogam) season by season

Winter December to February Quiet, but open

Dec-Feb is snowy and stark with homestay bukharis working overtime; the Kupwara road usually stays open but services are thin.

Summer March to May Prime time

Apr-May is the sweet spot: fruit blossom, paddy transplanting and the Kalaroos caves an easy side trip up the valley.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Jul-Aug is humid green with light Kashmir rain, fine for orchard walks; army checkposts add time, carry original photo ID.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Sep-Oct is golden harvest and woodsmoke, the valley at its most photogenic; nights turn cold fast after mid-Oct.

What Lolab Valley (Sogam) is actually like

No traveller scene: family homestays and eco-huts around Sogam and Chandigam, orchard walks, soldiers at the road posts.

Things to do in Lolab Valley

The local places worth your time in and around Lolab Valley, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Kalaroos Caves

    Cave · 12 km out

    Caves in the ridge above Madmadav that villagers say tunnel all the way to Russia; take a local guide, a torch and grip shoes.

  • Satbaran

    Heritage · 12 km out

    One rock cut block with seven doorways near the caves, nobody agrees on its date; five minutes off the road, no ticket.

  • Bangus Valley

    Walk · 40 km out

    Wide alpine meadow bowl beyond Rajwar with Gujjar flocks and no buildings; jeep only, June to September, ID at the army post.

  • Chandigam

    Village · 8 km out

    Walnut and paddy village with an eco park and a few homestays; the orchard lanes are the best flat walk in the valley.

Getting to Lolab Valley (Sogam)

Srinagar 100 km via Sopore and Kupwara, 3.5-4 h; bus to Kupwara then shared cab to Sogam, carry photo ID.
