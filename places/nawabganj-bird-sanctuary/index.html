Nawabganj Bird Sanctuary (Unnao) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is the whole point: pintail, shoveller and greylag on the water, sarus cranes in the fields, 8 C dawns.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-Jun the lake shrinks and shimmers at 45 C, the migrants are long gone and the tower is an oven by 9 am.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

Jul-Sep the lake is brim full and empty of birds, the walking bund floods and the boats stop running.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

November is the build-up month as the first flocks drop in: warm days, and the paths finally dry underfoot.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Nawabganj Bird Sanctuary (Unnao)?

December to February is peak season in Nawabganj Bird Sanctuary (Unnao). March and November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Dec-Feb is the whole point: pintail, shoveller and greylag on the water, sarus cranes in the fields, 8 C dawns.

How many days do you need in Nawabganj Bird Sanctuary (Unnao)?

1 day is the usual stay. A watchtower, a bund walk and a small state tourist complex; birders with scopes at dawn, nobody else.

How do you get to Nawabganj Bird Sanctuary (Unnao)?

On the Lucknow-Kanpur highway, 45 km from either; any bus between the two drops you at the sanctuary gate. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Nawabganj Bird Sanctuary (Unnao) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.
