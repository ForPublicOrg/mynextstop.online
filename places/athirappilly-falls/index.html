Athirappilly Falls season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Dec-Jan the falls still run decently with cooler trekking weather, but well past monsoon peak

Summer March to May Do not go

Skip if the waterfall is the point - by April-May it's reduced to a thin trickle over bare rock

Monsoon June to September Prime time

This is peak season here - the falls roar at full width; stick to viewing platforms, rocks are lethal

Autumn October to November Shoulder

October-November still has strong flow with clearer skies than the monsoon months

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Athirappilly Falls?

June to September is peak season in Athirappilly Falls. October to January is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid February to May. This is peak season here - the falls roar at full width; stick to viewing platforms, rocks are lethal.

How many days do you need in Athirappilly Falls?

1 day is the usual stay. Mostly a day-trip crowd of families and photographers; better as a stop than a base.

How do you get to Athirappilly Falls?

Fly to Cochin, 1.5-2h taxi/bus into the Sholayar forest range. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Athirappilly Falls expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
