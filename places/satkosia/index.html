Satkosia Gorge (Tikarpada) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is the season: 10-28 C, gharial and mugger out on the sandbars and river camping at Tikarpada.

Summer March to May Shoulder

April-May touches 43 C in the gorge and a riverside tent becomes an oven; the camps wind down anyway.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

The Mahanadi runs brown and violent Jun-Sep, boating stops and the eco-tourism camps close for the season.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is a wet, uncertain gamble; camps reopen around November when the river drops and clears.

What Satkosia Gorge (Tikarpada) is actually like

Tented forest camps on the sand at Tikarpada and Chhotkei; boatmen and gharial keepers, very few tourists

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Satkosia Gorge (Tikarpada)?

November to February is peak season in Satkosia Gorge (Tikarpada). March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to September. October is a wet, uncertain gamble; camps reopen around November when the river drops and clears.

How many days do you need in Satkosia Gorge (Tikarpada)?

2 days is the usual stay. Tented forest camps on the sand at Tikarpada and Chhotkei; boatmen and gharial keepers, very few tourists.

How do you get to Satkosia Gorge (Tikarpada)?

Angul railhead is 55 km; Bhubaneswar 160 km by road; book the Tikarpada camps online in advance. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Satkosia Gorge (Tikarpada) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
