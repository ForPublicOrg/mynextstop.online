Deoghar (Baidyanath Dham) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is the calm season at 8-26 C: short darshan queues, time to climb Trikut on foot and reach Basukinath.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March is fine; April-June the Santhal Parganas hit 42 C and the temple courtyard stone is too hot to stand on.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

July-August is Shravani Mela, when millions of kanwariyas arrive: hours-long queues, no rooms, a spectacle not a rest.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October and November are dry, mild and quiet after the mela, easily the best time for an unhurried darshan.

Signature festival

Jul/Aug: Shravani Mela kanwar pilgrimage

Things to do in Deoghar

The local places worth your time in and around Deoghar, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Naulakha Mandir

    Temple · 2 km out

    Tall white tower modelled on Belur Math, built by a Bengali rani; open through the day and far calmer than the main queue.

  • Basukinath Temple

    Temple · 42 km out

    Shiva's court, where pilgrims finish what they begin at Deoghar; buses run all day, the evening arti is the crowded hour.

  • Trikut Pahar

    Viewpoint · 16 km out

    Three peaked hill with a ropeway and a shrine on top; check the ropeway is actually running before you make the trip.

  • Tapovan Hills

    Cave · 10 km out

    Rock hill with a Shiva cave and an old ashram at the foot; twenty minutes up bare rock, go early, no shade or water.

  • Satsang Ashram

    Heritage · 4 km out

    Anukul Thakur's ashram quarter of presses and dispensaries; Bengali pilgrims fill it, visitors may walk most of the grounds.

  • Nandan Pahar

    Park · 3 km out

    Hilltop park with a small zoo, boating and the town spread below; entry ticket, come for sunset, it closes by evening.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Deoghar (Baidyanath Dham)?

November to February is peak season in Deoghar (Baidyanath Dham). March and July to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. October and November are dry, mild and quiet after the mela, easily the best time for an unhurried darshan.

How many days do you need in Deoghar (Baidyanath Dham)?

2 days is the usual stay. Pilgrim town of dharamshalas, peda shops and pandas; easy to be alone in, but you are always in somebody's queue.

How do you get to Deoghar (Baidyanath Dham)?

Jasidih Junction 7 km on the Howrah-Delhi main line; Deoghar airport 12 km with Delhi and Kolkata flights. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Deoghar (Baidyanath Dham) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Deoghar (Baidyanath Dham)?

Jul/Aug: Shravani Mela kanwar pilgrimage. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Deoghar?

Naulakha Mandir, Basukinath Temple, Trikut Pahar, Tapovan Hills, Satsang Ashram and Nandan Pahar. Tall white tower modelled on Belur Math, built by a Bengali rani; open through the day and far calmer than the main queue.
