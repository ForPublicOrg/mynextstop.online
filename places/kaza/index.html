Kaza (Spiti) season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Brutally cold (-15 to -25C), most stays shut, but Shimla-Kinnaur road stays open for snow-leopard trips.

Summer March to May Shoulder

The Manali-Kaza road via Kunzum Pass opens by late May/Jun, ending isolation, starting the season.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Spiti's real peak - a rain-shadow desert, so this is HP's warmest, driest, greenest window.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Kunzum shuts by late Oct, cutting the Manali shortcut, but harvest and clear light reward Kinnaur route.

Signature festival

Aug: Ladarcha trade fair

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Kaza (Spiti)?

June to September is peak season in Kaza (Spiti). October to May is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. No month is a write-off here. Spiti's real peak - a rain-shadow desert, so this is HP's warmest, driest, greenest window.

How many days do you need in Kaza (Spiti)?

7 days is the usual stay. Shared taxis and Zostel Spiti gather a small rotating cast of overlanders.

How do you get to Kaza (Spiti)?

Fly Bhuntar, 10-12h via Kunzum (Jun-Oct) or the Shimla-Kinnaur road. Practical ways in: road.

Is Kaza (Spiti) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Kaza (Spiti)?

Aug: Ladarcha trade fair. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
