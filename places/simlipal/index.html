Simlipal National Park (Baripada) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Nov-Feb is the core season: plateau mornings near 8 C and elephants out on the meadows before the heat.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March-April pulls animals to the waterholes; by May it is 42 C at the gates with real forest-fire risk.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Shut for the monsoon from about 16 June: the interior tracks and stream crossings simply wash away.

Autumn October to November Prime time

The gates reopen in the second half of October - 18 Oct in 2025 - so confirm the date before you book.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Simlipal National Park (Baripada)?

November to February is peak season in Simlipal National Park (Baripada). March to April and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to September. The gates reopen in the second half of October - 18 Oct in 2025 - so confirm the date before you book.

How many days do you need in Simlipal National Park (Baripada)?

2 days is the usual stay. Forest camps at Chahala and Gurguria booked weeks ahead; you need a jeep, and malaria prophylaxis here.

How do you get to Simlipal National Park (Baripada)?

Baripada is the base, 20 km to Pithabata gate; Balasore railhead 60 km, entry permit booked online. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Simlipal National Park (Baripada) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
