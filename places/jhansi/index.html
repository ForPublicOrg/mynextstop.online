Jhansi season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Best months at 8-25 °C: the fort ramparts and the Rani Mahal murals are walkable all day, Orchha 18 km off.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Bundelkhand bakes from April and touches 47 °C in May; the bare granite ramparts carry no shade at all.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Skip Jul-Sep: 34 °C at saturating humidity, slick fort steps and long ramparts with nowhere to shelter.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October cools fast and the low light on the fort walls is superb; the least crowded good window all year.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Jhansi?

November to February is peak season in Jhansi. March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to September. October cools fast and the low light on the fort walls is superb; the least crowded good window all year.

How many days do you need in Jhansi?

1 day is the usual stay. A blunt railway city with one great fort; many travellers sleep in Orchha and come in only for the day.

How do you get to Jhansi?

Jhansi Jn is a major Delhi-Chennai and Delhi-Mumbai railhead; autos to the fort 3 km, Orchha jeeps 18 km. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Jhansi expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.
