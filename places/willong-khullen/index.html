Willong Khullen season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is dry, cold and clear, 2 C mornings on the ridge; the menhirs stand in stubble fields and the 39 km interior road from Maram is at its firmest for a Sumo

Summer March to May Prime time

Mar-Apr is dry with peach and plum blossom in the village and haze from jhum burning; May brings the first storms and the road starts to soften

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

June is wet but passable in a jeep; Jul-Aug turn the Maram-Peren road into mud with slips, so wait; September is green, the paddy tall around the stones, roads drying

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov is harvest time: ripe paddy up to the menhirs, cold clear mornings, and the village at its most open to visitors before the winter

What Willong Khullen is actually like

A Maram village of tin roofs and paddy, no hotel: ask the village authority for a room or sleep back in Senapati; dusk empties the site

Getting to Willong Khullen

Fly to Imphal; Kohima buses or Sumos to Maram Centre on NH2 (3 h), then 39 km on NH129A towards Peren by hired car or jeep

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Willong Khullen?

October to March is peak season in Willong Khullen. April to June and September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid July to August. Oct-Nov is harvest time: ripe paddy up to the menhirs, cold clear mornings, and the village at its most open to visitors before the winter.

How many days do you need in Willong Khullen?

1 day is the usual stay. A Maram village of tin roofs and paddy, no hotel: ask the village authority for a room or sleep back in Senapati; dusk empties the site.

How do you get to Willong Khullen?

Fly to Imphal; Kohima buses or Sumos to Maram Centre on NH2 (3 h), then 39 km on NH129A towards Peren by hired car or jeep. Practical ways in: flight and road.

Is Willong Khullen expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
