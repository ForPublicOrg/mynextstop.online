Summer March to May Shoulder

Hot, glary and humid on the open rock terrain by Apr-May, best done in the early morning only

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Rain-shadow effect keeps this coast mostly dry through the SW monsoon, hot and humid but workable

Autumn October to November Do not go

NE monsoon rain picks up through Oct-Nov, rough surf, sudden downpours can wash out temple-hopping plans

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Mahabalipuram (Mamallapuram)?

December to February is peak season in Mahabalipuram (Mamallapuram). March and June to September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to May and October to November. Best window - cool evenings on the beach, comfortable for climbing over Krishna's Butterball all day.

How many days do you need in Mahabalipuram (Mamallapuram)?

1 day is the usual stay. A real backpacker beach vibe near Chennai - surf schools and cafes help you meet people.

How do you get to Mahabalipuram (Mamallapuram)?

1hr drive/bus down the ECR from Chennai, frequent buses from CMBT. Practical ways in: flight and road.

Is Mahabalipuram (Mamallapuram) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.

Is there a festival in Mahabalipuram (Mamallapuram)?

Dec-Jan: Mahabalipuram Dance Festival at the Shore Temple. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
