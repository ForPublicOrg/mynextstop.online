Mount Tiyi (Wokha) season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Cold, dry and mostly clear; Dec and Jan are fine for the climb, and rhododendron buds start opening by late Feb.

Summer March to May Prime time

Mar and Apr are peak bloom, with the ridge flushed red by rhododendrons; May turns cloudy with early rain.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Avoid: steep forest trails turn to slick mud, leeches come out and cloud hides the summit views for weeks.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Clear post-rain skies over the Doyang valley, and Lotha villages celebrate the Tokhu Emong harvest in Nov.

Signature festival

Tokhu Emong (Nov)

What Mount Tiyi (Wokha) is actually like

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Mount Tiyi (Wokha)?

March to April and October to November is peak season in Mount Tiyi (Wokha). May and December to February is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. Mar and Apr are peak bloom, with the ridge flushed red by rhododendrons; May turns cloudy with early rain.

How many days do you need in Mount Tiyi (Wokha)?

2 days is the usual stay. Lotha Naga hill-town life, church bells, smoked pork at small eateries and a few local hikers at weekends.

How do you get to Mount Tiyi (Wokha)?

Base at Wokha town, about 80 km (3 h) from Kohima; Dimapur airport and railhead are about 125 km. Nagaland ILP needed. Practical ways in: road.

Is Mount Tiyi (Wokha) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Mount Tiyi (Wokha)?

Tokhu Emong (Nov). Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Mount Tiyi?

Wokha Village, Doyang River and Doyang HEP Dam. Old Lotha village beside the town and the usual base for the climb; ask about a local guide here.
