Yelagiri season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Cool, clear and quiet - the best months for the Punganur lake boat ride and the short Swamimalai trek

Summer March to May Shoulder

Warm days draw Bangalore weekend crowds escaping plains heat, but it's mild hill warmth, not a true escape

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Rain picks up and viewpoints cloud over, roads can be slippery on the 14-hairpin Ghat approach

Autumn October to November Prime time

Skies clear post-monsoon by Oct, greenery is at its best, still fairly quiet outside weekends

Things to do in Yelagiri

The local places worth your time in and around Yelagiri, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Swamimalai Hill

    Trek · 4 km out

    The plateau's own summit walk, about two hours up through scrub from Mangalam; start by 7 am and carry your own water

  • Punganur Lake

    Lake · 1 km out

    Small boating lake at the middle of the plateau with pedal boats and a lawn; tickets until about 6 pm, loud at weekends

  • Jalagamparai Falls

    Waterfall · 15 km out

    Attaru river fall below the hill past Athanavur; rough last stretch then a short scramble, only worth it after rain

  • Velavan Temple

    Temple · 2 km out

    Hillside Murugan temple up a flight of steps off the lake road; quiet at 7 am, packed on Tamil festival days

  • Yelagiri Nature Park

    Park · 1 km out

    Landscaped park beside Punganur Lake with an aquarium and a musical fountain; small ticket, best late afternoon

  • Nilavoor Lake

    Lake · 4 km out

    Quiet reservoir on the far side of the plateau by Nilavoor village; no stalls and no boats, walk out at dawn

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Yelagiri?

November to February is peak season in Yelagiri. March and September to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to August. Skies clear post-monsoon by Oct, greenery is at its best, still fairly quiet outside weekends.

How many days do you need in Yelagiri?

2 days is the usual stay. A low-key weekend escape for Bangalore/Chennai crowds, not a backpacker circuit.

How do you get to Yelagiri?

Nearest rail Jolarpettai (8km); self-drive 3.5hr from Bangalore. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Yelagiri expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

Is there a festival in Yelagiri?

May: Yelagiri Summer Festival (Tamil Nadu Tourism). Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Yelagiri?

Swamimalai Hill, Punganur Lake, Jalagamparai Falls, Velavan Temple, Yelagiri Nature Park and Nilavoor Lake. The plateau's own summit walk, about two hours up through scrub from Mangalam; start by 7 am and carry your own water.
