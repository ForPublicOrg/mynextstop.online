Rajmahal (Sahibganj) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Nov-Feb is dry and 10-25C: walk the Jami Masjid at Hadaf, Singhi Dalan on the Ganga bank and the Baradari, then take a boat at dawn for the Gangetic dolphins

Summer March to May Shoulder

March is still workable early in the day; Apr-May the Ganga plain hits 44C with hot loo winds through the ruins, so not now

Monsoon June to September Quiet, but open

Jun-Sep the Ganga swells to the horizon and floods the low ghats; humid, boats stop in spate, but the Rajmahal hills behind turn green and Moti Jharna runs

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct is warm and wet-green with the river still high; Nov cools, dolphin boats resume and the Mandro fossil park and Udhwa lake birds come into season

What Rajmahal (Sahibganj) is actually like

No tourists to speak of; a Sahibganj hotel or a basic Rajmahal lodge, chai at the ghat and curious locals walking you to the ruins

Getting to Rajmahal (Sahibganj)

Sahibganj station on the loop line, 6 h from Howrah, 5 h from Patna; Rajmahal 30 km / 1 h by bus or auto, or the branch train

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Rajmahal (Sahibganj)?

November to February is peak season in Rajmahal (Sahibganj). March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to May. Oct is warm and wet-green with the river still high; Nov cools, dolphin boats resume and the Mandro fossil park and Udhwa lake birds come into season.

How many days do you need in Rajmahal (Sahibganj)?

2 days is the usual stay. No tourists to speak of; a Sahibganj hotel or a basic Rajmahal lodge, chai at the ghat and curious locals walking you to the ruins.

How do you get to Rajmahal (Sahibganj)?

Sahibganj station on the loop line, 6 h from Howrah, 5 h from Patna; Rajmahal 30 km / 1 h by bus or auto, or the branch train. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Rajmahal (Sahibganj) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
