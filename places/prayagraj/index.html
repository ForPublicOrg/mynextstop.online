Prayagraj (Triveni Sangam) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Peak: the Magh Mela tent city rises on the sand from mid-Jan, 8 °C dawns, rowboats to the Sangam all day.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-Jun runs to 45 °C with hot loo winds off the dry riverbed and the Sangam is a glare pit by 9 am.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Jul-Sep the Ganga and Yamuna swallow the mela ground and lower ghats; boats stop and lanes waterlog.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October drains the flood and Kartik bathers return: warm clear days, cheap boats and the ghats near empty.

Signature festival

Jan/Feb: Magh Mela on the Sangam sands

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Prayagraj (Triveni Sangam)?

November to February is peak season in Prayagraj (Triveni Sangam). March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to September. October drains the flood and Kartik bathers return: warm clear days, cheap boats and the ghats near empty.

How many days do you need in Prayagraj (Triveni Sangam)?

2 days is the usual stay. Pilgrim families, university students and mela sadhus; cheap dharamshalas, traveller crowds only in mela season.

How do you get to Prayagraj (Triveni Sangam)?

Prayagraj Jn on the Delhi-Howrah main line; airport 15 km, shared autos to the Sangam ghat in 20 min. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Prayagraj (Triveni Sangam) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.

Is there a festival in Prayagraj (Triveni Sangam)?

Jan/Feb: Magh Mela on the Sangam sands. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
