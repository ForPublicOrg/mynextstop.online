Masinagudi (Mudumalai) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is 14-30 C with clear evenings, reliable elephant and gaur sightings on the Moyar tracks and easy jeep bookings.

Summer March to May Prime time

Mar-Apr is prime as waterholes shrink and animals concentrate; May adds 36 C and violent pre-monsoon thunderstorms.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Jun-Sep is the southwest monsoon and the worst stretch for sightings; the Kallatty ghat to Ooty shuts on landslip days.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Oct-Nov northeast monsoon showers green everything, but tall grass hides the animals and sightings drop off sharply.

What Masinagudi (Mudumalai) is actually like

Jungle lodges, jeep drivers and a thin backpacker trickle; all vehicle movement on the reserve roads stops at 9pm.

Things to do in Masinagudi

The local places worth your time in and around Masinagudi, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Theppakadu Elephant Camp

    Wildlife · 8 km out

    The reserve's working elephant camp on the Moyar; feeding and bathing about 8.30 am and 5.30 pm, the bus safari books here.

  • Bokkapuram

    Village · 5 km out

    Scrub and lodge hamlet under the ridge; walk the estate tracks at dusk for gaur and elephant, but be back before dark.

  • Kalhatty Falls

    Waterfall · 11 km out

    Falls in a shola beside the 36 hairpin ghat road up to Ooty; a short scramble down, thin outside the monsoon months.

  • Moyar Gorge

    Viewpoint · 15 km out

    Deep canyon where the Moyar cuts the plateau and vultures ride the updrafts; go with a guide, the tracks in are unmarked.
