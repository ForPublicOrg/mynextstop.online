Manginapudi Beach (Machilipatnam) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is the window: 28 C, firm dark sand at low tide, and the old Dutch cemetery in town an easy walk away.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-Jun the delta sits at 42 C with humidity near 80 percent, and water this shallow gives no relief at all.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Jun-Sep the sea turns muddy and rough with no lifeguards anywhere, and Bandar streets flood after a heavy night.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Oct-Nov is pleasant between systems, but one Bay depression can shut the beach road and the buses for two days.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Manginapudi Beach (Machilipatnam)?

December to February is peak season in Manginapudi Beach (Machilipatnam). March and October to November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to September. Dec-Feb is the window: 28 C, firm dark sand at low tide, and the old Dutch cemetery in town an easy walk away.

How many days do you need in Manginapudi Beach (Machilipatnam)?

1 day is the usual stay. Sunday families and snack stalls on the sand, empty midweek; plain lodges in Machilipatnam town are the beds.

How do you get to Manginapudi Beach (Machilipatnam)?

Machilipatnam railhead on the branch line from Vijayawada, autos 11 km out to Manginapudi through the day. Practical ways in: train and road.
