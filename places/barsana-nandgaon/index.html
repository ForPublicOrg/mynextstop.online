Barsana & Nandgaon season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Peak at 8-25 °C: Radha Rani temple stairs at sunrise and a Braj that stays rural instead of tour-bus busy.

Summer March to May Prime time

Lathmar Holi falls in late Feb or March and is worth the chaos; after it Braj climbs past 45 °C and empties.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

Jun-Aug is 38 °C at high humidity with village lanes churned to mud; only Sept's Radhashtami is worth it.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is clean, warm and near-empty between festivals, and the 8 km Barsana-Nandgaon walk is at its best.

Signature festival

Feb/Mar: Lathmar Holi at Barsana and Nandgaon (dates shift yearly)

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Barsana & Nandgaon?

November to March is peak season in Barsana & Nandgaon. October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to August. October is clean, warm and near-empty between festivals, and the 8 km Barsana-Nandgaon walk is at its best.

How many days do you need in Barsana & Nandgaon?

2 days is the usual stay. Village ashrams and simple guesthouses; sadhus and Braj pilgrims, plus a scrum of photographers at Holi.

How do you get to Barsana & Nandgaon?

Mathura Jn 45 km, shared tempos and buses take 1.5 h; Delhi 130 km by road, no railway station in Barsana. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Barsana & Nandgaon expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Barsana & Nandgaon?

Feb/Mar: Lathmar Holi at Barsana and Nandgaon (dates shift yearly). Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
