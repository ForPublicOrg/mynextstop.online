Ranakpur season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Low winter sun reaches through to all 1,444 carved pillars, but the Udaipur-Jodhpur day-trip cars all pull in mid-morning - come early.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Very hot by midday, though the marble interior stays surprisingly cool underfoot.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

The surrounding forest valley turns vividly green. One of the region's prettier monsoon drives.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Clear skies and comfortable temperatures for the drive and temple visit.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Ranakpur?

November to February is peak season in Ranakpur. March and July to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Clear skies and comfortable temperatures for the drive and temple visit.

How many days do you need in Ranakpur?

2 days is the usual stay. A half-day temple stop, not a stay: barely any traveler scene to speak of.

How do you get to Ranakpur?

1.5h drive from Udaipur, often combined with Kumbhalgarh. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Ranakpur expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
