Abhaneri (Chand Baori) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Nov-Feb gives crisp 8-24 °C mornings and long shadows down the well, though Jaipur-Agra tour buses roll through from mid-morning.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-Jun eastern Rajasthan sits at 42-45 °C; the lower flights of the baori hold a little cool but the journey out is punishing.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jul-Sep rain greens the fields and part-fills the well, atmospheric and quiet, with the late-Sep festival as the season's bonus.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct is the sweet spot: 18-33 °C, harvest-green country roads and the stepwell at its most photogenic in low angled sun.

Signature festival

Late Sep: Abhaneri Festival at Chand Baori (dates shift yearly)

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Abhaneri (Chand Baori)?

October to February is peak season in Abhaneri (Chand Baori). March and July to September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Oct is the sweet spot: 18-33 °C, harvest-green country roads and the stepwell at its most photogenic in low angled sun.

How many days do you need in Abhaneri (Chand Baori)?

1 day is the usual stay. Day-trippers off the Jaipur-Agra highway stay an hour; a village guesthouse gets you the baori alone at opening time.

How do you get to Abhaneri (Chand Baori)?

Bandikui Jn 8 km on the Jaipur-Agra line, then auto; Jaipur airport 95 km; highway buses stop at Sikandra. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Abhaneri (Chand Baori) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Abhaneri (Chand Baori)?

Late Sep: Abhaneri Festival at Chand Baori (dates shift yearly). Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
