Srinagar season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Cold - highs near 7C, sub-zero nights, gardens bare - but this is snow season, with the Gulmarg ski runs an hour away.

Summer March to May Prime time

March is cool; by April the Tulip Garden blooms and May weather is close to perfect.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

June stays lovely; Jul-Aug bring Amarnath Yatra convoys, checkpoints and higher prices, easing by Sep.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is the best month - Chinars turn gold and crowds thin; November colder but still gorgeous.

Signature festival

Apr: Tulip Festival at Indira Gandhi Memorial Tulip Garden

Things to do in Srinagar

The local places worth your time in and around Srinagar, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Dal Lake Floating Vegetable Market

    Market · 6 km out

    Growers trade lotus stem and greens boat to boat at first light; be in a shikara by 5 am, it is over within the hour.

  • Jamia Masjid, Nowhatta

    Mosque · 4 km out

    Wooden hall on hundreds of deodar pillars in the old city; visit outside prayer times, Friday noon is packed, cover your head.

  • Shankaracharya Temple

    Temple · 6 km out

    Shiva temple on the hill over the lake, up a couple of hundred steps; phones are barred at the top, go late afternoon.

  • Nishat Bagh

    Garden · 11 km out

    Twelve Mughal terraces rising from the Dal under old chinars; small ticket, quietest before 9 am when the buses arrive.

  • Shalimar Bagh

    Garden · 15 km out

    Jahangir's garden of water channels and chinar shade at the top of the lake; ticketed, the channels run only some hours.

  • Pari Mahal

    Heritage · 9 km out

    Terraced Mughal school ruin on the Zabarwan slope above Chashme Shahi; taxi up for sunset, the whole lake lies below.

  • Hazratbal Shrine

    Shrine · 9 km out

    White marble shrine on the Dal shore holding a relic of the Prophet; separate entrance for women, avoid Friday crowds.

  • Nigeen Lake

    Lake · 8 km out

    Quieter arm of the Dal with older houseboats and cleaner water; people swim here, shikaras cost less than on the Boulevard.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Srinagar?

April to June and September to October is peak season in Srinagar. July to August and November to March is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. No month is a write-off here. March is cool; by April the Tulip Garden blooms and May weather is close to perfect.

How many days do you need in Srinagar?

3 days is the usual stay. Houseboat stays feel like a floating community, easy to link up for Gulmarg or Pahalgam.

How do you get to Srinagar?

Fly direct from Delhi (~1.5h); well-connected from most metros. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Srinagar expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Srinagar?

Apr: Tulip Festival at Indira Gandhi Memorial Tulip Garden. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Srinagar?

Dal Lake Floating Vegetable Market, Jamia Masjid, Nowhatta, Shankaracharya Temple, Nishat Bagh, Shalimar Bagh and Pari Mahal, and more. Growers trade lotus stem and greens boat to boat at first light; be in a shikara by 5 am, it is over within the hour.
