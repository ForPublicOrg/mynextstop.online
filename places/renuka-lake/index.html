Renuka Lake (Renuka Ji) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

December-February is the season proper: about 20 °C days, morning mist on the water and the lakeside parikrama at its calmest.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March is still pleasant at 650 m; by May-June the foothills push past 38 °C and the lake shrinks, start at dawn or skip it.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

July-September is humid and intensely green with the lake brimming; cloudbursts occasionally cut the Nahan road for hours.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October cools quickly; the five-day Renuka Ji fair in November packs the lakeshore, the liveliest and busiest week by far.

Signature festival

Nov: Renuka Ji fair (five days around Kartik Ekadashi)

What Renuka Lake (Renuka Ji) is actually like

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Renuka Lake (Renuka Ji)?

November to February is peak season in Renuka Lake (Renuka Ji). March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to June. October cools quickly; the five-day Renuka Ji fair in November packs the lakeshore, the liveliest and busiest week by far.

How many days do you need in Renuka Lake (Renuka Ji)?

1 day is the usual stay. Pilgrims, picnicking families and birders on the lake loop; HPTDC's lakeside hotel is the solo default.

How do you get to Renuka Lake (Renuka Ji)?

Buses from Nahan (45 km, 1.5 h) or Paonta Sahib; Chandigarh airport is about 3 h via Nahan. Practical ways in: flight and road.

Is Renuka Lake (Renuka Ji) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

Is there a festival in Renuka Lake (Renuka Ji)?

Nov: Renuka Ji fair (five days around Kartik Ekadashi). Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
