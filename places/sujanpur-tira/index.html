Sujanpur Tira season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb mild 20 C days for the fort climb, the Narbadeshwar temple murals and the riverside; the Beas runs clear and green and nights are cold enough for a shawl

Summer March to May Prime time

March has the Holi fair, when the three-day mela fills the chaugan; Apr-May climb past 38 C on the bare Tira hill, so go at dawn or wait for autumn

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

June is hot; Jul-Aug the Beas swells brown, the fort ruins turn green and the temples are quiet; September is humid but the town is at its lushest

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov clear and warm, the best time for the ridge walk from the fort to the Gauri Shankar temple and a Beas sunset without the Holi crowds

Signature festival

Mar: Holi fair, three days on the chaugan, the biggest in Himachal

What Sujanpur Tira is actually like

A sleepy town of temple keepers and boys playing cricket on the chaugan; a few basic hotels and an HPTDC lodge, evenings on the river

Getting to Sujanpur Tira

Fly Gaggal (60 km, 2 h); buses from Hamirpur (22 km, 45 min) or Palampur (40 km, 1.5 h); nearest rail Una, 3 h

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Sujanpur Tira?

November to March is peak season in Sujanpur Tira. April and September to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. No month is a write-off here. Oct-Nov clear and warm, the best time for the ridge walk from the fort to the Gauri Shankar temple and a Beas sunset without the Holi crowds.

How many days do you need in Sujanpur Tira?

2 days is the usual stay. A sleepy town of temple keepers and boys playing cricket on the chaugan; a few basic hotels and an HPTDC lodge, evenings on the river.

How do you get to Sujanpur Tira?

Fly Gaggal (60 km, 2 h); buses from Hamirpur (22 km, 45 min) or Palampur (40 km, 1.5 h); nearest rail Una, 3 h. Practical ways in: flight and road.

Is Sujanpur Tira expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Sujanpur Tira?

Mar: Holi fair, three days on the chaugan, the biggest in Himachal. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
