Bhitarkanika National Park season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is prime: crocodiles bask on every mudbank and waders crowd the creeks, bar a short census closure.

Summer March to May Shoulder

The park shuts 1 May to 31 Jul for crocodile breeding, and April before that is 37 C and airless on a boat.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

Closed until 31 July; August and September reopen wet, with swollen creeks and boatmen who cancel often.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Oct-Nov works once the rain stops, but a Bay of Bengal cyclone can shut the jetties at a day's notice.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Bhitarkanika National Park?

December to February is peak season in Bhitarkanika National Park. March to April and October to November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to July. Dec-Feb is prime: crocodiles bask on every mudbank and waders crowd the creeks, bar a short census closure.

How many days do you need in Bhitarkanika National Park?

2 days is the usual stay. Forest rest houses at Dangmal and eco-camps near Khola; you share the boat with birders, not backpackers.

How do you get to Bhitarkanika National Park?

Bhadrak railhead 90 km, bus to Rajnagar, then a boat from Khola or Gupti; book the forest permit online. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Bhitarkanika National Park expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
