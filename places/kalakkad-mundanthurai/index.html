Kalakkad-Mundanthurai (Mundanthurai) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

December still catches the tail of the northeast monsoon; Jan-Feb is dry, 20-32 C and leech-free, the window that works.

Summer March to May Prime time

March is the last dry month; Apr-Jun the Ambasamudram plains below hit 39 C and fire-season restrictions tighten access.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun-Aug brings southwest spill over the crest, leeches on every trail and river spates that close the upper dam road.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Sep-Nov the northeast monsoon soaks the eastern slope, greenest of all, but expect leeches and washed-out trail days.

What Kalakkad-Mundanthurai (Mundanthurai) is actually like

Forest department dorms and cottages, a checkpost and almost no other visitors; the eco-tourism here is all on foot.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Kalakkad-Mundanthurai (Mundanthurai)?

January to March is peak season in Kalakkad-Mundanthurai (Mundanthurai). September to December is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to August. December still catches the tail of the northeast monsoon; Jan-Feb is dry, 20-32 C and leech-free, the window that works.

2 days is the usual stay. Forest department dorms and cottages, a checkpost and almost no other visitors; the eco-tourism here is all on foot.
