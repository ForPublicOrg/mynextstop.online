Rumbak (Hemis National Park) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

December is quiet and the cats stay high; Jan-Feb is the snow leopard window, spotters scanning the Husing and Tarbung ridges from dawn at minus 20 C.

Summer March to May Prime time

March still holds cats close to the village; through April and May the thaw sets in, the herds move up and the valley empties.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Jun-Sep Ladakh gets almost no rain, the pasture is green and Rumbak is day one of the Markha trek over 4,900 m Ganda La.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

October is cold, clear and quiet once the trekkers have gone; November is bare, with the cats still high on the ridges.

What Rumbak (Hemis National Park) is actually like

Ten flat-roofed houses running rotating homestays, a floor mattress and a dung stove; evenings are thukpa and scope talk.

Things to do in Rumbak

The local places worth your time in and around Rumbak, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Zingchen

    Village · 7 km out

    Road head with a Hemis park check post; pay the wildlife fee here, then walk up the gorge to Rumbak.

  • Yurutse

    Village · 4 km out

    One house hamlet at about 4200 m above Rumbak at the foot of Ganda La; trekkers stay with the family before the pass.

  • Ganda La

    Trek · 10 km out

    Pass of about 4980 m on the trek from Rumbak over to Skiu in the Markha valley; most groups take two days for it.

  • Stok La

    Trek · 8 km out

    Pass of about 4900 m, also called Namlung La, on the trail from Rumbak over to Stok village; a steep climb and a night out.

  • Spituk Gompa

    Monastery · 23 km out

    Gelugpa monastery on a hill above the Indus beside Leh airport, passed on the road out to Zingchen.

Getting to Rumbak (Hemis National Park)

Taxi from Leh to the Zingchen road head, about 1h, then a 3h walk up the gorge; no shop past Spituk, carry cash.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Rumbak (Hemis National Park)?

January to March and July to August is peak season in Rumbak (Hemis National Park). April, June and September to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. No month is a write-off here. December is quiet and the cats stay high; Jan-Feb is the snow leopard window, spotters scanning the Husing and Tarbung ridges from dawn at minus 20 C.

How many days do you need in Rumbak (Hemis National Park)?

3 days is the usual stay. Ten flat-roofed houses running rotating homestays, a floor mattress and a dung stove; evenings are thukpa and scope talk.

How do you get to Rumbak (Hemis National Park)?

Taxi from Leh to the Zingchen road head, about 1h, then a 3h walk up the gorge; no shop past Spituk, carry cash. Practical ways in: flight and road.

Is Rumbak (Hemis National Park) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Rumbak?

Zingchen, Yurutse, Ganda La, Stok La and Spituk Gompa. Road head with a Hemis park check post; pay the wildlife fee here, then walk up the gorge to Rumbak.
