Gandikota season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Coolest, clearest months - the only realistic season for a full day on the canyon rim.

Summer March to May Shoulder

One of India's hottest regions, 43-45C - the exposed canyon edge turns genuinely dangerous.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Lighter rain than the coast, but June still carries brutal heat before things cool off.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October: the gorge below fills a jade-green, temps dropping, great late-afternoon light.

What Gandikota is actually like

A sparse ruin for photographers and campers - unfenced edges mean caution after dark.

Things to do in Gandikota

The local places worth your time in and around Gandikota, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Madhavaraya Temple

    Heritage · in town

    Ruined Vijayanagara temple inside the fort walls, carved pillars and a half fallen gopuram; free, and best at first light

  • Ranganatha Swamy Temple

    Heritage · in town

    The fort's other big temple, plainer than Madhavaraya and just as empty; five minutes down the same lane, no ticket

  • Jamia Masjid

    Heritage · in town

    Two minaret mosque from the Qutb Shahi years standing empty inside the fort walls; five minutes, nobody charges

  • Mylavaram Dam

    Reservoir · 22 km out

    Reservoir on the Penna below Jammalamadugu, quiet water and a long bund road; nothing to eat there, so carry lunch

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Gandikota?

November to February is peak season in Gandikota. March and July to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. October: the gorge below fills a jade-green, temps dropping, great late-afternoon light.

How many days do you need in Gandikota?

2 days is the usual stay. A sparse ruin for photographers and campers - unfenced edges mean caution after dark.

How do you get to Gandikota?

Fly/train to Kadapa, then 1.5h taxi to the gorge. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Gandikota expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

What are the best things to do in Gandikota?

Madhavaraya Temple, Ranganatha Swamy Temple, Jamia Masjid and Mylavaram Dam. Ruined Vijayanagara temple inside the fort walls, carved pillars and a half fallen gopuram; free, and best at first light.
