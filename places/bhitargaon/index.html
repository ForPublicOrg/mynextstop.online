Bhitargaon season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Nov-Feb at 8-25 C is the time to read the terracotta panels on the 5th-century brick tower in low raking light.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-Jun hits 45 C in a village with one tea stall and no shade; the brick throws heat back at you all afternoon.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

Jul-Sep the lanes off the Ghatampur road turn to mud and the brickwork stays dark and streaked with damp.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October and November are dry and clear, the fields around are green after the rains, and you will be alone.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Bhitargaon?

November to February is peak season in Bhitargaon. March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. October and November are dry and clear, the fields around are green after the rains, and you will be alone.

How many days do you need in Bhitargaon?

1 day is the usual stay. An ASI enclosure in a farming village with a caretaker and no facilities; you sleep back in Kanpur.

How do you get to Bhitargaon?

35 km south of Kanpur off the Ghatampur road; buses from Kanpur, then a shared auto out to the village. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Bhitargaon expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.
