Bhubaneswar season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is the window: 15-28 C, dry mornings at Lingaraj and the Khandagiri caves, Adivasi Mela in January.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-May hits 42 C with sticky coastal air; the sandstone temples radiate heat and you give up by 10 am.

Monsoon June to September Quiet, but open

Jun-Sep is muggy and wet in bursts, but the temples, caves and state museum stay open and rooms get cheap.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October dries out fast and November is near-perfect, though a Bay of Bengal cyclone can still eat a week.

Signature festival

Jan: Adivasi Mela tribal fair

Things to do in Bhubaneswar

The local places worth your time in and around Bhubaneswar, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Lingaraj Temple

    Temple · 5 km out

    The 11th century Shiva temple that anchors the old town and is still working; non Hindus see it from the viewing platform.

  • Udayagiri and Khandagiri Caves

    Heritage · 8 km out

    Jain monk cells cut into two facing hills before the Christian era, with the Hathigumpha inscription; go early, the rock heats up.

  • Mukteshwar Temple

    Temple · 4 km out

    Small 10th century temple with a carved torana arch, the finest stone work in the city; free, and quiet before the tour buses.

  • Dhauli Shanti Stupa

    Heritage · 8 km out

    Ashoka's rock edict and a white peace pagoda above the Daya river where the Kalinga war ended; best at sunset, autos wait below.

  • Nandankanan Zoological Park

    Wildlife · 16 km out

    Zoo and lion safari in sal forest with white tigers and a lake boat; closed Mondays, and a half day once you add the queue.

  • Museum of Tribal Arts and Artefacts

    Museum · 4 km out

    Full scale huts of Odisha's tribes with their tools, ornaments and drums, near CRP Square; free entry, allow an hour.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Bhubaneswar?

November to February is peak season in Bhubaneswar. March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to May. October dries out fast and November is near-perfect, though a Bay of Bengal cyclone can still eat a week.

How many days do you need in Bhubaneswar?

2 days is the usual stay. Government-town calm with a real hostel scene near KIIT, cheap autos, and pilgrims rather than backpackers.

How do you get to Bhubaneswar?

Biju Patnaik airport and Bhubaneswar junction are in the city; Puri and Konark are 1-2 h by bus. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Bhubaneswar expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.

Is there a festival in Bhubaneswar?

Jan: Adivasi Mela tribal fair. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Bhubaneswar?

Lingaraj Temple, Udayagiri and Khandagiri Caves, Mukteshwar Temple, Dhauli Shanti Stupa, Nandankanan Zoological Park and Museum of Tribal Arts and Artefacts. The 11th century Shiva temple that anchors the old town and is still working; non Hindus see it from the viewing platform.
