Things to do in Ooty

The local places worth your time in and around Ooty, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Doddabetta Peak

    Viewpoint · 10 km out

    At 2637 m the highest point in the Nilgiris, with a telescope house on top; go by 8 am, before the haze and the buses.

  • Government Botanical Garden

    Garden · 2 km out

    Terraced lawns laid out in 1848 around a fossil tree trunk; opens 7 am, small ticket, quietest in the first hour.

  • Ooty Lake

    Lake · 1 km out

    The 1824 boating lake below the town; pedal boats from 9 am, and the far bank walk is calmer than the fairground end.

  • Pykara Falls and Lake

    Waterfall · 20 km out

    Shola forest, a boathouse and a stepped fall on the Mysore road; boat tickets stop by 5 pm and the steps down stay wet.

  • Avalanche Lake

    Lake · 25 km out

    Shola and a trout stream in a valley the forest department keeps; entry by booked jeep only, no private cars past the gate.

  • Government Rose Garden

    Garden · 2 km out

    Terraced beds of some 2800 rose varieties on Elk Hill; May is peak bloom and the top row gives a wide view over town.

  • St. Stephen's Church

    Church · 1 km out

    1829 garrison church whose teak beams were hauled up from Tipu's palace at Srirangapatna; the old graveyard is behind.

  • Wenlock Downs (9th Mile)

    Viewpoint · 12 km out

    Rolling open grassland every Tamil film has used for a song; the 6th to 9th mile stretch walks well, no shade, windy.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Ooty (Udhagamandalam)?

April to May and October is peak season in Ooty (Udhagamandalam). September and November to March is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to August. Peak hill-season crowds (Apr-May), pleasant 15-25C days, book ahead as plains tourists flood in.

How many days do you need in Ooty (Udhagamandalam)?

3 days is the usual stay. More honeymooners than backpackers, but Zostel and the toy-train crowd help you link up.

How do you get to Ooty (Udhagamandalam)?

Fly to Coimbatore (85km, 3hr) or train to Mettupalayam + toy train. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Ooty (Udhagamandalam) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.

Is there a festival in Ooty (Udhagamandalam)?

May: Ooty Summer Festival (flower and fruit show). Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Ooty?

Doddabetta Peak, Government Botanical Garden, Ooty Lake, Pykara Falls and Lake, Avalanche Lake and Government Rose Garden, and more. At 2637 m the highest point in the Nilgiris, with a telescope house on top; go by 8 am, before the haze and the buses.
