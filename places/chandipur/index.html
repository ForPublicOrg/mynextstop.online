Chandipur (Balasore) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is the season: 12-27 C, a firm exposed seabed to walk at low tide and red crabs everywhere.

Summer March to May Shoulder

April-May is 40 C on an unshaded flat and pre-monsoon squalls off the Bay start turning the sea ugly.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Jun-Sep the tidal flat is a grey soup, the shacks shut and the whole vanishing-sea trick is lost.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is cyclone month on this coast; from mid-November the light is clean and the crowds are thin.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Chandipur (Balasore)?

November to February is peak season in Chandipur (Balasore). March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to September. October is cyclone month on this coast; from mid-November the light is clean and the crowds are thin.

How many days do you need in Chandipur (Balasore)?

1 day is the usual stay. A quiet retiree-and-family beach with an OTDC Panthanivas and small hotels; walk the seabed at dawn.

How do you get to Chandipur (Balasore)?

Balasore railhead is 16 km with autos all day; the beach shuts for DRDO missile tests now and then. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Chandipur (Balasore) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.
