Kheerganga season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

A genuine snow trek for those with proper gear - magical frozen campsite, not for casual solo hikers.

Summer March to May Prime time

May-Jun bring the clearest trail after snowmelt and the most reliable weather for the climb.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Jun holds up; Jul-Aug bring leeches, slick rock sections and real flash-flood risk on the river.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov are the classic window - dry trail, clear ridgeline views, hot spring feels best in the cold.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Kheerganga?

May to June and October to November is peak season in Kheerganga. September and December to April is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid July to August. May-Jun bring the clearest trail after snowmelt and the most reliable weather for the climb.

How many days do you need in Kheerganga?

4 days is the usual stay. Communal tent camps and a shared hot spring make it easy to bond with fellow trekkers.

How do you get to Kheerganga?

Fly Bhuntar, drive to Barshaini (1.5h), then trek 9-10km/5-6h. Practical ways in: road.

Is Kheerganga expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.
