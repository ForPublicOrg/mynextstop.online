Khejarla season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

At 8-26 C this works well: a 17th-century red sandstone fort, a potters' quarter, and silence once the day tours go.

Summer March to May Shoulder

April-June is 45 C of open Marwar plain, the jharokhas catch nothing at midday, and the village shuts down until dusk.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

July-September brings about 350 mm, the scrub greens and the fort looks its best, but it stays 35 C and humid.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October-November is dry and clear, and it is a quieter fort village than Osian if you want nobody else around.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Khejarla?

November to February is peak season in Khejarla. March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. October-November is dry and clear, and it is a quieter fort village than Osian if you want nobody else around.

How many days do you need in Khejarla?

1 day is the usual stay. The fort is a heritage hotel so beds are mid-range; the village has potters and weavers and no other travellers.

How do you get to Khejarla?

Road leg only: Jodhpur 85 km on the Jaipur highway, bus to Bilara then a shared jeep the last 18 km. Practical ways in: road.

Is Khejarla expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
