Dudhsagar Falls (Mollem) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Jan the forest department jeeps from Collem run all day and the falls still carry real post-monsoon volume.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-May reduces the drop to thin white threads and the Mollem forest hits 37 °C; go somewhere else entirely.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Jun-Sep the jeep safari shuts, and walking the railway track is banned year round on heavy fines; trains only.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is the pick once the safari reopens mid-month: full monsoon flow, dry roads and cool early mornings.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Dudhsagar Falls (Mollem)?

October to January is peak season in Dudhsagar Falls (Mollem). February to March is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to September. October is the pick once the safari reopens mid-month: full monsoon flow, dry roads and cool early mornings.

How many days do you need in Dudhsagar Falls (Mollem)?

1 day is the usual stay. Shared jeeps from Collem pack you in with weekenders; homestays at Collem and Mollem are basic but cheap.

How do you get to Dudhsagar Falls (Mollem)?

Kulem (Collem) railhead 12 km, then a booked forest department jeep; no private vehicles past Collem. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Dudhsagar Falls (Mollem) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.
