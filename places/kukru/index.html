Kukru (Khamla Plateau) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Jan mornings dip near frost at 1,100 m, the air goes glass-clear and the sunrise and sunset points are all yours.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-May hovers around 34 C, hazy but far kinder than the 42 C Betul plains below; the coffee and jamun shade helps.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun-Sep mist pours over the ridge and the plateau turns deep green; the Bhainsdehi approach stays open, just drive it slow.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov is the sweet spot: washed skies, slopes still green and the eco jungle camp tents empty midweek.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Kukru (Khamla Plateau)?

October to February is peak season in Kukru (Khamla Plateau). March and June to September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. No month is a write-off here. Oct-Nov is the sweet spot: washed skies, slopes still green and the eco jungle camp tents empty midweek.

How many days do you need in Kukru (Khamla Plateau)?

2 days is the usual stay. MP Ecotourism jungle camp tents and rooms with caretaker-cooked thalis; often you are the only guest, bring a book.

How do you get to Kukru (Khamla Plateau)?

Betul railhead 70 km (2 h) via Bhainsdehi; taxi or own wheels, the last stretch is narrow estate road. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Kukru (Khamla Plateau) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
