Jaisalmer season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Prime season: cold desert nights under the stars, comfortable dune safaris by day.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Among India's hottest towns, past 45C with sandstorms; genuinely avoid this window.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Rain is minimal but heat lingers through the monsoon months with zero relief.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Temperatures start dropping by late October, dune camps become bearable again.

Things to do in Jaisalmer

The local places worth your time in and around Jaisalmer, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Patwon ki Haveli

    Heritage · 1 km out

    Five joined merchant mansions from the 1800s with the town's best carved facade; one wing is a museum, the rest are shops

  • Sam Sand Dunes

    Dunes · 42 km out

    The dune strip everyone camps on, camel ride then a music night; loud and busy, Khuri south of town is the quieter version

  • Gadisar Lake

    Lake · 1 km out

    Rainwater tank ringed by ghats and shrines under the Tilon ki Pol gate; boats from 8 am, best at first light for the birds

  • Jaisalmer Fort Jain Temples

    Temple · in town

    Seven linked yellow stone temples of the 12th to 15th centuries inside the living fort; visitors are let in mornings only

  • Bada Bagh

    Heritage · 6 km out

    Row of royal chhatris on a bare ridge above an old mango garden; sunset is the shot, and the wind turbines are in it too

  • Kuldhara

    Village · 18 km out

    Abandoned Paliwal Brahmin village of straight stone lanes, empty since the 1800s; small ticket, gates shut around 6 pm

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Jaisalmer?

November to February is peak season in Jaisalmer. March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to September. Temperatures start dropping by late October, dune camps become bearable again.

How many days do you need in Jaisalmer?

4 days is the usual stay. Desert-camp bonfires and shared jeep safaris turn strangers into a travel crew fast.

How do you get to Jaisalmer?

Overnight train from Jodhpur (5h) or fly direct into Jaisalmer. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Jaisalmer expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.

Is there a festival in Jaisalmer?

Feb: Jaisalmer Desert Festival. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Jaisalmer?

Patwon ki Haveli, Sam Sand Dunes, Gadisar Lake, Jaisalmer Fort Jain Temples, Bada Bagh and Kuldhara. Five joined merchant mansions from the 1800s with the town's best carved facade; one wing is a museum, the rest are shops.
