Nongkhnum Island (Langshiang Falls) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is dry and blue: the Kynshi shrinks to green pools, the sand flats double in size and island camping is at its safest.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar-Apr stays warm and hazy but fine for the falls viewpoints; May's first storms make the river tracks greasy and slow.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun-Aug the Kynshi runs in spate: the beach disappears, crossings are dangerous and the track from Nongstoin slides; stay away.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov is the sweet spot: Langshiang still thunders at near-monsoon volume while the sand beach re-emerges for tents.

What Nongkhnum Island (Langshiang Falls) is actually like

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Nongkhnum Island (Langshiang Falls)?

October to February is peak season in Nongkhnum Island (Langshiang Falls). March to April and September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to August. Oct-Nov is the sweet spot: Langshiang still thunders at near-monsoon volume while the sand beach re-emerges for tents.

How many days do you need in Nongkhnum Island (Langshiang Falls)?

2 days is the usual stay. Khasi herders and rare Shillong campers; sleep in a simple Nongstoin lodge or camp on the island with a local guide.

How do you get to Nongkhnum Island (Langshiang Falls)?

Shillong to Nongstoin 92 km by shared sumo, then 16 km of rough track; hire wheels locally; no permits. Practical ways in: road.

Is Nongkhnum Island (Langshiang Falls) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
