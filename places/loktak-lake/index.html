Loktak Lake season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Best season: clear water, migratory birds, easiest sangai deer sightings on the phumdis.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March-April still good for boating; by May it turns hot and humid ahead of the rains.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

The lake floods significantly, phumdis break loose and drift, park access often restricted.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October water is still settling post-monsoon, boat trips resume through the month.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Loktak Lake?

November to February is peak season in Loktak Lake. March to April and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to September. October water is still settling post-monsoon, boat trips resume through the month.

How many days do you need in Loktak Lake?

3 days is the usual stay. Quiet stilted fishing villages; check current travel advisories before going.

How do you get to Loktak Lake?

Fly Imphal, 1h drive to Moirang. ILP + PAP; check advisories. Practical ways in: flight and road.

Is Loktak Lake expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
