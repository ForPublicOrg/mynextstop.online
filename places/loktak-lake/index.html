Loktak Lake season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Best season: clear water, migratory birds, easiest sangai deer sightings on the phumdis.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March-April still good for boating; by May it turns hot and humid ahead of the rains.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

The lake floods significantly, phumdis break loose and drift, park access often restricted.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October water is still settling post-monsoon, boat trips resume through the month.

Things to do in Loktak Lake

The local places worth your time in and around Loktak Lake, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Keibul Lamjao National Park

    Wildlife · 12 km out

    The floating park and last home of the sangai deer; climb the Pabot hill watch tower at dawn for any chance of one.

  • Sendra Island

    Viewpoint · 5 km out

    Hillock in the middle of the lake with a tourist lodge and cafe; the rings of floating phumdi read best at sunrise.

  • INA Memorial, Moirang

    Museum · 8 km out

    Where the INA flag first flew on Indian soil in 1944, with a small museum of the Imphal campaign; an hour is enough.

  • Thanga

    Village · 9 km out

    Fishing village of stilt houses on a lake island; the homestays here arrange the two hour boat out among the phumdi.

  • Karang Island

    Island · 13 km out

    The largest island in the lake, reached by shared boat from Thanga; walking the ridge round it takes about two hours.

Getting to Loktak Lake

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Loktak Lake?

November to February is peak season in Loktak Lake. March to April and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to September. October water is still settling post-monsoon, boat trips resume through the month.

How many days do you need in Loktak Lake?

3 days is the usual stay. Quiet stilted fishing villages; check current travel advisories before going.

How do you get to Loktak Lake?

Fly Imphal, 1h drive to Moirang. ILP + PAP; check advisories. Practical ways in: flight and road.

Is Loktak Lake expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

What are the best things to do in Loktak Lake?

Keibul Lamjao National Park, Sendra Island, INA Memorial, Moirang, Thanga and Karang Island. The floating park and last home of the sangai deer; climb the Pabot hill watch tower at dawn for any chance of one.
