Gavi season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Nov-Feb is the window: cool lake mornings, hornbills and gaur on guided walks, clear ridgelines; KFDC beds sell out, book ahead.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March-May is warm but shaded at 1,150 m; wildlife pulls in to the waterholes and plantation tracks stay walkable before the rains.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

June-Aug the reserve gets hammered: leeches on every trail, forest roads slither and the camp sits socked in cloud; wait it out.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Sep is still dripping; October turns lush and misty, the lake brims and midweek you can have the whole camp to yourself.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Gavi?

November to February is peak season in Gavi. March to May and September to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to August. Sep is still dripping; October turns lush and misty, the lake brims and midweek you can have the whole camp to yourself.

How many days do you need in Gavi?

2 days is the usual stay. KFDC swiss tents and the Green Mansions lodge; a handful of guests, forest staff for company, elephants heard after dark.

How do you get to Gavi?

Entry on a KFDC booking: jeep from Vandiperiyar (28 km) or the dawn Pathanamthitta-Kumily KSRTC bus. Practical ways in: road.

Is Gavi expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
