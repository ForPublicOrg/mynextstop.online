Wankaner season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

At 11-30 C the tour makes sense: the clock tower, hunting trophies, and an underground stepwell pool you can swim in.

Summer March to May Shoulder

April-June reaches 43 C on the Machchhu plain, and that subterranean spring-fed pool is genuinely the only relief.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

July-September brings about 500 mm; the Machchhu runs full, the palace grounds green up and room rates are lowest.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October-November is dry and warm, and it pairs with Morbi's tile bazaars 30 km north or Rajkot 40 km back down NH-27.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Wankaner?

November to February is peak season in Wankaner. March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. October-November is dry and warm, and it pairs with Morbi's tile bazaars 30 km north or Rajkot 40 km back down NH-27.

How many days do you need in Wankaner?

1 day is the usual stay. Two palace hotels run by the family and little else; you tour with a retainer and meet no other travellers all day.

How do you get to Wankaner?

Wankaner Junction on the Ahmedabad-Rajkot line; Rajkot 40 km by bus on NH-27, about 1 h. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Wankaner expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
