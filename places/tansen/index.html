Tansen (Palpa) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Fog often fills the valleys at dawn while Srinagar hill sits above it in the sun, with the Himalaya on clear days.

Summer March to May Prime time

Mar is clear and warm for the walk down to Rani Mahal; Apr and May bring haze and hot afternoons.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Steep lanes get slippery and cloud covers the peaks in Jul and Aug, and the Siddhartha Highway can see slides.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Best season: clear views of Dhaulagiri and Annapurna from Srinagar Danda and cool evenings in the bazaar.

What Tansen (Palpa) is actually like

Steep cobbled lanes, Newari sweets and Dhaka topi shops, a few old guesthouses and hardly any tourists.

Things to do in Tansen

The local places worth your time in and around Tansen, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Rani Mahal

    Palace · 7 km out

    1890s palace on the Kali Gandaki built by Khadga Shumsher in memory of his wife; a long walk down or a jeep road.

  • Srinagar Danda

    Viewpoint · 1 km out

    Pine forest hill about 30 min above the bazaar, with a Himalayan panorama on clear mornings.

  • Amar Narayan Temple

    Temple · in town

    Three-tiered pagoda from the early 1800s with carved roof struts, set in a walled garden with stone spouts.

  • Tansen Durbar

    Palace · in town

    Palpa's old governor's palace on the main square, rebuilt after it burned in 2006, with the old town gate nearby.

  • Ridi Bazaar

    Village · 13 km out

    Pilgrim town where the Ridi Khola meets the Kali Gandaki, with the Rishikesh temple and a Makar Sankranti fair.

Getting to Tansen (Palpa)

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Tansen (Palpa)?

February to March and October to December is peak season in Tansen (Palpa). January, April to May and September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to August. Best season: clear views of Dhaulagiri and Annapurna from Srinagar Danda and cool evenings in the bazaar.

How many days do you need in Tansen (Palpa)?

2 days is the usual stay. Steep cobbled lanes, Newari sweets and Dhaka topi shops, a few old guesthouses and hardly any tourists.

How do you get to Tansen (Palpa)?

Bhairahawa airport and the Sunauli border are about 3 h by road via Butwal on the Siddhartha Highway. Practical ways in: flight and road.

Is Tansen (Palpa) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.

What are the best things to do in Tansen?

Rani Mahal, Srinagar Danda, Amar Narayan Temple, Tansen Durbar and Ridi Bazaar. 1890s palace on the Kali Gandaki built by Khadga Shumsher in memory of his wife; a long walk down or a jeep road.
