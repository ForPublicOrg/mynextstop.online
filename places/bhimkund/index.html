Bhimkund (Bajna) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is ideal: mild days for the forest drive to Bajna, and light through the hole in the cave roof turns the still pool a deep blue.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March and April work if you go early, since the cave stays cool though the roads bake; May is brutal Bundelkhand heat, so leave it.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

June is fierce heat then storms; Jul-Aug the forest greens but village roads get muddy, and September is a fair start as the rains ease.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov bring clear water, dry roads and green country around Bajna, the best season to pair the kund with a Khajuraho stay.

What Bhimkund (Bajna) is actually like

No hotels or restaurants nearby, just a few snack stalls; stay in Khajuraho or Chhatarpur and carry food and water.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Bhimkund (Bajna)?

October to February is peak season in Bhimkund (Bajna). March to April and September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to June. Oct-Nov bring clear water, dry roads and green country around Bajna, the best season to pair the kund with a Khajuraho stay.

How many days do you need in Bhimkund (Bajna)?

1 day is the usual stay. No hotels or restaurants nearby, just a few snack stalls; stay in Khajuraho or Chhatarpur and carry food and water.

How do you get to Bhimkund (Bajna)?

Khajuraho airport about 92 km; Chhatarpur railhead about 77 km by road; taxis from either. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Bhimkund (Bajna) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

What are the best things to do in Bhimkund?

Bajna, Jatashankar Dham and Gulganj Fort. Village in Buxwaha tahsil that gives the kund its address; the road to the cave runs through it.
