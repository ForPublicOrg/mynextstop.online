Kalpathy (Palakkad) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is the season for the agraharam rows: 21 C mornings, filter coffee at the corner shop and the Kalpathy river running low and clear under the weir.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March warms fast and Apr-May push past 40 C through the Palakkad gap; the lanes empty between 11 am and 4 pm, so this is a dawn and dusk town.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun-Sep the rain is heavy but the tiled roofs and deep verandahs were built for it; the river fills and the streets are at their greenest and quietest.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October cools things off and mid November brings Ratholsavam, when chariots too big for the lanes are hauled through them. Book a bed weeks ahead.

Signature festival

Nov: Kalpathy Ratholsavam, three wooden chariots pulled through the streets

Things to do in Kalpathy

The local places worth your time in and around Kalpathy, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Kalpathy Viswanatha Swamy Temple

    Temple · in town

    Shiva temple on the Kalpathy river at the head of the agraharams; its chariot festival, the Ratholsavam, falls in November

  • Jainimedu Jain Temple

    Temple · 2 km out

    15th century Jain temple built by diamond merchants from Karnataka, dedicated to the Tirthankara Chandraprabha

  • Palakkad Fort

    Fort · 4 km out

    Fort in the middle of town, built in its present form in 1766 under Hyder Ali and taken by the British in 1790

  • Malampuzha Dam

    Reservoir · 8 km out

    Dam at the foot of the Western Ghats with gardens around the reservoir, boating, a ropeway and a rock garden

  • Dhoni Waterfalls

    Waterfall · 10 km out

    Guided 4 km uphill forest trek to the falls; buy tickets at the forest office at the foothills and ask the batch times

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Kalpathy (Palakkad)?

November to February is peak season in Kalpathy (Palakkad). March and September to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. No month is a write-off here. October cools things off and mid November brings Ratholsavam, when chariots too big for the lanes are hauled through them. Book a bed weeks ahead.

How many days do you need in Kalpathy (Palakkad)?

2 days is the usual stay. Heritage houses taking guests on the agraharam streets, temple mess meals, shutters down by 9 pm; town traffic stays 2 km away.

How do you get to Kalpathy (Palakkad)?

Palakkad Junction railhead 5 km by autorickshaw, Coimbatore airport 55 km east through the gap. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Kalpathy (Palakkad) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.

Is there a festival in Kalpathy (Palakkad)?

Nov: Kalpathy Ratholsavam, three wooden chariots pulled through the streets. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Kalpathy?

Kalpathy Viswanatha Swamy Temple, Jainimedu Jain Temple, Palakkad Fort, Malampuzha Dam and Dhoni Waterfalls. Shiva temple on the Kalpathy river at the head of the agraharams; its chariot festival, the Ratholsavam, falls in November.
