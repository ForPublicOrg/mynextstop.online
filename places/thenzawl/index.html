Thenzawl (Vantawng Falls) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is dry and easy in the weaving sheds, but Vantawng thins to two white threads down its 200 m cliff.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March-April is hazy with jhum smoke and the fall is at its weakest; from May the Aizawl road starts washing out.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Jun-Sep the fall is huge and useless at once, because the gorge fills with cloud and the viewing tower sees nothing.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is the month it lines up: full post-monsoon flow, clear gorge air and the deer park green after the rain.

Things to do in Thenzawl

The local places worth your time in and around Thenzawl, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Chawngchilhi Puk

    Cave · 10 km out

    Cliff cave tied to the Mizo legend of the girl and the serpent; take a torch and a local pointer, the path is unmarked

  • Thenzawl Golf Resort

    Activity · 5 km out

    A full course cut into the hills outside town and rarely busy; day green fees and club hire at the clubhouse desk

  • Thenzawl Deer Park

    Wildlife · 6 km out

    Forest department enclosure of sambar and barking deer in a wooded valley; early morning is the only time they are out

  • Serchhip

    Village · 25 km out

    District town up the road with the nearest bank, pharmacy and sumo counters for Aizawl; useful rather than worth a look

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Thenzawl (Vantawng Falls)?

October to December is peak season in Thenzawl (Vantawng Falls). January to April is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to September. October is the month it lines up: full post-monsoon flow, clear gorge air and the deer park green after the rain.

How many days do you need in Thenzawl (Vantawng Falls)?

1 day is the usual stay. Weaver-run guesthouses and a state lodge; day visitors from Aizawl at the tower, no traveller scene at all.

How do you get to Thenzawl (Vantawng Falls)?

Shared Sumo from Aizawl, 85 km and 3 h; fall viewing tower 5 km on; ILP for non-Mizoram Indians. Practical ways in: road.

Is Thenzawl (Vantawng Falls) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

What are the best things to do in Thenzawl?

Chawngchilhi Puk, Thenzawl Golf Resort, Thenzawl Deer Park and Serchhip. Cliff cave tied to the Mizo legend of the girl and the serpent; take a torch and a local pointer, the path is unmarked.
