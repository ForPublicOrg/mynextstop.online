Bhairabkunda season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb the river runs clear and shallow over white stones and Bhutan's ridges stand sharp; weekend picnic parties fill the banks, weekdays are quiet

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar-Apr are warm and green with the planted forest full of birdsong and the river still wadeable; by May the heat passes 32 C and the storms begin

Monsoon June to September Quiet, but open

Jun-Sep the Jampani comes down in spate, the sandbanks go under and the camp shuts; the hills vanish into cloud, so leave it for autumn

Autumn October to November Prime time

October dries the tracks and the eco camp reopens; November is the best of it, cool clear dawns and the river back to a swimming stream

Signature festival

Jan: Bhairabkunda Tourism Festival by the river

What Bhairabkunda is actually like

A community eco camp with tents and a guest house in the planted forest, Bodo and Nepali villages; picnickers by day, quiet nights

Things to do in Bhairabkunda

The local places worth your time in and around Bhairabkunda, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Bhairabkunda Shiva Mandir

    Temple · in town

    Shiva temple at the river meeting point, just inside Bhutan; pilgrims bathe at the confluence and offer puja at Makar Sankranti.

  • Jia Dhansiri River

    River · in town

    Forms here where the Jampani from Bhutan meets the Bhairabi; picnic crowds fill the banks from November to early February.

  • Gethsemane Man-made Forest

    Park · 3 km out

    Forest of about 6 sq km planted by a group of 35 Bodo youths, now with over a million trees of 35 species.

  • Barnadi Wildlife Sanctuary

    Wildlife · 52 km out

    26 sq km sanctuary set up in 1980 for the pygmy hog and hispid hare, on the Udalguri and Baksa border to the west.

Getting to Bhairabkunda

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Bhairabkunda?

November to February is peak season in Bhairabkunda. March to April and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. No month is a write-off here. October dries the tracks and the eco camp reopens; November is the best of it, cool clear dawns and the river back to a swimming stream.

How many days do you need in Bhairabkunda?

2 days is the usual stay. A community eco camp with tents and a guest house in the planted forest, Bodo and Nepali villages; picnickers by day, quiet nights.

How do you get to Bhairabkunda?

Train or bus to Udalguri or Tangla, then 24 km by shared sumo or taxi; from Guwahati about 4 h by road via Mangaldai. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Bhairabkunda expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

Is there a festival in Bhairabkunda?

Jan: Bhairabkunda Tourism Festival by the river. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Bhairabkunda?

Bhairabkunda Shiva Mandir, Jia Dhansiri River, Gethsemane Man-made Forest and Barnadi Wildlife Sanctuary. Shiva temple at the river meeting point, just inside Bhutan; pilgrims bathe at the confluence and offer puja at Makar Sankranti.
