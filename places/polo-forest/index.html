Polo Forest (Vijaynagar) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb mornings drop near 8 °C in the valley, good for camping and birding, though the forest browns off by February.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-Jun bakes at 42 °C, the forest goes to grey scrub and the ruins offer no shade. There is nothing to come for.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Jul-Aug is dramatic, but the river crossings towards the Sharneshwar temple flood and forest tracks turn to mud.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Sep-Nov is why you come: 15th-century ruins in wet green forest, water still running, 20 °C nights under canvas.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Polo Forest (Vijaynagar)?

September to December is peak season in Polo Forest (Vijaynagar). January to March and July to August is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Jul-Aug is dramatic, but the river crossings towards the Sharneshwar temple flood and forest tracks turn to mud.

How many days do you need in Polo Forest (Vijaynagar)?

2 days is the usual stay. Weekend campers from Ahmedabad and nobody midweek; tented camps and a forest rest house, no cafes.

How do you get to Polo Forest (Vijaynagar)?

Himatnagar railhead 70 km, ST bus to Vijaynagar then a jeep to Abhapur; the last bus back leaves early. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Polo Forest (Vijaynagar) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
