Kyanjin Gompa (Langtang) season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Snow on the trail and nights far below freezing; Dec still has clear views, but in Jan few lodges stay open.

Summer March to May Prime time

Rhododendron forests bloom in Mar and Apr, and mornings are clear for Kyanjin Ri; May brings afternoon cloud.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Landslide risk is real in this valley, trails are muddy and the peaks stay hidden; most trekkers skip Jun to Aug.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Best time: clear Langtang Lirung views, yak pastures turning gold and fresh yak cheese at the factory.

What Kyanjin Gompa (Langtang) is actually like

Tamang lodges rebuilt after the 2015 quake, yak cheese and apple pie, and small trekking groups with guides.

Things to do in Kyanjin Gompa

The local places worth your time in and around Kyanjin Gompa, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Kyanjin Ri

    Viewpoint · 2 km out

    4773 m hill above the village, a 3 to 4 h return climb at dawn for a full view of Langtang Lirung.

  • Tserko Ri

    Trek · 3 km out

    Harder 4984 m summit east of Kyanjin, a long day hike with the widest glacier panorama in the valley.

  • Kyanjin Cheese Factory

    Food · in town

    Yak cheese factory set up in the 1950s with Swiss help; buy a slab of hard cheese to carry back down.

  • Langtang

    Village · 6 km out

    Village rebuilt above the site buried by the 2015 avalanche, with a memorial chorten beside the trail.

  • Lama Hotel

    Village · 15 km out

    Forest teahouse stop by the Langtang Khola, where red pandas are sometimes seen, a day above Syabrubesi.

Getting to Kyanjin Gompa (Langtang)

Kathmandu to Syabrubesi by jeep or bus in 7 to 8 h, then 3 days on foot. Langtang National Park permit needed.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Kyanjin Gompa (Langtang)?

March to April and October to November is peak season in Kyanjin Gompa (Langtang). February, May, September and December is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid January and June to August. Rhododendron forests bloom in Mar and Apr, and mornings are clear for Kyanjin Ri; May brings afternoon cloud.

How many days do you need in Kyanjin Gompa (Langtang)?

6 days is the usual stay. Tamang lodges rebuilt after the 2015 quake, yak cheese and apple pie, and small trekking groups with guides.

How do you get to Kyanjin Gompa (Langtang)?

Kathmandu to Syabrubesi by jeep or bus in 7 to 8 h, then 3 days on foot. Langtang National Park permit needed. Practical ways in: road.

Is Kyanjin Gompa (Langtang) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Kyanjin Gompa?

Kyanjin Ri, Tserko Ri, Kyanjin Cheese Factory, Langtang and Lama Hotel. 4773 m hill above the village, a 3 to 4 h return climb at dawn for a full view of Langtang Lirung.
