Mumbai season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Best weather all year - dry and mild, ideal for walking Colaba and the Fort district.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Hot, humid and sticky before the rains - the most draining time to sightsee on foot.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Heavy rain floods streets and stalls local trains - dramatic but genuinely disruptive.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Ganpati energy fills September; October settles into pleasant post-monsoon weather.

Signature festival

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Mumbai?

November to February is peak season in Mumbai. March and September to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to August. Ganpati energy fills September; October settles into pleasant post-monsoon weather.

How many days do you need in Mumbai?

2 days is the usual stay. Big anonymous metro - Colaba/Bandra hostels fill with a young backpacker and expat crowd.

How do you get to Mumbai?

Fly into Mumbai (BOM) or overnight train from anywhere in the country. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Mumbai expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.

Is there a festival in Mumbai?

Aug/Sep: Ganesh Chaturthi (Ganpati Visarjan) - huge processions. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
