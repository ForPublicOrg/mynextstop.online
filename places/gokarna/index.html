Gokarna season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Calm sea, full shack scene across Om, Kudle and Paradise beaches, best weather of the year.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Hot and humid, sea still swimmable but the shoulder-season charm is gone.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Sea turns rough and brown, most shacks shut, rip currents make swimming dangerous.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October's a hidden gem - cliffs still green, sea calming, a fraction of the December crowd.

Signature festival

Things to do in Gokarna

The local places worth your time in and around Gokarna, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Mahabaleshwar Temple

    Temple · in town

    The Atmalinga temple the town grew around; men enter bare chested, no phones or cameras, morning darshan is the calmest.

  • Kudle Beach

    Beach · 3 km out

    First beach south of town, about 20 minutes over the headland on foot; shacks and yoga decks, strong rips through the monsoon.

  • Om Beach

    Beach · 6 km out

    Two bays shaped like an om and the busiest of the strip; auto from town or the cliff path from Kudle, seafood shacks at sunset.

  • Half Moon Beach

    Beach · 7 km out

    Small cove past Om reached by a 30 minute cliff path or a boat; a handful of huts, carry cash, there is no ATM past town.

  • Paradise Beach

    Beach · 9 km out

    Last cove on the trail, clear water between rocks; camping was cleared out years ago, come for the day and boat back before dark.

  • Mirjan Fort

    Fort · 21 km out

    Laterite fort with a moat and overgrown stairways off the Kumta road; quiet on weekday mornings, any Kumta bus drops you at the turn.

  • Koti Tirtha

    Heritage · in town

    Temple tank in the middle of town where pilgrims perform rites for the dead; watch from the steps, no swimming, best light early.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Gokarna?

November to February is peak season in Gokarna. March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to September. October's a hidden gem - cliffs still green, sea calming, a fraction of the December crowd.

How many days do you need in Gokarna?

3 days is the usual stay. Backpacker beach-hop scene close to Goa's energy but mellower - easy to find a crew.

How do you get to Gokarna?

Train to Gokarna Rd, or fly Goa/Hubli then 2-3h taxi. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Gokarna expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Made for solo travellers.

Is there a festival in Gokarna?

Feb/Mar: Shivaratri car festival at Mahabaleshwar Temple. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Gokarna?

Mahabaleshwar Temple, Kudle Beach, Om Beach, Half Moon Beach, Paradise Beach and Mirjan Fort, and more. The Atmalinga temple the town grew around; men enter bare chested, no phones or cameras, morning darshan is the calmest.
