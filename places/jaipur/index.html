Jaipur season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Peak season: crisp mornings, clear fort views, and the biggest crowds of the year.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Brutal heat past 42C by April; sightsee only at dawn/dusk, or skip it entirely.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Cooler and green with the Teej festival in August, though midday heat and humidity persist.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Warm days easing into pleasant nights. A good transition before the winter rush.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Jaipur?

November to February is peak season in Jaipur. March and July to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Warm days easing into pleasant nights. A good transition before the winter rush.

How many days do you need in Jaipur?

2 days is the usual stay. Hostel-heavy and social: easiest Rajasthan city to instantly find a travel group.

How do you get to Jaipur?

Fly into Jaipur (Sanganer) or 4.5h train/bus from Delhi. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Jaipur expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.

Is there a festival in Jaipur?

Jan: Jaipur Literature Festival. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
