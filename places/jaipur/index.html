Jaipur season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Peak season: crisp mornings, clear fort views, and the biggest crowds of the year.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Brutal heat past 42C by April; sightsee only at dawn/dusk, or skip it entirely.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Cooler and green with the Teej festival in August, though midday heat and humidity persist.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Warm days easing into pleasant nights. A good transition before the winter rush.

Things to do in Jaipur

The local places worth your time in and around Jaipur, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Amber Fort

    Fort · 11 km out

    Kachhwaha hill palace with the mirrored Sheesh Mahal; be at the gate by 8 am to beat the buses, and walk up rather than ride

  • City Palace

    Palace · in town

    Still the maharaja's home, with the four painted doorways of Pritam Niwas Chowk; the basic ticket leaves out Chandra Mahal

  • Hawa Mahal

    Heritage · in town

    1799 honeycomb screen of 953 windows; shoot it from the cafes opposite, the way in is the lane behind, east light at 8 am

  • Jantar Mantar

    Heritage · in town

    Jai Singh's 1734 stone observatory with a 27 m sundial accurate to seconds; take a guide, the instruments explain nothing

  • Nahargarh Fort

    Viewpoint · 6 km out

    Ridge fort above the city, the standard sunset over Jaipur; get up by 5 pm and drive down, the step path is unlit after dark

  • Panna Meena ka Kund

    Heritage · 11 km out

    Symmetrical stepwell of crossing stairs five minutes below Amber; free to see, and climbing on the steps is not allowed

  • Johari Bazaar

    Market · in town

    Old city street for jewellery, lehariya and sweets; most shops shut Sunday, and LMB here is the standard thali stop

  • Albert Hall Museum

    Museum · 2 km out

    Indo Saracenic museum in Ram Niwas garden, carpets, armour and an Egyptian mummy; open till 5 pm, lit up again after 7

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Jaipur?

November to February is peak season in Jaipur. March and July to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Warm days easing into pleasant nights. A good transition before the winter rush.

How many days do you need in Jaipur?

2 days is the usual stay. Hostel-heavy and social: easiest Rajasthan city to instantly find a travel group.

How do you get to Jaipur?

Fly into Jaipur (Sanganer) or 4.5h train/bus from Delhi. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Jaipur expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.

Is there a festival in Jaipur?

Jan: Jaipur Literature Festival. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Jaipur?

Amber Fort, City Palace, Hawa Mahal, Jantar Mantar, Nahargarh Fort and Panna Meena ka Kund, and more. Kachhwaha hill palace with the mirrored Sheesh Mahal; be at the gate by 8 am to beat the buses, and walk up rather than ride.
