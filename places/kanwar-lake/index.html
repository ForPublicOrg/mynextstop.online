Kanwar Lake (Kabar Taal) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is what it is for: bar-headed geese, pintail, coot and marsh harriers on the open water, a fisherman's boat at dawn from Manjhaul for a few hundred rupees

Summer March to May Shoulder

By March the ducks leave and the water shrinks to channels among wheat and makhana fields; April is thin and May bakes at 43 C over a mostly dry bed, so wait

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

June is hot over a shrunken bed; from Jul the Burhi Gandak floods the basin back into one sheet of water, lotus and makhana in leaf, jacanas and storks, no migrants

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Oct is warm and green with the first waders; by late Nov the migrants are in and the light is soft, a good window before winter fog shuts down mornings

What Kanwar Lake (Kabar Taal) is actually like

Fishermen and makhana farmers, no tourist trade; a basic lodge in Manjhaul or hotels in Begusarai 22 km south, evenings very quiet

Getting to Kanwar Lake (Kabar Taal)

Begusarai station (Patna 3h by train), then bus or shared auto 22 km north to Manjhaul; a hired car from Patna is about 3h

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Kanwar Lake (Kabar Taal)?

December to February is peak season in Kanwar Lake (Kabar Taal). March and November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to June. Dec-Feb is what it is for: bar-headed geese, pintail, coot and marsh harriers on the open water, a fisherman's boat at dawn from Manjhaul for a few hundred rupees.

How many days do you need in Kanwar Lake (Kabar Taal)?

1 day is the usual stay. Fishermen and makhana farmers, no tourist trade; a basic lodge in Manjhaul or hotels in Begusarai 22 km south, evenings very quiet.

How do you get to Kanwar Lake (Kabar Taal)?

Begusarai station (Patna 3h by train), then bus or shared auto 22 km north to Manjhaul; a hired car from Patna is about 3h. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Kanwar Lake (Kabar Taal) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
