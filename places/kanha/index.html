Kanha National Park season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Crisp sal-forest mornings, mist and good tiger and barasingha sightings. Best season.

Summer March to May Shoulder

40C+ by April-June, but wildlife concentrates at waterholes. Good sightings if you can bear it.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Core zone closed 1 July-30 September for the breeding season. Forest lush but off-limits.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Reopens 1 October, sal forest still green from rains, quieter than the Dec-Jan rush.

What Kanha National Park is actually like

Naturalist lodges: bonfire chat about sightings, no backpacker or nightlife scene.

Things to do in Kanha National Park

The local places worth your time in and around Kanha National Park, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Kanha Meadows

    Wildlife · 22 km out

    The central grassland where the hard ground barasingha herd grazes; morning drives halt here, first light is the time.

  • Bamni Dadar

    Viewpoint · 25 km out

    The sunset hill you are allowed out of the jeep on, looking over sal forest; only on an afternoon drive in the Kanha zone.

  • Shravan Tal

    Lake · 20 km out

    Forest pool in the Kanha zone tied to the Shravan Kumar story; a standard safari halt, good for gaur and sambar.

  • Kanha Museum

    Museum · 1 km out

    Forest department interpretation centre at Khatia gate with maps, skulls and a film; free, good for the non safari half day.

  • Khatia Buffer Trail

    Walk · 1 km out

    Guided morning walk in the buffer forest, booked at Khatia gate; no tigers to speak of, the birding is the point.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Kanha National Park?

November to February is peak season in Kanha National Park. March to June and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid July to September. Reopens 1 October, sal forest still green from rains, quieter than the Dec-Jan rush.

How many days do you need in Kanha National Park?

3 days is the usual stay. Naturalist lodges: bonfire chat about sightings, no backpacker or nightlife scene.

How do you get to Kanha National Park?

Fly to Jabalpur (165km) or Nagpur (250km), then taxi. Practical ways in: flight and road.

Is Kanha National Park expensive, and does it get crowded?

Splurge by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

What are the best things to do in Kanha National Park?

Kanha Meadows, Bamni Dadar, Shravan Tal, Kanha Museum and Khatia Buffer Trail. The central grassland where the hard ground barasingha herd grazes; morning drives halt here, first light is the time.
