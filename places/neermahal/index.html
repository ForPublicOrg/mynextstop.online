Neermahal & Rudrasagar Lake (Melaghar) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Nov-Feb is the season at 12-27 C, with migratory waterfowl on Rudrasagar and the palace lit up across the water.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March is still pleasant, but by April and May it is 36 C and humid on an open boat with no shade to speak of.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Do not come Jun-Sep: 210-410 mm of rain a month, an open unroofed boat as the only way to the palace, and no waterfowl left.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is warm and quiet with the lake at its fullest, and rooms at the lakeside lodge are easy to come by.

Things to do in Neermahal & Rudrasagar Lake

The local places worth your time in and around Neermahal & Rudrasagar Lake, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Sepahijala Wildlife Sanctuary

    Wildlife · 18 km out

    Clouded leopard enclosure and free ranging spectacled langurs in a rubber forest; langurs show early, gates shut by 4 pm

  • Tripura Sundari Temple (Matabari)

    Temple · 25 km out

    Shakti peetha on a mound at Udaipur, red brick with a turtle tank; goat sacrifice happens here, skip it if that troubles you

  • Bhuvaneswari Temple (Udaipur)

    Temple · 27 km out

    Little Gomati bank temple beside the old palace ruin, the setting of Tagore's Bisarjan; ten minutes, free, rarely busy

  • Gunabati Group of Temples

    Heritage · 27 km out

    Three tall brick temples of the 1660s in a field outside Udaipur; ASI protected, no ticket and almost no visitors

  • Kamalasagar Kali Temple

    Temple · 30 km out

    Kali temple above a big tank right at the Bangladesh fence, border posts in view; a large fair fills the bank each April

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Neermahal & Rudrasagar Lake (Melaghar)?

November to February is peak season in Neermahal & Rudrasagar Lake (Melaghar). March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to September. October is warm and quiet with the lake at its fullest, and rooms at the lakeside lodge are easy to come by.

How many days do you need in Neermahal & Rudrasagar Lake (Melaghar)?

1 day is the usual stay. Agartala day-trippers clear out by evening, and the lakeside tourist lodge lets you stay for the lit palace.

How do you get to Neermahal & Rudrasagar Lake (Melaghar)?

Agartala 53 km, buses to Melaghar about 1.5 h; Agartala airport 60 km; boats run from the ghat till dusk. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Neermahal & Rudrasagar Lake (Melaghar) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Neermahal & Rudrasagar Lake?

Sepahijala Wildlife Sanctuary, Tripura Sundari Temple (Matabari), Bhuvaneswari Temple (Udaipur), Gunabati Group of Temples and Kamalasagar Kali Temple. Clouded leopard enclosure and free ranging spectacled langurs in a rubber forest; langurs show early, gates shut by 4 pm.
