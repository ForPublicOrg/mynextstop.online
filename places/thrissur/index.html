Thrissur season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is dry at 22-33°C and best for Vadakkumnathan at dusk, the Sakthan market and a day out at Kerala Kalamandalam.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Late April or early May brings Pooram: 36°C, a 36-hour siege of elephants and melam on the maidan, and no rooms left.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

June-July drowns Thrissur under 700 mm a month; the temple grounds turn to mud and the festival calendar is finished.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct is wet with retreating monsoon storms; November clears up and the temple-festival season begins to fill again.

Signature festival

Apr/May: Thrissur Pooram at Vadakkumnathan

Things to do in Thrissur

The local places worth your time in and around Thrissur, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Vadakkunnathan Temple

    Temple · in town

    The Shiva temple on the wooded mound at the centre of town, and the Pooram ground; Hindus only inside, men without shirts.

  • Our Lady of Dolours Basilica

    Church · 1 km out

    Puthan Pally, the tall Indo Gothic basilica; the Bible Tower beside it has a lift up to a view over the whole town, small ticket.

  • Shakthan Thampuran Palace

    Palace · 1 km out

    Late 1700s palace of Dutch lines, now a museum of bronzes with megalithic burial jars in the garden; closed Mondays, allow an hour.

  • Kerala Kalamandalam

    Heritage · 30 km out

    The arts university at Cheruthuruthy; the Day with the Masters tour sits you in on kathakali and mizhavu classes, book ahead.

  • Peechi Dam

    Reservoir · 22 km out

    Reservoir and garden at the edge of a sanctuary, with boating on the water; buses from the Round take an hour, mobbed on Sundays.

  • Paramekkavu Bhagavathy Temple

    Temple · 1 km out

    One of the two temples that stage Thrissur Pooram in April or May; the big fireworks go off around three in the morning.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Thrissur?

November to February is peak season in Thrissur. March to April is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to July. Oct is wet with retreating monsoon storms; November clears up and the temple-festival season begins to fill again.

How many days do you need in Thrissur?

2 days is the usual stay. A temple-and-drum town of percussion shops and Kalamandalam students, with cheap lodges right around the Round.

How do you get to Thrissur?

Thrissur station sits on the Shoranur-Ernakulam main line; Kochi airport 55 km, state buses from the Round. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Thrissur expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Thrissur?

Apr/May: Thrissur Pooram at Vadakkumnathan. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Thrissur?

Vadakkunnathan Temple, Our Lady of Dolours Basilica, Shakthan Thampuran Palace, Kerala Kalamandalam, Peechi Dam and Paramekkavu Bhagavathy Temple. The Shiva temple on the wooded mound at the centre of town, and the Pooram ground; Hindus only inside, men without shirts.
