Touphema Village season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is cold and clear at 3-18 C: morung-style huts, kitchen fires, rice beer and Sekrenyi feasting in late February.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar-Apr is mild with blossom on the terraces below the hill; May turns humid as the first hard showers arrive.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

Jun-Aug the hut verandas stare into fog while side roads off the Wokha highway chew up; damp, empty, skip it.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov is harvest gold on the terraces and the run-up to Hornbill; book ahead, December overflow fills the huts.

Signature festival

Feb: Sekrenyi celebrated in the village (late Feb)

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Touphema Village?

November to February is peak season in Touphema Village. March to April and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to August. Oct-Nov is harvest gold on the terraces and the run-up to Hornbill; book ahead, December overflow fills the huts.

How many days do you need in Touphema Village?

2 days is the usual stay. Community-run huts with home food and rice beer; a handful of guests, mostly Hornbill-season overflow from Kohima.

How do you get to Touphema Village?

ILP online; Dimapur airport-railhead to Kohima 3 h, then 41 km north on the Wokha road, about 1.5 h more. Practical ways in: road.

Is Touphema Village expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Touphema Village?

Feb: Sekrenyi celebrated in the village (late Feb). Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
