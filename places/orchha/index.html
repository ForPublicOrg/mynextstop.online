Orchha season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Cool mornings on Betwa ghats, cafes full of long-stay backpackers. The best season.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Sandstone cenotaphs turn to heat traps, 44C+ by May; only dawn walks are bearable.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Betwa swells, reflection pools turn dramatic; rain keeps crowds away, humid though.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Weather breaks by mid-October, river still full, temples glow at sunset: quieter lead-in.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Orchha?

October to February is peak season in Orchha. March and July to September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Weather breaks by mid-October, river still full, temples glow at sunset: quieter lead-in.

How many days do you need in Orchha?

2 days is the usual stay. Laid-back riverside stopover: rooftop cafes and easy chats with fellow travellers.

How do you get to Orchha?

Train/flight to Jhansi (16km), then auto or local bus. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Orchha expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.
