Champakulam (Kuttanad) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is the dry window: 30 C days, a punt boat through the canals at dawn and paddy being cut a metre below sea level behind the bunds.

Summer March to May Quiet, but open

Mar-May turn humid and the canals drop; the Thanneermukkom barrage holds the salt back, so the water still works if you go out before 8 am.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun-Sep the fields flood, race crews train at dusk and the village smells of wet paddy; in a bad year Kuttanad goes under and the buses stop.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov the fields drain, the light turns clean and canal traffic goes back to being purely local once the Onam race season is over.

Signature festival

Jun/Jul: Champakulam Moolam Vallamkali, the oldest snake boat race

What Champakulam (Kuttanad) is actually like

Homestays on the bund and country boat families who take you out; evenings are toddy shops, church bells and no houseboat music

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Champakulam (Kuttanad)?

November to February is peak season in Champakulam (Kuttanad). June to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. No month is a write-off here. Oct-Nov the fields drain, the light turns clean and canal traffic goes back to being purely local once the Onam race season is over.

How many days do you need in Champakulam (Kuttanad)?

2 days is the usual stay. Homestays on the bund and country boat families who take you out; evenings are toddy shops, church bells and no houseboat music.

How do you get to Champakulam (Kuttanad)?

Alappuzha 16 km by bus or about 2h on the state ferry up the Pamba, Kottayam railhead 30 km east. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Champakulam (Kuttanad) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Champakulam (Kuttanad)?

Jun/Jul: Champakulam Moolam Vallamkali, the oldest snake boat race. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
