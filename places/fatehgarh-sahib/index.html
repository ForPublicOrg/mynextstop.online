Fatehgarh Sahib (Sirhind) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Late Dec is when lakhs arrive for the Jor Mela; the rest of Dec-Feb is 5-20 C, foggy and near-deserted.

Summer March to May Prime time

March still suits the Aam Khas Bagh ruins, but Apr-Jun runs 40-44 C over open brick with no shade at all.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

Skip Jul-Aug: 34 C and sticky, the Sirhind ruins go weedy and the Rauza Sharif courtyard turns to puddles.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct is still warm at 32 C; Nov cools nicely but early-Nov stubble smoke off the Malwa fields can be thick.

Signature festival

Dec: Shaheedi Jor Mela (around 25-27 Dec)

Things to do in Fatehgarh Sahib

The local places worth your time in and around Fatehgarh Sahib, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Gurdwara Fatehgarh Sahib

    Gurudwara · in town

    Where Guru Gobind Singh's youngest sons were bricked into a wall in 1705; free sarai beds and langar, packed for the December mela.

  • Rauza Sharif

    Shrine · 1 km out

    Dargah of Sheikh Ahmad Sirhindi, drawing Naqshbandi pilgrims from as far as Afghanistan; quiet on weekdays, heads covered.

  • Aam Khas Bagh

    Heritage · 2 km out

    Mughal halting garden on the Grand Trunk Road with a hamam and Sheesh Mahal ruins; free, unlabelled, best in early light.

  • Gurdwara Jyoti Sarup

    Gurudwara · 2 km out

    The cremation spot of the two sahibzadas and Mata Gujri, out towards Sirhind town; five minutes by auto, part of the mela route.

  • Diwan Todar Mal's Haveli

    Heritage · 1 km out

    Shell of the haveli of the merchant who bought the cremation ground with gold coins; a brick ruin behind a fence, ten minutes.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Fatehgarh Sahib (Sirhind)?

November to March is peak season in Fatehgarh Sahib (Sirhind). October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to August. Oct is still warm at 32 C; Nov cools nicely but early-Nov stubble smoke off the Malwa fields can be thick.

How many days do you need in Fatehgarh Sahib (Sirhind)?

1 day is the usual stay. Sarai beds at the gurdwara and langar all day; a working pilgrim town, not a tourist one.

How do you get to Fatehgarh Sahib (Sirhind)?

Sirhind Junction on the Delhi-Amritsar main line, 5 km out; Chandigarh 55 km by frequent bus. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Fatehgarh Sahib (Sirhind) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.

Is there a festival in Fatehgarh Sahib (Sirhind)?

Dec: Shaheedi Jor Mela (around 25-27 Dec). Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Fatehgarh Sahib?

Gurdwara Fatehgarh Sahib, Rauza Sharif, Aam Khas Bagh, Gurdwara Jyoti Sarup and Diwan Todar Mal's Haveli. Where Guru Gobind Singh's youngest sons were bricked into a wall in 1705; free sarai beds and langar, packed for the December mela.
