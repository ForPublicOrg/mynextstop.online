Aranmula season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is dry at 22-33°C: the Parthasarathy temple at dawn, the mirror workshops open to visitors and the Pamba low and clear.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar-May pushes 36°C and the Pamba shrinks to sandbanks, leaving the metal-mirror and Vastuvidya workshops as the only reason to come.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Rain is constant through June and July, but four days after Thiruvonam the Pamba carries dozens of snake boats and the village fills.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Oct-Nov brings north-east monsoon evenings; mornings stay clear, the paddy is green and there is nobody else around.

Signature festival

Aug/Sep: Aranmula Uthrattathi snake boat race

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Aranmula?

December to February is peak season in Aranmula. March and August to November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to July. Dec-Feb is dry at 22-33°C: the Parthasarathy temple at dawn, the mirror workshops open to visitors and the Pamba low and clear.

How many days do you need in Aranmula?

1 day is the usual stay. A working temple village of boat-club sheds and mirror-makers; homestays only here, hotels 10 km away in Chengannur.

How do you get to Aranmula?

Chengannur station 10 km on the main line, then an auto; Kochi airport 100 km, Trivandrum airport 110 km. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Aranmula expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Aranmula?

Aug/Sep: Aranmula Uthrattathi snake boat race. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
