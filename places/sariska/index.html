Sariska Tiger Reserve season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Best combination of cool, comfortable safaris and thinning vegetation for sightings.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Hot, but animals cluster at shrinking waterholes, which can mean better tiger and leopard sightings.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Core zone closed 1 Jul-30 Sep for the breeding season; safaris pause and June turns punishing anyway.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Reopens in October, but tall grass from the monsoon limits visibility until deeper winter.

What Sariska Tiger Reserve is actually like

Safari-lodge crowd is mostly domestic families; sociable at breakfast, not a hangout.

Things to do in Sariska Tiger Reserve

The local places worth your time in and around Sariska Tiger Reserve, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Pandupol Hanuman Temple

    Temple · 22 km out

    Temple in a gorge deep inside the reserve with a waterfall behind it; pilgrim buses run in on Tuesdays and Saturdays.

  • Kankwari Fort

    Fort · 20 km out

    Square fort on a plateau in the core where Aurangzeb jailed Dara Shikoh; jeep only and a separate permit at the gate.

  • Neelkanth Mahadev Temple

    Heritage · 32 km out

    Cluster of 9th century carved temple ruins on the reserve's south edge; a rough hour by road, bring your own vehicle.

  • Bhartrihari Temple

    Shrine · 10 km out

    Shrine to the king who turned ascetic, busy with sadhus and pilgrims; the September fair fills the road for days.

  • Sariska Palace

    Heritage · 1 km out

    The Alwar maharaja's hunting lodge by the park gate, tangled in litigation and mostly shut; a look from outside only.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Sariska Tiger Reserve?

December to February is peak season in Sariska Tiger Reserve. March to May and October to November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. Best combination of cool, comfortable safaris and thinning vegetation for sightings.

How many days do you need in Sariska Tiger Reserve?

2 days is the usual stay. Safari-lodge crowd is mostly domestic families; sociable at breakfast, not a hangout.

How do you get to Sariska Tiger Reserve?

2.5h drive from Delhi or 1h from Alwar train station. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Sariska Tiger Reserve expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

What are the best things to do in Sariska Tiger Reserve?

Pandupol Hanuman Temple, Kankwari Fort, Neelkanth Mahadev Temple, Bhartrihari Temple and Sariska Palace. Temple in a gorge deep inside the reserve with a waterfall behind it; pilgrim buses run in on Tuesdays and Saturdays.
