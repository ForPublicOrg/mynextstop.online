Mysuru season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Cool and clear, palace lit up nightly, Dasara processions if dates align in October.

Summer March to May Do not go

Dry heat climbs to 36-38C by April-May; sightseeing is fine but midday is rough.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

A rain-shadow pocket keeps monsoon mild here - overcast, green, none of the coastal chaos.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Dasara runs through October - elephants, illuminated palace, huge crowds but unmissable.

Signature festival

Oct: Mysuru Dasara - ten days of processions and palace illumination

Things to do in Mysuru

The local places worth your time in and around Mysuru, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Mysore Palace

    Palace · in town

    Amba Vilas, the Wodeyar seat; shoes off at the gate, and the whole facade is lit 7 to 7.45 pm on Sundays

  • Chamundi Hill

    Temple · 13 km out

    Chamundeshwari temple above the city, 1008 steps past the monolithic Nandi; city buses run up all day

  • Devaraja Market

    Market · in town

    Old covered rows of flowers, bananas and kumkum cones; go before 10 am and expect the first price to halve

  • Srirangapatna

    Heritage · 16 km out

    Tipu's island capital: Ranganathaswamy temple, Daria Daulat and the Gumbaz; any Bengaluru bus drops you there

  • Brindavan Gardens

    Garden · 21 km out

    Terraced gardens below the KRS dam with a musical fountain at 7 pm; weekends are packed and buses back fill

  • St Philomena's Cathedral

    Church · 2 km out

    Neo Gothic twin spires, one of the tallest churches in India; the crypt below is open and quiet after lunch

  • Vinayaka Mylari

    Food · 2 km out

    Butter soft masale dose at a few tables on Nazarbad main road; mornings and evenings only, queue moves fast

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Mysuru?

October to January is peak season in Mysuru. February and June to September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid March to May. Dasara runs through October - elephants, illuminated palace, huge crowds but unmissable.

How many days do you need in Mysuru?

2 days is the usual stay. Walkable heritage core with a small hostel scene and plenty of Bengaluru daytrippers.

How do you get to Mysuru?

3h train or bus from Bengaluru, frequent departures. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Mysuru expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.

Is there a festival in Mysuru?

Oct: Mysuru Dasara - ten days of processions and palace illumination. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Mysuru?

Mysore Palace, Chamundi Hill, Devaraja Market, Srirangapatna, Brindavan Gardens and St Philomena's Cathedral, and more. Amba Vilas, the Wodeyar seat; shoes off at the gate, and the whole facade is lit 7 to 7.45 pm on Sundays.
