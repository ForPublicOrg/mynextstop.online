Sasihitlu Beach season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb has the cleanest, flattest water of the year: gentle beginner surf, warm evenings and a sandbar you can walk right around.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar-May is humid at 34 C with a sea like bathwater; May brings the first real swell and the pre-monsoon contests down at Mulki.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun-Jul the Shambhavi comes down brown and fast, rips run at the mouth and lessons stop; late Aug and Sep clean up for surfers who know how.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov the rain quits, the river runs clear again and the beach empties; the best weeks of the year to learn without an audience.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Sasihitlu Beach?

November to February is peak season in Sasihitlu Beach. March and September to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to July. Oct-Nov the rain quits, the river runs clear again and the beach empties; the best weeks of the year to learn without an audience.

How many days do you need in Sasihitlu Beach?

2 days is the usual stay. A fishing hamlet, a surf school with dorm beds and one tea shack; sunset on the sandbar with maybe ten other people.

How do you get to Sasihitlu Beach?

Train to Mulki on the Konkan line, then 6 km by auto; Mangaluru airport is 25 km down the coast road. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Sasihitlu Beach expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.
