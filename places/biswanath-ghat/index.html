Biswanath Ghat season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

The river drops and the ruined Shiva temple at the Burhiganga confluence emerges, so worship and visitors return.

Summer March to May Shoulder

A mela on the third day of Bohag Bihu in mid Apr is the liveliest day; by May the river rises and heat builds.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Avoid: the Brahmaputra floods, the confluence temple sits underwater and riverside paths and fields are cut off.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Oct and Nov are clear and dry; the river is still high but falling, and the sunsets over the water are superb.

Signature festival

Bohag Bihu mela (Apr)

Things to do in Biswanath Ghat

The local places worth your time in and around Biswanath Ghat, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Biswanath Temple

    Temple · in town

    Shiva temple that gives the ghat its Gupta Kashi name; the old shrine at the river confluence is a winter-only visit.

  • Bhiragaon

    Village · 5 km out

    Village where, by a custom begun in 1684 under King Gadadhar Singha, the Shiva idol is carried for a day of worship.

  • Biswanath Chariali

    Market · 7 km out

    District town with hotels, taxis and tea-garden roads; the base for most visitors to the ghat.

  • Nagshankar Temple

    Temple · 22 km out

    Old Shiva temple at Sootea, 15 km west of Biswanath Chariali, with a pond of rare black softshell turtles.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Biswanath Ghat?

December to February is peak season in Biswanath Ghat. March to April and October to November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to September. The river drops and the ruined Shiva temple at the Burhiganga confluence emerges, so worship and visitors return.

How many days do you need in Biswanath Ghat?

2 days is the usual stay. Village homestays, river-fish lunches and morning puja bells; most hotels are in Biswanath Chariali nearby.

How do you get to Biswanath Ghat?

About 7 km south of Biswanath Chariali and its railway station; Tezpur airport is about 55 km by road. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Biswanath Ghat expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Biswanath Ghat?

Bohag Bihu mela (Apr). Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Biswanath Ghat?

Biswanath Temple, Bhiragaon, Biswanath Chariali and Nagshankar Temple. Shiva temple that gives the ghat its Gupta Kashi name; the old shrine at the river confluence is a winter-only visit.
