Salkhan Fossils Park (Sonbhadra) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Cool dry months to wander the open hillside where 1400-million-year stromatolite rings stripe the rock, usually alone.

Summer March to May Shoulder

April-June the bare Kaimur scarp bakes past 43 °C with zero shade on the fossil beds; dawn visits only, if at all.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

Rain greens the scarp and nearby Kaimur falls start running, but the fossil beds turn slick and buses crawl.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October-November is the sweet spot: rinsed air, green cliffs and easy walking weather across the fossil beds.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Salkhan Fossils Park (Sonbhadra)?

November to February is peak season in Salkhan Fossils Park (Sonbhadra). March and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. October-November is the sweet spot: rinsed air, green cliffs and easy walking weather across the fossil beds.

How many days do you need in Salkhan Fossils Park (Sonbhadra)?

1 day is the usual stay. A geology pilgrimage, not a scene: you, a caretaker and school groups; sleep in Robertsganj's plain hotels.

How do you get to Salkhan Fossils Park (Sonbhadra)?

Robertsganj railhead 12 km, autos to Salkhan village; Varanasi 90 km, an honest day trip by car. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Salkhan Fossils Park (Sonbhadra) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
