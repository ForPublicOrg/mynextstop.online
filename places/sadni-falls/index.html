Sadni Falls (Gumla) season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Dec-Feb the Sankh runs thin but clear, plateau days are crisp and nights cold, good for pairing the falls with Netarhat sunrises.

Summer March to May Mixed, read the detail

March is still mild up here, but by Apr-May the flow nearly stops and the scarp is bare rock; skip the falls and stay on the plateau.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Jul-Sep the river coils down the scarp in full flow; forest roads off the Netarhat plateau turn muddy, so go in a sturdy car.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov is prime: strong water, dry roads and clear views over the forested plateau edge, before winter thins the fall.

What Sadni Falls (Gumla) is actually like

Remote tribal hill country near Rajadera village with no facilities at the falls; stay in Netarhat's lodges and carry food.

When is the best time to visit Sadni Falls (Gumla)?

August to October is peak season in Sadni Falls (Gumla). July and November to December is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June.
