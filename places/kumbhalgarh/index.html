Kumbhalgarh season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Best weather for the fort walls and the wildlife sanctuary's forest walks.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Hot and dusty; the long uphill walk along the wall gets punishing by midday.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Aravalli hills turn green and waterfalls appear, though the ghat road can get slick.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Pleasant and clear, with good visibility along the wall's full stretch.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Kumbhalgarh?

November to February is peak season in Kumbhalgarh. March and July to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Pleasant and clear, with good visibility along the wall's full stretch.

How many days do you need in Kumbhalgarh?

2 days is the usual stay. Remote and resort-driven; you'll likely be the only solo guest most nights.

How do you get to Kumbhalgarh?

2h drive/taxi from Udaipur; no direct train. Practical ways in: flight and road.

Is Kumbhalgarh expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
