Ranipuram season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb brings mist in the sholas and 14 C on top; the 6 km climb from the forest gate is dry underfoot and the view runs west to the sea haze.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March still gives long clear views before the heat haze; by Apr-May the grass is burnt yellow, so start the climb by 7 am and carry all your water.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun-Jul the gate closes on the heaviest rain and leeches own the grass; September reopens to green slopes, full streams and cloud on the ridge.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov is the green window: waist-high grass, cloud shadow crossing the ridge and almost nobody on the trail on a weekday.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Ranipuram?

October to February is peak season in Ranipuram. March and August to September is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to July. Oct-Nov is the green window: waist-high grass, cloud shadow crossing the ridge and almost nobody on the trail on a weekday.

How many days do you need in Ranipuram?

2 days is the usual stay. A DTPC cottage and a forest rest house at the gate, homestays down in Panathady; evenings are frogs, stars and patchy phone signal.

How do you get to Ranipuram?

Kanhangad railhead 48 km, then bus or shared taxi via Panathady to the forest gate, about 2h. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Ranipuram expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.
