Kaleshwaram season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec to Feb mornings start near 14 C with mist over both rivers, and the Shivaratri jatara in February or March fills the village with tents.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March is still walkable at dawn. April crosses 42 C and May nears 46 in this Godavari belt, when the sangam sand is unusable by nine.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun to Aug both rivers run in spate, the ghat steps go under and approach roads get cut. September drops the water and leaves it green.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October leaves the Godavari wide and the fields green. November is dry, cool and empty, the best month to walk the sandbanks at the joining.

Signature festival

Feb/Mar: Maha Shivaratri jatara at the sangam

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Kaleshwaram?

November to February is peak season in Kaleshwaram. March and September to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to August. October leaves the Godavari wide and the fields green. November is dry, cool and empty, the best month to walk the sandbanks at the joining.

How many days do you need in Kaleshwaram?

2 days is the usual stay. A one-street temple village of choultries and tea stalls, pilgrims gone by evening, the sangam steps left to you.

How do you get to Kaleshwaram?

Mancherial railhead, then about 100 km by bus via Chennur, 3 h; Karimnagar buses come the other way via Manthani. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Kaleshwaram expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Kaleshwaram?

Feb/Mar: Maha Shivaratri jatara at the sangam. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
