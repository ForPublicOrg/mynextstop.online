Reiek season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Clearest ridge views, cool hiking weather, best season to see the peak's rock formations.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Pleasant into April, green hillsides, then humidity builds toward the rains.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Same wet Mizoram monsoon belt as Aizawl. Trails turn slick, views vanish into cloud.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Cloud lifts through October and the Bangladesh plains come back into view from 1,465 m; nights turn cool and the heritage village empties.

What Reiek is actually like

Half-day escape from Aizawl: cultural-village shows, mostly domestic day-trippers.

Things to do in Reiek

The local places worth your time in and around Reiek, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Reiek Tlang

    Walk · 1 km out

    Steep forty minute climb through forest to the rock summit looking out at the Bangladesh plains; slippery when wet

  • Mizo Heritage Village

    Heritage · in town

    Replica village of thatched chiefs' houses at the foot of the hill; small ticket, and it fills up for the Anthurium Festival

  • Tuirihiau Falls

    Waterfall · 4 km out

    Fall you can walk in behind, down a rough path below the village; the return climb is steep, skip it alone after rain

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Reiek?

November to February is peak season in Reiek. March to April and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to September. Cloud lifts through October and the Bangladesh plains come back into view from 1,465 m; nights turn cool and the heritage village empties.

How many days do you need in Reiek?

2 days is the usual stay. Half-day escape from Aizawl: cultural-village shows, mostly domestic day-trippers.

How do you get to Reiek?

1h drive from Aizawl. ILP + PAP for foreigners. Practical ways in: flight and road.

Is Reiek expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Reiek?

Reiek Tlang, Mizo Heritage Village and Tuirihiau Falls. Steep forty minute climb through forest to the rock summit looking out at the Bangladesh plains; slippery when wet.
