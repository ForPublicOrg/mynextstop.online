Baratang Island (Limestone Caves) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Mar seas stay calm for the creek run to the caves and Parrot Island's dusk roost; mornings bring the day-trip convoy rush

Summer March to May Prime time

April still works on calm days before pre-monsoon swells; by May squalls build and boatmen start cancelling runs

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Jun-Sep the SW monsoon means cancelled boats and a soaked convoy road; day trips routinely abort, so sit it out

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Oct swells ease and November clears; stay the night and you reach the caves before the Port Blair convoys arrive

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Baratang Island (Limestone Caves)?

December to March is peak season in Baratang Island (Limestone Caves). April and November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. Dec-Mar seas stay calm for the creek run to the caves and Parrot Island's dusk roost; mornings bring the day-trip convoy rush.

How many days do you need in Baratang Island (Limestone Caves)?

2 days is the usual stay. A jetty bazaar, basic lodges and a government guest house; day-trippers vanish by 2 pm leaving you with the fishermen.

How do you get to Baratang Island (Limestone Caves)?

From Port Blair by ATR road convoy through the Jarawa reserve (no stops or photos), then Middle Strait ferry. Practical ways in: flight and road.

Is Baratang Island (Limestone Caves) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
