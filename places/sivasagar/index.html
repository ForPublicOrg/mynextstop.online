Sivasagar season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Nov-Feb is 10-25 C and dry, and Rang Ghar, Talatal Ghar and the Sivadol tank all fit into one long walking day.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March-April is warm and green with Bihu in mid-April, but by May it is 35 C, sticky and full of evening squalls.

Monsoon June to September Mixed, read the detail

Jun-Aug the Brahmaputra floods, feeder roads go under, and the brick monuments sit in mud and mosquitoes.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October drains the paddy, the light goes gold, and the Charaideo maidams 28 km away are almost empty of people.

Signature festival

Jan: Me-Dam-Me-Phi (Ahom ancestor rites, 31 Jan)

Things to do in Sivasagar

The local places worth your time in and around Sivasagar, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Rang Ghar

    Heritage · 3 km out

    Two storey Ahom amphitheatre of the 1740s where kings watched buffalo fights; small ticket, the lawn stays open until dusk.

  • Sivadol

    Temple · in town

    Ahom Shiva temple of 1734 on the bank of the great tank, about 30 m high; shoes off at the steps, walk the bund at sunset.

  • Talatal Ghar

    Heritage · 5 km out

    Seven storey Ahom palace at Rangpur, the underground floors and their tunnels sealed; go early, the courtyards give no shade.

  • Charaideo Maidams

    Heritage · 28 km out

    Ahom royal burial mounds, a Unesco site since 2024; shared taxis to Sonari pass the gate, allow two hours with the museum.

  • Gargaon Palace

    Palace · 13 km out

    Kareng Ghar of 1752, the Ahom court palace, with a stair up to the roof terrace; usually empty, the caretaker holds the key.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Sivasagar?

November to February is peak season in Sivasagar. March to April and October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to August. October drains the paddy, the light goes gold, and the Charaideo maidams 28 km away are almost empty of people.

How many days do you need in Sivasagar?

2 days is the usual stay. Small tea-belt town, cycle-rickshaws between monuments, no traveller scene but easy hotels by the tank.

How do you get to Sivasagar?

Jorhat airport 60 km; Simaluguri railhead 20 km; buses from Jorhat and Dibrugarh, 2-3 h. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Sivasagar expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Sivasagar?

Jan: Me-Dam-Me-Phi (Ahom ancestor rites, 31 Jan). Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Sivasagar?

Rang Ghar, Sivadol, Talatal Ghar, Charaideo Maidams and Gargaon Palace. Two storey Ahom amphitheatre of the 1740s where kings watched buffalo fights; small ticket, the lawn stays open until dusk.
