Ramanagara (Ramadevarabetta) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is the climbing season: 27 C, dry granite, and the best odds of a long-billed vulture off the cliff face.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-May the rock is over 40 C by nine and the stepped climb up Ramadevarabetta is punishing after mid-morning.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun-Sep rain here is patchy, but lichen makes the domes greasy, so it turns into a walking and birdwatching trip.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov is clear and green after the rains, and the morning silk cocoon market is worth an hour before you climb.

Things to do in Ramanagara

The local places worth your time in and around Ramanagara, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Government Silk Cocoon Market

    Market · in town

    Sacks of yellow cocoons weighed and auctioned, the biggest such market in Asia; trading is done by noon, free

  • Janapada Loka

    Museum · 8 km out

    Folk museum of masks, leather puppets and Yakshagana costume on the Mysuru road; allow two hours, small fee

  • Savandurga

    Trek · 25 km out

    One of Asia's largest monoliths, two hours up bare rock from the temple at the base; no shade, start by 7 am

  • Revanasiddeshwara Betta

    Trek · 12 km out

    Boulder hill with a cave shrine on top, steps most of the way; monkeys take anything left unzipped

  • Kanva Reservoir

    Lake · 12 km out

    Quiet backwater under the granite hills, good for birds and an hour of nothing; no boats, no food, carry lunch

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Ramanagara (Ramadevarabetta)?

November to February is peak season in Ramanagara (Ramadevarabetta). March and June to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to May. Oct-Nov is clear and green after the rains, and the morning silk cocoon market is worth an hour before you climb.

How many days do you need in Ramanagara (Ramadevarabetta)?

1 day is the usual stay. Bengaluru boulderers at weekends, temple walkers on the hill, empty midweek; plain lodges in town if you stay.

How do you get to Ramanagara (Ramadevarabetta)?

Ramanagara railhead on the Bengaluru-Mysuru line 50 km out; autos 5 km to the Ramadevarabetta gate. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Ramanagara (Ramadevarabetta) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Ramanagara?

Government Silk Cocoon Market, Janapada Loka, Savandurga, Revanasiddeshwara Betta and Kanva Reservoir. Sacks of yellow cocoons weighed and auctioned, the biggest such market in Asia; trading is done by noon, free.
