Igatpuri season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Cool and clear, good for the Vipassana course or a straightforward day trek.

Summer March to May Do not go

Hot, dry and brown, the least scenic time - even the meditation centre gets stifling.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Peak season for trekkers - Kalsubai and the ghats turn lush, though trails get slippery.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Waterfalls taper off through October-November but the greenery and cool air linger.

Things to do in Igatpuri

The local places worth your time in and around Igatpuri, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Dhamma Giri Vipassana Academy

    Activity · 2 km out

    Where Vipassana returned to India; 10 day silent courses are free but book months ahead, casual visitors see only the pagoda

  • Tringalwadi Fort

    Fort · 10 km out

    Hill fort above Tringalwadi lake with a rock cut cave at its base; about an hour up from the village, slippery in rain

  • Camel Valley Waterfall

    Waterfall · 7 km out

    Monsoon fall in a gorge shaped like a camel's back, 20 minutes off the highway; dry and pointless after October

  • Vihigaon Waterfall

    Waterfall · 20 km out

    Wide monsoon curtain near Kasara where operators rappel down the face; weekdays only, weekends turn into a queue

  • Kalsubai Peak

    Trek · 45 km out

    Maharashtra's highest point at 1646 m with ladders bolted to the last rock; start from Bari by 6 am, 3 hours up

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Igatpuri?

July to September is peak season in Igatpuri. June and October to February is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid March to May. Peak season for trekkers - Kalsubai and the ghats turn lush, though trails get slippery.

How many days do you need in Igatpuri?

2 days is the usual stay. Silent Vipassana retreats draw a global crowd; monsoon weekends bring trekkers to Kalsubai.

How do you get to Igatpuri?

2h train from Mumbai or Nashik Road, Igatpuri sits on the main line. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Igatpuri expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Igatpuri?

Dhamma Giri Vipassana Academy, Tringalwadi Fort, Camel Valley Waterfall, Vihigaon Waterfall and Kalsubai Peak. Where Vipassana returned to India; 10 day silent courses are free but book months ahead, casual visitors see only the pagoda.
