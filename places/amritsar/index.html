Amritsar season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Cold mornings at the Golden Temple are atmospheric, especially the pre-dawn Palki ceremony.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Punjab plains heat hits past 40C, though the temple's marble floors stay cooling underfoot.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Humid with heavy rain bursts, but the temple complex runs fully and is far less crowded.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Comfortable temperatures building toward Diwali, when the temple is spectacularly lit.

Things to do in Amritsar

The local places worth your time in and around Amritsar, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Harmandir Sahib (Golden Temple)

    Gurudwara · in town

    Gold-leaf shrine on a tank, the centre of the Sikh world; free round the clock, cover your head, eat in the langar hall.

  • Jallianwala Bagh

    Heritage · in town

    The 1919 massacre garden, bullet-marked wall and the well people jumped into; free, one narrow lane in, shuts 7.30 pm.

  • Partition Museum

    Museum · 1 km out

    Objects and oral histories of 1947 in the old Town Hall; ticketed, closed Mondays, two hours and a heavy walk back out.

  • Attari-Wagah Border Ceremony

    Activity · 28 km out

    Flag lowering shouted across the gate at sunset; free stands fill by 4 pm, no bags allowed, shared taxis go from near the temple.

  • Gobindgarh Fort

    Fort · 3 km out

    Ranjit Singh's brick city fort, now a museum campus with a film and an evening light show; ticketed, allow two hours.

  • Kesar Da Dhaba

    Food · 1 km out

    Running since 1916 in the Chowk Passian lanes; overnight dal, parathas in ghee, thali with lassi, cash only and a wait.

  • Durgiana Temple

    Temple · 2 km out

    Hindu temple on its own tank, built to the Golden Temple's plan but far quieter; ten minutes by auto from Hall Bazaar.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Amritsar?

November to February is peak season in Amritsar. March and July to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Comfortable temperatures building toward Diwali, when the temple is spectacularly lit.

How many days do you need in Amritsar?

2 days is the usual stay. The Golden Temple's langar hall makes it genuinely easy to meet people.

How do you get to Amritsar?

Fly into Amritsar (ATQ) or 6h train from Delhi/Chandigarh. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Amritsar expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs busy at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.

Is there a festival in Amritsar?

Apr: Baisakhi at the Golden Temple. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Amritsar?

Harmandir Sahib (Golden Temple), Jallianwala Bagh, Partition Museum, Attari-Wagah Border Ceremony, Gobindgarh Fort and Kesar Da Dhaba, and more. Gold-leaf shrine on a tank, the centre of the Sikh world; free round the clock, cover your head, eat in the langar hall.
