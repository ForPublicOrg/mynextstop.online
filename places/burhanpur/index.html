Burhanpur season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is 12-30 C, right for the Shahi Qila hammam paintings and a walk into the Kundi Bhandara water tunnel.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-Jun is among the hottest stretches in Madhya Pradesh at 45 C, and the Tapti drops to sand - skip it entirely.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jul-Aug is humid with heavy spells; the tunnel walk gets closed when water levels rise, so check before you go.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov is dry and pleasant, good for Asirgarh fort 20 km out and the near-empty Ahukhana garden by the river.

Things to do in Burhanpur

The local places worth your time in and around Burhanpur, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Shahi Qila

    Fort · in town

    Riverside Mughal palace where Shah Jahan lived; the hammam ceiling holds a painted dome many read as a Taj sketch.

  • Khooni Bhandara

    Heritage · 2 km out

    Working 1615 underground waterworks that still supply parts of town; steps lead down one shaft to the channel, carry a torch.

  • Asirgarh Fort

    Fort · 20 km out

    The hilltop key to the Deccan, with an old Shiva temple on top; 20 km north, road most of the way then steps.

  • Dargah-e-Hakimi

    Shrine · 4 km out

    Dawoodi Bohra pilgrimage complex of marble tombs in clipped gardens; free to enter, cover arms and legs, shoes off at the tomb.

  • Jama Masjid

    Mosque · in town

    1590 mosque in the middle of the bazaar carrying inscriptions in both Arabic and Sanskrit; step in between prayers.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Burhanpur?

November to February is peak season in Burhanpur. March and September to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to June. Oct-Nov is dry and pleasant, good for Asirgarh fort 20 km out and the near-empty Ahukhana garden by the river.

How many days do you need in Burhanpur?

2 days is the usual stay. Untouristed Mughal town where you will be the only visitor at the fort; simple hotels near the station.

How do you get to Burhanpur?

On the Mumbai-Delhi Central line, 8 h from Mumbai and 6 h from Bhopal; autos cover the town. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Burhanpur expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Burhanpur?

Shahi Qila, Khooni Bhandara, Asirgarh Fort, Dargah-e-Hakimi and Jama Masjid. Riverside Mughal palace where Shah Jahan lived; the hammam ceiling holds a painted dome many read as a Taj sketch.
