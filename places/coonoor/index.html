Coonoor season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Cool and clear, fewer fog days than Ooty, good for tea-estate walks without a jacket-in-the-mist fight

Summer March to May Prime time

Warm pleasant days among tea gardens, a quieter overflow alternative to a packed Ooty

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Autumn October to November Prime time

Post-monsoon clarity, tea estates glisten green, a calm low-key alternative to Ooty's Oct crowds

Things to do in Coonoor

The local places worth your time in and around Coonoor, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Sim's Park

    Garden · 2 km out

    Terraced botanical garden dropping into a ravine, tree ferns and a small lake; ticket at the gate, quiet before 10 am.

  • Dolphin's Nose

    Viewpoint · 10 km out

    Cliff jutting over the Catherine Falls gorge with the plains beyond; cloud swallows it after 11 am, go at opening.

  • Lamb's Rock

    Viewpoint · 8 km out

    Rock ledge over the Coimbatore plains on the same road as Dolphin's Nose; short walk in, monkeys grab open food.

  • Law's Falls

    Waterfall · 6 km out

    Falls in a bend of the Mettupalayam ghat road with a picnic spot below; a trickle in summer, worth it after rain.

  • Highfield Tea Factory

    Tea estate · 3 km out

    Working factory that walks you from withering to grading in 20 min; free tasting, and they push the shop hard after.

  • Droog Fort

    Fort · 14 km out

    Ruined Tipu era hill post reached by a 3 km climb through tea and shola; no shade at the top, carry your own water.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Coonoor?

April to May and October is peak season in Coonoor. September and November to March is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to August. Warm pleasant days among tea gardens, a quieter overflow alternative to a packed Ooty.

How many days do you need in Coonoor?

2 days is the usual stay. Sleepy tea-estate town, homestays over hostels - better for quiet solo walks than a crowd.

How do you get to Coonoor?

Fly to Coimbatore (70km, 2.5hr) or toy train from Mettupalayam. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Coonoor expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Coonoor?

Sim's Park, Dolphin's Nose, Lamb's Rock, Law's Falls, Highfield Tea Factory and Droog Fort. Terraced botanical garden dropping into a ravine, tree ferns and a small lake; ticket at the gate, quiet before 10 am.
