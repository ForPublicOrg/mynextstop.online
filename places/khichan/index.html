Khichan (Demoiselle Cranes) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Peak: up to 20,000 kurjan cranes drop onto the chugga ghar feeding ground at first light and again mid-morning.

Summer March to May Shoulder

April-June there is not a single crane left, and Phalodi is one of India's hottest towns, regularly past 47 C.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

June and July are empty and hot at 42 C; the first birds only trickle in right at the end of August once the rain stops.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Numbers build fast through October and the flocks are already worth the trip by mid-November, with no crowd at all.

What Khichan (Demoiselle Cranes) is actually like

A Jain-Marwari village with one crane hide and two homestays; birders and nobody else, so bring your own patience.

Getting to Khichan (Demoiselle Cranes)

Phalodi railhead 5 km, with Jodhpur and Jaisalmer trains; autos to Khichan, and feeding starts around sunrise.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Khichan (Demoiselle Cranes)?

November to February is peak season in Khichan (Demoiselle Cranes). March and September to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to August. Numbers build fast through October and the flocks are already worth the trip by mid-November, with no crowd at all.

How many days do you need in Khichan (Demoiselle Cranes)?

1 day is the usual stay. A Jain-Marwari village with one crane hide and two homestays; birders and nobody else, so bring your own patience.

How do you get to Khichan (Demoiselle Cranes)?

Phalodi railhead 5 km, with Jodhpur and Jaisalmer trains; autos to Khichan, and feeding starts around sunrise. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Khichan (Demoiselle Cranes) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
