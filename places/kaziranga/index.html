Kaziranga National Park season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Peak safari season: dry grass, easy rhino and elephant sightings, all safaris running.

Summer March to May Prime time

May is the tail end before closure. Hot, grass tall, park shuts by month's end.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Fully closed: the Brahmaputra floods the park every year, no safaris run at all.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October reopening: grass still tall so sightings are trickier, but crowds are thin.

What Kaziranga National Park is actually like

Resort-lodge crowd doing sunrise safaris. Easy to team up and split a jeep.

Things to do in Kaziranga National Park

The local places worth your time in and around Kaziranga National Park, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Kohora (Central) Range

    Wildlife · in town

    Main gate: dawn elephant ride from Mihimukh, then a jeep loop past the beels; book the elephant a day ahead, slots go fast.

  • Bagori (Western) Range

    Wildlife · 16 km out

    Open grass and water on every side, the range where rhino turn up most reliably; first jeeps at sunrise, shut May to October.

  • Agoratoli (Eastern) Range

    Wildlife · 33 km out

    Quiet birding range around Sohola beel, pelicans and fish eagles in winter; animals sit far off, carry your own binoculars.

  • Hathikuli Organic Tea Estate

    Tea estate · 8 km out

    Organic garden on the highway that runs right up to the park boundary; walk the rows early, the factory takes visitors on request.

  • Kaziranga National Orchid and Biodiversity Park

    Garden · 5 km out

    Wild orchids, bamboo and an evening Bihu dance with rice beer tasting; ticketed, the obvious filler between the two safari slots.

  • Kakochang Falls

    Waterfall · 30 km out

    Small falls among coffee and rubber plantations near Bokakhat; go soon after the rains, by spring it is a trickle on rough road.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Kaziranga National Park?

November to April is peak season in Kaziranga National Park. October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid May to September. October reopening: grass still tall so sightings are trickier, but crowds are thin.

How many days do you need in Kaziranga National Park?

2 days is the usual stay. Resort-lodge crowd doing sunrise safaris. Easy to team up and split a jeep.

How do you get to Kaziranga National Park?

4-5h drive/bus from Guwahati airport. No ILP. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Kaziranga National Park expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

What are the best things to do in Kaziranga National Park?

Kohora (Central) Range, Bagori (Western) Range, Agoratoli (Eastern) Range, Hathikuli Organic Tea Estate, Kaziranga National Orchid and Biodiversity Park and Kakochang Falls. Main gate: dawn elephant ride from Mihimukh, then a jeep loop past the beels; book the elephant a day ahead, slots go fast.
