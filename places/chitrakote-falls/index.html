Chitrakote Falls (Bastar) season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Falls thin to a scenic curtain, but easiest season for Kanger Valley caves and tribal haats.

Summer March to May Do not go

Indravati runs low, falls reduce to a trickle over bare rock. Hot, dusty, skip unless for markets.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

Chitrakote's real fame: Indravati floods the 300m ledge into a genuine roar; roads turn muddy though.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Falls still strong from the monsoon, Bastar's weeks-long Dussehra usually falls now. Best time to come.

Signature festival

Aug-Oct: Bastar Dussehra, a 75-day tribal festival.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Chitrakote Falls (Bastar)?

July to October is peak season in Chitrakote Falls (Bastar). November to February is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid March to June. Chitrakote's real fame: Indravati floods the 300m ledge into a genuine roar; roads turn muddy though.

How many days do you need in Chitrakote Falls (Bastar)?

4 days is the usual stay. Remote tribal heartland: expect local guides and market-goers, barely any tourists.

How do you get to Chitrakote Falls (Bastar)?

Fly/train to Jagdalpur via Raipur (7-8h), then local taxi. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Chitrakote Falls (Bastar) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Mid-range by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

Is there a festival in Chitrakote Falls (Bastar)?

Aug-Oct: Bastar Dussehra, a 75-day tribal festival. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.
