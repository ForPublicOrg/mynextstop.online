Naldurg season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

December and January are easy walking on two kilometres of rampart; February warms fast, but the Pani Mahal stays cool inside the dam wall itself.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March is walkable if you start at seven; April and May bake the black rock past 43 C with no shade on the walls and the Bori riverbed dry, so skip both.

Monsoon June to September Prime time

June and July are green and pleasant; from August the Bori fills and the Nar and Madi falls pour over the dam right past the palace windows.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October and November are the best of it: the twin falls still running, mild days on the bastions and the fort largely to yourself on a weekday.

When is the best time to visit Naldurg?

August to January is peak season in Naldurg. February to March and June to July is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to May.
