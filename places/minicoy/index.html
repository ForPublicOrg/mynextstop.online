Minicoy (Maliku) season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Mar is the calm season: a huge sheltered lagoon, tuna boats unloading at the jetty and 25-31°C with almost no rain.

Summer March to May Prime time

April and May stay warm and clear early on, then the pre-monsoon swell arrives and the long sail down from Kochi turns rough.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

June-Sep is the closed season here: ships are curtailed, the lagoon is churned up and there is no reliable way to plan a trip.

Autumn October to November Shoulder

Oct is still unsettled; November settles down and the island returns to its slow rhythm of tuna fishing and boat repair.

What Minicoy (Maliku) is actually like

A matrilineal, Mahl-speaking society closer to the Maldives than to Kerala; you sleep in a tourist home, not a resort

Things to do in Minicoy

The local places worth your time in and around Minicoy, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Minicoy Lighthouse

    Viewpoint · 3 km out

    British-built 1885 tower with a spiral stair to the gallery and the whole atoll below; usually open late afternoon only.

  • Viringili Island

    Island · 4 km out

    Small islet off the southern end, once a quarantine colony and now empty; a short boat hop your tourist home arranges.

  • Minicoy Lagoon

    Beach · in town

    One of Lakshadweep's largest lagoons, pale and shallow along the west; the tuna boats come in at dusk on the harbour side.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Minicoy (Maliku)?

December to March is peak season in Minicoy (Maliku). April and October to November is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. Dec-Mar is the calm season: a huge sheltered lagoon, tuna boats unloading at the jetty and 25-31°C with almost no rain.

How many days do you need in Minicoy (Maliku)?

4 days is the usual stay. A matrilineal, Mahl-speaking society closer to the Maldives than to Kerala; you sleep in a tourist home, not a resort.

How do you get to Minicoy (Maliku)?

Entry permit mandatory; ship from Kochi 20-26 h, or the Agatti flight plus a permitted vessel south. Practical ways in: flight.

Is Minicoy (Maliku) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Splurge by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

What are the best things to do in Minicoy?

Minicoy Lighthouse, Viringili Island and Minicoy Lagoon. British-built 1885 tower with a spiral stair to the gallery and the whole atoll below; usually open late afternoon only.
