Hogenakkal Falls season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is the sweet spot: moderate flow, coracles running all day and 18-30 C for the oil-massage-and-fish-fry routine.

Summer March to May Shoulder

March is workable, but by Apr-May Dharmapuri sits at 40 C and the river is at its lowest, so the falls run to a trickle.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun-Jul the Cauvery starts rising, but through Aug-Sep the falls come down in spate and coracle rides are simply banned.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov is the best of the year here, with full but safe flow, green gorge walls and boats back out on the water.

What Hogenakkal Falls is actually like

Coracle touts, roadside fish fry and oil masseurs working on the rocks; a TTDC hotel and a few lodges if you stay.

Getting to Hogenakkal Falls

Dharmapuri railhead 46 km with hourly buses, Bengaluru 130 km, Salem 115 km, no bus back out after about 6pm.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Hogenakkal Falls?

October to February is peak season in Hogenakkal Falls. March and June to July is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to May and August to September. Oct-Nov is the best of the year here, with full but safe flow, green gorge walls and boats back out on the water.

How many days do you need in Hogenakkal Falls?

1 day is the usual stay. Coracle touts, roadside fish fry and oil masseurs working on the rocks; a TTDC hotel and a few lodges if you stay.

How do you get to Hogenakkal Falls?

Dharmapuri railhead 46 km with hourly buses, Bengaluru 130 km, Salem 115 km, no bus back out after about 6pm. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Hogenakkal Falls expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.
