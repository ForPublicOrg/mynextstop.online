Ilam (tea gardens) season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Clear skies for Kanchenjunga at the Antu Danda sunrise, cold mornings and misty tea slopes; little picking happens.

Summer March to May Prime time

First flush picking begins in Mar and Apr, the gardens are bright green and views are still fairly clear.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Heavy rain, leeches and slips on hill roads; the tea grows fast but the views disappear behind cloud.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Best time: clear Himalayan views, autumn flush tea and comfortable walks through the Kanyam and Ilam gardens.

What Ilam (tea gardens) is actually like

Homestays among the tea bushes, milk tea and churpi cheese, Nepali couples and Siliguri weekenders.

Things to do in Ilam

The local places worth your time in and around Ilam, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Ilam Tea Estate

    Tea estate · in town

    The oldest tea garden in Nepal, planted in the 1860s, with walking paths right above the bazaar.

  • Kanyam

    Tea estate · 17 km out

    Sweeping tea slopes on the highway where everyone stops for photos and momos; busy at weekends.

  • Antu Danda

    Viewpoint · 22 km out

    Ridge near the border above 2000 m, famous for sunrise over Kanchenjunga and a sea of clouds.

  • Mai Pokhari

    Lake · 11 km out

    Sacred wetland lake in forest, a Ramsar site with nine bays, about 1 h by road north of Ilam.

  • Fikkal

    Market · 14 km out

    Highway bazaar between Ilam and Kanyam with tea factories, where the road branches off to Pashupatinagar.

Getting to Ilam (tea gardens)

Bhadrapur airport is 77 km, about 3 h up the Mechi Highway; from the Kakarbhitta border allow 3 to 4 h.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Ilam (tea gardens)?

March to April and October to November is peak season in Ilam (tea gardens). May, September and December to February is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to August. Best time: clear Himalayan views, autumn flush tea and comfortable walks through the Kanyam and Ilam gardens.

How many days do you need in Ilam (tea gardens)?

2 days is the usual stay. Homestays among the tea bushes, milk tea and churpi cheese, Nepali couples and Siliguri weekenders.

How do you get to Ilam (tea gardens)?

Bhadrapur airport is 77 km, about 3 h up the Mechi Highway; from the Kakarbhitta border allow 3 to 4 h. Practical ways in: flight and road.

Is Ilam (tea gardens) expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.

What are the best things to do in Ilam?

Ilam Tea Estate, Kanyam, Antu Danda, Mai Pokhari and Fikkal. The oldest tea garden in Nepal, planted in the 1860s, with walking paths right above the bazaar.
