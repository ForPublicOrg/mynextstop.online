Munsiyari season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Snowbound and beautiful in December, but January-February often cuts off roads and shuts guesthouses

Summer March to May Prime time

April-May is prime trekking season; March is still cold as roads reopen after winter

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

June is calm before the storm; July-September bring landslides that regularly cut the remote road

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is prime: post-monsoon clarity gives the best Panchachuli views; November turns cold fast

Things to do in Munsiyari

The local places worth your time in and around Munsiyari, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Khaliya Top

    Trek · 9 km out

    Meadow at 3500 m facing the Panchachuli wall, 6 km of road to Balati then a 3 km climb; snow shuts it December to March.

  • Tribal Heritage Museum

    Museum · 2 km out

    One man's collection of Johar valley Bhotia tools, wool and trade relics at Nanasem; knock at the house, small donation.

  • Thamri Kund

    Lake · 10 km out

    Small forest lake above town where musk deer drink at dawn; take a local guide, the last 2 km is a walking track only.

  • Nanda Devi Temple

    Temple · 1 km out

    Stone temple on the spur above the bazaar, the best free spot for the Panchachuli sunrise; ten minutes uphill on foot.

  • Birthi Falls

    Waterfall · 35 km out

    126 m fall beside the Thal road, worth a stop on the way in or out; 5 minutes from the tea stall, heaviest after the rains.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Munsiyari?

April to May and October is peak season in Munsiyari. March, June and November to December is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid January to February and July to September. April-May is prime trekking season; March is still cold as roads reopen after winter.

How many days do you need in Munsiyari?

4 days is the usual stay. A trekking basecamp of serious hikers; guesthouse owners become your main company.

How do you get to Munsiyari?

Fly Kathgodam, then 8-9h drive via Almora; shared jeeps run. Practical ways in: road.

Is Munsiyari expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.

What are the best things to do in Munsiyari?

Khaliya Top, Tribal Heritage Museum, Thamri Kund, Nanda Devi Temple and Birthi Falls. Meadow at 3500 m facing the Panchachuli wall, 6 km of road to Balati then a 3 km climb; snow shuts it December to March.
