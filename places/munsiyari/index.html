Munsiyari season by season

Winter December to February Shoulder

Snowbound and beautiful in December, but January-February often cuts off roads and shuts guesthouses

Summer March to May Prime time

April-May is prime trekking season; March is still cold as roads reopen after winter

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

June is calm before the storm; July-September bring landslides that regularly cut the remote road

Autumn October to November Prime time

October is prime: post-monsoon clarity gives the best Panchachuli views; November turns cold fast

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Munsiyari?

April to May and October is peak season in Munsiyari. March, June and November to December is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid January to February and July to September. April-May is prime trekking season; March is still cold as roads reopen after winter.

How many days do you need in Munsiyari?

4 days is the usual stay. A trekking basecamp of serious hikers; guesthouse owners become your main company.

How do you get to Munsiyari?

Fly Kathgodam, then 8-9h drive via Almora; shared jeeps run. Practical ways in: road.

Is Munsiyari expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
