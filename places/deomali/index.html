Deomali season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb the summit drops near 4 C at dawn with an inversion filling the valleys; carry a real jacket.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar-May is hazy and dusty with washed-out views, though the top still runs 8-10 C cooler than Koraput.

Monsoon June to September Do not go

Jun-Sep the last hairpins are fog and running mud, the camp shuts and the view is a wall of grey.

Autumn October to November Prime time

October gives the clearest air of the year and grass still green from the rain; the best sunrises here.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Deomali?

October to February is peak season in Deomali. March to May is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to September. October gives the clearest air of the year and grass still green from the rain; the best sunrises here.

How many days do you need in Deomali?

1 day is the usual stay. A tented nature camp near the top and dhaba villages below; bikers and Odia weekenders, no foreign faces.

How do you get to Deomali?

70 km from Koraput via Semiliguda by shared jeep or hired bike; the last climb is steep and unsealed. Practical ways in: road.

Is Deomali expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Doable solo with some planning.
