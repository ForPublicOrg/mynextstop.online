Sri Surya Pahar season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Ideal: cool, dry days for scrambling between the carved boulders, with clear views over the Brahmaputra plains.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Mar is fine early in the day; by Apr heat and humidity build, and May is sticky with afternoon storms.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun to Aug bring heavy rain and slippery rock paths; Sep eases off, with lush green slopes around the carvings.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct and Nov are pleasant again, the hill is green and the site museum is a cool break between climbs.

What Sri Surya Pahar is actually like

Local pilgrims at the Surya temple, a small ASI museum and quiet paths; eat and sleep in Goalpara town

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Sri Surya Pahar?

November to February is peak season in Sri Surya Pahar. March to May and September to October is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid June to August. Oct and Nov are pleasant again, the hill is green and the site museum is a cool break between climbs.

How many days do you need in Sri Surya Pahar?

1 day is the usual stay. Local pilgrims at the Surya temple, a small ASI museum and quiet paths; eat and sleep in Goalpara town.

How do you get to Sri Surya Pahar?

About 13 km south-east of Goalpara town and its railway station; Guwahati airport is about 110 km by road. Practical ways in: flight, train and road.

Is Sri Surya Pahar expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs quiet at the busiest time of year. Easy solo.

What are the best things to do in Sri Surya Pahar?

ASI Site Museum, Surya Pahar, Urpad Beel and Pancharatna. Small ASI museum of finds from the 1992 to 2001 excavations, terracotta plaques and sculpture; see it first.
