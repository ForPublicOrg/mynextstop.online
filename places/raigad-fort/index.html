Raigad Fort season by season

Winter December to February Prime time

Dec-Feb is 14-32 C, clear enough to see the Konkan from Hirkani Buruj and cool enough for the steps.

Summer March to May Shoulder

Apr-May is 38 C and humid on a treeless plateau where the ruins give almost no shade at midday at all.

Monsoon June to September Shoulder

Jun-Sep brings 3,500 mm and the ropeway suspends in high wind, though 6 June coronation day still packs out.

Autumn October to November Prime time

Oct-Nov is the best time here, plateau green, waterfalls still running and the air properly washed clean.

Signature festival

Jun: Shiv Rajyabhishek Din, coronation day on 6 June

Things to do in Raigad Fort

The local places worth your time in and around Raigad Fort, with how far each sits from the centre. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps.

  • Jagadishwar Temple

    Temple · 1 km out

    Shivaji's own Shiva temple on the plateau, his samadhi right behind it; be there at dawn before the ropeway loads

  • Raj Sabha

    Heritage · 1 km out

    Coronation hall with a replica throne under the Meghdambari canopy; stand at the Nagarkhana gate to see the scale

  • Takmak Tok

    Viewpoint · 2 km out

    The execution cliff at the far end of the plateau, a straight drop of 1,200 ft; railed now, the wind is serious

  • Hirakani Buruj

    Viewpoint · 1 km out

    Bastion above the face a milkmaid is said to have climbed down at night; the fort's best light is here at sunset

  • Maha Darwaja

    Heritage · 3 km out

    Main gate on the old stepped route, twin bastions and a blind turn; you only see it if you climb, not ride up

  • Rajmata Jijau Samadhi

    Heritage · 6 km out

    Jijabai's memorial at Pachad, at the foot of the climb; a walled garden five minutes off the road, usually empty

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Raigad Fort?

October to February is peak season in Raigad Fort. March and June is shoulder season: thinner crowds, a bit more risk in the weather. Avoid April to May and July to September. Oct-Nov is the best time here, plateau green, waterfalls still running and the air properly washed clean.

How many days do you need in Raigad Fort?

2 days is the usual stay. Basic rooms on top or cheap lodges in Pachad; ropeway day crowds, then dawn on the ruins is yours.

How do you get to Raigad Fort?

Mangaon on the Konkan Railway is 30 km off, then bus to Pachad and 1,737 steps or a 4 min ropeway. Practical ways in: train and road.

Is Raigad Fort expensive, and does it get crowded?

Shoestring by Indian travel standards, and it runs steady at the busiest time of year. Fine solo.

Is there a festival in Raigad Fort?

Jun: Shiv Rajyabhishek Din, coronation day on 6 June. Time a trip around it and you see the place at its loudest, but book beds early.

What are the best things to do in Raigad Fort?

Jagadishwar Temple, Raj Sabha, Takmak Tok, Hirakani Buruj, Maha Darwaja and Rajmata Jijau Samadhi. Shivaji's own Shiva temple on the plateau, his samadhi right behind it; be there at dawn before the ropeway loads.
