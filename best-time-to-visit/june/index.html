Best places to visit in India in June

47 of 589 curated destinations are in peak season in June, and 432 are ones to stay away from. No listicle lies: every verdict here comes from the place's own season window.

← May All months July →

Where India is at its best in June

Each line below is the reason this place works in the monsoon, written for that place, not a generic blurb.

  • Kheerganga Himachal Pradesh

    Hot-spring trek camp, a backpacker rite of passage

    Jun holds up; Jul-Aug bring leeches, slick rock sections and real flash-flood risk on the river.

    TrekMountainsOffbeatSnow

  • Leh Ladakh

    Ladakh's backpacker capital and high-pass road-trip hub

    Peak season - both highways open, dry rain-shadow weather, hostels and cafes buzzing.

    MountainsBackpackerCulture

  • Aharbal Falls Jammu and Kashmir

    The Veshaw crashes 25 m through a pine gorge, Kashmir's Niagara

    Jul-Sep rain keeps the fall fat and the walk up to Kungwattan meadow green; short showers, slick steps, local day-trippers.

    WaterfallsMountainsOffbeat

  • Badrinath & Mana Uttarakhand

    Char Dham shrine at 3100 m with Mana village 3 km on

    July-August the Alaknanda valley slides; the highway above Joshimath closes for hours or days and the yatra thins right out.

    SpiritualMountainsHeritage

  • Agumbe Karnataka

    Rainforest village known as South India's Cherrapunji

    This is the season - 700cm+ of rain, roaring falls, king cobras out, but leeches everywhere.

    OffbeatWaterfallsWildlife

  • Athirappilly Falls Kerala

    An 80ft waterfall curtain at its thundering best in monsoon

    This is peak season here - the falls roar at full width; stick to viewing platforms, rocks are lethal

    WaterfallsTrek

  • Chail Himachal Pradesh

    Deodar hill station with the world's highest cricket ground

    July-August is fog, drizzle and occasional slips on the Kandaghat road; atmospheric if you like reading indoors, thin otherwise.

    MountainsHeritage

  • Alchi (Sham Valley) Ladakh

    11th-century Kashmiri murals in a low Indus orchard village

    Jun-Sep stays dry with apricots ripening in July; the Choskor murals are unlit, so come when light reaches the doors.

    HeritageSpiritualOffbeat

  • Doodhpathri Jammu and Kashmir

    Milky Shaliganga meadows under pine, Kashmir's gentler Gulmarg

    Jul-Aug is green and warm rather than wet, but weekend picnic crowds and pony touts peak; Jun and Sep are the quiet wins.

    MountainsOffbeat

  • Kedarnath Uttarakhand

    Himalayan Shiva shrine at 3583m, open just 6 months

    Technically open but this is landslide and flash-flood country. The 2013 disaster hit in this window

    SpiritualTrekMountains

  • Churdhar (via Nohradhar) Himachal Pradesh

    Sirmaur's 3,647 m Shiva summit above a sea of foothills

    June holds dry; July-August is a slippery viewless slog on clay tracks, and September clears into a superb climbing window.

    TrekSpiritualMountains

  • Aryan Valley (Dha & Garkon) Ladakh

    Flower-crowned Brokpa villages and apricot terraces on the Indus

    Jul-Aug barely sees rain in this rain-shadow gorge: apricots dry on rooftops, harvest in full swing, days around 25 C.

    OffbeatCultureMountains

  • Manasbal Lake Jammu and Kashmir

    Lotus-choked lake under Nur Jahan's Jarokha Bagh terrace

    Jul-Aug is peak lotus, solid pink across the north shore; Jun and Sep are quieter but still worth the shikara hour.

    LakesOffbeatWildlife

  • Sankri (Har Ki Dun) Uttarakhand

    Govind park trailhead for Har Ki Dun and Kedarkantha

    July-August the Tons valley road from Purola slips badly and the Har Ki Dun trail turns into leech-ridden mud all the way.

    TrekMountainsOffbeat

  • Kaza (Spiti) Himachal Pradesh

    Spiti's cold-desert capital - monasteries and moonscapes

    Spiti's real peak - a rain-shadow desert, so this is HP's warmest, driest, greenest window.

    MountainsOffbeatDesertCulture

  • Lamayuru Ladakh

    Moonland badlands under one of Ladakh's oldest gompas

    Jun-Sep is dry high desert, not monsoon - best light on the moonland, homestays open, Yuru Kabgyat in June or July.

    HeritageSpiritualMountains

  • Naranag Jammu and Kashmir

    Ruined 8th-century Shiva temples at the Gangabal trailhead

    Jun-Sep is the trekking window: two days up to Gangabal under Harmukh, with guides and ponies hired in the village.

    TrekHeritageOffbeat

  • Harsil Uttarakhand

    Deodar and apple village on the Bhagirathi below Gangotri

    July-August is the Bhagirathi valley's landslide season; the highway above Uttarkashi has stranded pilgrims for days on end.

    MountainsOffbeatSnow

  • Parashar Lake Himachal Pradesh

    Floating-island lake with a pagoda temple, camping spot

    Jun is fine; Jul-Aug turn the approach road muddy and landslide-prone, with fog hiding the views.

    LakesTrekMountainsOffbeat

  • Nubra Valley Ladakh

    Cold-desert valley of sand dunes and quiet monasteries

    Prime time - Hunder's dunes, camel rides and Diskit monastery open up, easy loop with Turtuk.

    DesertMountainsOffbeat

  • Pahalgam Jammu and Kashmir

    Pine-forested valley base for Amarnath Yatra and easy treks

    June is lovely, but Jul-Aug bring Amarnath Yatra convoys and traffic curbs; September calms again.

    MountainsTrekSpiritual

  • Sangla Himachal Pradesh

    Baspa valley base camp with the old Kamru Fort

    Jun is fine; Jul-Aug bring serious rockfall risk on the Karcham-Wangtu stretch, avoid driving in.

    MountainsOffbeatHeritageTrek

  • Pangong Tso Ladakh

    The '3 Idiots' lake - a 134km turquoise line into Tibet

    Peak season for the famous turquoise water, though Jul-Aug can mean queues of cars at the viewpoint.

    LakesMountainsOffbeat

  • Patnitop Jammu and Kashmir

    Deodar ridge on the Jammu-Srinagar highway under Nathatop snow

    June is clear and green, but Jul-Aug is Ramban landslide season on NH44 with six-hour halts; Sep drains off slowly.

    MountainsSnowTrek

  • Shangarh (Sainj Valley) Himachal Pradesh

    Sacred meadow village at the quiet gate of the Great Himalayan NP

    July-August bring landslide-prone Sainj roads, leeches and a sodden meadow; June and late September are the workable edges.

    OffbeatMountainsTrek

  • Turtuk Ladakh

    Balti village near the LoC, opened to tourists only in 2010

    Best time - apricot trees fruiting in July-August, full village life visible, easy add-on from Hunder.

    OffbeatCultureHeritage

  • Sonamarg Jammu and Kashmir

    Meadow of Gold below Zoji La, base for the Great Lakes trek

    Prime season - the Kashmir Great Lakes Trek runs Jul-Sep and the road onward to Ladakh is fully open.

    MountainsTrekSnow

  • Tabo Himachal Pradesh

    Thousand-year-old mud monastery with intact Indo-Tibetan murals

    Tabo sits in rain shadow and stays dry, but July-August landslides in the Kinnaur gorge below can add a full day to the drive.

    HeritageSpiritualOffbeat

  • Drass Ladakh

    One of India's coldest towns, under the Kargil war ridges

    Jun-Sep is dry and 20 C by day, Tiger Hill and Tololing visible from the highway, the memorial busiest around 26 July.

    OffbeatMountainsHeritage

  • Srinagar Jammu and Kashmir

    Houseboats on Dal Lake, Mughal gardens, J&K's summer capital

    June stays lovely; Jul-Aug bring Amarnath Yatra convoys, checkpoints and higher prices, easing by Sep.

    LakesHeritageCulture

  • Bharmour (Manimahesh) Himachal Pradesh

    Gaddi shepherd capital with 84 temples under Manimahesh Kailash

    July is landslide month on the Chamba road, and the Aug-Sep Manimahesh yatra puts lakhs of pilgrims on a one-lane hill road.

    SpiritualTrekOffbeat

  • Hanle Ladakh

    India's first Dark Sky Reserve, home to a major observatory

    Practical peak - road open, homestays running, still-dark clear nights since Ladakh barely gets rain.

    OffbeatDesertWildlife

  • Yusmarg Jammu and Kashmir

    Pine-rimmed shepherd meadow and the walk to Nilnag lake

    Jun and Sep are the quiet good months; Jul-Aug is warm rather than wet but fills with Srinagar day-trippers and pony touts.

    MountainsOffbeatTrek

  • Chitkul Himachal Pradesh

    Last inhabited village toward the Indo-Tibet border

    Jun holds up; Jul-Aug bring rockfall-prone stretches on the Karcham-Sangla road, a real risk.

    MountainsOffbeatTrekSnow

  • Kargil Ladakh

    Highway garrison town, gateway to Suru Valley

    Normal transit season - highway fully open, a natural base for the Kargil War Memorial at Drass.

    HeritageCultureOffbeat

  • Bhaderwah Jammu and Kashmir

    Chenab-valley bowl of lavender fields and Nag temples

    June turns the lavender fields purple; Jul-Aug rain loosens the Batote-Doda road, then Sep clears for the maize harvest.

    MountainsOffbeatTrek

Common questions

Where should you travel in India in June?

47 of the 589 destinations in this catalogue are in peak season in June, with another 86 in shoulder season. The states with the most places at their best are Himachal Pradesh (16), Ladakh (13), Jammu and Kashmir (12) and Uttarakhand (4).

Where should you avoid in India in June?

432 places in this catalogue are marked avoid in June: closed park gates, snowed-in passes, cancelled ferries, landslide roads or plain 45 degree heat. Abhaneri (Chand Baori), Agartala & Unakoti, Agatti Island (Lakshadweep), Agra, Ahmedabad and Ahobilam are among them.

Is June a good time to visit India?

June falls in the monsoon season. It is a narrower month, roughly 8 per cent of the catalogue is at its best, so it pays to pick the region carefully rather than the country.
