Best places to visit in India in July

43 of 589 curated destinations are in peak season in July, and 354 are ones to stay away from. No listicle lies: every verdict here comes from the place's own season window.

Where India is at its best in July

Each line below is the reason this place works in the monsoon, written for that place, not a generic blurb.

  • Leh Ladakh

    Ladakh's backpacker capital and high-pass road-trip hub

    Peak season - both highways open, dry rain-shadow weather, hostels and cafes buzzing.

    MountainsBackpackerCulture

  • Kaza (Spiti) Himachal Pradesh

    Spiti's cold-desert capital - monasteries and moonscapes

    Spiti's real peak - a rain-shadow desert, so this is HP's warmest, driest, greenest window.

    MountainsOffbeatDesertCulture

  • Manasbal Lake Jammu and Kashmir

    Lotus-choked lake under Nur Jahan's Jarokha Bagh terrace

    Jul-Aug is peak lotus, solid pink across the north shore; Jun and Sep are quieter but still worth the shikara hour.

    LakesOffbeatWildlife

  • Bhandardara Maharashtra

    Dam-lake basecamp for Maharashtra's highest peak, Kalsubai

    Randha Falls and the valley go full postcard-green, though trails turn slick on weekends.

    MountainsLakesWaterfallsTrek

  • Shivanasamudra Falls Karnataka

    Twin Kaveri cataracts in full thunder after the rains

    Jul-Sep is the spectacle: Gaganachukki becomes a churning wall of whitewater and the viewing rails pack out on weekends.

    Waterfalls

  • Saputara Gujarat

    Gujarat's only hill station, on the Maharashtra border

    Valleys turn green and Gira Falls runs full - this is when it actually looks alpine.

    MountainsLakesOffbeat

  • Chitrakote Falls (Bastar) Chhattisgarh

    India's widest waterfall in the heart of tribal Bastar

    Chitrakote's real fame: Indravati floods the 300m ledge into a genuine roar; roads turn muddy though.

    WaterfallsOffbeatCulture

  • Chorla Ghat Goa

    Cloud-forest edge of Goa above the Vazra Sakla waterfall

    Jul-Sep is the point: Vazra Sakla thunders across the valley, moss and mist everywhere; expect rain most hours and pack for it.

    MountainsWaterfallsOffbeat

  • Athirappilly Falls Kerala

    An 80ft waterfall curtain at its thundering best in monsoon

    This is peak season here - the falls roar at full width; stick to viewing platforms, rocks are lethal

    WaterfallsTrek

  • Mandu Madhya Pradesh

    Ruined Malwa plateau city famous for its monsoon mist

    Mandu's real season: mist over Roopmati's pavilion, tanks fill, roads can get slick though.

    HeritageOffbeatCulture

  • Menal (Falls & Temples) Rajasthan

    Monsoon waterfall plunging into a gorge by 11th century temples

    Jul-Sep is the show: the Menali river leaps off the plateau in full spate with spray drifting over the shikharas; wet rock, so mind the rim.

    WaterfallsHeritageOffbeat

  • Courtallam (Kutralam) Tamil Nadu

    Nine falls that run when the rest of Tamil Nadu is bone dry

    Jul-Aug is the Saral season, when southwest monsoon spilling through the Shencottah gap fills all nine falls and packs them.

    WaterfallsOffbeat

  • Valley of Flowers Uttarakhand

    UNESCO alpine meadow of rare flora, open Jul-Sep only

    The only real window: park opens in June but flowers aren't out yet; peak bloom is Jul-Aug, fading by mid-Sep

    TrekWildlifeOffbeat

  • Markha Valley Ladakh

    Ladakh's classic homestay trek below Kang Yatse's white pyramid

    Jul-Sep is prime: rain-shadow dry trails, parachute-tent cafes up, and a homestay bed with dinner waiting in every hamlet.

    TrekOffbeatMountains

  • Chandratal Himachal Pradesh

    Crescent glacial lake at 4300 m on the Kunzum side of Spiti

    July-August is the actual season, dry in Lahaul's rain shadow, 5-18C by day and near freezing in a tent at night.

    LakesMountainsOffbeat

  • Naranag Jammu and Kashmir

    Ruined 8th-century Shiva temples at the Gangabal trailhead

    Jun-Sep is the trekking window: two days up to Gangabal under Harmukh, with guides and ponies hired in the village.

    TrekHeritageOffbeat

  • Igatpuri Maharashtra

    Vipassana meditation hub and monsoon trekking base

    Peak season for trekkers - Kalsubai and the ghats turn lush, though trails get slippery.

    MountainsSpiritualTrek

  • Agumbe Karnataka

    Rainforest village known as South India's Cherrapunji

    This is the season - 700cm+ of rain, roaring falls, king cobras out, but leeches everywhere.

    OffbeatWaterfallsWildlife

  • Wilson Hills (Dharampur) Gujarat

    Mist-soaked Western Ghats tableland above the Valsad plains

    Jul-Sep the plateau lives inside cloud: Shankar falls and the roadside cascades run full, everything drips green, and Surat mobs it on Sundays.

    MountainsOffbeat

  • Alchi (Sham Valley) Ladakh

    11th-century Kashmiri murals in a low Indus orchard village

    Jun-Sep stays dry with apricots ripening in July; the Choskor murals are unlit, so come when light reaches the doors.

    HeritageSpiritualOffbeat

  • Miyar Valley (Urgos) Himachal Pradesh

    Lahaul's glacier-headed side valley of July wildflower meadows

    July-September is peak and largely rain-shadowed: wildflower meadows, blue-sheep slopes and day walks toward the Miyar glacier.

    OffbeatTrekMountains

  • Sonamarg Jammu and Kashmir

    Meadow of Gold below Zoji La, base for the Great Lakes trek

    Prime season - the Kashmir Great Lakes Trek runs Jul-Sep and the road onward to Ladakh is fully open.

    MountainsTrekSnow

  • Lonavala Maharashtra

    Mumbai-Pune's monsoon getaway of waterfalls and forts

    This is what Lonavala is built for - waterfalls thunder and valleys turn electric green.

    MountainsWaterfalls

  • Gokak Falls Karnataka

    Horseshoe cataract with a century-old hanging bridge at its crest

    Jul-Sep is the event: a curtain nearly 170 m wide in full roar; the hanging bridge often shuts at peak flow, so you watch from the banks

    WaterfallsHeritageOffbeat

  • Aryan Valley (Dha & Garkon) Ladakh

    Flower-crowned Brokpa villages and apricot terraces on the Indus

    Jul-Aug barely sees rain in this rain-shadow gorge: apricots dry on rooftops, harvest in full swing, days around 25 C.

    OffbeatCultureMountains

  • Pangi Valley (Killar) Himachal Pradesh

    Himachal's remotest inhabited valley over the 4,400 m Sach Pass

    July-September is the season, the valley half in rain shadow; rain still makes the cliff-cut approach roads genuinely scary.

    OffbeatMountainsTrek

  • Gurez Valley (Dawar) Jammu and Kashmir

    Dard-Shin log-house valley on the Kishanganga behind Razdan Pass

    Jun-Sep is Gurez at its best: 8-22 C, dry-ish, Habba Khatoon peak lit at dusk, and every homestay in Dawar open.

    OffbeatMountainsCulture

  • Amboli Maharashtra

    Maharashtra's wettest hill station - falls and king cobras

    One of India's wettest hill stations - waterfalls roar and frogs and king cobras appear.

    MountainsWaterfallsOffbeat

  • Jog Falls Karnataka

    India's second-highest plunge waterfall on the Sharavathi

    The real deal - July-September the Sharavathi thunders down in full four-channel force.

    WaterfallsOffbeat

  • Lamayuru Ladakh

    Moonland badlands under one of Ladakh's oldest gompas

    Jun-Sep is dry high desert, not monsoon - best light on the moonland, homestays open, Yuru Kabgyat in June or July.

    HeritageSpiritualMountains

  • Pin Valley (Mudh) Himachal Pradesh

    Road-end village in the snow leopard valley below Pin-Parvati

    July-August is the valley's best month, dry in the rain shadow, with the Pin-Bhaba and Pin-Parvati crossings both open.

    OffbeatMountainsTrek

  • Warwan Valley (Inshan) Jammu and Kashmir

    Glacier-green valley over Margan Top that tourism never reached

    Jul-Sep is the window: barley-green villages, the icy Warwan river and day walks toward Sukhnai under light valley rain.

    OffbeatTrekMountains

  • Gaganbawda Maharashtra

    Kolhapur's rain-drenched ghat edge above the Konkan drop

    Jul-Sep is the show: the wettest corner of Kolhapur district, every cliff runs white and the Bhuibawada ghat drives through cloud.

    OffbeatMountainsWaterfalls

  • Nubra Valley Ladakh

    Cold-desert valley of sand dunes and quiet monasteries

    Prime time - Hunder's dunes, camel rides and Diskit monastery open up, easy loop with Turtuk.

    DesertMountainsOffbeat

  • Pangong Tso Ladakh

    The '3 Idiots' lake - a 134km turquoise line into Tibet

    Peak season for the famous turquoise water, though Jul-Aug can mean queues of cars at the viewpoint.

    LakesMountainsOffbeat

  • Turtuk Ladakh

    Balti village near the LoC, opened to tourists only in 2010

    Best time - apricot trees fruiting in July-August, full village life visible, easy add-on from Hunder.

    OffbeatCultureHeritage

Common questions

Where should you travel in India in July?

43 of the 589 destinations in this catalogue are in peak season in July, with another 95 in shoulder season. The states with the most places at their best are Ladakh (15), Maharashtra (5), Himachal Pradesh (5) and Jammu and Kashmir (5).

Where should you avoid in India in July?

354 places in this catalogue are marked avoid in July: closed park gates, snowed-in passes, cancelled ferries, landslide roads or plain 45 degree heat. Abbott Mount (Lohaghat), Agartala & Unakoti, Agatti Island (Lakshadweep), Ahmedabad, Ahobilam and Aizawl are among them.

Is July a good time to visit India?

July falls in the monsoon season. It is a narrower month, roughly 7 per cent of the catalogue is at its best, so it pays to pick the region carefully rather than the country.
