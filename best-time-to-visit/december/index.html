Best places to visit in India in December

438 of 589 curated destinations are in peak season in December, and 37 are ones to stay away from. No listicle lies: every verdict here comes from the place's own season window.

Where India is at its best in December

Each line below is the reason this place works in the winter, written for that place, not a generic blurb.

  • Pushkar Rajasthan

    Holy lake town, Rajasthan's backpacker capital

    Cool lake mornings, full-moon aarti, and the Camel Fair crowd. This is peak Pushkar.

    SpiritualBackpackerLakes

  • Gokarna Karnataka

    Temple town turned backpacker beach-hopping strip

    Calm sea, full shack scene across Om, Kudle and Paradise beaches, best weather of the year.

    BeachBackpackerSpiritual

  • Kanchipuram Tamil Nadu

    Pallava sandstone temples and silk looms on the same street

    Dec-Feb at 20-30 C is when you can walk Kailasanathar, Ekambareswarar and Varadaraja in one long unhurried day.

    HeritageSpiritualCulture

  • Varkala Kerala

    Cliffside beach town with yoga shalas and sunset cafes

    Peak season - calm turquoise water, warm days, cliff cafes and yoga shalas all in full swing

    BeachBackpackerSpiritual

  • Mumbai Maharashtra

    Colonial architecture, street food and Bollywood energy

    Best weather all year - dry and mild, ideal for walking Colaba and the Fort district.

    CityHeritage

  • Gwalior Madhya Pradesh

    Hilltop fort, Jain rock colossi and Tansen's dhrupad town

    Dec-Feb is 8-25 C and the fort's sandstone glows; Tansen Samaroh in December fills the town with dhrupad singers.

    HeritageCityCulture

  • Srisailam Andhra Pradesh

    Jyotirlinga above the Krishna gorge inside Nallamala forest

    Nov-Feb is the window: 28 C, the Krishna gorge clear from the Patalaganga ropeway and darshan queues manageable midweek.

    SpiritualHeritage

  • Fatehpur Sikri Uttar Pradesh

    Akbar's red sandstone capital, abandoned after fourteen years

    Peak at 7-24 °C: low sun on red sandstone and a near-empty Panch Mahal if you are through the gate by 7 am.

    HeritageCulture

  • Ahmedabad Gujarat

    UNESCO old city of pols, stepwells and street food

    Best weather of the year and kite-festival season - great for the old-city heritage walk.

    CityHeritageCulture

  • Bhubaneswar Odisha

    Temple city of 700 shrines plus the Khandagiri Jain caves

    Dec-Feb is the window: 15-28 C, dry mornings at Lingaraj and the Khandagiri caves, Adivasi Mela in January.

    CityHeritageSpiritual

  • Kolkata West Bengal

    India's cultural capital - colonial streets, great food

    Best months in the city. Cool, dry, Puja afterglow still in the air, easy all-day sightseeing.

    CityCultureHeritage

  • Chandubi Lake Assam

    Earthquake-born forest lake in Rabha country near Guwahati

    Nov-Feb is calm and clear at 10-25 C: winter ducks on the water, Rabha village walks, and the lakeside festival in early January.

    LakesOffbeatWildlife

  • Bodh Gaya Bihar

    Where the Buddha attained enlightenment - Mahabodhi Temple

    Peak pilgrim season: cool days, and occasional Dalai Lama teachings draw international crowds.

    SpiritualHeritage

  • Dawki & Shnongpdeng Meghalaya

    Glass-clear Umngot river: boating, cliff camps, border

    Peak clarity: the Umngot turns glass-clear turquoise, boats look like they float on air.

    LakesBackpackerOffbeat

  • Amritsar Punjab

    The Golden Temple and its free kitchen for all

    Cold mornings at the Golden Temple are atmospheric, especially the pre-dawn Palki ceremony.

    SpiritualCultureCity

  • Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep) Andaman & Nicobar Islands

    Andaman's backpacker-diving hub with Radhanagar Beach

    Best all-round season: calm water at Radhanagar, good dive visibility, full hostel scene.

    BeachIslandBackpacker

  • Anjuna-Vagator Goa

    North Goa's flea-market, trance-party, hostel strip

    Peak season - every shack, hostel and trance party is open, beaches packed with travellers.

    BeachPartyBackpacker

  • Deoghar (Baidyanath Dham) Jharkhand

    Jyotirlinga at the end of the 105 km Sultanganj kanwar walk

    Dec-Feb is the calm season at 8-26 C: short darshan queues, time to climb Trikut on foot and reach Basukinath.

    SpiritualHeritage

  • Bhongir Fort (Bhuvanagiri) Telangana

    An 11th-century fort welded to one bald granite dome

    Nov-Feb is the climbing season: cool granite, about 45 minutes to the top and the Deccan flat to the horizon.

    HeritageTrek

  • Jagdalpur (Bastar) Chhattisgarh

    Bastar's tribal capital, weekly haats and bell-metal workshops

    Nov-Feb is 12-30 C, the season for village haats, the anthropological museum and day trips out to Chitrakote.

    CultureCityOffbeat

  • Sultanpur National Park (Bird Sanctuary) Haryana

    Ramsar jheel where Siberian migrants land an hour from Delhi

    Dec-Feb is the whole point: over 100 migratory species on the jheel at 4-18 C, though Delhi smog greys the light.

    WildlifeLakesOffbeat

  • Aizawl Mizoram

    Mizoram's hillside capital: churches, cafes, rock music

    Best weather: cool, dry, clear views across the hill ridges the city is built on.

    CityCultureMountains

  • Khonoma Nagaland

    Angami stone village that banned hunting and kept its terraces

    Nov-Feb is clear at 4-18 C, the alder terraces are bare gold and Sekrenyi in late February is the village at full tilt.

    OffbeatCultureTrek

  • Manali Himachal Pradesh

    Himalayan backpacker basecamp for treks and easy adventure

    Dec brings snow and NYE crowds; Jan-Feb are colder, calmer, still fine for Solang snow trips.

    MountainsBackpackerTrekParty

  • Agatti Island (Lakshadweep) Lakshadweep

    Coral lagoon island - Lakshadweep's permit-only gateway

    Peak season - flat calm lagoon, best snorkeling visibility, though limited flights mean booking months ahead

    IslandBeachOffbeat

  • Chabimura Tripura

    Boat past giant gods carved into Gomati river cliffs

    Nov-Feb is ideal at 12-27 C: a cool green gorge and the huge Chakrak-ma Durga panel glowing in low sun from the boat.

    HeritageOffbeat

  • Nag Tibba Uttarakhand

    Weekend snow summit at 3020 m above Pantwari village

    December-March is the point of this trek: knee-deep snow on the ridge, -5C at camp, and Bandarpoonch clear to the north.

    TrekSnowMountains

  • Basar Arunachal Pradesh

    Galo valley of rice terraces, cane bridges and clean villages

    Dec-Feb is dry and mild at 5-22 C: cane bridges over clear streams, harvest-gold terraces and BasCon filling homestays in early December.

    OffbeatCultureTrek

  • Daman Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu

    Portuguese forts, dark-sand beaches and legal bars

    Nov-Feb at 15-30 °C is the time for the Moti Daman fort walls, the old churches and a walk on dark sand.

    BeachHeritage

  • Gulmarg Jammu and Kashmir

    India's top ski resort - Gondola to 3,979m, real powder

    Peak Gulmarg - reliable powder, Gondola to Apharwat Peak, India's best skiing and snowboarding scene.

    SnowMountains

  • Chandigarh Chandigarh

    India's planned modernist city, orderly and green

    Crisp and clear: the best season to walk Sukhna Lake and the Rock Garden.

    CityCulture

  • Delhi Delhi

    Mughal monuments and India's transit hub

    Pleasant days but dense smog and cold nights, especially December-January: carry a mask.

    CityHeritageCulture

  • Loktak Lake Manipur

    Floating phumdi islands, world's only floating park

    Best season: clear water, migratory birds, easiest sangai deer sightings on the phumdis.

    LakesWildlifeOffbeat

  • Pondicherry (incl. Auroville) Puducherry

    French Quarter beach town next to the Auroville commune

    Peak season - cool 20-29C, dry air, best for cycling the French Quarter and long beach walks

    BeachHeritageSpiritualBackpacker

  • Namchi Sikkim

    South Sikkim pilgrim town with a 108-ft Shiva and Char Dham

    December is cold and bright with big Kanchenjunga views; January-February brings fog and the town shuts early.

    SpiritualMountainsHeritage

  • Jaipur Rajasthan

    Pink City gateway to Rajasthan's forts and bazaars

    Peak season: crisp mornings, clear fort views, and the biggest crowds of the year.

    HeritageCityCulture

Common questions

Where should you travel in India in December?

438 of the 589 destinations in this catalogue are in peak season in December, with another 108 in shoulder season. The states with the most places at their best are Rajasthan (39), Karnataka (35), Tamil Nadu (31) and Maharashtra (30).

Where should you avoid in India in December?

37 places in this catalogue are marked avoid in December: closed park gates, snowed-in passes, cancelled ferries, landslide roads or plain 45 degree heat. Alchi (Sham Valley), Aryan Valley (Dha & Garkon), Badrinath & Mana, Bhaderwah, Bharmour (Manimahesh) and Chandratal are among them.

Is December a good time to visit India?

December falls in the winter season. It is one of the strong months: roughly 74 per cent of the catalogue is at its best.
