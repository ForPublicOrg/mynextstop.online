Best places to visit in India in March

135 of 745 curated destinations are in peak season in March, and 59 are ones to stay away from. No listicle lies: every verdict here comes from the place's own season window.

Where India is at its best in March

Each line below is the reason this place works in the summer, written for that place, not a generic blurb.

  • Kasol Himachal Pradesh

    Parvati valley's trance-backpacker hub, gateway to treks

    Prime season - Mar-May bring warm days, full guesthouses and a steady flow of backpackers.

    BackpackerPartyMountainsTrek

  • Rishikesh Uttarakhand

    Yoga capital and rafting hub on the Ganges

    March is pleasant, but April-May heat climbs fast and the ghats fill with day-trippers

    SpiritualBackpackerCulture

  • Shergaon Arunachal Pradesh

    Sherdukpen pine-forest village at 2,000 m, the quiet turn-off on the Tawang road

    Mar-May is orchid and rhododendron time in the pines; days reach 20 C, the forest walks are dry and the Eco Camp fills on weekends with Guwahati families

    CultureOffbeatMountains

  • Darjeeling West Bengal

    Queen of the Hills - tea, toy train, Kanchenjunga views

    Mar-Apr is peak: rhododendrons bloom and skies are clearest, but it's crowded, book ahead.

    MountainsHeritageCulture

  • Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep) Andaman & Nicobar Islands

    Andaman's backpacker-diving hub with Radhanagar Beach

    Hot and sticky (32C+) by April-May, but seas stay calm and diving visibility can peak pre-monsoon.

    BeachIslandBackpacker

  • Kolukkumalai Tamil Nadu

    Sunrise above a sea of clouds at the world's highest tea estate

    Apr-May mornings still deliver before haze builds; tour the 1930s orthodox tea factory through the warm middle hours

    MountainsOffbeat

  • Hoollongapar Gibbon Sanctuary (Jorhat) Assam

    India's only ape, in a rainforest patch cut by a railway line

    April-May is humid and full of insects, but the gibbons still call by 6 am, so go early and be out by mid-morning.

    WildlifeOffbeat

  • Gangtok Sikkim

    Sikkim's capital and gateway: monasteries, MG Marg cafés

    Mar-Apr is peak (flower festival, clearest skies); by May haze builds ahead of the monsoon.

    MountainsCityCulture

  • Agatti Island (Lakshadweep) Lakshadweep

    Coral lagoon island - Lakshadweep's permit-only gateway

    April is still workable with warm calm seas; by May pre-monsoon swell and humidity climb

    IslandBeachOffbeat

  • Benreu (Mount Pauna) Nagaland

    Zeliang village at 1,900 m under Mount Pauna, a moss-forest climb with Barail views

    March is still clear and cool for the 2-3 h climb; by April the plains haze creeps up and afternoon storms begin, though the moss forest is greenest and orchids are out.

    MountainsTrekOffbeatCulture

  • Barsana & Nandgaon Uttar Pradesh

    Braj hill villages where Lathmar Holi is fought with sticks

    Lathmar Holi falls in late Feb or March and is worth the chaos; after it Braj climbs past 45 °C and empties.

    CultureSpiritualOffbeat

  • Kunkeshwar (Devgad) Maharashtra

    Black stone Shiva temple standing on the Devgad sand

    Late February to April is alphonso season and the Shivratri yatra; May turns sticky at 34 C with the sea like a bath, so keep to dawn and dusk.

    BeachSpiritualOffbeat

  • Anandpur Sahib Punjab

    Birthplace of the Khalsa and the Nihang horsemen of Hola Mohalla

    March is the peak week for Hola Mohalla, then Apr-Jun bakes the Shivalik foothills at 42 C and empties out.

    SpiritualHeritageCulture

  • Morni Hills Haryana

    Chandigarh's nearest hills, low-key and quiet

    A genuine escape from Chandigarh's plains heat, several degrees cooler up in the hills.

    MountainsOffbeatLakes

  • Bhadra Tiger Reserve (Muthodi) Karnataka

    Teak and shola tiger reserve with a rest house at the gate

    Mar-Apr is the sighting season as the forest dries and everything walks to the Bhadra backwater; May hits 35 C with evening storms.

    WildlifeOffbeatTrek

  • Jowai (Nartiang & Krangsuri) Meghalaya

    Jaintia monolith field, a Shakti Peetha and blue Krangsuri pools

    March-April is warm and green and Krangsuri holds its turquoise colour right up until the first heavy rains.

    OffbeatHeritageWaterfalls

  • Tamdil Lake (Saitual) Mizoram

    Forest-rimmed lake at 780 m near Saitual, with a lakeside lodge and kayaks

    March is cool and clear enough for the forest trails; April and May turn hazy and humid, though at 780 m the lake stays cooler than Aizawl and the boats still run.

    LakesOffbeatWildlife

  • Gulmarg Jammu and Kashmir

    India's top ski resort - Gondola to 3,979m, real powder

    March can still have late-season snow; by May meadows green up for golf and short hikes.

    SnowMountains

  • Thattekad (Salim Ali Bird Sanctuary) Kerala

    Salim Ali's richest peninsular birding forest on the Periyar

    March still delivers with the leaf litter dry and birds pinned to water, but by May it is 37°C and walks have to end by nine.

    WildlifeOffbeat

  • Mount Abu Rajasthan

    Rajasthan's only hill station, oasis from the heat

    Peak season for a reason. The one hill station where plains-dwellers escape 45C heat.

    MountainsLakesTrek

  • Achanakmar Tiger Reserve (Maikal Hills) Chhattisgarh

    Hilly sal forest and Baiga hamlets on the road to Amarkantak

    March stays cool at dawn and April pulls gaur and sloth bear to the waterholes; by May the ridge bakes past 42C and dust hangs over the track.

    WildlifeMountainsOffbeat

  • Velavadar (Blackbuck National Park) Gujarat

    Grassland savanna of blackbuck, wolves and harrier roosts

    Apr-May bakes at 43 °C on shadeless grassland; safaris run at dawn only, then the gates shut for the rains in June.

    WildlifeOffbeat

  • Rumbak (Hemis National Park) Ladakh

    Snow leopard homestay village inside Hemis National Park

    March still holds cats close to the village; through April and May the thaw sets in, the herds move up and the valley empties.

    WildlifeTrekOffbeatMountains

  • Amarkantak Madhya Pradesh

    Source of the Narmada on a 1,000 m Maikal plateau

    At 1,043 m Mar-May tops out near 35 C, far kinder than Bilaspur below, and Kapildhara still has some water in it.

    SpiritualMountainsOffbeat

  • Willong Khullen Manipur

    Maram Naga ridge village ringed by 7 m menhirs no one counts the same way twice

    Mar-Apr is dry with peach and plum blossom in the village and haze from jhum burning; May brings the first storms and the road starts to soften

    HeritageOffbeatCultureMountains

  • Trishna Wildlife Sanctuary (Belonia) Tripura

    Sal forest and grassland where Tripura's gaur herds graze at dawn

    Mar-Apr give the best sightings as water shrinks to a few pools, but it is 33 C by noon and storms roll in from late April; May is hot, humid and empty

    WildlifeOffbeat

  • McLeod Ganj Himachal Pradesh

    Dalai Lama's home-in-exile - Tibetan culture hub

    Peak season - Mar-May bring warm days, packed meditation courses and full Dharamkot guesthouses.

    SpiritualCultureBackpackerMountains

  • Haridwar Uttarakhand

    Har Ki Pauri, the ghat where the Ganga leaves the hills

    March is fine at 20-32C, but by May-June it is 42C on open stone and the evening aarti crush becomes genuinely unpleasant.

    SpiritualHeritageCity

  • Ziro Valley Arunachal Pradesh

    Apatani tribal valley of paddy fields and music festival

    Lovely window: paddy fields turning green, pleasant days before the rains set in.

    CultureOffbeatBackpacker

  • Sandakphu West Bengal

    West Bengal's highest point - sunrise over 4 of 5 top peaks

    Apr rhododendrons carpet the ridge red and pink; by May haze builds as monsoon approaches.

    TrekMountainsSnow

  • Neil Island (Shaheed Dweep) Andaman & Nicobar Islands

    The quiet, bicycle-friendly Andaman island next door

    Warm and humid through April-May but ferries usually still run. Fewer crowds than Havelock.

    BeachIsland

  • Manapad Tamil Nadu

    Black rock cape, a church of 1581 and a lagoon built for kiting

    March is the last good month; by Apr-May the black rock cape radiates 37 C and the wind drops between the two monsoons.

    BeachOffbeatHeritage

  • Kaziranga National Park Assam

    One-horned rhino capital: jeep and elephant safaris

    May is the tail end before closure. Hot, grass tall, park shuts by month's end.

    Wildlife

  • Namchi Sikkim

    South Sikkim pilgrim town with a 108-ft Shiva and Char Dham

    March-April is warm, flowering and clear; by late May the pre-monsoon build-up greys out the ridge most afternoons.

    SpiritualMountainsHeritage

  • Bangaram Island Lakshadweep

    Uninhabited coral islet with a glowing night lagoon

    April is hot, still and doable; by May the pre-monsoon swell builds and the Agatti boat transfer starts getting cancelled.

    IslandBeachOffbeat

  • Khonoma Nagaland

    Angami stone village that banned hunting and kept its terraces

    March-April is warm with rhododendron on the tragopan sanctuary ridge before the pre-monsoon cloud closes in.

    OffbeatCultureTrek

Common questions

Where should you travel in India in March?

135 of the 745 destinations in this catalogue are in peak season in March, with another 518 in shoulder season. The states with the most places at their best are Himachal Pradesh (19), Uttarakhand (16), Arunachal Pradesh (10) and West Bengal (9).

Where should you avoid in India in March?

59 places in this catalogue are marked avoid in March: closed park gates, snowed-in passes, cancelled ferries, landslide roads or plain 45 degree heat. Agumbe, Athirappilly Falls, Badami-Aihole-Pattadakal, Badrinath & Mana, Bangus Valley (Bungus) and Bhandardara are among them.

Is March a good time to visit India?

March falls in the summer season. It is a narrower month, roughly 18 per cent of the catalogue is at its best, so it pays to pick the region carefully rather than the country.
