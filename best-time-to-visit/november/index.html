Best places to visit in India in November

430 of 589 curated destinations are in peak season in November, and 31 are ones to stay away from. No listicle lies: every verdict here comes from the place's own season window.

Where India is at its best in November

Each line below is the reason this place works in the autumn, written for that place, not a generic blurb.

  • Pushkar Rajasthan

    Holy lake town, Rajasthan's backpacker capital

    The Camel Fair in November draws huge crowds; October is calmer and still pleasant.

    SpiritualBackpackerLakes

  • Gokarna Karnataka

    Temple town turned backpacker beach-hopping strip

    October's a hidden gem - cliffs still green, sea calming, a fraction of the December crowd.

    BeachBackpackerSpiritual

  • Mumbai Maharashtra

    Colonial architecture, street food and Bollywood energy

    Ganpati energy fills September; October settles into pleasant post-monsoon weather.

    CityHeritage

  • Gwalior Madhya Pradesh

    Hilltop fort, Jain rock colossi and Tansen's dhrupad town

    Oct-Nov is the easy window - 30 C days, clear light on Jai Vilas and the Sas-Bahu temples, and no festival crush.

    HeritageCityCulture

  • Darjeeling West Bengal

    Queen of the Hills - tea, toy train, Kanchenjunga views

    Best window of the year: post-monsoon clarity, Kanchenjunga visible daily, Puja-season crowds.

    MountainsHeritageCulture

  • Rishikesh Uttarakhand

    Yoga capital and rafting hub on the Ganges

    Best window of the year. Rafting resumes, skies clear, the whole backpacker scene wakes up

    SpiritualBackpackerCulture

  • Ahmedabad Gujarat

    UNESCO old city of pols, stepwells and street food

    Heat breaks by October, comfortable and clear heading into festival season.

    CityHeritageCulture

  • Alleppey (Alappuzha) Backwaters Kerala

    Venice of the East - houseboats through the backwaters

    October is gorgeous - lush paddy fields, comfortable temps, thinning crowds before winter rush

    LakesCulture

  • Fatehpur Sikri Uttar Pradesh

    Akbar's red sandstone capital, abandoned after fourteen years

    Oct-Nov is the sweet spot: clean air, green Sikri ridge, and the Agra day-trip rush thins out after 4 pm.

    HeritageCulture

  • Kasol Himachal Pradesh

    Parvati valley's trance-backpacker hub, gateway to treks

    The busiest, best window of the year - clear valley views, trance parties, easy trekking weather.

    BackpackerPartyMountainsTrek

  • Srisailam Andhra Pradesh

    Jyotirlinga above the Krishna gorge inside Nallamala forest

    Oct-Nov the reservoir is full after the rains and the forest is green, with pilgrim numbers still manageable.

    SpiritualHeritage

  • Bhubaneswar Odisha

    Temple city of 700 shrines plus the Khandagiri Jain caves

    October dries out fast and November is near-perfect, though a Bay of Bengal cyclone can still eat a week.

    CityHeritageSpiritual

  • Dawki & Shnongpdeng Meghalaya

    Glass-clear Umngot river: boating, cliff camps, border

    October water is still settling from monsoon silt, better from November onward.

    LakesBackpackerOffbeat

  • Chandubi Lake Assam

    Earthquake-born forest lake in Rabha country near Guwahati

    October steadies and stays green; by November the first migrants land and weekdays give you the whole lake to yourself.

    LakesOffbeatWildlife

  • Bodh Gaya Bihar

    Where the Buddha attained enlightenment - Mahabodhi Temple

    Weather turns pleasant by November, a good lead-in to the winter pilgrim season.

    SpiritualHeritage

  • Dassam Falls Jharkhand

    The Kanchi river's clean 44 m curtain drop near Taimara village

    Oct-Nov is best: post-monsoon volume with drier, safer steps and soft light through the Taimara valley forest.

    WaterfallsOffbeat

  • Gangtok Sikkim

    Sikkim's capital and gateway: monasteries, MG Marg cafés

    Post-monsoon clarity returns: the best combination of clear peaks and reliable road access.

    MountainsCityCulture

  • Tiruvannamalai Tamil Nadu

    Barefoot 14 km girivalam loop around a hill read as a lingam

    Oct-Nov brings rain and then Karthigai Deepam, when a million pilgrims arrive and every room in town is long gone.

    SpiritualTrekBackpacker

  • Basar Arunachal Pradesh

    Galo valley of rice terraces, cane bridges and clean villages

    Oct-Nov dries the valley into harvest gold with clear skies for village walks; book homestays early if you plan to stay on for BasCon.

    OffbeatCultureTrek

  • Anjuna-Vagator Goa

    North Goa's flea-market, trance-party, hostel strip

    Shacks rebuild through October; by November the season is back in full swing.

  • Amritsar Punjab

    The Golden Temple and its free kitchen for all

    Comfortable temperatures building toward Diwali, when the temple is spectacularly lit.

    SpiritualCultureCity

  • Bhongir Fort (Bhuvanagiri) Telangana

    An 11th-century fort welded to one bald granite dome

    Oct-Nov is clear and quiet on weekdays; you can do the fort and be back in Hyderabad for dinner by train.

    HeritageTrek

  • Jagdalpur (Bastar) Chhattisgarh

    Bastar's tribal capital, weekly haats and bell-metal workshops

    October is the peak - the Dussehra rath is dragged through town, Danteshwari's rituals run for days, book beds early.

    CultureCityOffbeat

  • Dzukou Valley Nagaland

    Rolling green trekking valley famous for summer lilies

    The sweet spot: late blooms linger, then the valley turns lush green with the clearest views.

    TrekMountainsOffbeat

  • Aizawl Mizoram

    Mizoram's hillside capital: churches, cafes, rock music

    October skies clear up nicely, a good shoulder window with fewer visitors.

    CityCultureMountains

  • Chabimura Tripura

    Boat past giant gods carved into Gomati river cliffs

    Oct-Nov is lush from the rains with a full, calm river: the best light and water levels of the whole year.

    HeritageOffbeat

  • Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep) Andaman & Nicobar Islands

    Andaman's backpacker-diving hub with Radhanagar Beach

    Sea starts settling in October though still choppy early on; hostels reopen, notably quieter.

    BeachIslandBackpacker

  • Daman Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu

    Portuguese forts, dark-sand beaches and legal bars

    October dries out and by November the light is clean; weekends stay loud with drinkers, weekdays are calm.

    BeachHeritage

  • Pinjore (Yadavindra Gardens) Haryana

    Seven-terrace Mughal water garden at the foot of the Shivaliks

    Oct-Nov is the sweet spot with clear Shivalik air, 12-28 C and the Bhima Devi carvings free of tour buses.

    HeritageOffbeatCulture

  • Mansar Lake Jammu and Kashmir

    Forest-ringed Shivalik lake with a Sheshnag shrine and turtles

    Oct dries out and cools, then Nov is properly good: wetland birds arriving at Surinsar-Mansar and boating back on.

    LakesSpiritualOffbeat

  • Chandigarh Chandigarh

    India's planned modernist city, orderly and green

    Mild and clear: a comfortable time to explore the sector grid on foot.

    CityCulture

  • Delhi Delhi

    Mughal monuments and India's transit hub

    The best window: clearer skies and mild temperatures before winter smog sets back in.

    CityHeritageCulture

  • Loktak Lake Manipur

    Floating phumdi islands, world's only floating park

    October water is still settling post-monsoon, boat trips resume through the month.

    LakesWildlifeOffbeat

  • Jaipur Rajasthan

    Pink City gateway to Rajasthan's forts and bazaars

    Warm days easing into pleasant nights. A good transition before the winter rush.

    HeritageCityCulture

  • Hampi Karnataka

    Vijayanagara ruins amid giant granite boulders

    Post-monsoon green fading to gold, cool temps arriving - genuinely the sweet spot of the year.

    HeritageOffbeat

    Takht Sachkhand Hazur Sahib, a Sikh takht on the Godavari

    Gurta Gaddi Diwas lights up the takht in Oct or Nov and sangats arrive from Punjab by whole trainloads.

Common questions

Where should you travel in India in November?

430 of the 589 destinations in this catalogue are in peak season in November, with another 121 in shoulder season. The states with the most places at their best are Rajasthan (36), Karnataka (35), Maharashtra (30) and Madhya Pradesh (27).

Where should you avoid in India in November?

31 places in this catalogue are marked avoid in November: closed park gates, snowed-in passes, cancelled ferries, landslide roads or plain 45 degree heat. Chandratal, Dhankar, Drass, Gulmarg, Gurez Valley (Dawar) and Hanle are among them.

Is November a good time to visit India?

November falls in the autumn season. It is one of the strong months: roughly 73 per cent of the catalogue is at its best.
