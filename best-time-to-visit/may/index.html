Best places to visit in India in May

68 of 589 curated destinations are in peak season in May, and 358 are ones to stay away from. No listicle lies: every verdict here comes from the place's own season window.

Where India is at its best in May

Each line below is the reason this place works in the summer, written for that place, not a generic blurb.

  • Kasol Himachal Pradesh

    Parvati valley's trance-backpacker hub, gateway to treks

    Prime season - Mar-May bring warm days, full guesthouses and a steady flow of backpackers.

  • Almora Uttarakhand

    Understated Kumaoni market town with Himalayan views

    Warm days, cool nights: a calmer, cheaper alternative to Nainital's summer crowds

  • Aharbal Falls Jammu and Kashmir

    The Veshaw crashes 25 m through a pine gorge, Kashmir's Niagara

    Apr-Jun snowmelt is peak thunder: the Veshaw explodes through the notch and spray soaks the lower ledge, go early on weekends.

    WaterfallsMountainsOffbeat

  • Ziro Valley Arunachal Pradesh

    Apatani tribal valley of paddy fields and music festival

    Lovely window: paddy fields turning green, pleasant days before the rains set in.

    Misty Palani lake town loved by trekkers, backpackers

  • Alchi (Sham Valley) Ladakh

    11th-century Kashmiri murals in a low Indus orchard village

Common questions

Where should you travel in India in May?

68 of the 589 destinations in this catalogue are in peak season in May, with another 103 in shoulder season. The states with the most places at their best are Himachal Pradesh (29), Uttarakhand (16), Jammu and Kashmir (10) and Arunachal Pradesh (4).

Where should you avoid in India in May?

358 places in this catalogue are marked avoid in May: closed park gates, snowed-in passes, cancelled ferries, landslide roads or plain 45 degree heat. Abhaneri (Chand Baori), Agartala & Unakoti, Agra, Agumbe, Ahmedabad and Ahobilam are among them.

Is May a good time to visit India?

May falls in the summer season. It is a narrower month, roughly 12 per cent of the catalogue is at its best, so it pays to pick the region carefully rather than the country.
