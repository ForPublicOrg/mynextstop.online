How the seasons work here

Winter runs December to February, summer March to May, the monsoon June to September, and autumn October to November. Every destination carries its own peak, shoulder and avoid months on top of that, because a monsoon month in the Western Ghats and a monsoon month in Ladakh are not the same trip.
