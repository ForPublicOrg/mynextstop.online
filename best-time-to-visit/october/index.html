Common questions

Where should you travel in India in October?

249 of the 745 destinations in this catalogue are in peak season in October, with another 454 in shoulder season. The states with the most places at their best are Himachal Pradesh (42), Uttarakhand (35), Karnataka (18) and Jammu and Kashmir (15).

Where should you avoid in India in October?

18 places in this catalogue are marked avoid in October: closed park gates, snowed-in passes, cancelled ferries, landslide roads or plain 45 degree heat. Agasthyakoodam (Athirumala), Barnawapara Sanctuary, Dudhwa National Park, Kanyakumari, Katarniaghat Wildlife Sanctuary and Mahabalipuram (Mamallapuram) are among them.

Is October a good time to visit India?

October falls in the autumn season. It is one of the strong months: roughly 33 per cent of the catalogue is at its best.
