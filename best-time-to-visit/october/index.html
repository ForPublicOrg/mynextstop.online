Where India is at its best in October

Each line below is the reason this place works in the autumn, written for that place, not a generic blurb.

    Parvati valley's trance-backpacker hub, gateway to treks

    The busiest, best window of the year - clear valley views, trance parties, easy trekking weather.

Common questions

Where should you travel in India in October?

189 of the 589 destinations in this catalogue are in peak season in October, with another 371 in shoulder season. The states with the most places at their best are Himachal Pradesh (36), Uttarakhand (25), Karnataka (15) and Madhya Pradesh (11).

Where should you avoid in India in October?

15 places in this catalogue are marked avoid in October: closed park gates, snowed-in passes, cancelled ferries, landslide roads or plain 45 degree heat. Barnawapara Sanctuary, Dudhwa National Park, Kanyakumari, Katarniaghat Wildlife Sanctuary, Mahabalipuram (Mamallapuram) and Manapad are among them.

Is October a good time to visit India?

October falls in the autumn season. It is a narrower month, roughly 32 per cent of the catalogue is at its best, so it pays to pick the region carefully rather than the country.
