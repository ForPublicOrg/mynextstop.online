Common questions

How many trek destinations are there in India?

This catalogue lists 123 tagged trek, across 25 states and union territories. It is a curated list, not an exhaustive one: places earn a spot on the strength of the landscape or the heritage.

When is the best time for a trek trip in India?

January, November and December put the most of them in peak season. Each place carries its own window, though, so check the one you are aiming at.

Which states have the most trek destinations?

Himachal Pradesh (20), Kerala (10), Maharashtra (10), Uttarakhand (9) and Karnataka (9).
