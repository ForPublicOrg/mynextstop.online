Common questions

How many beach destinations are there in India?

This catalogue lists 47 tagged beach, across 13 states and union territories. It is a curated list, not an exhaustive one: places earn a spot on the strength of the landscape or the heritage.

When is the best time for a beach trip in India?

January, February and December put the most of them in peak season. Each place carries its own window, though, so check the one you are aiming at.

Which states have the most beach destinations?

Maharashtra (6), Kerala (6), Tamil Nadu (6), Karnataka (5) and Lakshadweep (4).
