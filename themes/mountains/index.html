Common questions

How many mountains destinations are there in India?

This catalogue lists 165 tagged mountains, across 25 states and union territories. It is a curated list, not an exhaustive one: places earn a spot on the strength of the landscape or the heritage.

When is the best time for a mountains trip in India?

April, October and November put the most of them in peak season. Each place carries its own window, though, so check the one you are aiming at.

Which states have the most mountains destinations?

Himachal Pradesh (35), Uttarakhand (21), Maharashtra (12), Jammu and Kashmir (11) and Tamil Nadu (10).
