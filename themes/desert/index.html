Common questions

How many desert destinations are there in India?

This catalogue lists 16 tagged desert, across 4 states and union territories. It is a curated list, not an exhaustive one: places earn a spot on the strength of the landscape or the heritage.

When is the best time for a desert trip in India?

January, February and November put the most of them in peak season. Each place carries its own window, though, so check the one you are aiming at.

Which states have the most desert destinations?

Rajasthan (10), Gujarat (3), Ladakh (2) and Himachal Pradesh (1).
