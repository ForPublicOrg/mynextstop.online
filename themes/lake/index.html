Common questions

How many lakes destinations are there in India?

This catalogue lists 78 tagged lakes, across 26 states and union territories. It is a curated list, not an exhaustive one: places earn a spot on the strength of the landscape or the heritage.

When is the best time for a lakes trip in India?

January, February and December put the most of them in peak season. Each place carries its own window, though, so check the one you are aiming at.

Which states have the most lakes destinations?

Kerala (7), Rajasthan (7), Himachal Pradesh (7), Tamil Nadu (7) and Maharashtra (5).
