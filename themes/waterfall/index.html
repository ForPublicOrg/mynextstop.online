Common questions

How many waterfalls destinations are there in India?

This catalogue lists 48 tagged waterfalls, across 19 states and union territories. It is a curated list, not an exhaustive one: places earn a spot on the strength of the landscape or the heritage.

When is the best time for a waterfalls trip in India?

October, November and December put the most of them in peak season. Each place carries its own window, though, so check the one you are aiming at.

Which states have the most waterfalls destinations?

Karnataka (7), Maharashtra (6), Meghalaya (4), Tamil Nadu (4) and Odisha (4).
