Common questions

How many heritage destinations are there in India?

This catalogue lists 245 tagged heritage, across 31 states and union territories. It is a curated list, not an exhaustive one: places earn a spot on the strength of the landscape or the heritage.

When is the best time for a heritage trip in India?

January, February and December put the most of them in peak season. Each place carries its own window, though, so check the one you are aiming at.

Which states have the most heritage destinations?

Rajasthan (30), Uttar Pradesh (20), Tamil Nadu (18), Gujarat (17) and Karnataka (17).
