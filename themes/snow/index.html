Common questions

How many snow destinations are there in India?

This catalogue lists 17 tagged snow, across 6 states and union territories. It is a curated list, not an exhaustive one: places earn a spot on the strength of the landscape or the heritage.

When is the best time for a snow trip in India?

April, May and October put the most of them in peak season. Each place carries its own window, though, so check the one you are aiming at.

Which states have the most snow destinations?

Himachal Pradesh (6), Uttarakhand (4), Jammu and Kashmir (3), Sikkim (2) and Arunachal Pradesh (1).
