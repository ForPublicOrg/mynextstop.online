Common questions

How many spiritual destinations are there in India?

This catalogue lists 117 tagged spiritual, across 25 states and union territories. It is a curated list, not an exhaustive one: places earn a spot on the strength of the landscape or the heritage.

When is the best time for a spiritual trip in India?

February, November and December put the most of them in peak season. Each place carries its own window, though, so check the one you are aiming at.

Which states have the most spiritual destinations?

Uttar Pradesh (11), Karnataka (10), Himachal Pradesh (9), Maharashtra (9) and Tamil Nadu (9).
