Common questions

How many culture destinations are there in India?

This catalogue lists 142 tagged culture, across 33 states and union territories. It is a curated list, not an exhaustive one: places earn a spot on the strength of the landscape or the heritage.

When is the best time for a culture trip in India?

January, November and December put the most of them in peak season. Each place carries its own window, though, so check the one you are aiming at.

Which states have the most culture destinations?

Rajasthan (12), Kerala (11), Himachal Pradesh (10), Uttar Pradesh (9) and Gujarat (9).
